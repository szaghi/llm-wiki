# Results

Theorems, proved properties and barriers.

* [A-stability barriers for decoupled and internally consistent multirate methods](/results/multirate-a-stability-barriers.md) — Two impossibility theorems for multirate and GARK methods: no consistent decoupled GARK method is A-stable on the real 2D test problem, and an internally consistent multirate GARK method of order exactly one is scalar A-stable for only finitely many multirate ratios.
* [Energy stability of the six-step BDF method](/results/bdf6-energy-stability.md) — Akrivis, Chen, Yu and Zhou prove by the energy technique that six-step BDF is unconditionally stable for linear parabolic problems: no Nevanlinna–Odeh multiplier exists for BDF6, but the relaxed multiplier (13/9, -25/36, 1/9) with Toeplitz positivity suffices, and the estimate extends to forcing and time-dependent operators.
* [Equivalence of DeC and ADER](/results/dec-ader-equivalence.md) — Modern ADER is a deferred-correction scheme with low-order operator Dy − r(y_n), and DeC is an ADER-type Galerkin scheme with characteristic-function test functions; via Abgrall's contraction lemma, ADER with M+1 time nodes and K iterations has order min(M+1, K).
* [Negative-coefficient barrier for splitting methods](/results/negative-coefficient-barrier.md) — Every splitting or composition method with real coefficients and order at least 3 has at least one negative $a_j$ and at least one negative $b_j$, so some sub-flow runs backward in time; the page gives the proof, the consequences for parabolic and irreversible problems, and the three escapes (commutator flows, complex coefficients, processing or effective order).
* [Order conditions of splitting and composition methods](/results/splitting-order-conditions.md) — The order conditions of splitting and composition methods, in four equivalent forms (BCH in a Lyndon basis, words and shuffles, Lyndon multi-indices in $b_j$ and cumulative times, odd-index multi-indices for compositions), with the numbers of independent conditions per order for general, RKN and symmetric-composition methods.
* [Runge–Kutta order conditions and simplifying assumptions](/results/runge-kutta-order-conditions.md) — A Runge–Kutta method has order p iff its elementary weights equal 1/γ(τ) for every rooted tree of at most p vertices; the page gives the conditions to order five in matrix form, their growth with p, simplifying assumptions B and C, related condition families, numerical constructions, stage-count barriers and records, and the extensions to partitioned, additive and multiderivative methods.
* [Stability of variable-step BDF2 under step-ratio bounds](/results/variable-step-bdf2-stability.md) — Variable-step BDF2 is proved stable under bounds on the step ratios, and each bound secures a different estimate: zero-stability for ratios below 1 + sqrt 2 (sharp), a parabolic energy estimate for ratios up to 1.9398 (two multipliers), and robust Navier–Stokes error bounds under the ratio and ratio-variation hypotheses of García-Archilla and Novo.
* [Unconditional strong stability of diagonal Padé schemes and its energy law](/results/diagonal-pade-strong-stability.md) — Sun, Wei and Wu prove that every diagonal $(m,m)$ Padé approximation, the stability function of the $m$-stage Gauss method, obeys on linear seminegative problems an exact energy law that truncates the continuous one with the same weights, giving unconditional strong stability and energy-dissipation error $O(h^{2m})$.
* [Uniform accuracy of IMEX schemes for hyperbolic relaxation systems](/results/uniform-accuracy-relaxation-systems.md) — For linear hyperbolic relaxation systems with Fourier–Galerkin space discretization, certain IMEX Runge–Kutta schemes of orders 2 and 3 and IMEX-BDF schemes of orders 1 to 4 converge with error constants independent of the relaxation time ε, so they keep their order in the intermediate regime where IMEX schemes usually lose it.
* [Weak-stage-order barriers](/results/weak-stage-order-barriers.md) — Order barriers relating stages $s$, order $p$, weak stage order $q_{\mathrm w}$ and the number of distinct abscissae for general, diagonally implicit and explicit Runge–Kutta methods, derived from the orthogonality of the invariant subspaces generated by the weights and by the stage-order residuals; includes the explicit bound $p+q_{\mathrm w}\le s+1$.

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Benjamin–Bona–Mahony equation](/benchmarks/benjamin-bona-mahony-equation.md) — The BBM equation u_t + u_x + u u_x − u_txx = 0 with periodic solitary-wave solutions and linear, quadratic and cubic invariants; its semidiscretizations test invariant preservation by Gauss–Legendre, relaxation and pseudo-energy-preserving methods.
* [Brusselator](/benchmarks/brusselator.md) — The one-dimensional advection–diffusion–reaction Brusselator, a three-species stiff reaction system with a fast relaxation term, is the shared test of IMEX, multirate and nonlinear-solver studies; the page gives the variants, parameters and splittings used and what each test showed.
* [Burgers equation](/benchmarks/burgers-equation.md) — Inviscid Burgers u_t + (u²/2)_x = 0, whose smooth data steepen into a shock, and viscous Burgers with a manufactured solution; semidiscretized by entropy-conservative or entropy-stable DG they test relaxation, superviscosity filtering, IMEX and multirate entropy behaviour, and order reduction.
* [Isentropic vortex](/benchmarks/isentropic-vortex.md) — Exact smooth solution of the compressible Euler equations, a vortex advected by a uniform stream, used to measure temporal convergence order, conservation and cost of time integrators: explicit pairs with PID control, fully implicit Runge–Kutta methods, paired and many-stage stabilized explicit methods, and relaxation ADER-DG.
* [Kepler problem](/benchmarks/kepler-problem.md) — The planar two-body problem with three invariants (energy, angular momentum, Laplace–Runge–Lenz vector), and its perturbed variant with a precessing orbit, used to test invariant-preserving relaxation methods and the modified equation of Störmer–Verlet.
* [Prothero–Robinson problem](/benchmarks/prothero-robinson-problem.md) — The scalar stiff test problem $y'=\lambda(y-g(t))+g'(t)$, whose smooth exact solution $g$ is independent of the stiffness $\lambda$; it isolates the stiff-regime error of a one-step method, exposes order reduction through the stage-order residuals, and grounds weak stage order and Scholz's Rosenbrock conditions. Includes the multirate Kværnø–Prothero–Robinson variant.
* [Robertson problem](/benchmarks/robertson-problem.md) — Robertson's three-species chemical kinetics system, with rate constants spanning nine orders of magnitude and solutions integrated to t = 1e5 or 1e11; a stiff test with real negative Jacobian eigenvalues on which explicit methods fail and implicit deferred-correction and ADER variants are compared.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — Three-dimensional periodic vortex flow used to test time integrators on compressible-flow semidiscretizations: inviscid at low Mach number as a stability-limited test for explicit pairs and step-size controllers, and viscous at Re 1600 for optimized many-stage Runge–Kutta methods.
* [Van der Pol oscillator](/benchmarks/van-der-pol-oscillator.md) — The nonlinear relaxation oscillator y1' = y2, y2' = mu (1 - y1^2) y2 - y1, whose parameter mu tunes it from non-stiff to very stiff; the sources use it to test step-size controllers and to show that weak stage order and deferred correction do not keep their design orders on a stiff nonlinear problem.

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Allen–Cahn equation](/benchmarks/allen-cahn-equation.md) — The Allen–Cahn reaction–diffusion equation, a stiff semilinear phase-field model with interface width epsilon; the sources use a 1D travelling front, a 2D forced shrinking circle and a 1D double-well problem to test diagonal SDC, adaptive SDC and Parareal with optimized coarse propagators.
* [Benjamin–Bona–Mahony equation](/benchmarks/benjamin-bona-mahony-equation.md) — The BBM equation u_t + u_x + u u_x − u_txx = 0 with periodic solitary-wave solutions and linear, quadratic and cubic invariants; its semidiscretizations test invariant preservation by Gauss–Legendre, relaxation and pseudo-energy-preserving methods.
* [Broadwell model](/benchmarks/broadwell-model.md) — Three-velocity discrete kinetic model whose stiff collision term relaxes to a local Maxwellian with relaxation time ε; used nonlinear and linearized to test positivity, asymptotic preservation and uniform accuracy of IMEX time integrators from the kinetic to the fluid regime.
* [Brusselator](/benchmarks/brusselator.md) — The one-dimensional advection–diffusion–reaction Brusselator, a three-species stiff reaction system with a fast relaxation term, is the shared test of IMEX, multirate and nonlinear-solver studies; the original six-species reaction ODE serves as a production–destruction test; the page gives the variants, parameters and splittings used and what each test showed.
* [Burgers equation](/benchmarks/burgers-equation.md) — Inviscid Burgers u_t + (u²/2)_x = 0, whose smooth data steepen into a shock, and viscous Burgers with manufactured or periodic solutions; the sources use it to test relaxation, superviscosity filtering, IMEX and multirate entropy behaviour, order reduction from boundary data, TASE stability, bound-preserving limiting of high-order Runge–Kutta, single-stage Lax–Wendroff and multiderivative schemes, and Parareal coarse propagators.
* [Colliding acoustic pulses](/benchmarks/colliding-acoustic-pulses.md) — Klein's low-Mach test of two acoustic pulses that collide on a periodic domain; the sources run it with implicit and IMEX integrators at acoustic Courant numbers above one to check how well large steps resolve acoustics, and find the pulses captured at moderate Courant numbers but damped at very large ones.
* [Double Mach reflection](/benchmarks/double-mach-reflection.md) — Woodward and Colella's Mach 10 shock reflecting off a 30-degree wedge, run to t = 0.2; the sources use it to measure the cost of asynchronous local time stepping against a global step, the start-up transient that error-based step-size control handles while CFL-based control crashes, and single-stage Lax–Wendroff against Runge–Kutta flux reconstruction.
* [FitzHugh–Nagumo model](/benchmarks/fitzhugh-nagumo-model.md) — The FitzHugh–Nagumo reaction–diffusion system of nerve conduction, a stiff method-of-lines test with a cubic excitable reaction; used to show unconditional stability and efficiency of TASE–Runge–Kutta methods with an inexact Jacobian and to compare second-order stabilized Chebyshev methods.
* [Flow past a cylinder (Schäfer–Turek)](/benchmarks/flow-past-cylinder.md) — The Schäfer–Turek two-dimensional time-dependent flow past a cylinder at Reynolds number 100, with John's reference maxima of drag 2.95 and lift 0.48: setup, the time integrators run on it (Radau IIA, Lobatto IIIC, Gauss, adaptive BDF2 with explicit or linearized convection) and what each run showed.
* [Gray–Scott model](/benchmarks/gray-scott-model.md) — The two-species Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion system on a periodic square, discretized by finite differences, is a stiff two-dimensional benchmark; the page gives the parameter sets of two sources and what they measured with exponential Runge–Kutta and linearly implicit multistep methods.
* [Gresho vortex](/benchmarks/gresho-vortex.md) — Stationary incompressible vortex with piecewise-linear velocity, run as a compressible flow at Mach numbers down to 10^-4 to test whether all-Mach IMEX and semi-implicit time integrators keep the vortex and its kinetic energy with a step independent of the Mach number.
* [Heat equation](/benchmarks/heat-equation.md) — The linear heat equation u_t = Delta u + g, semi-discretized to y' = Ly + g with -L symmetric positive definite, is the model parabolic problem; the parallel-in-time sources use it to show one-iteration ParaDiag solves, Parareal contraction factors and low-rank Parareal convergence, and many other pages use it as a stiff linear test.
* [Isentropic vortex](/benchmarks/isentropic-vortex.md) — Exact smooth solution of the compressible Euler equations, a vortex advected by a uniform stream or at rest, used to measure temporal convergence order, conservation and cost of time integrators: explicit pairs with PID control, fully implicit Runge–Kutta methods, paired and many-stage stabilized explicit methods, single-stage Lax–Wendroff schemes, relaxation ADER-DG, and low-Mach IMEX and semi-implicit schemes.
* [Kelvin–Helmholtz instability](/benchmarks/kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md) — A periodic shear layer whose perturbation rolls up into vortices; the sources use an inviscid Euler version to test multirate P-ERK families, error-based step-size control of Runge–Kutta and single-stage Lax–Wendroff schemes under shock capturing, AMR and positivity limits, and a resistive-MHD version to compare exponential integrators, Leja and Krylov evaluation, controllers and implicit BDF.
* [Kepler problem](/benchmarks/kepler-problem.md) — The planar two-body problem with three invariants (energy, angular momentum, Laplace–Runge–Lenz vector), and its perturbed variant with a precessing orbit, used to test invariant-preserving relaxation methods and the modified equation of Störmer–Verlet.
* [Korteweg–de Vries equation](/benchmarks/korteweg-de-vries-equation.md) — The dispersive KdV equation u_t + 6uu_x + u_xxx = 0, its generalized and linear variants and its 2D relative, the Kadomtsev–Petviashvili equation; the third derivative makes explicit steps scale like Δx³, so the sources test explicit–implicit–null stabilization, energy-relaxed IMEX, positivity-corrected SDIRK and exponential Parareal on them.
* [Linear advection equation](/benchmarks/linear-advection-equation.md) — The constant- or variable-coefficient advection equation with its exact translated solution; the sources use it to verify temporal order, to compute von Neumann CFL limits of single-step and multiderivative schemes, to test bound-preserving limiting with non-SSP Runge–Kutta methods, boundary-induced order reduction, step-size control and implicit-stage solvers.
* [Linear hyperbolic relaxation system](/benchmarks/hyperbolic-relaxation-system.md) — Hyperbolic systems with a stiff relaxation source of strength 1/ε, linear or Jin–Xin type, whose ε → 0 limit is a scalar conservation law; the standard test of IMEX, asymptotic-preserving and uniformly accurate time integrators across the kinetic, intermediate and equilibrium regimes.
* [Nonlinear Schrödinger equation](/benchmarks/nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md) — The cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation on periodic domains, Fourier-discretized so that its stiff linear part is diagonal with a purely imaginary spectrum; the sources use it to test exponential Runge–Kutta methods, exponential Parareal with repartitioning, and adaptive spectral deferred correction against additive Runge–Kutta pairs.
* [Prothero–Robinson problem](/benchmarks/prothero-robinson-problem.md) — The scalar stiff test problem $y'=\lambda(y-g(t))+g'(t)$, whose smooth exact solution $g$ is independent of the stiffness $\lambda$; it isolates the stiff-regime error of a one-step method, exposes order reduction through the stage-order residuals, and grounds weak stage order and Scholz's Rosenbrock conditions. Includes the multirate Kværnø–Prothero–Robinson variant.
* [Robertson problem](/benchmarks/robertson-problem.md) — Robertson's three-species chemical kinetics system, with rate constants spanning nine orders of magnitude and solutions integrated to t = 1e5 or 1e11; a stiff test with real negative Jacobian eigenvalues on which explicit methods fail, implicit deferred-correction and ADER variants are compared, and positivity-preserving schemes are tested with near-zero initial data.
* [Sod shock tube](/benchmarks/sod-shock-tube.md) — The one-dimensional Euler Riemann problem of Sod, whose solution has a rarefaction, a contact and a shock; the sources use it to check that asynchronous, adaptive, semi-implicit, IMEX and single-stage Lax–Wendroff time integrators stay non-oscillatory at shocks, with qualitative comparisons against reference runs.
* [Stratospheric reaction problem](/benchmarks/stratospheric-reaction-problem.md) — Sandu's six-species oxygen–nitrogen stratospheric chemistry with diurnal photolysis, two linear invariants and concentrations spanning sixteen orders of magnitude; the sources use two variants that differ in one reaction, one rate factor and the initial nitrogen oxides, to test positivity-preserving integrators.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — Periodic vortex flow used to test time integrators on compressible-flow semidiscretizations: inviscid 3D at low Mach number as a stability-limited test for explicit pairs and step-size controllers, viscous 3D for optimized many-stage, semi-implicit and implicit two-derivative methods at large steps, and 2D steady at Mach numbers down to 10^-4 for the Mach scaling of IMEX schemes.
* [Van der Pol oscillator](/benchmarks/van-der-pol-oscillator.md) — The nonlinear relaxation oscillator y1' = y2, y2' = mu (1 - y1^2) y2 - y1, whose parameter mu tunes it from non-stiff to very stiff; the sources use it to test step-size controllers, including adaptive SDC, and to show that weak stage order and deferred correction do not keep their design orders on a stiff nonlinear problem.
* [Vlasov–Poisson equation (Landau damping, two-stream instability)](/benchmarks/vlasov-poisson-equation.md) — The electrostatic Vlasov–Poisson system for a plasma distribution function, with the bump-on-tail, Landau-damping, two-stream and Penning-trap set-ups; the sources use it as a hyperbolic kinetic test for Parareal with exponential Runge–Kutta propagators and with particle-in-Fourier and particle-in-cell propagators.

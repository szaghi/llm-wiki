# Results

Theorems, proved properties, impossibility results.

* [Accuracy conditions on WENO nonlinear weights](/results/weno-weight-accuracy-conditions.md) — How close the nonlinear weights must be to the linear weights for a WENO flux difference to keep its design order: Jiang–Shu's per-flux condition, Henrick et al.'s necessary-and-sufficient set (with the condition missing from Jiang–Shu), the simple sufficient condition ω − d = O(h³) at fifth order, and its general form.
* [Godunov's order barrier for monotone linear schemes](/results/godunov-order-barrier.md) — A linear two-level scheme keeps monotone data monotone iff all its coefficients are nonnegative, and no such scheme for linear advection is second order unless the Courant number is an integer; with Harten's chain monotone ⇒ TVD ⇒ monotonicity preserving, a linear TVD scheme is first order, so every higher-order capturing scheme must be nonlinear.
* [Nonconvexity of ideal MHD and compound waves](/results/mhd-nonconvexity.md) — Ideal MHD is nonconvex: near a vanishing tangential field the fast or the slow characteristic field is neither genuinely nonlinear nor linearly degenerate, so a shock can carry an attached rarefaction (a compound wave containing an intermediate shock); whether such solutions are physical, and so whether the MHD Riemann problem is unique, is unsettled in the corpus.
* [Order barriers and optimal coefficients of SSP time discretizations](/results/ssp-order-barriers.md) — What strong-stability-preserving Runge–Kutta and multistep methods can and cannot reach: SSP coefficient at most 1 for s-stage order-s methods, no four-stage fourth-order method without the downwind operator, multistep step-count barriers, and no implicit SSP method above first order.

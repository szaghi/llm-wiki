# Results

Theorems, proved properties, impossibility results.

* [Accuracy conditions on WENO nonlinear weights](/results/weno-weight-accuracy-conditions.md) — How close the nonlinear weights must be to the linear weights for a WENO flux difference to keep its design order: Jiang–Shu's sufficient condition, Henrick et al.'s necessary-and-sufficient set (with the condition missing from Jiang–Shu), the simple sufficient condition ω − d = O(h³) at fifth order, and its general form.
* [Order barriers and optimal coefficients of SSP time discretizations](/results/ssp-order-barriers.md) — What strong-stability-preserving Runge–Kutta and multistep methods can and cannot reach: SSP coefficient at most 1 for m-stage order-m methods, no four-stage fourth-order method without the downwind operator, multistep step-count barriers, and no implicit SSP method above first order.

# Summary

Shu's CIME lecture notes, self-contained and code-oriented. The text
read is the ICASE Report 97-65 (84 pp., NTRS 19980007543); the slug and
resource follow its published form, the LNM 1697 chapter (same title),
by bundle law. **Scoped read:** §§1–4.2 in full (1D and multi-D
reconstruction, ENO and WENO approximation, FV and FD schemes, systems,
boundary conditions, provable properties, subcell resolution,
artificial compression, other building blocks, TVD RK and multistep
methods, the Lax–Wendroff procedure); §4.3 (Hamilton–Jacobi) in part;
§5 (applications) skimmed. The reference list (83 entries) was
extracted whole. Merged into most method pages of this batch.

# Key claims

1. **Problems 2.1 and 2.2** and their identity through the primitive;
   constants $c_{rj}$ (Table 2.1) for uniform grids.
2. **FD restriction**: conservative point-value differences above second
   order only on uniform or smoothly varying grids (§2.1.2).
3. **ENO properties** for piecewise smooth data (accuracy up to the
   discontinuity, monotone in the shocked cell, TVB), with a proof sketch
   of monotonicity (§2.2.1); biasing to a preferred stencil, $b = 2$.
4. **WENO recipe** (§2.2.2): four ENO drawbacks; weight design
   requirements; Jiang–Shu indicator (2.61) and its $k = 2, 3$ forms,
   "satisfied, even near smooth extrema"; seventh and higher order not
   yet extensively tested.
5. **FV vs FD** (§§2.3, 3.3): equivalent for linear 1D; FV works on any
   mesh but multi-D FV above second order needs tensor reconstruction and
   flux quadrature; FD about four times cheaper in 2D — recommended.
6. **Monotone fluxes** ordered by dissipation; their differences shrink
   at high order (Fig. 2.2).
7. **Systems** (§2.3.5): component-wise adequate at low order;
   characteristic procedures 2.8–2.10 for robustness.
8. **Provable properties**: second-order ENO TVD; WENO converges for
   smooth solutions; no convergence result for ENO above second order;
   against modifying schemes only for proofs.
9. **Time discretization** (§4.2): TVD RK (optimal second and third
   order; no four-stage fourth-order method with nonnegative
   coefficients; a preferred one with $\mathcal{C} = 0.936$; low-storage
   third order $\approx 0.32$), TVD multistep, the Lax–Wendroff procedure;
   a non-TVD RK2 with TVD MUSCL oscillates.

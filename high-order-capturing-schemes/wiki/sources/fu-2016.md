# Summary

Addresses two WENO shortcomings for turbulence: excess dissipation and
loss of robustness at very high order when several discontinuities fall
in one stencil. Merged into [TENO](/methods/teno.md), with updates to
WENO-Z, WENO-CU6, MP limiting, the modified-wavenumber and
weight-condition pages and the benchmarks.

# Key claims

1. **Review** of WENO-JS, WENO-Z, WENO-CU6, WENO-CU6-M and their limits
   (§§1–2), including that MP bounds fail to keep the formal order.
2. **TENO** (§3): stencil widths (eq. 21), optimal weights to $K = 8$
   (Table 2), measure (eq. 22, $C_w = 1$, $q = 6$), cutoff (eqs. 25–27),
   weaker order condition (eq. 29), $\tau_K$ general form (Appendix A).
3. **Optimization** (§4) by a dispersion–dissipation relation; TENO5-opt
   and TENO6-opt; parameters in Table 5.
4. **Validation** (§5): Gaussian advection, Sod, Lax, blast waves,
   Shu–Osher, double Mach, Rayleigh–Taylor, 2D viscous shock tube;
   WENO-CU6-M fails two tests.

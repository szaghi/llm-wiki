# Summary

Completes the flux-based ENO of [Shu & Osher (1988)](/sources/shu-1988.md):
the numerical flux approximates $\mathcal{H}(x_{i+1/2})$, obtained by ENO
interpolation of the flux primitive $h\sum f(u_j)$; new upwind building
blocks; contact sharpening; systems and multi-D; many tests, including
the problem now called the Shu–Osher problem. Merged into
[conservative finite difference](/methods/conservative-finite-difference.md),
[contact sharpening](/methods/contact-sharpening.md),
[characteristic decomposition](/methods/characteristic-decomposition.md)
and [Shu–Osher problem](/benchmarks/shu-osher-problem.md).

# Key claims

1. **Lemma 2.1** and the primitive-of-the-flux construction; it explains
   the 1988 equivalence with cell-average ENO (linear 1D). The 1988
   correction-term form has $a_2 = -1/24$, $a_4 = 7/5760$ (eq. 1.9,
   read from the page image).
2. **Upwinding by the first stencil point**; starting downwind was
   unstable for Burgers' equation even with ENO stencil choice to third
   order.
3. **ENO-Roe** (Algorithm 2.1): uniformly high order even at sonic points
   but admits expansion shocks; **ENO-LLF** (2.2): local LF speed, a
   monotone flux, twice the cost; **ENO-RF** (2.3): Roe except where
   $f'$ changes sign; preferred (half the cost, best resolution, always
   entropy-correct in tests). Viscosity LF > LLF > Roe.
4. **Subcell resolution** (Algorithm 3.1) and **artificial compression**
   (3.2, parameter 2–5) translated to fluxes and RK; compression only in
   linearly degenerate fields for Euler; naive 2D subcell resolution fails.
5. **Systems** in local characteristic fields with Roe or mean Jacobian;
   multi-D dimension by dimension; the decomposition costs a multiple of
   the vector operations at third order compared with second (the factor
   is illegible in the text layer).
6. **Tests**: non-convex Riemann problems, contact sharpening on four
   linear profiles, 2D Burgers Riemann problems, a boundary-layer-like
   steady problem, Sod and Lax, interacting blast waves, Mach 3 shock
   interacting with a density sine wave (ENO-RF-3 with 200 points ≈
   MUSCL TVD with 800), Mach 8 shock / compressible turbulence in 2D.

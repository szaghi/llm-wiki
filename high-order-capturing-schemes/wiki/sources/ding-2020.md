# Summary

The paper asks what a conservative finite-difference scheme actually
conserves when the domain has inflow, outflow or wall boundaries, and
fixes ILW-based fifth-order WENO so that a high-order total mass is
conserved exactly. Merged into the ILW page (new section
"Conservation"), the conservative-FD, WENO, extrapolation and SSP pages,
and the blast-wave, double-Mach and new
[supersonic cylinder](/benchmarks/supersonic-cylinder-flow.md) benchmarks.

# Key claims

1. **Second-order mass** (§1, eqs. 1.5–1.7). $h\sum_j u_j$ is spectrally
   accurate for periodic or compactly supported data and $O(h^2)$
   otherwise.
2. **Mock–Lax weights** (Lemma 1, eqs. 2.11–2.14, page images).
   Endpoint-corrected quadrature with weights equal to 1 away from the
   boundary; for fifth order, $\tfrac{251}{720}, \tfrac{299}{240}, \tfrac{211}{240}, \tfrac{739}{720}$
   at the four boundary points, and polynomials in the cut distance
   otherwise.
3. **Only the final stage** (eqs. 2.9–2.10). Conservation is enforced
   through the RK-averaged flux of the last TVD-RK3 stage.
4. **One flux in 1D** (eqs. 2.15–2.18). Summation by parts reduces the
   change of the weighted mass to boundary terms. Matching the inflow
   term to the time integral of $f(g)$ fixes $\hat f_{-1/2}$. The outflow
   flux is left unchanged.
5. **Systems and walls** (§§2.1, 2.3). Componentwise. At a reflecting
   wall only mass and energy fluxes are modified; momentum is not
   conserved and need not be.
6. **2D** (§2.3, eqs. 2.32–2.41, Appendix A). Polygon quadratures over
   cut cells give non-unique weights. The defect is spread over one inflow
   flux per grid line in proportion to the local flux magnitudes, with $x$-
   or $y$-fluxes chosen by a 45° rule.
7. **Results** (§§2.2, 2.4). Conservation errors $10^{-15}$–$10^{-16}$
   against $10^{-3}$–$10^{-8}$ for ILW/SILW, with fifth order and shock
   resolution unchanged. In a long blast-wave run the non-conservative
   shock is misplaced (Fig. 2.5).
8. **Tests.** Linear advection, Burgers (with a shock entering through
   the inflow boundary), interacting blast waves, 2D advection on
   cut-cell domains, 2D Burgers, double Mach reflection on the real
   wedge, Mach 3 flow past a cylinder. Fifth-order WENO-JS with
   Lax–Friedrichs splitting, TVD-RK3 at CFL 0.6, and
   $\Delta t = O(h^{5/3})$ for accuracy runs.

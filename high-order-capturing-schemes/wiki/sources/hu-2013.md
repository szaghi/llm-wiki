# Summary

Instead of limiting reconstructed values, limit the fluxes: a simple
add-on to any conservative FV or FD scheme. The archived file is the
arXiv v5 preprint (17 Sep 2012), titled "Positivity-preserving flux
limiters for high-order conservative schemes"; the filename suggests
the JCP version, but page 1 is the preprint. The slug and resource
follow the published version, verified on Crossref (JCP 242:169–180,
June 2013, with a changed title). Merged into the new
[flux limiter](/methods/positivity-preserving-flux-limiter.md) page and
the positivity benchmarks.

# Key claims

1. **Convex split** (§2.2): $u^{n+1}_i = \frac12(u_i + 2\lambda\hat f_{i-1/2}) + \frac12(u_i - 2\lambda\hat f_{i+1/2})$;
   the LF halves are positive under CFL $\le 1/2$ (eqs. 11–13).
2. **Cut-off limiter**: density first, then pressure; per-cell factors
   from $(1-\theta)g(u^{\mathrm{LF},\pm}) + \theta g(u^\pm) = \epsilon_g$,
   minimum per interface; $\epsilon_g = \min\{10^{-13}, g^0_{\min}\}$
   (checked on the page images).
3. **Accuracy** (§2.3): an $O(h^{k+1})$ flux change under CFL $\le \sigma/2$
   (eq. 20), less restrictive than Zhang–Shu's.
4. **Advection test** (§2.4): less accurate than Zhang–Shu at CFL 0.5,
   equal at $1/12$, more accurate below.
5. **2D** (§2.5): the update is split into $x$ and $y$ parts with partition weights summing to 1, dimension by dimension.
6. **Tests** (§3): with WENO5 — double rarefaction, planar and 2D Sedov,
   Mach 2000 jet; with WENO-CU6-M1 — blast waves, Leblanc, DMR at
   $240\times60$, Mach 6 shock–helium bubble. These are cases the
   unlimited schemes cannot compute.

# Summary

A systematic stability study of the ILW and simplified-ILW boundary
treatments, on linear advection, with WENO's linear-weight schemes as
inner schemes. Merged into the new
[inverse Lax–Wendroff](/methods/inverse-lax-wendroff.md) page and into
the SSP Runge–Kutta, WENO and finite-difference pages.

# Key claims

1. **Inner schemes** (§2.1): upwind-biased conservative FD of orders
   3–13, with one more point on the upwind side. These are WENO with
   linear weights.
2. **Boundary data in RK3 stages** (§2.2): the naive time matching
   gives second order. Carpenter et al.'s matching keeps third.
3. **ILW** (§2.3): ghost values by Taylor expansion from the boundary,
   with derivatives from $g(t)$ through the PDE; explicit formulas up to
   the third derivative.
4. **SILW** (§2.4): ILW for $k \le k_d - 1$, extrapolation from interior
   points for the rest. Using $g$ in the extrapolant makes it less
   stable (Vilar–Shu).
5. **Analysis** (§3): GKS normal modes for the quarter-plane problems
   and eigenvalue spectra of the full matrix agree, semi-discrete and
   fully discrete. ILW is stable for all $C_a$ under the periodic CFL
   limit. Outflow extrapolation is stable (Goldberg).
6. **Thresholds** (Tables 3.1–3.2): $(\lambda_{\mathrm{cfl}})_{\max}$
   1.62, 1.43, 1.24, 1.12, 1.04, 0.99 and $(k_d)_{\min}$ 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10
   for orders 3–13.
7. **Experiments** (§4): instability just below each threshold and
   stability at it. Nonlinear Burgers is more stable than predicted,
   because the effective CFL near the boundary is lower. Euler system
   tests are included.

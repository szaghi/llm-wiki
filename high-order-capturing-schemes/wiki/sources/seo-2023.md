# Summary

Merged into the new [HOW-MHD](/software/how-mhd.md) software page, the
constrained-transport page (new section on staggered FD-CT), the SSP,
WENO, characteristic and positivity pages, and the Alfvén-wave, field-loop,
oblique shock-tube, rotor and blast benchmarks.

# Key claims

1. **Spatial scheme** (§2.2, eqs. 1–8, page images): the Jiang–Wu form,
   Jiang–Shu weights with $\varepsilon = 10^{-8}$, local Lax–Friedrichs over
   six points, Ryu–Jones (adiabatic) and Kim et al. (isothermal)
   eigenvectors. Other WENO variants were tried, without data (§1).
2. **Time** (§2.3): SSPRK(5,4) at CFL 1.50818004975927, its SSP
   coefficient, about 50% faster than RK4 at CFL 0.8.
3. **High-order CT** (§2.4, eqs. 12–30): upwinded parts of the WENO
   induction fluxes plus interpolated cross terms; a $\tfrac1{24}$ transverse
   correction (eq. 23); fourth- or sixth-order staggered differences
   (eq. 27); sixth-order interpolation of the zone field (eq. 30). Plain
   differences of the edge fields are not enough. The discrete divergence
   stays at round-off (Fig. 11).
4. **Tests** (§3): 3D linear waves and circularly polarized Alfvén waves
   (fifth order against second with the old CT); 2D and 3D field loops;
   reconnection; the oblique Ryu–Jones–Frank shock tube; Tóth's rotor;
   a 3D blast (eq. 47 prints the pressures swapped); jet launching;
   isothermal and adiabatic turbulence (HOW-MHD on $128^3$ resembles a
   second-order TVD code on $256^3$).

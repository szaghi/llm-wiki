# Summary

Every derivative of the original ILW divides by $f'(g)$, which fails
where the wind at the boundary passes through zero. Separate Taylor
expansions of $u$ and $f(u)$ at the ghost points remove that division.
A new WENO-type extrapolation is added. Merged into the ILW (new section
on sign-changing wind), weighted-extrapolation, conservative-FD and
characteristic-decomposition pages, and four benchmarks.

# Key claims

1. **Motivation** (§§1, 2.1). Original ILW needs $f'(u)$ bounded away from
   zero; the real difficulty is a time-dependent transition through a
   sonic point.
2. **Scalar treatment** (§2.1, eqs. 2.6–2.8, page images). Ghost fluxes
   from their own expansion with $f^{*(0)} = f(g)$, $f^{*(1)} = -g'$ and
   higher derivatives extrapolated; ghost states with $u^{*(0)} = g$ and
   all derivatives extrapolated. The Lax–Friedrichs flux uses both
   (Remark 2.1).
3. **Old extrapolation criticized** (eqs. 2.9–2.10). Tan–Shu linear
   weights are powers of $h$ and $q$ is problem dependent, which breaks
   self-similarity.
4. **New extrapolation** (eqs. 2.11–2.15). Nested stencils combined
   multi-resolution style with constant $d_k$ and WENO-Z-type weights,
   $\varepsilon = 10^{-4}$, $\beta_0 = 0.1\beta_1$. Lagrange extrapolation
   is used where smooth (Remark 2.3).
5. **Euler** (§§2.2–2.3). Four cases by the signs of the characteristic
   speeds at the boundary; the missing component from extrapolated
   outgoing characteristic variables; a solid wall as zero normal mass
   flux; a rotated normal frame in 2D; 2D extrapolation from a
   least-squares degree-4 fit (§2.4).
6. **Limits** (§4). The 2D cost is not negligible. With only the first
   derivative from ILW, stability may fail above fifth order.
7. **Tests** (§3). Variable-coefficient advection and Burgers with
   sign-changing wind, 1D Euler with a sign-changing velocity, interacting
   blast waves with ILW walls, 2D advection on a square and a disc, 2D
   Burgers and Euler, the isentropic vortex, double Mach reflection on the
   real ramp, and Mach 3 flow past a cylinder. Fifth-order WENO with
   Lax–Friedrichs splitting and third-order TVD RK; no CFL number is
   printed.

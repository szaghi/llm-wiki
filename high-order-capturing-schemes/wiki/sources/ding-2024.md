# Key claims

1. **Admissible set** (§1, eq. 4): convex for any EOS with $e > 0$ when
   $\rho > 0$.
2. **Literature** (§1): Christlieb et al.'s positivity rests on a
   Lax–Friedrichs property "later proven rigorously" by Wu (2018); Cheng et
   al. proved it only in 1D under assumptions; positivity for the standard
   system is tied to a discrete divergence-free condition (Wu 2018).
3. **Discrete divergence of traces** (eq. 9) and **projection** (§2.3,
   eqs. 25–36): cell-wise constant shifts, minimum norm (Prop. 2.3),
   $O(h^k)$, so the order is kept (Thm. 2.7).
4. **Source term** (eq. 12): jumps of the normal field times the source
   vector at interface averages.
5. **Theorem 2.1** (eqs. 15–16): positivity of the forward-Euler cell
   averages under $\Delta t(\alpha_x/h_x + \alpha_y/h_y) < \hat w_1$, with
   speeds enlarged by a normal-field-jump term. All three ingredients are
   essential. SSP-RK3 keeps it (Remark 2.2).
6. **Limiter** (§2.4, eqs. 37–38, Thm. 2.9): scaling of the point values
   and of one remainder state; the second step by a ratio, with no
   quadratic solve; it keeps the discrete divergence zero.
7. **Proof** (§3): the divergence coupling defeats convex decomposition;
   geometric quasilinearization instead.
8. **Reconstruction** (§2.2): van Albada at second order; WENO-Z with
   characteristic decomposition and 4-point Gauss–Lobatto nodes at fifth,
   because 3-point Gauss gives a negative midpoint weight.
9. **Tests** (§4, CFL 0.3): low-pressure vortex (fifth order), Orszag–Tang
   (failure at $t = 3.11$ without projection and limiter), rotor, blast
   (final time not printed), magnetized jet.

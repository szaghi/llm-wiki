# Summary

Brings Ghosh–Baeder's compact-reconstruction idea to finite volumes,
combined with HLLC and the Zhang–Shu positivity limiter. The archived
file is arXiv:1402.5618v2 (24 Jun 2014); the published version, checked
on Crossref, is J. Comput. Phys. 274:505–523 (2014), with the same
title. Merged into the new
[compact-reconstruction WENO](/methods/compact-reconstruction-weno.md)
page and into the positivity and benchmark pages.

# Key claims

1. **Compact candidates** (§2.2): Pirozzoli's fifth-order upwind compact
   scheme split into three third-order compact stencils, with linear
   weights $\frac{2}{10}, \frac{5}{10}, \frac{3}{10}$ and WENO-Z weights
   ($q = 2$, $\tau_5$, $\varepsilon = 10^{-13}$); eqs. (2.9)–(2.14) checked
   on the page images.
2. **Systems** (§2.3): characteristic-wise, $3\times3$ block-tridiagonal
   solve by a chasing method.
3. **HLLC positivity** (§2.4): with Batten et al.'s speeds the star states
   stay admissible, and the first-order scheme is positive under
   $\Delta t\Vert\vert u\vert + c\Vert_\infty/h \le 1$ (Remark 1); the high-order
   scheme under the $\hat w_1$ bound (Theorem 1).
4. **Limiter** (§2.4, Remark 2): the Zhang–Shu algorithm on the
   compact interface values; accuracy holds because the polynomial is
   local.
5. **Tests** (§3, 1D): fifth order on advection; cheaper than WENO-JS at
   equal $L_1$ error, especially with conservative-variable
   reconstruction; sharper than WENO-JS and WENO-Z on Lax, Sod,
   Shu–Osher and blast waves; positive on near-vacuum, strong-shock,
   Sedov and Leblanc problems, with HLLC-induced oscillations near
   vacuum. Extension to multi-D is left to future work.

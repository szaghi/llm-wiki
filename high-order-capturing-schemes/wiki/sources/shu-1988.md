# Summary

Two simplifications of ENO for multi-D and forcing terms: a class of
high-order explicit Runge–Kutta methods that are TVD whenever forward
Euler is, and an ENO construction on fluxes of point values instead of
cell averages. The archived file is the ICASE report version (May 1987)
of the JCP article; the slug follows the published version (bundle law).
Merged into [SSP Runge–Kutta](/methods/ssp-runge-kutta.md),
[conservative finite difference](/methods/conservative-finite-difference.md),
[ENO](/methods/eno-reconstruction.md) and
[TVD](/properties/total-variation-diminishing.md).

# Key claims

1. **TVD time discretization** (§II): Shu–Osher form (2.12) with
   $a_{ik} \ge 0$; TVD under CFL $\lambda \le \lambda_0 \min a_{ik}/\vert b_{ik}\vert$
   with $\tilde{\mathcal{L}}$ for negative $b_{ik}$ (Proposition 2.1),
   extensible to any convex functional (Remark 2.1).
2. **Optimal methods**: second order = Heun, CFL 1 (2.15–2.16); third
   order (2.18), CFL 1, needing only the last stage's operator; fourth
   order cannot avoid negative $b_{ik}$ in their search; a fifth-order
   method is given.
3. **Flux ENO** (§III): Lax–Friedrichs splitting $f^\pm$, ENO
   interpolation of $f^\pm$ point values with correction terms of even
   order; equivalent to cell-average ENO with RP for linear equations
   (stencil choice aside); second order is a TVD minmod-type scheme;
   E-flux building blocks lose accuracy at sonic points. A
   non-conservative "essentially conservative" variant has full
   monotone-scheme convergence theory with a capping term but gave a
   wrong shock speed without it and smeared shocks with it; abandoned.
4. **Preliminary results** (§IV): Burgers 1D and 2D (full order in smooth
   regions, clean shocks), rotated 2D linear advection over 8 periods,
   non-convex Riemann problems and Buckley–Leverett (upwind building
   block sharper than LF).
5. Very high order TVD schemes (up to 15th) exist but degenerate at
   extrema and smear over wide stencils.

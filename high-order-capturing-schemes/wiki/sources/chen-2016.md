# Summary

A scheme designed for the BATL block-adaptive tree library: fifth order,
a small stencil, no characteristic decomposition, curvilinear and
adaptive grids. Merged into the alternative-FD, MP-limiter,
conservative-FD, characteristic-decomposition and high-order AMR pages,
the Lax and double-Mach benchmarks, and the new
[free-stream preservation](/properties/free-stream-preservation.md) page.

# Key claims

1. **Design and choices** (§1). MP schemes are simpler and cheaper than
   WENO and work without characteristic decomposition; in the authors'
   experience MP5 is as good as or better than CWENO5 (no data).
   Characteristic FD needs Roe matrices and is expensive; FD with global
   Lax–Friedrichs splitting was "quite diffusive". The route follows Del
   Zanna's "FD-Primitive", equivalent to an explicit WCNS of Deng et al.
2. **Face values** (§2.2, eqs. 3–4, page image). The five-point formula
   $(3, -20, 90, 60, -5)/128$ on primitive point values, limited by MP5.
3. **Flux correction** (eqs. 5–6, page image). Second- and
   fourth-difference corrections from the Riemann flux and cell-centre
   physical fluxes only, so three cells per side suffice. The correction
   is unlimited, so the scheme is not monotonicity-preserving (§§2.2, 6).
   Why it is needed: the face-flux difference alone is second order
   (§2.3, eqs. 8–10).
4. **Free streams** (§3, eqs. 12–19). Thomas–Lombard metrics with the
   same difference operator as the fluxes and a fixed six-point face
   interpolation of the coordinates make the metric residual vanish to
   round-off.
5. **Ghost cells at resolution changes** (§4, eqs. 20–26). Dimension-by-
   dimension fifth-order (sometimes fourth-order) interpolation with an
   MP-like limiter for non-uniform stencils; restriction remote,
   prolongation local; blocks of at least 6 cells.
6. **No refluxing** (§4.3). Berger–Colella flux correction would make the
   scheme second order at resolution changes; conservation holds to
   truncation error there and in regridding.
7. **Order** (§4.5, eq. 27). The local order drops to 4 or 3 at
   resolution changes, but those sets have lower dimension, so the $L^1$
   order stays 5.
8. **Tests** (§5). Interpolation of quartics; 2D acoustic wave and density
   peaks on refined Cartesian, polar and 3D grids (fifth-order $L^1$,
   Tables 1–5; global order 2 with second-order ghost cells, Table 2);
   square wave; Lax shock tube in 1D and 2D; free stream on a refined
   spherical shell ($2.5\times10^{-14}$); double Mach reflection on three
   dynamic levels, costing about 3 times the second-order scheme
   (Table 6). SSP-RK3, HLLE and one global time step throughout.

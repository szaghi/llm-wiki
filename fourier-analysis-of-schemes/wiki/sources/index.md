# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Arfken, Weber & Harris (2013) — Mathematical Methods for Physicists (7th ed.)](/sources/arfken-2013.md) — Graduate textbook of mathematical methods; read for this bundle in its chapters on Fourier series and integral transforms, which corroborate the founding source's series, transform, Parseval, convolution and Laplace results under a different sign convention, and add the quantified Gibbs overshoot, coefficient-decay rules, transform solutions of the wave, heat and Poisson equations, the Bromwich inversion, and the discrete Fourier transform with aliasing and the FFT.
* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering (3rd ed.)](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Undergraduate textbook of mathematical methods; read for this bundle in its chapters on Fourier series, integral transforms (Fourier and Laplace) and the finite-difference sections of numerical methods, which supply the series and transform definitions, convergence and Gibbs statements, Parseval, convolution and correlation theorems, Laplace operational rules, basic difference operators, and illustrated (not analysed) instabilities of forward Euler and Milne's method.

* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering (3rd ed.)](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Undergraduate textbook of mathematical methods; read for this bundle in its chapters on Fourier series, integral transforms (Fourier and Laplace) and the finite-difference sections of numerical methods, which supply the series and transform definitions, convergence and Gibbs statements, Parseval, convolution and correlation theorems, Laplace operational rules, basic difference operators, and illustrated (not analysed) instabilities of forward Euler and Milne's method.

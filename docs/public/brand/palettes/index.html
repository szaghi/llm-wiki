LLM-WIKI · palettes

The Sumi family look of llm-wiki kept intact: brush mark, seal, AMR ground, display face in capitals, near-square buttons. Only the 13 colour tokens change. The site uses Hasegawa Tōhaku since 2026-11-09; this page archives the 30 alternatives considered, against the original Sumi palette at the top: painters read as pigment decisions, neon and vibrant palettes, popular editor themes and a keycap set.

Every pair is measured against the Sumi rules: ink 13:1, muted text 5.3:1, links 4.8:1 on ground and ground-alt, button text 5:1, control borders 4.5:1. Seal is a fill, never text; 3:1 keeps the mark legible as a graphic. Values under a rule show as low. The mark here is recoloured by token for comparison only: adopting a palette means regenerating it, never hand-editing paths.

Editor themesPopular code-editor and terminal themes, with their published colour values. Judged by WCAG, not by the Sumi rules: Sumi asks 13:1 for body text, which no mid-dark editor ground can reach even with pure white, and which would bleach every theme into the same black and white. Here body text needs 7:1 (AAA); muted text, links and button text 4.5:1 (AA); borders, focus ring and seal 3:1. Where an official value misses, it moves in lightness until it passes, and the move is listed under its mockup.

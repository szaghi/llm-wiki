::: info Why the law says what it says
This page renders the repository's `DECISIONS.md`, a lightweight architecture decision record: one entry per design
decision, with its context, the decision and its consequences. The [constitution](/reference/constitution) states the
rules; this record argues for them. To challenge a rule, reopen its decision here rather than deviating from it. The
file itself is [on GitHub](https://github.com/szaghi/llm-wiki/blob/main/DECISIONS.md).
:::

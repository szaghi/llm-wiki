# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Arfken, Weber & Harris (2013) — Mathematical Methods for Physicists, 7th ed. (Chs. 3, 4)](/sources/arfken-2013.md) — Textbook of mathematical physics; this bundle reads its vector analysis with orthogonal curvilinear coordinates, integral theorems and potential theory (Ch. 3) and its tensors in general coordinates, Jacobians and differential forms with the exterior derivative and Stokes' theorem for forms (Ch. 4).
* [Jiang et al. (2024) — GMEC I: field-aligned coordinates, shifted metrics and symbolic finite differences](/sources/jiang-2024.md) — Part I of the GMEC code papers; this bundle reads its field-aligned coordinates on straight-field-line flux coordinates, the metric and Jacobian, the vector operators in component form, Scott's shifted metric and a new simple shifted metric, arbitrary-order finite differences with precomputed metric coefficients, and the numerical instability at the twist-shift boundary.
* [Liu et al. (2024) — GMEC II: field-aligned coordinates and meshes for the hybrid code](/sources/liu-2024.md) — Part II of the GMEC code papers; this bundle reads its global field-aligned coordinates on PEST flux coordinates with the twist-shift condition, the Jacobian and the full contravariant metric of the field-aligned system, the cofactor inversion to the covariant metric, the metric of the flux system from R(Ψ,ϑ), z(Ψ,ϑ), and the treatment of the field-aligned instability by dissipation.
* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering, 3rd ed. (Chs. 10, 11, 26)](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Textbook of applied mathematics; this bundle reads its vector calculus (Ch. 10), line, surface and volume integrals with the divergence, Stokes and Green theorems (Ch. 11) and tensors from Cartesian to general curvilinear coordinates with metric, Christoffel symbols and covariant derivative (Ch. 26).
* [Seto et al. (2023) — BOUT++ coordinate systems and a 2D field solver for low toroidal mode numbers](/sources/seto-2023.md) — BOUT++ extension paper; this bundle reads its three coordinate systems (orthogonal flux-surface, field-aligned, quasi-ballooning with shifted metric and shifted radial derivative), the twist-shift periodicity and FFT phase-shift transform, and the derivation of the perpendicular Laplacian and its metric coefficients, discretized per toroidal mode with and without the flute-ordering approximation.

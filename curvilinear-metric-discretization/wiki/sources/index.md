# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Arfken, Weber & Harris (2013) — Mathematical Methods for Physicists, 7th ed. (Chs. 3, 4)](/sources/arfken-2013.md) — Textbook of mathematical physics; this bundle reads its vector analysis with orthogonal curvilinear coordinates, integral theorems and potential theory (Ch. 3) and its tensors in general coordinates, Jacobians and differential forms with the exterior derivative and Stokes' theorem for forms (Ch. 4).
* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering, 3rd ed. (Chs. 10, 11, 26)](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Textbook of applied mathematics; this bundle reads its vector calculus (Ch. 10), line, surface and volume integrals with the divergence, Stokes and Green theorems (Ch. 11) and tensors from Cartesian to general curvilinear coordinates with metric, Christoffel symbols and covariant derivative (Ch. 26).

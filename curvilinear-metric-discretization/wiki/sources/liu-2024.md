# Summary

Z. Y. Liu, P. Y. Jiang, S. Y. Liu, L. L. Zhang and G. Y. Fu, "Development of
a gyrokinetic-MHD energetic particle simulation code. II. Linear simulations
of Alfvén eigenmodes driven by energetic particles", *Physics of Plasmas*
**31**, 073905 (2024), published online 11 July 2024 (submitted 4 March,
accepted 11 June 2024); DOI 10.1063/5.0206762, read from page 1 of the PDF.
Version read: the publisher's version of record (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0), 14 PDF
pages: an AIP cover page, then article pages 073905-1 to -13. Locators are
article page numbers (PDF page = article page + 1).

The paper presents the hybrid version of [GMEC](/software/gmec.md): the
reduced-MHD solver of Part I ([Jiang et al. (2024)](/sources/jiang-2024.md))
coupled to gyrokinetic $\delta f$ particles for energetic-particle-driven
Alfvén eigenmodes, with verification on particle orbits and linear
benchmarks.

**Scoped read.** This bundle read the abstract, introduction and §II A
(pp. 1–2), §II B, *Field-aligned coordinates and meshes*, and §II C,
*Numerical approaches* (pp. 3–5, including the guiding-centre equations
written in the field-aligned metric, eqs. (10)–(14)), §II D, *Analytical and
numerical equilibria* (p. 5, eqs. (23)–(28)), the convergence part of
Appendix C (p. 12), and the complete reference list (pp. 12–13). Equations
were checked on 110-dpi page images of article pp. 2–6. Not read here: the
particle loading and $\delta f$ algebra beyond eq. (14), the verification
and benchmark results (§III) and the other appendices, which concern the
plasma physics. The reference list was merged complete.

# Key claims

1. **Global field-aligned coordinates** $\xi^1=(\Psi-\Psi_1)/\Delta\Psi$,
   $\xi^2=\vartheta$, $\xi^3=\varphi-\int_0^\vartheta q_{\mathrm{loc}}\,d\vartheta'$
   on PEST coordinates, the axis excluded; uniform node-centred grid in
   $\xi^1\in[0,1]$ and cell-centred grids on $[-\pi,\pi]$ in $\xi^2,\xi^3$; the
   field lines cover the whole flux surface, unlike a flux tube (eqs. (8)–(9),
   p. 3, figs. 1–2). → [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md).
2. **Twist-shift condition** as eq. (9), with a sign of the shift opposite to
   Jiang et al. and Seto et al. → **contradiction** opened on
   [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md).
3. **Jacobian and metric.** $J=(\nabla\xi^1\times\nabla\xi^2\cdot\nabla\xi^3)^{-1}$,
   $g^{ij}=\nabla\xi^i\cdot\nabla\xi^j$, $g_{ij}=\mathbf a_i\cdot\mathbf a_j$
   (p. 3); the six contravariant components of the field-aligned metric from
   those of the flux system, $J=\Delta\Psi$ times the flux-system Jacobian
   (eq. (26), p. 5), the covariant components by cofactors (eq. (27)), and
   the flux-system metric from $R(\Psi,\vartheta)$, $z(\Psi,\vartheta)$
   (eq. (28)). → [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md),
   [metric tensor](/definitions/metric-tensor.md), [Jacobian](/definitions/jacobian.md),
   [magnetic flux coordinates](/definitions/magnetic-flux-coordinates.md).
4. **PEST coordinates**: $\vartheta$ fixed by requiring the flux-system
   Jacobian over $R^2$ to be a flux function; an $O(\varepsilon^2)$ analytic
   solution for circular surfaces (eq. (24), p. 5).
   → [magnetic flux coordinates](/definitions/magnetic-flux-coordinates.md).
5. **Metrics stored, not differenced**: $q$, densities, pressure, $B$,
   $J_\parallel$, $J$, $g^{ij}$, $g_{ij}$ and their first derivatives (second
   for $B$) are precomputed on the mesh and stored in an equilibrium file; the
   scheme "can be applicable to any curvilinear coordinates" (pp. 3, 5).
   → [GMEC](/software/gmec.md).
6. **Flute ordering in the Poisson problem**: dropping $\nabla_\parallel$ terms
   reduces the 3D vorticity–potential problem to 2D, the dropped term being of
   order $k_\parallel/k_\perp$ (p. 3).
   → [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md).
7. **Instability by dissipation**: the field-aligned instability is
   suppressed by a vorticity diffusion of normalised size $10^{-6}$ instead of
   the shifted metric, with growth rate and frequency insensitive to smaller
   values (p. 2; App. C, p. 12). → [shifted metric](/methods/shifted-metric.md).
8. **Operators in the field-aligned metric**: the contravariant components of
   $\nabla\times\hat{\mathbf b}$ and of $-\hat{\mathbf b}\times\nabla\phi$, and
   the angle between $\mathbf a^1$ and $\mathbf a^2$ from $g^{12}/\sqrt{g^{11}g^{22}}$
   (eqs. (10)–(14), p. 4). → [curl](/definitions/curl.md),
   [covariant and contravariant bases](/definitions/covariant-and-contravariant-bases.md).

## Recorded as printed (in the sections read)

* **Eq. (9)**, twist-shift $\delta\phi(x,y+2\pi,z)=\delta\phi(x,y,z-2\pi q)$:
  with eq. (8), $z=\varphi-\int_0^\theta\nu\,d\theta'$, the shift has the
  opposite sign to that implied by the definition (compiler's check); the
  contradiction with Jiang et al. and Seto et al. is open on the concept page.
* **Eq. (27)**, $g_{il}=J^2\sum_{j,k,m,n}\varepsilon_{ijk}\varepsilon_{lmn}(g^{jm}g^{kn}-g^{jn}g^{km})$:
  with the printed unrestricted sums the right side is four times the
  inverse of $[g^{ij}]$; the formula is correct if the sums run over
  $j<k$, $m<n$, or with a factor $\tfrac12$ and the single product
  $g^{jm}g^{kn}$ (compiler's check by expanding the $i=l=1$ term; on the
  [metric tensor](/definitions/metric-tensor.md) page).
* **Jacobian sign**: $J_{\psi,\theta,\varphi}=DR$ with
  $D=rr_\psi\theta_{s,\theta}$ (circular case) and
  $J_{\psi,\theta,\varphi}=(R_\psi Z_\theta-R_\theta Z_\psi)R$ (VMEC case) are
  written for the ordering $(R,Z,\varphi)$; the metric formulas depend only on
  $J^2$.
* "Jacobi" is used for "Jacobian" throughout.

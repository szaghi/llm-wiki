# Summary

H. Seto, B. D. Dudson, X.-Q. Xu and M. Yagi, "A BOUT++ extension for full
annular tokamak edge MHD and turbulence simulations", *Computer Physics
Communications* **283**, 108568 (2023), issue of February 2023 (received 14
August 2022, accepted 19 October 2022); DOI 10.1016/j.cpc.2022.108568, read
from page 1 of the PDF. Version read: the publisher's typeset PDF, 18 pages
numbered 1–18 (PDF page = printed page).

The paper extends [BOUT++](/software/bout-plus-plus.md) (version 3.1) with a
two-dimensional field solver for the $n_\varphi=0$ and low-$n_\varphi$
components, posed in orthogonal flux coordinates, combined with the standard
flute-ordered radial solver for higher modes; it is verified on linear ideal
ballooning modes and applied to pedestal-collapse turbulence in a full torus.

**Scoped read.** This bundle read §1 (pp. 1–2), §2, *Coordinate systems*
(pp. 2–4), §3, the field solvers and the hybrid scheme (pp. 4–5), the
verification of §4 as far as it measures the flute-ordering error (p. 7,
fig. 3), the volume-element definition of §5.1 (eq. (38), p. 9), the summary
(p. 13), Appendix A, the derivation of the geometric coefficients (pp. 14–16),
and the complete reference list (pp. 16–18). Equations were checked on 110-dpi
page images of pp. 2–5 and 14–16. Not read here: the reduced-MHD models, the
DWRBM pedestal-collapse simulations and their diagnostics (§§4–5 beyond the
above), which concern plasma physics. The reference list was merged
complete.

# Key claims

1. **Orthogonal flux-surface coordinates** with diagonal metric
   $h_\Psi=1/(B_{\mathrm p}R)$, $h_\vartheta^2=(\partial_\vartheta R)^2+(\partial_\vartheta z)^2$,
   $h_\varphi=R$, bi-periodic on a $1/N$ wedge (eqs. (1)–(4), pp. 2–3), and
   $\sqrt g=h_\vartheta/B_{\mathrm p}$ for both this and the field-aligned
   system (p. 9). → [magnetic flux coordinates](/definitions/magnetic-flux-coordinates.md),
   [scale factors](/definitions/scale-factors.md), [Jacobian](/definitions/jacobian.md).
2. **Field-aligned coordinates** $\xi^3=\varphi-\chi$, pitch
   $q_{\mathrm{loc}}=B_{\mathrm t}h_\vartheta/(B_{\mathrm p}R)$, bases,
   $\mathbf B=\nabla\xi^3\times\nabla\xi^1$, the sheared radial derivative
   $\partial_1=\partial_\Psi+I\partial_\varphi$ and its secular cell
   deformation, and the twist-shift condition (eqs. (5)–(9), p. 3).
   → [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md).
3. **FFT phase-shift transform** between field-aligned and flux coordinates
   (eqs. (10)–(11), p. 3); the two systems coincide for $n_\varphi=0$
   (p. 4). → [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md).
4. **Quasi-ballooning coordinates**: shifted bases free of $I$, combining
   the shifted radial derivative (Dimits 1993) and the shifted metric (Scott
   2001), with no mixed $\Psi$–$\xi^2$ derivatives (eqs. (12)–(14),
   pp. 3–4). → [shifted metric](/methods/shifted-metric.md),
   [covariant and contravariant bases](/definitions/covariant-and-contravariant-bases.md).
5. **Perpendicular Laplacian** $\nabla^2-\nabla\cdot(\hat{\mathbf b}\hat{\mathbf b}\cdot\nabla)$
   derived from the conservative divergence of the covariant-basis gradient,
   with $\hat{\mathbf b}=\mathbf a_2/\sqrt{g_{22}}$, and written as an expanded
   operator with explicit metric coefficients in quasi-ballooning and in
   orthogonal flux coordinates (App. A, eqs. (A.1)–(A.32), pp. 14–16).
   → [Laplacian](/definitions/laplacian.md), [gradient](/definitions/gradient.md),
   [divergence](/definitions/divergence.md).
6. **Flute-ordering error**: the radial 1D solver is invalid for
   $n_\varphi=0$; against the full 2D operator, ideal-ballooning growth rates
   differ by about 1% at $n_\varphi=20$ and 36% at $n_\varphi=5$, consistent
   with an $O(1/n_\varphi)$ correction (pp. 2, 4, 7). The 2D grid must resolve
   $m_\vartheta\sim n_\varphi q$ (p. 5).
   → [field-aligned coordinates](/definitions/field-aligned-coordinates.md).
7. **Discretization**: radial 1D operator with second-order central
   differences and the Thomas algorithm; the earlier $n_\varphi=0$ 2D operator
   with second-order five-point stencils; the new 2D operator with
   fourth-order central differences on a 9-point star stencil, PETSc GMRES and
   Hypre AMG (pp. 4–5). → [BOUT++](/software/bout-plus-plus.md).

## Recorded as printed (in the sections read)

* **Eq. (A.12)** prints $L^{33}_{\mathrm{qb}}=g^{ww}=B_0^2/(R^2B_{t0}^2)$. From
  eq. (13), $g^{ww}=\lvert\nabla\varphi-q_{\mathrm{loc}}\nabla\vartheta\rvert^2=1/R^2+q_{\mathrm{loc}}^2/h_\vartheta^2=B^2/(R^2B_{\mathrm p}^2)$,
  and only this value reproduces the printed $L^{33}_{\mathrm{fs}}=B_{\mathrm p}^2/(R^2B^2)$
  of eq. (A.28) through $L^{33}_{\mathrm{qb}}+q_{\mathrm{loc}}L^{23}_{\mathrm{qb}}+q_{\mathrm{loc}}^2L^{22}_{\mathrm{qb}}$:
  $B_{t0}$ in (A.12) should be $B_{p0}$ (compiler's check).
* **Eq. (A.20)** prints $\partial^2f/\partial y\partial z=\partial^2f/\partial\theta\partial\zeta+\partial^2f/\partial\zeta^2$;
  the factor $\nu$ on the second term is missing (eq. (A.21) uses it).
* **Eqs. (A.24), (A.27)** write the flux-coordinate coefficients with
  $\partial_y$ for $\partial_\theta$; equivalent for axisymmetric
  coefficients.
* **§4 vs fig. 2:** the inner radial boundary is $\psi=0.6$ in the text and
  $0.4$ in the caption of fig. 2.

# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Block-structured AMR](/definitions/block-structured-amr.md) — Adaptive refinement by a hierarchy of properly nested rectangular patches aligned to coarse grid lines, refined in space and time by a fixed ratio.
* [Discrete exterior calculus (DEC)](/definitions/discrete-exterior-calculus.md) — Calculus built directly on simplicial complexes: forms as cochains, exterior derivative as coboundary, diagonal Hodge star from circumcentric primal-dual volume ratios.
* [Finite element exterior calculus (FEEC)](/definitions/finite-element-exterior-calculus.md) — Finite element spaces of differential forms organized into de Rham subcomplexes with bounded cochain projections; stability and correct convergence follow from the structure.
* [Staggered grid](/definitions/staggered-grid.md) — Mesh arrangement placing different physical quantities at different locations — cell centers, faces, edges, nodes — within one cell.
* [Support-operator method (mimetic discretization)](/definitions/support-operator-method.md) — Discretize one prime operator to satisfy its integral identity exactly; define the remaining operators as its adjoints in discrete inner products.
* [Summation by parts (SBP)](/definitions/summation-by-parts.md) — Difference operators mimicking integration by parts in a discrete inner product, so continuous energy estimates transfer verbatim to the semi-discretization.
* [Whitney forms (discrete differential forms)](/definitions/whitney-forms.md) — Finite elements that extend cochain data on mesh facets into piecewise-polynomial differential forms, with exterior derivative commuting with the extension.

# GLM divergence cleaning

The generalized-Lagrange-multiplier formulation of
[Dedner et al. (2002)](/sources/dedner-2002.md): augment MHD with a scalar
$\psi$ (discrete field $\psi_h$, [notation](/notation.md)) coupled through
$$\partial_t \mathbf{B} + \nabla\cdot(uB^T - Bu^T) + \nabla\psi = 0, \qquad
\mathcal{D}(\psi) + \nabla\cdot\mathbf{B} = 0 .$$
For every choice of the linear operator $\mathcal{D}$, the divergence error
and $\psi$ obey the *same* PDE — so choosing $\mathcal{D}$ chooses the
error dynamics:

| $\mathcal{D}(\psi)$ | Error equation | Family |
|---|---|---|
| $0$ | Poisson (instantaneous removal) | elliptic = [projection](/methods/projection-method.md) (Brackbill–Barnes, rediscovered) |
| $\psi/c_p^2$ | heat equation | parabolic (Marder) |
| $\partial_t\psi/c_h^2$ | wave equation, speed $c_h$ | hyperbolic |
| $\partial_t\psi/c_h^2 + \psi/c_p^2$ | telegraph equation | **mixed** (recommended): transport *and* damping |

## Properties

* **Constraint class: controlled, not held.** No discrete divergence is
  conserved ([exact discrete divergence](/properties/exact-discrete-divergence.md)
  does not apply); errors are continuously generated by the base scheme and
  continuously advected out/damped. The guarantee is boundedness and
  smallness, tunable via $c_h$ (grid-maximal admissible speed) and $c_p$.
* **Conservation intact**: the only source term lives in the unphysical
  $\psi$ equation — the physical conservation laws are unmodified, avoiding
  the non-conservation failure mode of the
  [eight-wave](/methods/eight-wave.md) source terms while keeping the same
  advect-the-error philosophy.
* **Hyperbolic and local**: the 9-wave GLM–MHD system is diagonalizable
  with real eigenvalues; cleaning waves $\pm c_h$ decouple from the MHD
  eigensystem. The method installs as an add-on around any Riemann-solver
  code, explicit and local, on arbitrary (including unstructured) grids —
  its design point, in contrast to global elliptic solves and to
  staggered-mesh machinery.
* **No damping CFL tax**: mixed-correction damping is a source term, so
  $c_p$ is unrestricted by stability; $c_p \to \infty$ recovers the purely
  hyperbolic variant.

## Production realization: CTU-GLM

[Mignone & Tzeferacos (2010)](/sources/mignone-tzeferacos-2010.md) build
the fully cell-centered unsplit CTU-GLM scheme (PLUTO) and benchmark it
head-to-head — same code, same integrator — against eight-wave and
staggered CT: competitive with CT in accuracy and robustness while
"considerably easier to implement." Two additions to the family record:
the **EGLM** variant (Dedner's divergence source terms in momentum/energy)
trades conservation for robustness in strongly magnetized flows, and the
damping parameter acquires the physical reading
$\alpha = \Delta t_d/\Delta t_a$. Their stated motive for the collocated
route: single-location variables ease extension to adaptive grids, extra
physics, and higher order.

On AMR the architectural argument cashes out: in PLUTO-Chombo the
magnetic field is just another cell-centered conserved variable, so level
transfer needs **no dedicated magnetic operators** — standard conservative
interpolation, averaging, and refluxing handle everything, for MHD and
RMHD alike ([Mignone et al. (2012)](/sources/mignone-2012.md)). The
constraint class is unchanged (controlled, not held); what is bought is
uniformity of the AMR machinery across all physics.

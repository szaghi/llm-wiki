# Summary

The monograph of the
[support-operator method](/definitions/support-operator-method.md):
systematic construction of conservative finite-difference methods on
general — distorted, non-orthogonal, logically rectangular — grids, with
downloadable software. The book-length consolidation of the program whose
research articles this bundle compiles
([Hyman & Shashkov (1997)](/sources/hyman-shashkov-1997.md) and the
[MFD review](/sources/lipnikov-2014.md) periodize it as the systematic
foundation phase).

# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Jiang & Shu (1996) — Efficient Implementation of Weighted ENO Schemes](/sources/jiang-shu-1996.md) — The WENO-JS paper: point-value finite-difference WENO with a new smoothness indicator that gives fifth order from three-point stencils, characteristic-wise systems, a convergence proof for smooth solutions and scalar and Euler tests; no MHD and no divergence content.
* [Colella & Woodward (1984) — The Piecewise Parabolic Method (PPM) for Gas-Dynamical Simulations](/sources/colella-woodward-1984.md) — The full algorithmic description of PPM: conservative zone parabolae with fourth-order interface values and a monotonicity constraint, contact steepening, Lagrangian, remap and characteristic-tracing Eulerian gas dynamics, and the extra dissipation (flattening, artificial viscosity) it needs; pure hydrodynamics.
* [Brackbill & Barnes (1980) — The effect of nonzero ∇·B on the numerical solution of the magnetohydrodynamic equations](/sources/brackbill-barnes-1980.md) — Five-page Note identifying the force parallel to the field, (B·B)∇·B, that the conservative Maxwell-stress momentum equation produces when ∇·B ≠ 0, and removing it with a nonconservative momentum equation instead of a projection.
* [Powell (1994) — An approximate Riemann solver for magnetohydrodynamics (that works in more than one dimension)](/sources/powell-1994.md) — The ICASE report that introduced the eight-wave scheme: a non-singular modification of the primitive MHD Jacobian found from four eigenstructure criteria, the divergence wave, the source term proportional to ∇·B, and a first-order Roe-type test on a rotated shock tube.
* [Ryu, Miniati, Jones & Frank (1998) — A divergence-free upwind code for multidimensional magnetohydrodynamic flows](/sources/ryu-1998.md) — Retrofits staggered-mesh constrained transport into a second-order Harten-TVD MHD code: face-centred field, edge EMF averaged from two TVD transport fluxes per direction, exact zero net face flux, no projection; the transport-flux CT variant.
* [Balsara & Spicer (1999) — A staggered mesh algorithm using high order Godunov fluxes to ensure solenoidal magnetic fields in MHD simulations](/sources/balsara-spicer-1999.md) — The primary source of flux-CT: edge EMFs as signed four-face averages of a Godunov scheme's upwind magnetic fluxes, a discrete-Stokes face update, an optional energy correction for low-beta positivity, and a shock-switched directional variant; introduces the MHD rotor test.
* [Brio & Wu (1988) — An upwind differencing scheme for the equations of ideal magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/brio-wu-1988.md) — Roe-type upwind scheme for the 1D ideal-MHD system with constant normal field: an exact Roe matrix for gamma = 2 only, a renormalized right-eigenvector set well defined at the MHD degeneracies, the coplanar shock tube with a slow compound wave, and a proof that the MHD system is nonconvex.
* [Hoppe, Winter, Adami & Adams (2022) — ALPACA: a level-set based sharp-interface multiresolution solver for conservation laws](/sources/hoppe-2022.md) — The CPC record of ALPACA, an MPI-parallel C++20 finite-volume code for compressible multi-phase flow on a block octree with Harten-type multiresolution refinement, adaptive local time stepping, a cited resolution-jump flux correction and a one-byte interface tagging system, weak-scaled to 512 SuperMUC-NG nodes; no divergence-constraint content.
* [Irsalinda, Bakar, Harun, Idris & Surono (2026) — A dynamic mesh refinement in Physic-Informed Neural Networks for handling Stiff Partial Differential Equations](/sources/irsalinda-2026.md) — Residual-driven adaptive resampling of PINN collocation points (Aug-MR PINN), called mesh refinement but outside the bundle's AMR sense, with no divergence content; its comparison table does not match its own result tables, its baselines get smaller point budgets, its convergence lemma assumes its conclusion, and it reports losses but no errors or costs.
* [Aftosmis, Berger & Murman (2004) — Applications of space-filling-curves to Cartesian methods for CFD](/sources/aftosmis-berger-murman-2004.md) — Extended abstract proposing Morton and Peano–Hilbert space-filling curves as one data structure for ordering, partitioning, multigrid coarsening and inter-mesh transfer on adapted Cartesian cut-cell meshes: one O(N log N) sort, then O(N) passes; Peano–Hilbert partitions carry 4% more communication faces than ideal, and the solver scales to about 430× on 512 CPUs.
* [Almgren et al. (2010) — CASTRO: A new compressible astrophysical solver. I. Hydrodynamics and self-gravity](/sources/almgren-2010.md) — Code paper for CASTRO on BoxLib: unsplit PPM on subcycled block-structured AMR (ratio 2 or 4, proper nesting without parent–child links, refluxing), and a multilevel self-gravity synchronization — level Poisson solves, a lagged composite correction and a flux-register-driven two-level correction solve; no divergence constraint.
* [Jaber, Essel & Sullivan (2025) — GPU-native adaptive mesh refinement with application to lattice Boltzmann simulations](/sources/jaber-essel-sullivan-2025.md) — A single-GPU AMR in which mesh, adaptation and solver all stay on the device: a block forest of quadtrees/octrees stored as integer ID lists with explicit neighbor links (no space-filling curve), 2:1 balance, and a subcycled lattice-Boltzmann solver coupled across levels by two interpolated ghost layers and averaged interface cells without time interpolation; no conservation or divergence content.
* [Knapp, Holke, Spenke, Burstedde & Dreyer (2026) — A Morton-type space-filling curve for pyramid subdivision and hybrid adaptive mesh refinement](/sources/knapp-2026.md) — Preprint adding pyramids to t8code's forest-of-trees AMR: a pyramid refines into 6 pyramids and 4 tetrahedra so that the split commutes with cubic refinement, a Morton-type index over (anchor, level, type) proved to be a space-filling-curve index, element and forest algorithms, and scaling near-ideal to 2^15 processes, with runs up to 2^17 (about 63% efficiency from 2^14 to 2^17); no divergence content.
* [Ranocha, Ostaszewski & Heinisch (2020) — Discrete vector calculus and Helmholtz Hodge decomposition for classical FD SBP operators](/sources/ranocha-2020.md) — For collocated tensor-product SBP operators, div curl = 0 and curl grad = 0 hold exactly, but the potential-existence converses and the Helmholtz-Hodge decomposition fail by explicitly characterised grid-oscillation modes, removable by an orthogonal filter.
* [Shashkov (1996) — Conservative finite-difference methods on general grids](/sources/shashkov-1996.md) — The support-operator monograph: systematic construction of conservative FD methods on general (distorted, non-orthogonal) grids, with accompanying software.
* [Bossavit (1998) — Computational electromagnetism: variational formulations, complementarity, edge elements](/sources/bossavit-1998.md) — The canonical edge-element book: variational computational EM built on Whitney elements, with complementarity (dual formulations giving two-sided bounds) as an organizing principle.
* [Harlow & Welch (1965) — Numerical calculation of time-dependent viscous incompressible flow with free surface](/sources/harlow-welch-1965.md) — Introduces the MAC method: the original staggered grid and pressure-Poisson enforcement of a face-difference discrete divergence of velocity.
* [Wu (2018) — Positivity-preserving analysis of numerical schemes for ideal magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/wu-2018.md) — First rigorous positivity analysis of Lax-Friedrichs FV/DG schemes for multidimensional ideal MHD: positivity holds under, and in general only under, a discrete divergence-free condition on the scheme's interface fields.
* [Wu & Shu (2018) — A provably positive discontinuous Galerkin method for multidimensional ideal MHD](/sources/wu-shu-2018.md) — Provably positivity-preserving high-order DG for multidimensional MHD via the symmetrizable (Godunov-Powell) form: locally divergence-free basis plus penalty terms from the source term remove the need for a discrete divergence-free condition, at the price of exact conservation.
* [Wu & Shu (2019) — Provably positive high-order schemes for ideal MHD: analysis on general meshes](/sources/wu-shu-2019.md) — Extends the MHD positivity analysis to HLL-type fluxes on polytopal meshes in any dimension: generalized discrete divergence, explicit PP wave speeds, upwind penalty discretization of the Godunov-Powell source, and a proof that smooth solutions of the symmetrizable system stay positive for any div B.
* [Yee (1966) — Numerical solution of initial boundary value problems involving Maxwell's equations in isotropic media](/sources/yee-1966.md) — Introduces the Yee lattice and leapfrog FDTD scheme: E on edges, H on faces, curl-form updates chosen so conducting-boundary conditions are imposed naturally.
* [Balsara (2001) — Divergence-free adaptive mesh refinement for magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/balsara-2001.md) — Second-order divergence-free evolution on block-structured AMR: divergence-free reconstruction, prolongation, area-weighted restriction, and electric-field correction under subcycling.
* [Chorin (1968) — Numerical solution of the Navier-Stokes equations](/sources/chorin-1968.md) — Introduces the projection method: advance an auxiliary velocity, then Hodge-decompose it into a discretely divergence-free field plus a discrete pressure gradient.
* [Berta, Mignone, Bugli & Mattia (2024) — A 4th-order accurate finite volume method for ideal classical and special relativistic MHD based on pointwise reconstructions](/sources/berta-2024.md) — Fourth-order FV-CT for MHD and RMHD in PLUTO: point-value MP5/WENO-Z reconstruction (one Riemann solve per face), the Mignone-Del Zanna UCT composition formula at fourth order, and a discontinuity-detector order-reduction fallback.
* [Jones, McNally & Reynolds (2026) — A constrained-transport embedded boundary method for compressible resistive MHD](/sources/jones-2026.md) — 2D cut-cell CT for resistive MHD on Cartesian meshes with static, moving and two-material embedded boundaries: ghost data enters only through edge EMFs, so the divergence stays exact in the active domain while boundary flux is not conserved.
* [Pakmor, Guillet, Bieri, Pfrommer, Springel & Teyssier (2026) — A comparison of numerical schemes for driven subsonic MHD turbulence](/sources/pakmor-2026.md) — Preprint comparing CT (Athena++, Ramses), Powell and Dedner (Arepo, Arepo-dg) on driven subsonic small-scale-dynamo boxes: no systematic advantage for exact divergence, differences tracking numerical dissipation, and an unstable moving-mesh vector-potential CT.
* [Jiang & Wu (1999) — A high-order WENO finite difference scheme for the equations of ideal MHD](/sources/jiang-1999.md) — Fifth-order characteristic WENO finite-difference scheme for ideal MHD with Lax-Friedrichs splitting and Brio-Wu-normalized eigenvectors; a Poisson projection of the field is needed for stability, as Orszag-Tang otherwise loses pressure positivity.
* [Christlieb, Feng, Seal & Tang (2016) — A high-order positivity-preserving single-stage single-step method for ideal MHD](/sources/christlieb-2016.md) — Single-stage single-step (Picard-integral Lax-Wendroff) FD-WENO for MHD with unstaggered potential-based CT and a positivity flux limiter whose guarantee rests on an LF-positivity conjecture later refuted by Wu (2018).
* [Balsara, Käppeli, Boscheri & Dumbser (2023) — Curl constraint-preserving reconstruction and the guidance it gives for mimetic scheme design](/sources/balsara-2023.md) — Edge-collocated, globally curl-preserving WENO-like reconstruction (2D to fourth order, 3D to third) with vertex potentials from multidimensional Riemann solvers: the edge-vertex mirror of divergence-free reconstruction and CT.
* [Seo & Ryu (2023) — HOW-MHD: a high-order WENO-based MHD code with a high-order constrained transport algorithm](/sources/seo-2023.md) — Fifth-order FD-WENO MHD code (adiabatic and isothermal) whose staggered CT is raised to high order by edge point-value fluxes and fourth/sixth-order differences, lifting 3D wave convergence from second to fifth order.
* [Blomquist, West, Binswanger & Theillard (2024) — Stable nodal projection method on octree grids](/sources/blomquist-2024.md) — Node-collocated incompressible projection on non-graded quad/octrees: not a true projection, but an iteration whose limit is the exact one, contracting on uniform grids via the staggered complex, with stability on adaptive grids set by the divergence's hanging-node ghost rule.
* [Balsara, Bhoriya & Shu (2026) — An alternative finite difference WENO-like scheme with physical constraint preservation for divergence-preserving hyperbolic systems](/sources/balsara-2026.md) — Hybrid scheme to ninth order: zone-centred point values by alternative-FD WENO, face-averaged fields by a Yee-type Stokes update with edge EMFs from a 2D LLF/HLL Riemann solver, plus a time-explicit positivity (PCP) strategy, for CED, MHD and RMHD.
* [Balsara & Kim (2016) — Subluminal RMHD with an ADER-WENO predictor and a multidimensional Riemann solver](/sources/balsara-kim-2016.md) — Fourth-order finite-volume scheme for special-relativistic MHD on a Yee-type mesh: reconstruction of the four-velocity keeps the velocity subluminal, an ADER predictor works in primitive variables, and the edge electric fields come from a two-dimensional MuSIC Riemann solver at each edge instead of averaged 1D fluxes.
* [Tomida, Sadanari, Takasao & Iwasaki (2026) — Systematic comparison between constrained transport and mixed divergence cleaning methods for astrophysical MHD simulations](/sources/tomida-2026.md) — Same-code (Athena++) comparison of CT and Dedner GLM cleaning: a time-step-dependent cleaning speed and localized fields make GLM create spurious, non-solenoidal field (orders-of-magnitude false amplification in collapse runs); fixed parameters and the EGLM energy term help, CT remains more robust.
* [Stone, Gardiner, Teuben, Hawley & Simon (2008) — Athena: a new code for astrophysical MHD](/sources/stone-2008.md) — Code paper for Athena: second-order unsplit Godunov MHD (CTU+CT and VL+CT) with face-centred fields and the Gardiner-Stone edge-EMF construction, a full 1D-3D test suite, and the field-loop checks that separate round-off from truncation-level preservation.
* [Couch, Carlson, Pajkos, O'Shea, Dubey & Klosterman (2021) — Towards performance portability in the Spark astrophysical MHD solver in the Flash-X simulation framework](/sources/couch-2021.md) — Performance-portability paper for Flash-X's Spark MHD solver (fifth-order WENO, HLLD, SSP-RK, cell-centred divergence control): pencil data layout, key-dictionary program synthesis for CPU/GPU, and AMR flux registers holding Runge-Kutta stage combinations.
* [Berger & Colella (1989) — Local adaptive mesh refinement for shock hydrodynamics](/sources/berger-colella-1989.md) — Defines block-structured AMR: properly nested rectangular patch hierarchies refined in space and time, with conservative averaging and flux correction at coarse-fine interfaces.
* [Strand (1994) — Summation by parts for finite difference approximations for d/dx](/sources/strand-1994.md) — Constructs and catalogues SBP first-derivative operators for diagonal, full, and restricted-full norms, with boundary/interior order combinations and explicit coefficients.
* [Almgren, Bell, Colella, Howell & Welcome (1998) — A conservative adaptive projection method for the variable density incompressible Navier-Stokes equations](/sources/almgren-1998.md) — Projection-method AMR with subcycling: MAC-projected advection velocities, approximate nodal projections, and a four-mismatch elliptic synchronization suite at coarse-fine interfaces.
* [Nordström & Carpenter (1999) — High order finite difference methods, multidimensional linear problems and curvilinear coordinates](/sources/nordstrom-carpenter-1999.md) — SBP-SAT boundary and interface conditions for multi-block curvilinear grids: conservative, strictly and strongly stable schemes conditional on metric conditions.
* [Powell et al. (1999) — A solution-adaptive upwind scheme for ideal magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/powell-1999.md) — Primary formulation of the eight-wave scheme: MHD solved in Godunov's symmetrizable form with source terms, the divergence advected as a passive scalar.
* [Brezzi, Lipnikov & Shashkov (2005) — Convergence of the mimetic finite difference method for diffusion problems on polyhedral meshes](/sources/brezzi-lipnikov-shashkov-2005.md) — First rigorous stability and optimal-convergence proof for mimetic FD on general polyhedral meshes, covering locally refined and nonmatching meshes as degenerate polyhedra.
* [Hirani (2003) — Discrete exterior calculus (PhD thesis, Caltech)](/sources/hirani-2003.md) — The thesis form of DEC: discrete-first development (not truncation of smooth calculus), circumcentric duals, and the proliferation of primal/dual operator variants unique to the discrete theory.
* [Li & Shu (2005) — Locally divergence-free discontinuous Galerkin methods for MHD equations](/sources/li-shu-2005.md) — DG with element-local exactly divergence-free polynomial bases for the magnetic field: cheaper than standard DG, same accuracy, improved stability — with interelement jumps unconstrained.
* [Desbrun, Hirani, Leok & Marsden (2005) — Discrete exterior calculus](/sources/desbrun-hirani-2005.md) — Ab initio calculus on simplicial complexes: discrete forms and vector fields with d, Hodge star, codifferential, wedge, Lie derivative on circumcentric primal-dual meshes; Stokes true by definition.
* [Arnold, Falk & Winther (2006) — Finite element exterior calculus, homological techniques, and applications](/sources/arnold-falk-winther-2006.md) — The FEEC founding review: two families of finite element differential forms arranged into de Rham subcomplexes reproducing cohomology, with stability tied to cochain projections.
* [Desbrun, Kanso & Tong (2008) — Discrete differential forms for computational modeling](/sources/desbrun-kanso-tong-2008.md) — Pedagogical DEC chapter whose distinctive content is the Whitney bridge: interpolating cochains to pointwise forms via Whitney k-forms, joining the DEC and FE-forms branches.
* [Fromang, Hennebelle & Teyssier (2006) — A high order Godunov scheme with constrained transport and adaptive mesh refinement for astrophysical magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/fromang-2006.md) — RAMSES-MHD: MUSCL-Hancock Godunov scheme with CT on tree-based (octree) AMR, with 2D-Riemann EMFs; production evidence on MRI and magnetized core collapse.
* [Hiptmair (2002) — Finite elements in computational electromagnetism](/sources/hiptmair-2002.md) — Acta Numerica review founding FE electromagnetics on discrete differential forms: Whitney elements, commuting-diagram interpolation, and the discrete de Rham theorem with cohomology.
* [Balsara (2004) — Second-order-accurate schemes for magnetohydrodynamics with divergence-free reconstruction](/sources/balsara-2004.md) — Consolidates divergence-free MHD scheme design: four open issues resolved via reconstruction-based zone averages, face-variable limiting, C0 normal continuity, and directly evaluated edge EMFs.
* [Gardiner & Stone (2008) — An unsplit Godunov method for ideal MHD via constrained transport in three dimensions](/sources/gardiner-stone-2008.md) — The 3D Athena algorithm: unsplit CTU integration with upwind CT electric fields, PPM interface states with divergence-balanced MHD source terms, 6- and 12-solve variants.
* [Mignone & Del Zanna (2021) — Systematic construction of upwind constrained transport schemes for MHD](/sources/mignone-2021.md) — General composition formula building UCT edge EMFs from any Jacobian-free 1D Riemann solver (new UCT-HLLD, UCT-GFORCE), with a six-way comparison showing the EMF averaging is as consequential as the Riemann solver.
* [Mignone & Tzeferacos (2010) — A second-order unsplit Godunov scheme for cell-centered MHD: the CTU-GLM scheme](/sources/mignone-tzeferacos-2010.md) — Fully cell-centered unsplit CTU scheme with mixed GLM cleaning, benchmarked head-to-head against eight-wave and staggered CT in PLUTO; competitive and simpler to implement.
* [Mignone, Tzeferacos & Bodo (2010) — High-order conservative finite difference GLM-MHD schemes for cell-centered MHD](/sources/mignone-tzeferacos-bodo-2010.md) — Third- and fifth-order point-value finite-difference MHD with GLM cleaning (WENO+3, LimO3, WENO-Z, MP5) in PLUTO; upwinds the (B_n, psi) subsystem separately and finds the optimal damping order-dependent.
* [Christiansen, Munthe-Kaas & Owren (2011) — Topics in structure-preserving discretization](/sources/christiansen-2011.md) — Acta Numerica review; its finite-element-systems part generalizes FEEC to polyhedral cellular complexes, with compatibility conditions yielding commuting interpolators and smoothed stable projections.
* [de Goes, Butts & Desbrun (2020) — Discrete differential operators on polygonal meshes](/sources/degoes-2020.md) — Graphics-production consolidation: a linear-precise mimetic gradient on arbitrary (non-planar, non-convex) polygons bootstraps a full operator family, VEM-style with MFD stabilization.
* [Trask, Bochev & Perego (2020) — A conservative, consistent, and scalable meshfree mimetic method](/sources/trask-2020.md) — Meshfree mimetic divergence on point clouds: virtual faces built from GMLS or graph-Laplacian moments satisfy a discrete divergence theorem and local conservation without any mesh.
* [Crane (2025) — Discrete differential geometry: an applied introduction](/sources/crane-2025.md) — Lecture-note consolidation of applied DDG: exterior calculus, discrete curvature, and DEC-style operators as standard curriculum, with the no-free-lunch principle for discrete definitions.
* [Zaghi, Salvadore, Di Mascio & Rossi (2023) — Efficient GPU parallelization of adaptive mesh refinement technique for high-order compressible solver with immersed boundary](/sources/zaghi-2023.md) — The published description of the ADAM block-octree AMR library at its 2023 state: identical Cartesian blocks, 2:1 balance, a Morton-keyed CPU hash map, a GPU-resident field with CUDA Fortran, second-order ghost interpolation and one global time step, with a WENO compressible Navier–Stokes application scaled to 256 V100 GPUs; no divergence-constraint content.
* [Zampa (2024) — Structure-preserving finite element and finite volume methods for nonlinear and time-dependent PDEs (PhD thesis, Trento)](/sources/zampa-2024.md) — The 2024 frontier: two exactly divergence-free hybrid schemes for visco-resistive MHD on unstructured mixed-element grids, one well-balanced, plus asymptotic-preserving low-Mach and multisymplectic compatible FE.
* [Kozdon, Erickson & Wilcox (2021) — Hybridized summation-by-parts finite difference methods](/sources/kozdon-2021.md) — Imports HDG hybridization into SBP-SAT elliptic solvers: independent trace variables on block faces, Schur elimination of volume unknowns, SPD global systems.
* [Stone, Tomida, White & Felker (2020) — The Athena++ adaptive mesh refinement framework: design and magnetohydrodynamic solvers](/sources/stone-2020.md) — Modern CT-AMR production framework: mesh abstracted from physics with face/edge data first-class, line-weighted EMF matching at interfaces, and bitwise EMF consistency enforced even on same-level shared edges.
* [Olivares et al. (2019) — Constrained transport and adaptive mesh refinement in the Black Hole Accretion Code](/sources/olivares-2019.md) — Staggered upwind-CT on AMR in GRMHD (BHAC), with coordinate-independent divergence-preserving prolongation operators; divergence-control choice shown to change accretion results qualitatively.
* [Wang & Petersson (2019) — Fourth order finite difference methods for the wave equation with mesh refinement interfaces](/sources/wang-petersson-2019.md) — Fourth-order SBP discretizations at hanging-node refinement interfaces: ghost-point (strong) vs SAT (weak) imposition compared, with energy stability and no time-step penalty for the ghost-point method.
* [Wang, Nissen & Kreiss (2017) — Convergence of finite difference methods for the wave equation in two space dimensions](/sources/wang-nissen-kreiss-2017.md) — Extends normal-mode accuracy analysis to 2D by diagonalization, separately treating truncation errors along entire boundaries and errors localized at corners.
* [Wang & Kreiss (2017) — Convergence of summation-by-parts finite difference methods for the wave equation](/sources/wang-kreiss-2017.md) — Normal-mode convergence theory: the two-order gain from localized boundary truncation error requires the determinant condition, which many common SBP-SAT discretizations fail at s = 0.
* [Felker & Stone (2018) — A fourth-order accurate finite volume method for ideal MHD via upwind constrained transport](/sources/felker-2018.md) — Fourth-order FV MHD on the McCorquodale-Colella base with UCT carried at the same order (PPM4 corner reconstruction, HLL four-state corner EMF); shows why the Athena E^c EMF caps at second order and that it is second-order UCT in limiting cases.
* [Friedrich, Winters, Del Rey Fernández, Gassner, Parsani & Carpenter (2018) — An entropy stable h/p non-conforming discontinuous Galerkin method with the summation-by-parts property](/sources/friedrich-2018.md) — Proves the standard mortar coupling loses entropy stability for nonlinear systems at h/p nonconforming interfaces, and modifies it to restore provable stability at unchanged parallel cost.
* [Derigs, Winters, Gassner, Walch & Bohm (2018) — Ideal GLM-MHD: about the entropy consistent nine-wave magnetic field divergence diminishing ideal magnetohydrodynamics equations](/sources/derigs-2018.md) — Unifies Powell terms and GLM cleaning into a nine-wave, Galilean-invariant, entropy-consistent system; shows some common cleaning extensions violate the second law; entropy-stable fluxes in FLASH.
* [Lundquist, Malan & Nordström (2018) — A hybrid framework for coupling arbitrary summation-by-parts schemes on general meshes](/sources/lundquist-2018.md) — Generalizes SBP-preserving interface coupling beyond tensor-product grids: stable non-collocated interfaces between arbitrary SBP schemes, including high-order FD to unstructured FV.
* [Almquist, Wang & Werpers (2019) — Order preserving interpolation for summation-by-parts operators at non-conforming grid interfaces](/sources/almquist-2018.md) — Restores full global convergence at nonconforming SBP interfaces for second-derivative PDEs via order-preserving interpolation pairs, circumventing the quadrature-order accuracy bound.
* [Ding & Wu (2024) — A new discretely divergence-free positivity-preserving high-order finite volume method for ideal MHD](/sources/ding-2024.md) — Provably positive high-order FV-WENO for MHD: a minimal-norm projection of reconstructed interface point values enforces a cell-local discrete divergence-free condition, combined with a jump-based Godunov-Powell source and a point-value positivity limiter.
* [Etienne, Liu & Shapiro (2010) — Relativistic magnetohydrodynamics in dynamical spacetimes: a new AMR implementation](/sources/etienne-2010.md) — GRMHD with the induction equation recast as vector-potential evolution: B = curl A is divergence-free on any grid, and the unconstrained A admits arbitrary AMR interpolation.
* [Etienne, Paschalidis, Liu & Shapiro (2012) — Relativistic MHD in dynamical spacetimes: improved EM gauge condition for AMR grids](/sources/etienne-2012.md) — Diagnoses spurious magnetic fields at refinement boundaries as zero-speed gauge modes of the algebraic gauge; the Lorenz gauge has only propagating modes and cures them.
* [Perot (2011) — Discrete conservation properties of unstructured mesh schemes](/sources/perot-2011.md) — Review distinguishing primary from secondary conservation: derived-quantity invariants are harder, materially improve fidelity, and depend on formulation more than discretization family.
* [Miniati & Martin (2011) — Constrained-transport magnetohydrodynamics with adaptive mesh refinement in CHARM](/sources/miniati-martin-2011.md) — 12-solve CTU+CT MHD on Chombo patch AMR with a reflux-curl synchronization; divergence-free ghost interpolation found neither practical nor necessary under subcycling.
* [Mignone et al. (2012) — The PLUTO code for adaptive mesh computations in astrophysical fluid dynamics](/sources/mignone-2012.md) — PLUTO-Chombo AMR for MHD/RMHD: cell-centered CTU with GLM cleaning, so the magnetic field needs no AMR machinery beyond standard conservative interpolation and refluxing.
* [Bonelle & Ern (2014) — Analysis of compatible discrete operator schemes for elliptic problems on polyhedral meshes](/sources/bonelle-ern-2014.md) — CDO schemes analyzed: topological laws exact, all error in the discrete Hodge operator, whose two design properties yield optimal energy error estimates on polyhedral meshes.
* [Del Rey Fernández, Hicken & Zingg (2014) — Review of summation-by-parts operators with simultaneous approximation terms for the numerical solution of partial differential equations](/sources/delreyfernandez-2014.md) — SBP-SAT consolidation review: the norm as a 2p-accurate quadrature, dual consistency and functional superconvergence, and the generalized-SBP unification with pseudo-spectral and DG operators.
* [Teixeira (2013) — Differential forms in lattice field theories: an overview](/sources/teixeira-2013.md) — Review of lattice EM via forms: ordinary vs twisted forms on primal/dual lattices, discrete Hodge construction routes, and a taxonomy of premetric inconsistencies on irregular lattices.
* [Kreeft & Gerritsma (2013) — Mixed mimetic spectral element method for Stokes flow: a pointwise divergence-free solution](/sources/kreeft-gerritsma-2013.md) — Arbitrary-order mimetic spectral elements on curvilinear quads/hexes: the projection commutes with the exterior derivative, so div/curl/grad are discretized exactly and Stokes solutions are pointwise divergence-free.
* [Palha, Rebelo, Hiemstra, Kreeft & Gerritsma (2014) — Physics-compatible discretization techniques on single and dual grids, with application to the Poisson equation of volume forms](/sources/palha-2014.md) — The framework paper of the Delft mimetic school: forms vs pseudo-forms with Hodge-star as the switch, dual-grid (staggered-FV-like) vs single-grid (FE-like) methods proved equivalent.
* [Lipnikov, Manzini & Shashkov (2014) — Mimetic finite difference method](/sources/lipnikov-2014.md) — The comprehensive 50-year review of mimetic discretization: consistency and stability conditions, families of schemes via non-unique inner products, degenerate cells and hanging nodes, higher order.
* [Nissen, Kormann, Grandin & Virta (2014) — Stable difference methods for block-oriented adaptive grids](/sources/nissen-2014.md) — SBP-SAT adaptive mesh refinement on block-oriented grids: stable treatment of junction/corner points where interfaces of different refinement meet, at a local accuracy cost.
* [Etienne, Paschalidis, Haas, Mösta & Shapiro (2015) — IllinoisGRMHD: an open-source, user-friendly GRMHD code for dynamical spacetimes](/sources/etienne-2015.md) — Open-source consolidation of the vector-potential GRMHD scheme with the damped generalized Lorenz gauge; roundoff-identical to the original code at twice the speed.
* [Kozdon & Wilcox (2015) — Stable coupling of nonconforming, high-order finite difference methods](/sources/kozdon-wilcox-2015.md) — Couples nonconforming SBP blocks through H-compatible projections onto an interface glue space of piecewise polynomials, with provable energy stability including SBP-DG coupling.
* [Arnold, Falk & Winther (2009) — Finite element exterior calculus: from Hodge theory to numerical stability](/sources/arnold-falk-winther-2009.md) — The abstract FEEC stability theory: on closed Hilbert complexes, a subcomplex plus a bounded cochain projection yield inherited cohomology and stable Hodge-Laplacian discretization.
* [Mattsson & Carpenter (2010) — Stable and accurate interpolation operators for high-order multiblock finite difference methods](/sources/mattsson-carpenter-2010.md) — Constructs SBP-preserving interface interpolation operators for nonconforming (2:1) multiblock grids, keeping strict stability, accuracy, and conservation of the base scheme.
* [Mattsson & Nordström (2004) — Summation by parts operators for finite difference approximations of second derivatives](/sources/mattsson-nordstrom-2004.md) — Constructs narrow-stencil second-derivative SBP operators sharing the first-derivative norm, with boundary closures two orders lower preserving design accuracy.
* [Christlieb, Rossmanith & Tang (2014) — Finite difference WENO schemes with constrained transport for ideal MHD](/sources/christlieb-2014.md) — Fourth-order unstaggered constrained transport: collocated FD-WENO MHD whose predicted field is replaced each stage by the fourth-order central curl of a WENO-HJ-evolved potential, holding the collocated central divergence exactly.
* [Clemens & Weiland (2001) — Discrete electromagnetism with the finite integration technique](/sources/clemens-weiland-2001.md) — Review of FIT: Maxwell's integral equations become exact topological matrix equations on a primal-dual grid doublet, with all discretization error confined to the material (Hodge) matrices.
* [Dedner et al. (2002) — Hyperbolic divergence cleaning for the MHD equations](/sources/dedner-2002.md) — Introduces GLM-MHD: the divergence constraint coupled through a generalized Lagrange multiplier, with divergence errors transported at finite speed and damped.
* [Hyman & Shashkov (1997) — Natural discretizations for the divergence, gradient, and curl on logically rectangular grids](/sources/hyman-shashkov-1997.md) — Builds discrete vector analysis on nonorthogonal logically rectangular grids: natural DIV/GRAD/CURL from coordinate-invariant definitions, with discrete exactness theorems including converses.
* [Tóth & Roe (2002) — Divergence- and curl-preserving prolongation and restriction formulas](/sources/toth-roe-2002.md) — Second-order prolongation/restriction preserving discrete divergence (and sometimes curl) at 2:1 interfaces, for staggered and vertex-collocated storage; locality fails beyond staggering.
* [Evans & Hawley (1988) — Simulation of MHD flows: a constrained transport method](/sources/evans-hawley-1988.md) — Introduces constrained transport (CT): staggered flux-based induction-equation update preserving the divergence constraint to machine roundoff.
* [Londrillo & Del Zanna (2000) — High-order upwind schemes for multidimensional magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/londrillo-delzanna-2000.md) — Derives staggered collocation from consistency arguments and constructs high-order divergence-free reconstruction and upwind EMFs for CT schemes.
* [Tóth (2000) — The ∇·B = 0 constraint in shock-capturing MHD codes](/sources/toth-2000.md) — Systematic comparison of seven divergence-control schemes; proves CT variants are pure finite-volume schemes and that conservation excludes exact Lorentz-force orthogonality.
* [Zaghi (2026) — ADAM verification record: seam divergence in collocated AMR solvers](/sources/zaghi-2026.md) — Verification docs and seam-divergence research threads of the ADAM code: exact interior divergence lost at 2:1 seams, a point-value restriction bound, five failed local fixes, and GLM's limits.
* [Londrillo & Del Zanna (2004) — The upwind constrained transport method](/sources/londrillo-delzanna-2004.md) — Formalizes upwind constrained transport: staggered face fields as primary data in reconstruction, Riemann solvers and four-state edge EMFs, making the divergence condition built-in and cancelling numerical monopoles.
* [Balsara & Kim (2004) — Divergence-cleaning vs staggered-mesh MHD](/sources/balsara-kim-2004.md) — Cross-compares two scalar projections, a spectral vector projection and a staggered-mesh CT scheme on supernova-driven MHD turbulence: all cleaning variants show spurious energetics, structures and spectra, attributed to the non-locality of the elliptic step.
* [Gardiner & Stone (2005) — An unsplit Godunov method for ideal MHD via constrained transport](/sources/gardiner-stone-2005.md) — 2D CTU+CT scheme: finite-volume/CT consistency relations, a systematic construction of corner EMFs that reduce to the 1D solver for grid-aligned flow (the contact-upwinded E^c), and the multidimensional MHD source terms PPM interface states require.
* [Balsara (2009) — Divergence-free reconstruction of magnetic fields and WENO schemes for MHD](/sources/balsara-2009.md) — Extends divergence-free reconstruction to third and fourth order in a Legendre-moment basis (face moments fix the interior; free constraints set by magnetic-energy minimization), giving finite-volume divergence-free WENO schemes that meet design order.
* [Zhang et al. (2019) — AMReX: a framework for block-structured adaptive mesh refinement](/sources/zhang-2019.md) — Four-page software paper for AMReX, the BoxLib successor: cell, face and node data, optional subcycling with unspecified multilevel synchronization, embedded boundaries, particles, geometric multigrid and BiCGStab, MPI+X and MPI+CUDA+X; no algorithms, tests or numbers, and no divergence content.

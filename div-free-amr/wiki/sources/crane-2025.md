# Summary

The standard applied-DDG curriculum: exterior algebra and calculus,
discrete forms and the DEC operator set, discrete curvature, geodesics,
and the Laplace–Beltrami toolchain, developed for practitioners. Ingested
as the corpus's pedagogical-consolidation marker: the machinery this
bundle traces from [Yee](/methods/yee-scheme.md) and mimetic FD through
[DEC](/definitions/discrete-exterior-calculus.md) is now teachable,
mainstream infrastructure. (No permanent DOI; the CMU project page is the
canonical, continuously updated source — identifier note per repo law.)

**Coverage of this ingestion (scoped):** table of contents and framing
chapters; body not compiled — its technical content is already covered by
the DEC/Whitney sources in this bundle at research grade.

# Key claims

1. **The no-free-lunch principle of discretization** (the notes' running
   theme): each smooth object admits several inequivalent discrete
   definitions, each preserving a different subset of the continuous
   properties — no discretization preserves all of them; picking the
   definition *is* picking the preserved structure. This is the
   pedagogical form of the operator-proliferation and
   scheme-family observations compiled from the research literature.

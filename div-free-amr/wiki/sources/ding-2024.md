# Summary

This paper carries the Wu / Wu–Shu positivity programme to **standard
finite-volume WENO**, which returns interface point values instead of
polynomials. The DG ingredient of
[Wu & Shu (2018)](/sources/wu-shu-2018.md), a locally divergence-free
polynomial basis, is not available there. It is replaced by a projection
of the reconstructed traces. The method discretizes the symmetrizable
system, uses the LF flux on uniform Cartesian meshes, and is proved
positive by geometric quasilinearization (GQL). It is merged into the
[positivity–divergence coupling](/results/positivity-divergence-coupling.md)
and [divergence-free reconstruction](/methods/divergence-free-reconstruction.md).

The file read is the arXiv v1 preprint (2023). The published version,
SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 46(1):A50–A79 (2024), was identified on Crossref by
title.

# Key claims

1. **DDF projection** (§2.3, Propositions 2.3–2.5, Theorems 2.6–2.7).
   * In each cell, the reconstructed normal-field traces on the cell's own
     faces are shifted by $\mp A_1$ and $\mp A_2$. This is the
     minimal-norm, Euclidean-projection correction that zeroes their
     face-quadrature divergence (eq. 23).
   * The map is an orthogonal projection ($P^2 = P$). At second order it is
     exactly a minimal slope correction of the linear reconstruction.
   * It keeps the reconstruction's order $k$ for $C^k$ fields, and needs no
     polynomial.
2. **The condition is cell-local** (eq. 9). It involves only the cell's
   own traces, so it is a discrete, point-value analogue of the local
   divergence-freeness of Li & Shu. It is *not* Wu (2018)'s face-averaged
   condition. Inter-cell divergence is left to the source term.
3. **Source term** (eq. 12). The source is
   $-\tfrac12[\![B_n]\!]\,S(\{\!\{U\}\!\})$ on each face at each quadrature
   node, evaluated at the *averaged* face state. The authors report it is
   new, and that it gives a milder positivity CFL than the Wu–Shu
   discretizations.
4. **Positivity theorem** (Theorem 2.1, §3). With the projection, a simple
   point-value positivity limiter (§2.4, which does not undo the
   projection) and this source term, the forward-Euler update is
   admissible under an LF CFL with viscosity raised by a jump term
   $\beta_\ell \sim [\![B_\ell]\!]/\sqrt{\rho}$. SSP-RK3 inherits this by
   convexity. The authors state that removing any one of the three
   ingredients may lose positivity.
5. **Numerics** (§4, second- and fifth-order, CFL 0.3).
   * Design order on a low-pressure vortex.
   * Orszag–Tang, rotor and blast: without the projection the discrete
     divergence grows fast, with oscillations and nonphysical structure.
     Without both the projection and the limiter, fifth-order WENO breaks
     down at $t = 3.11$ on Orszag–Tang. With the projection the divergence
     stays at about $10^{-13}$.
   * Jets at very high Mach number run robustly.
   * The monitored "divergence error" is the cell-local trace condition
     itself, which the projection zeroes each stage. It is a constraint on
     reconstructed data, not an evolved invariant.

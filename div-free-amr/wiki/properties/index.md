# Properties

Constraints and invariants a method can hold.

* [Exact discrete divergence](/properties/exact-discrete-divergence.md) — The algebraic invariant $D_h B_h = 0$: a specific discrete divergence of the evolved field vanishes identically, up to machine roundoff.
* [Free-stream preservation](/properties/free-stream-preservation.md) — A constant (free-stream) state remains exactly constant under the scheme, independent of grid refinement pattern, mesh distortion, or velocity field.
* [Secondary conservation](/properties/secondary-conservation.md) — Discrete conservation of derived quantities — kinetic energy, entropy, vorticity, divergence relations — not among the scheme's unknowns; harder than primary conservation and decisive for fidelity.

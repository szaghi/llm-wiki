# Results

Theorems, proved properties, impossibility results.

* [Discrete exactness theorems](/results/discrete-exactness-theorems.md) — On logically rectangular grids the natural discrete operators satisfy the vector-analysis theorems with converses: solenoidal iff a discrete curl, irrotational iff a discrete gradient.
* [CT–finite-volume equivalence](/results/ct-finite-volume-equivalence.md) — The Godunov CT variants are algebraically identical to pure finite-volume schemes on the cell-centered field, which conserve a corner-centered divergence exactly.
* [Conservation–orthogonality incompatibility](/results/conservation-orthogonality-incompatibility.md) — No conservative scheme can guarantee the discrete Lorentz acceleration is exactly orthogonal to the magnetic field in every cell; proved by counterexample.
* [MFD convergence on polyhedral meshes](/results/mfd-convergence.md) — The mixed-form mimetic finite difference method is stable and converges at optimal rates for scalar and flux variables on general polyhedral meshes, including degenerate elements.
* [Locality limits of divergence-preserving transfer](/results/transfer-operator-locality-limits.md) — At 2:1 interfaces, local divergence-preserving prolongation and restriction both exist only for face-staggered storage; vertex collocation loses local restriction, cell collocation loses both.
* [SBP operator existence and families](/results/sbp-operator-existence.md) — SBP first-derivative operators exist at every interior order; the achievable boundary orders and parameter families are catalogued per norm class.
* [Divergence source at collocated 2:1 seams](/results/collocated-seam-divergence-source.md) — A collocated scheme with exact interior divergence loses it at 2:1 seams: the seam injects a source that converges in h but grows in t at fixed h, and no local fix was found.
* [Restriction-compatibility bound for point-value prolongation](/results/point-value-restriction-compatibility.md) — For point-value DOFs with the conservation-consistent mean restriction, exact round-trip compatibility R P = I is incompatible with reproducing quadratics: compatible ghost fills are at most second-order.

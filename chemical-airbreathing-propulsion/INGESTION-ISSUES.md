### Contradictions

None opened. Judged qualifications (prose on the pages):
* **Wave speed against Chapman–Jouguet** (peng-2018 vs zhou-2018,
  [rotating detonation engine](wiki/definitions/rotating-detonation-engine.md)):
  peng's single wave at about 104 % of $D_{\mathrm{CJ}}$ (ethylene–air, hollow
  chamber, Laval nozzle) vs zhou's about 20 % deficit (hydrogen–air annulus with a
  turbine); different mixtures, geometries and $D_{\mathrm{CJ}}$ reference states.
* **Shock train and thermal throat** (yan-2018 vs ye-2018): corroborating, not
  conflicting.
* **Wanhainen correlations** (armbruster-2018's counter-evidence): attributed
  prose; Wanhainen is not ingested.

### Printed inconsistencies (details under "Recorded as printed" on each Source page)

* ye-2018: the +37.1 % specific-impulse gain cannot be reproduced from Table 5;
  thrust ≠ pressure force + drag in three Table 5 rows; Table 1 area ratios
  contradict Eq. 2.
* yan-2018: "+12 %" thrust vs +13.9 % in Table 5; "Case 0" Mach 3 vs 2.5; shock
  train at x/h = 22 upstream of the isolator start (25.1).
* zhou-2018: "1.5 s" for 0.15 s; Fig. 11 plots a stable speed at φ = 0.6, which
  §3.2 says never stabilised.
* peng-2018: Table 1 frequencies differ from text and figures; Figs. 10b and 11b
  appear identical; single-wave speed above C-J unexplained.
* armbruster-2018: peak-to-peak amplitude given as >75 %, 80 % and 86 % of
  chamber pressure; Eq. 5 does not vanish at zero velocity as printed.
* bai-2018: Table 2 gas/liquid ratios inconsistent with its flow rates.
* greer-2018: surface resistance 0.17 for 0.017 K/W; characteristic length
  0.63 m for about 0.07 m; heat of reaction 27.5 vs 34.6 MJ/kg Li.
* li-2018: Eq. 3 printed identical to Eq. 1; 315.15 K for 313.15 K.
* veale-2018: "higher for all" aluminised UTS contradicted by one Table 2 row.
* wang-2018-c: 57.7 % (abstract) vs 57.5 % (body); validation 11 % below the
  measured shear, outside the stated uncertainty.
* song-2018: abstract calls the single-orifice cases "less unstable", the body
  "more unstable"; blowoff limit defined as injection pressure, reported as an
  equivalence ratio.

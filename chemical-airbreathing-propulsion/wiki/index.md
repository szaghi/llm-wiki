# chemical-airbreathing-propulsion — Knowledge Bundle

Chemical rocket and air-breathing propulsion as physical and numerical problems: combustion instability and injector dynamics in liquid rocket engines, rotating and continuous detonation, scramjet and rocket-based combined-cycle combustors, flame holding and blowoff, hybrid and solid propellants, and combustion power systems.

**Start here:** the [Notation registry](/notation.md), which is normative
for every page, and the [update log](/log.md).

**Corpus:** 11 sources, all from *Acta Astronautica* vol. 151 (2018), copied
from the plasma-propulsion archive where they had been filed (D23). The
[bibliography](/bibliography.md) lists the works they cite.

## Reading guide

1. **Liquid rocket engines.** [High-frequency combustion instability](/definitions/combustion-instability.md)
   (Rayleigh criterion, injection coupling, the BKD combustor) and the injector
   side of it, [self-pulsation of swirl coaxial injectors](/definitions/injector-self-pulsation.md).
2. **Detonation-based propulsion.** [Rotating detonation engine](/definitions/rotating-detonation-engine.md)
   (propagation modes, wave speed against Chapman–Jouguet, turbine integration).
3. **Air-breathing high-speed propulsion.** [Rocket-based combined-cycle engine](/definitions/rbcc-engine.md),
   [thermal choking and the thermal throat](/definitions/thermal-choking.md), the
   [pre-combustion shock train](/definitions/precombustion-shock-train.md), the
   [variable-geometry combustor](/methods/variable-geometry-combustor.md),
   [boundary-layer combustion for skin-friction reduction](/methods/boundary-layer-combustion.md),
   the [cavity flameholder and lean blowoff](/definitions/cavity-flameholder.md), and the
   [global kinetic mechanisms for kerosene](/methods/global-kerosene-mechanisms.md) the
   simulations use.
4. **Propellants and power.** [Paraffin-wax hybrid fuel](/definitions/paraffin-hybrid-fuel.md),
   [thermo-oxidative aging of HTPB coatings](/methods/aging-constitutive-model-htpb.md)
   (hypothesis-grade fits), and [lithium-combustion power systems](/methods/metal-combustion-power-system.md)
   (model predictions).

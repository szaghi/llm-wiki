# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Armbruster et al. (2018) — Experimental investigation of self-excited combustion instabilities with injection coupling in a cryogenic rocket combustor](/sources/armbruster-2018.md) — Analyses a second, high-amplitude self-excited 1T instability (10.4–12.1 kHz) in DLR's 42-injector LOX/H2 combustor BKD, shows its frequency rising with amplitude in a way consistent with flame shortening, excludes detonation-like waves, shows that the NASA velocity-ratio and stability-limit correlations do not predict it, and proposes a triple coupling of the chamber 1T with the LOX-post 2L and hydrogen-injector 1L modes.
* [Bai et al. (2018) — Investigation of self-pulsation characteristics for a liquid-centered swirl coaxial injector with recess](/sources/bai-2018.md) — Cold-flow experiments and 2D axisymmetric-swirl VOF simulations of a recessed liquid-centered swirl coaxial injector that attribute self-pulsation to periodic blocking of the annular gas by the conical liquid sheet, fit the frequency linearly to the liquid Reynolds number, identify an 8–9 kHz ultra-high-frequency pulsation at high gas flow, and map the onset and vanishing boundaries in the momentum-flux plane.
* [Greer, Paul & Rattner (2018) — Analysis of lithium-combustion power systems for extreme environment spacecraft](/sources/greer-2018.md) — Steady lumped energy-balance and thermal-resistance model of a molten-lithium combustion reactor feeding a sodium thermosiphon and a Stirling or thermoelectric converter, applied to a Venus lander burning in-situ CO2 and a Europa lander carrying SF6, with mass comparisons against batteries and engineering requirements; no experimental validation.
* [Li et al. (2018) — Aging constitutive model of hydroxyl-terminated polybutadiene coating in solid rocket motor](/sources/li-2018.md) — Builds a phenomenological aging constitutive model of an HTPB solid-motor coating from 50-day thermo-oxidative aging at four temperatures: NMR crosslink density follows a power law in time with a modified-Arrhenius rate, and the stress is an aging function times a five-parameter polynomial hyperelastic equilibrium stress; a held-out 313.15 K test predicts crosslink density within 0.6 %.
* [Peng et al. (2018) — Experimental investigations on ethylene-air Continuous Rotating Detonation wave in the hollow chamber with Laval nozzle](/sources/peng-2018.md) — Direct-connect experiments of non-premixed ethylene–air rotating detonation in a hollow 100 mm chamber with seven Laval nozzles (contraction ratio 1–12) that map the lean limit, identify single-wave, two-wave and near-limit sawtooth modes, measure a single-wave speed slightly above the computed Chapman–Jouguet value, and rate propagation stability by the relative standard deviation of the instantaneous frequency.
* [Song et al. (2018) — Experimental study of near-blowoff characteristics in a cavity-based supersonic combustor](/sources/song-2018.md) — Direct-connect experiments at Mach 2.52 and 1600 K stagnation temperature on an ethylene-fuelled cavity-stabilised scramjet combustor with fuel injected upstream of the cavity, measuring lean blowoff limits against injection distance for one and four orifices and resolving near-blowoff local extinction, reignition and a five-step blowoff sequence by 20 000 frame/s luminosity imaging.
* [Veale et al. (2018) — The structural properties of paraffin wax based hybrid rocket fuels with aluminium particles](/sources/veale-2018.md) — Measures tensile and compressive properties of SasolWax 0907 paraffin, pure and with 40 wt% aluminium, against crosshead rate (1–100 mm/min) and temperature (23–40 °C), with DSC, thermal conductivity and a heat-penetration estimate, as input to structural analysis of hybrid-rocket grains.
* [Wang et al. (2018) — Analysis and reduction of skin-friction in a rocket-based combined-cycle engine flow path operating from Mach 1.5 to 6.0](/sources/wang-2018-c.md) — RANS computations of a complete kerosene-fuelled RBCC flow path at 16 flight points from Mach 1.5 to 6 that decompose skin friction by component and relate it to thrust (40.3% of thrust at Mach 6), plus a simplified step-duct computation in which hydrogen boundary-layer combustion lowers the mean wall shear stress by 57.5% at the cost of a local heat-flux rise.
* [Yan et al. (2018) — Effect of the heat release on the component coordination in the rocket-based combined cycle engine](/sources/yan-2018.md) — 3D RANS simulations of an integrated kerosene RBCC flow path with a strut rocket from Mach 3 to 6, validated by direct-connect and shock-tunnel freejet tests, showing that the pre-combustion shock train balances combustion pressure by strengthening as it moves upstream and that splitting fuel between injection stations keeps the inlet started and sets the best thermal-choking location for each flight Mach number.
* [Ye et al. (2018) — Investigation of RBCC performance improvements based on a variable geometry ramjet combustor](/sources/ye-2018.md) — Designs a kerosene RBCC combustor with an actuated expansion angle and geometric throat from isobaric constant-velocity heating relations, tests it in direct-connect runs at simulated Mach 2–6 with a low-flow rocket pilot and rocket-off mode transitions, and shows by RANS that at Mach 3 and 6 it beats a fixed-geometry (thermal-throat) combustor in combustion efficiency, total-pressure recovery and specific impulse.
* [Zhou et al. (2018) — Experimental study on a rotating detonation combustor with an axial-flow turbine](/sources/zhou-2018.md) — Hydrogen–air experiments in an annular rotating detonation combustor driving a real axial-flow turbine that compare spark-plug and pre-detonator ignition, show the same final single-wave state after formation times of about 0.159 s and 0.022 s, trace the spark-ignited mode sequence to the stable wave, and measure wave speeds about 20 % below the CEA Chapman–Jouguet values.

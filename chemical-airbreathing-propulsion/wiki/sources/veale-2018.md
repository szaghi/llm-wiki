# Summary

The paper characterises the mechanical response of SasolWax 0907, a brittle
microcrystalline paraffin used as a liquefying hybrid-rocket fuel, pure
(PW00) and with 40 wt% aluminium powder of 100 µm mean size (AL40). Tension
tests (ASTM D638 Type III) were run at 1, 10 and 100 mm/min and at 23, 30
and 40 °C; compression tests (ASTM D695) at 1 and 10 mm/min at room
temperature only. Aluminium raises the ultimate tensile strength by 10–20 %
and roughly doubles the tensile modulus at room temperature; the strength
rises 60–70 % from 1 to 100 mm/min and falls 65–75 % from 23 to 40 °C, where
failure becomes ductile. The paper also gives casting procedures and
rejection modes for aluminised specimens, DSC melting and crystallisation
onsets, thermal conductivities (0.46 against 1.36 W/(m·K)) and a 1D
semi-infinite estimate of heat penetration into the grain, as input to a
future constitutive model and grain structural analysis.

# Key claims

1. **Rate dependence at 23 °C** (§4.1, Table 1, Figs. 6–7). Strength and
   modulus rise, strain at maximum stress falls, with rate: PW00 1.78, 2.27,
   2.97 MPa and 512, 678, 781 MPa; AL40 2.02, 2.71, 3.30 MPa and 1025, 1162,
   1282 MPa at 1, 10, 100 mm/min. Shown.
2. **Aluminium** (§4.1, Table 1). AL40 strength 10–20 % above PW00 at room
   temperature (+13.5, +19.4, +11.1 % computed by the reader); maximum
   strain similar. Shown.
3. **Temperature** (§4.1, Table 2, Figs. 8–11). Strength falls and failure
   strain rises; at 30 and 40 °C the maximum stress no longer coincides with
   failure (ductile, necking minimal); rate dependence weaker; modulus drops
   sharply (PW00, 1 mm/min: 512, 308, 156 MPa at 23, 30, 40 °C). Shown.
4. **Compression** (§4.2, Table 3, Figs. 12–13). Compressive strength above
   tensile strength, compressive modulus below tensile; failure by bulging,
   base cracking, then angled shear. PW00 2.36 and 3.34 MPa, AL40 3.11 and
   4.03 MPa at 1 and 10 mm/min. Shown.
5. **Summary percentages** (§5). +60–70 % strength from 1 to 100 mm/min
   (computed: +67 %, +63 %); −65–75 % from 23 to 40 °C (computed: 68–75 %).
   Shown.
6. **DSC** (§3, Fig. 5). Melting onset 39.75 °C (Fig. 5 annotation 39.86 °C,
   105.8 J/g, peak 76.59 °C), crystallisation onset 89.27 °C; supplier
   congealing range 83–94 °C and melting point 108 °C (§2); above the melting
   onset the wax is too soft to test. Shown.
7. **Thermal conductivity** (§3). 0.46 ± 0.03 W/(m·K) pure, 1.36 ± 0.03
   W/(m·K) aluminised (TIM Tower). Shown.
8. **Heat penetration** (§3). 1D semi-infinite conduction, surface at 120 °C,
   radiation and convection neglected: the 30 °C isotherm at 1.37 mm (pure)
   and 1.69 mm (aluminised) after 1 s, less than the regression per second.
   Estimate; diffusivities not stated.
9. **Regression rate** (attributed to a thesis). Laboratory N₂O motor:
   predicted 1.63 and 1.82 mm/s, measured about 1.4 and 1.6 mm/s (pure,
   aluminised); the aluminised motor not tested at peak performance. Stated.
10. **Radial heat propagation** (attributed to Santi et al.). None measurable
    in an 80 s paraffin burn; may not hold for aluminised grains. Stated.
11. **Material** (§2). Quasi-isotropic (attributed); cast and machined
    specimens alike; C₅₀H₁₀₂, microcrystalline, brittle class;
    viscoelastic between −20 °C and +40 °C (stated, no citation).
12. **Comparison with Ryu et al.** (§4.2). Pure-wax strength 2.118 MPa
    (Ryu, 5 mm/min) against 2.27 MPa here (10 mm/min); compressive strength
    3.503 against 3.34 MPa; different compression specimens. Stated.

# Methods

* **Material.** SasolWax 0907 (Sasol Wax GmbH); aluminium powder, 100 µm,
  not handled inertly; 40 wt%.
* **Casting** (§2.1–2.2, Figs. 1, 3). Pure: melt at 110 °C in a kiln,
  mould preheated to 110 °C, pour, cool about 12 h in the switched-off kiln,
  demould with boiling water. Aluminised: melt at 110 °C, mould at 90 °C,
  stir continuously until 90 °C, pour. Split moulds. Rejection modes:
  layering, particle settling, localised solidification, voids; 5 %
  rejected.
* **Specimens.** ASTM D638 Type III dog-bones, 10 mm thick; ASTM D695
  cylinders 12.7 × 25.4 mm.
* **Testing.** Instron 5500R, 5 kN load cell, 50 mm extensometer;
  purpose-built thermal chamber; specimens soaked at least 8 h; surface
  temperature checked by thermal camera. Five specimens per case, one wax
  batch; some tests outsourced and matched.
* **Matrix.** Tension at 23 °C (1, 10, 100 mm/min) and 30, 40 °C (1, 10
  mm/min); compression at 23 °C (1, 10 mm/min).
* **Thermal.** DSC (second heat; rates not stated); TIM Tower conductivity.
* **Rates.** Crosshead extension rates in mm/min, called "strain rates" and
  "pull rates"; engineering stress; strain in %.

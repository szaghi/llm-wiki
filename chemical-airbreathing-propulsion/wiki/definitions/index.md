# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Cavity flameholder and lean blowoff](/definitions/cavity-flameholder.md) — A wall cavity that stabilises the flame of a supersonic combustor in its shear layer and recirculation zone, together with its lean blowoff limit, the leanest fuelling at which combustion stays continuous, which for passive (upstream) injection rises with injection distance and passes through a multi-step extinction sequence.
* [High-frequency combustion instability in liquid rocket engines](/definitions/combustion-instability.md) — Self-excited coupling of chamber acoustic modes with unsteady heat release (Rayleigh criterion), here in its injection-coupled form, where a transverse chamber mode locks onto injector resonances, shifts in frequency as the flame shortens, and is not predicted by the classic velocity-ratio correlations.
* [Paraffin-wax hybrid rocket fuel](/definitions/paraffin-hybrid-fuel.md) — Liquefying hybrid-rocket fuel of paraffin wax, brittle at room temperature and soft near its melting onset, whose tensile strength rises with loading rate, is higher with 40 wt% aluminium and falls by 65–75 % between 296 K and 313 K.
* [Pre-combustion shock train](/definitions/precombustion-shock-train.md) — The shock train that combustion back pressure drives into the isolator of a ramjet or dual-mode combustor, whose strength and position balance the combustor pressure against the inlet flow and which, pushed too far upstream, enters the inlet and cuts air capture.
* [Rocket-based combined-cycle engine](/definitions/rbcc-engine.md) — An air-breathing engine that integrates a rocket in a dual-mode ramjet/scramjet flow path so that one engine runs in ejector, ramjet, scramjet and rocket modes across the flight Mach range.
* [Rotating detonation engine](/definitions/rotating-detonation-engine.md) — Combustor in which one or more detonation waves travel continuously around an annular or hollow cylindrical chamber fed with fresh mixture at its head, characterized by its propagation mode, the wave speed relative to the Chapman–Jouguet speed, its formation transient and its coupling to exit nozzles and turbines.
* [Self-pulsation of gas–liquid swirl coaxial injectors](/definitions/injector-self-pulsation.md) — Pressure and flow-rate oscillation of a gas–liquid swirl coaxial injector sustained by time-delayed feedback between the liquid sheet and the gas flow; in a recessed liquid-centered injector it is attributed to periodic blocking of the annular gas by the conical liquid sheet, with a kHz frequency rising linearly with liquid Reynolds number and a jump to an 8–9 kHz band at high gas flow.
* [Thermal choking and the thermal throat](/definitions/thermal-choking.md) — Choking of a ramjet or dual-mode combustor by heat release rather than by a geometric throat, whose axial location, set by the fuel distribution, governs how inlet, isolator and combustor match across flight Mach numbers.

# Results

Theorems, proved properties and barriers.

* [Convergence of volume penalization](/results/convergence-of-volume-penalization.md) — Angot, Bruneau and Fabrie's theorems: as the penalization parameter tends to zero the penalized Navier–Stokes solution converges to the no-slip solution in the fluid and to zero in the body, with an error of order η^{1/4} in the fluid and η^{3/4} in the body for the L2 penalization, a sharper estimate for the H1 penalization, and the body force given by the limit of the penalty term.
* [First-order accuracy of regularized-delta forcing at interfaces](/results/first-order-accuracy-of-regularized-delta-forcing.md) — A singular interface force represented with a regularized delta function of width O(h) smears the jump in the normal derivative of the solution across the interface, so the method converges at first order near and, for the solution, everywhere, even though the delta function interpolates smooth fields to second order.

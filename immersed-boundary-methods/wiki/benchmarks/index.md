# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Flow past a circular cylinder](/benchmarks/flow-past-circular-cylinder.md) — The standard validation of immersed boundary methods for incompressible flow: steady separated flow at Re = 20–40 and periodic vortex shedding at Re ≈ 100–300, checked through drag and lift coefficients, recirculation length and Strouhal number, plus the impulsively started cylinder and the cylinder in a channel.
* [Flow past a sphere](/benchmarks/flow-past-sphere.md) — Three-dimensional and axisymmetric flow around a sphere at Re of order 100–1000, used to test whether an immersed boundary on a non-conforming grid captures separation from a smooth surface, through the recirculation length, surface pressure and wake regimes.
* [Shock–obstacle interaction](/benchmarks/shock-obstacle-interaction.md) — Compressible inviscid test problems in which a shock meets a solid body that is not aligned with the grid — shock reflection from a wedge or ramp (including double Mach reflection), supersonic flow past a wedge, prism or cylinder with an attached or bow shock, and moving shocks diffracting around a prism (Schardin's problem) or a cylinder — used to test immersed and embedded boundaries under strong discontinuities.

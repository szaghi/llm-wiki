# Methods

Algorithms and schemes.

* [Classical (Peskin) immersed boundary method](/methods/classical-immersed-boundary-method.md) — Peskin's formulation of fluid–structure interaction: an elastic structure described in Lagrangian coordinates exerts a force density that is spread to a fixed Eulerian grid with a regularized delta function and moves at the interpolated fluid velocity; its derivation, discretization, time stepping and the stiffness that limits explicit force computation.
* [Cut-cell (embedded-boundary) finite-volume method](/methods/cut-cell-method.md) — Sharp-interface finite-volume method on a Cartesian grid whose cells are clipped by the boundary: volume and face-area fractions describe the geometry, fluxes on the clipped faces and the wall flux enter a conservative update, and the small cut cells are stabilized by merging or redistribution; the compressible (Pember et al.) and incompressible (Ye et al.) founding versions.
* [Direct (discrete-time) forcing](/methods/direct-forcing.md) — Discrete-forcing immersed boundary method in which the force at a forcing point is solved from the discretized momentum equation so that the updated velocity equals a target value reconstructed from the boundary velocity; free of gains and of the stiffness of feedback forcing, with accuracy set by the velocity reconstruction and conservation of cut cells restored by a mass source.
* [Feedback forcing](/methods/feedback-forcing.md) — Continuous-forcing immersed boundary method that drives the fluid velocity at boundary points to the prescribed boundary velocity with a proportional–integral controller; a damped-oscillator interpretation fixes the gains, which make the equations stiff and limit the time step.
* [Flux redistribution for cut cells](/methods/redistribution.md) — Stabilization of explicit cut-cell finite-volume schemes that takes a stable non-conservative update in each small cut cell and redistributes the resulting conservation deficit to neighbouring cells, so that the scheme stays conservative with a time step set by the full cells; Pember et al.'s mass-weighted algebraic version and its adaptive-mesh extension.
* [Ghost fluid method](/methods/ghost-fluid-method.md) — Sharp-interface Eulerian method for multimaterial compressible flow: a level set locates the interface, every grid point near it carries the real fluid and a ghost fluid of the other material whose pressure and normal velocity are copied and whose entropy is extrapolated, and each fluid is advanced with an unmodified single-phase scheme.
* [Immersed interface method](/methods/immersed-interface-method.md) — Sharp-interface finite-difference method for problems with discontinuous coefficients, singular sources or prescribed jumps across an interface not aligned with a uniform grid: at irregular points the stencil coefficients and a right-hand-side correction are derived from Taylor expansions about the interface and the jump conditions, giving global second-order accuracy.
* [Volume penalization (Brinkman penalization)](/methods/volume-penalization.md) — Fictitious-domain immersed boundary method that solves the flow equations in the whole domain and models the body as a porous medium of vanishing permeability through a penalty term on its volume; the L2 (Darcy) and H1 (Brinkman) penalizations, their convergence as the penalty parameter vanishes, and force computation from the penalty term.

# Summary

Yang, Bi and Jiang make the high-order [ghost-cell](/methods/ghost-cell-method.md) extrapolation shock-adaptive: a weighted-least-squares polynomial with the wall condition at the boundary intercept gives a third-order ghost value, a first-order one is formed from boundary and nearby fluid data, and one WENO-type weight blends them ([high-order immersed boundary treatments](/methods/high-order-immersed-boundary.md)). The interior scheme is a modified third-order WCNS.

# Version read

Version of record, J. Fluids Struct. 142, 104517 (2026). §1, §3 and §5 read, §4 tests in outline.

# Key claims

1. Bilinear, inverse-distance, two-reference-point and biquadratic ghost-cell interpolations are second order at best (§1). **Stated**
2. One nonlinear weight blends third- and first-order ghost extrapolations: third order in smooth regions, first order at discontinuities (§3). **Derived**
3. A new smoothness indicator keeps third order of the WCNS at first- and second-order critical points (§2, §4.1). **Shown**
4. Design order is verified for ghost values and for Euler flow in a circular domain; shock–obstacle tests behave without oscillations (§4). **Shown**

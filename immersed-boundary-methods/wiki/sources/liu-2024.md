# Summary

Liu et al. revise the simplified [inverse Lax–Wendroff](/methods/inverse-lax-wendroff.md) treatment. Ghost values come from two steps: interpolation of the interior polynomial to artificial auxiliary points, then extrapolation through those values and the ILW boundary derivatives. Eigenvalue analysis fixes the auxiliary-point position so that the scheme is stable at the periodic CFL number for every cut fraction with fewer ILW terms, a gain that grows with order ([high-order immersed boundary treatments](/methods/high-order-immersed-boundary.md)).

# Key claims

1. The original simplified ILW is stable at the periodic CFL number for every cut fraction only at third order (§1). **Stated**
2. Ghost values from interpolation to auxiliary points plus extrapolation with ILW derivatives allow stability with fewer ILW terms (§2–§3). **Derived**
3. For third order with two ILW terms the auxiliary-point parameter must satisfy $\alpha\ge0.61$ (§3, Appendix A). **Shown**
4. Fifth-order WENO with TVD RK3 and boundary-data correction keeps the design order on scalar and Euler tests with curved boundaries (§4). **Shown**

# Operators

Discrete operators with an algebraic identity.

* [Regularized delta function $\delta_h$](/operators/regularized-delta-function.md) — The smoothed approximation of the Dirac delta that links Lagrangian boundary points to the Eulerian grid in the classical immersed boundary method: a tensor product of one-dimensional kernels $\varphi$ fixed by moment, even–odd and sum-of-squares conditions, with Peskin's 1972 hat kernel, his 4-point kernel and its cosine approximation, and the 3-point and higher-moment variants.
* [Spreading and interpolation operators $\mathcal S$, $\mathcal J$](/operators/spreading-and-interpolation.md) — The two coupling operators of the classical immersed boundary method built on the same regularized delta function: spreading maps Lagrangian densities to Eulerian densities, interpolation maps Eulerian values to Lagrangian points, and spreading is the adjoint of interpolation, which makes force, torque, momentum and power identical in the two descriptions.

# Properties

Constraints and invariants a method can hold.

* [Critical balance and the anisotropic MHD cascade](/properties/critical-balance.md) — Critical balance equates the linear Alfvén time and the nonlinear time at every scale of strong MHD turbulence; it fixes the parallel spectrum k_par^-2, the anisotropy l_par ~ lambda^(2/3) of Goldreich–Sridhar, the weak-to-strong transition scale, and frames imbalance and residual energy.
* [Dynamic alignment and intermittency in MHD turbulence](/properties/dynamic-alignment.md) — Dynamic alignment is the scale-dependent alignment of Elsasser increments that weakens the MHD nonlinearity and gives a k_perp^-3/2 spectrum; Schekochihin (2022) recasts it, to respect the RMHD rescaling symmetry, as an intermittency effect with sin(theta) ~ (lambda/lambda_CB)^(1/4).
* [Kinetic-energy preservation and split forms](/properties/kinetic-energy-preservation.md) — A discretization is kinetic-energy preserving when its convective terms neither create nor destroy discrete kinetic energy; this page gives the split-form and two-point-flux constructions, kinetic energy as an entropy with entropy-stable added dissipation, and why a dissipation-free baseline is robust but thermalizes without a subgrid model.
* [Numerical dissipation in scale-resolving simulation](/properties/numerical-dissipation.md) — Numerical dissipation is the kinetic-energy loss a discretization adds by itself; the sources show where in wavenumber and in the element it acts, how it is measured, that it competes with or replaces the subgrid model, that RANS-level artificial dissipation spoils hybrid RANS/LES, and that both too much and too little of it degrade LES.

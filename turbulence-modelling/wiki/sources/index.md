# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Abe (2021) — Detailed investigation of subgrid scale models in large-eddy simulation using high aspect-ratio grid spacing](/sources/abe-2021.md) — Tests five subgrid-scale models a priori and a posteriori in the free shear layer behind a backward-facing step on cells of aspect ratio about 100, and shows that the cube root of the cell volume suppresses the eddy viscosity there: the filter width must exclude a spacing much smaller than the others.
* [Agdestein & Sanderse (2025) — Discretize first, filter next: Learning divergence-consistent closure models for large-eddy simulation](/sources/agdestein-2025.md) — Defines LES closure targets as the discrete commutator error of a filtered staggered-grid DNS, proves that a face-averaging filter keeps filtered velocities divergence-free, and shows that a projected (divergence-consistent) CNN closure is stable where the unprojected form is not, on 2D and 3D forced turbulence.
* [Agrawal & Koutsourelakis (2024) — A probabilistic, data-driven closure model for RANS simulations with aleatoric, model uncertainty](/sources/agrawal-2024.md) — A Reynolds-stress closure made of a tensor-basis neural network plus a latent stochastic discrepancy field with a sparsity prior, trained by variational inference through an adjoint RANS solver on sparse mean-velocity and pressure data, with probabilistic predictions on a backward-facing step.
* [Agrawal et al. (2022) — Non-Boussinesq subgrid-scale model with dynamic tensorial coefficients](/sources/agrawal-2022.md) — Introduces the dynamic tensor-coefficient Smagorinsky model, a traceless non-Boussinesq SGS closure with four coefficients fitted to the Germano identity, and tests it a priori on channel DNS and a posteriori on isotropic turbulence, wall-modelled channel flow and the Gaussian speed bump.
* [Alauzet et al. (2022) — Periodic adjoints and anisotropic mesh adaptation in rotating frame for high-fidelity RANS turbomachinery applications](/sources/alauzet-2022.md) — Builds a metric-based anisotropic mesh-adaptation workflow for steady RANS turbomachinery on unstructured tetrahedral meshes, with periodic discrete adjoints, rotating-frame functionals and shape-preserving periodic remeshing, and shows mesh-converged LS89 and NASA Rotor 37 predictions with Spalart–Allmaras closure.
* [Bae & Koumoutsakos (2022) — Scientific multi-agent reinforcement learning for wall-models of turbulent flows](/sources/bae-2022.md) — Introduces SciMARL: wall grid points act as reinforcement-learning agents sharing one policy that rescales the local modelled wall stress, trained inside channel WMLES; the log-law-based variant predicts friction within 5% up to Re_tau=10^6 and a flat-plate boundary layer without retraining.
* [Basile et al. (2023) — Hybrid RANS/LES Simulations and Aeroacoustic Analysis of Jet Flows using an hp-Adaptive Discontinuous Galerkin Method](/sources/basile-2023.md) — Extends an hp-adaptive modal discontinuous Galerkin strategy to unsteady hybrid RANS/LES with ZDES mode 0+1 on the negative Spalart–Allmaras model, driving refinement by time-averaged error and modal-decay smoothness indicators, and computes the flow and far-field noise of the PPRIME Mach 0.9 round jet with fewer degrees of freedom than reference simulations.
* [Beck & Kurz (2023) — Toward discretization-consistent closure schemes for large eddy simulation using reinforcement learning](/sources/beck-2023.md) — Argues that implicitly filtered LES needs closures optimized together with the discretization, and uses reinforcement learning in FLEXI to set elementwise Smagorinsky coefficients or the DG/finite-volume blending factor as an implicit model, tested on forced isotropic turbulence.
* [Bernades et al. (2023) — Kinetic-energy- and pressure-equilibrium-preserving schemes for real-gas turbulence in the transcritical regime](/sources/bernades-2023.md) — Analyses the discrete pressure equation induced by kinetic-energy-preserving split forms for a general equation of state, shows that pressure equilibrium is then reachable at most to second order through a discrete chain rule, and proposes KGP-Pt, a KEP scheme evolving pressure that is exactly pressure-equilibrium preserving.
* [Blind et al. (2023) — Residual estimation for grid modification in wall-modeled large eddy simulation using unstructured high-order methods](/sources/blind-2023.md) — Transfers the Toosi–Larsson LES modelling-residual estimator and its optimal-spacing equidistribution to the DGSEM code FLEXI, with an element-local modal test filter and element-averaged residuals, and uses it to adapt hexahedral meshes with 2-to-1 mortars for wall-resolved and wall-modelled channels and a transonic airfoil.
* [Bouhafid et al. (2024) — Combined Reynolds-averaged Navier-Stokes/Large-Eddy Simulations for an aircraft wake until dissipation regime](/sources/bouhafid-2024.md) — Simulates an airliner wake from the jet to the dissipation regime by extruding a slice of an adapted-mesh RANS solution into a periodic temporal LES, seeded with synthetic turbulence scaled to the RANS Reynolds stresses, and studies vortex lifespan, stratification and plume dilution.
* [Böttler et al. (2023) — Flamelet modeling of thermo-diffusively unstable hydrogen-air flames](/sources/bottler-2023.md) — Compares a new curvature-extended flamelet/progress-variable manifold with an unstretched-flame manifold against detailed chemistry for a laminar lean hydrogen-air spherical expanding flame with thermo-diffusive instabilities, a priori and in coupled simulations; curvature in the manifold improves the local flame structure, global properties are close for both.
* [Cai et al. (2022) — Application of immersed boundary based turbulence wall modeling to the Ahmed body aerodynamics](/sources/cai-2022.md) — Wall-modelled LES of the 35° Ahmed body with a lattice Boltzmann solver on octree Cartesian grids, combining an immersed boundary, an explicit Lambert-W equilibrium wall model and a constrained-LES hybrid eddy viscosity; compares wall-law and no-slip treatments of the slant on two grids against experiment.
* [Cao et al. (2022) — High-order gas-kinetic scheme with parallel computation for direct numerical simulation of turbulent flows](/sources/cao-2022.md) — Parallelizes the high-order gas-kinetic scheme (BGK-based finite-volume flux, WENO reconstruction, two-stage fourth-order time stepping) with MPI and uses it as a DNS tool for the Taylor–Green vortex, channel flow at Re_tau=180 and supersonic decaying isotropic turbulence, quantifying its numerical dissipation.
* [Carlsson et al. (2023) — Investigation of low-dissipation low-dispersion schemes for incompressible and compressible flows in scale-resolving simulations](/sources/carlsson-2023.md) — Extends the second-order low-dissipation low-dispersion (LD2) scheme of an unstructured node-based finite-volume solver to shocked compressible flow (LD2C: density and sound-speed extrapolation, Ducros-weighted shock sensor) and tests it in decaying isotropic turbulence, IDDES of subsonic and supersonic channels, and DDES of a supersonic base flow.
* [Carreño Ruiz & D'Ambrosio (2022) — Validation of the γ-Reθ Transition Model for Airfoils Operating in the Very Low Reynolds Number Regime](/sources/carrenoruiz-2022.md) — Validates the γ-Re_θ transition model with k-ω SST in STAR-CCM+ on airfoils at chord Reynolds numbers 2×10⁴ to 2×10⁵, comparing two correlation calibrations and two separation-induced corrections against LES, DNS and experiments, and proposing a second parameter for the separation-induced correction.
* [Cato et al. (2023) — Comparison of different data-assimilation approaches to augment RANS turbulence models](/sources/cato-2023.md) — Compares six space-dependent correction fields of the Spalart–Allmaras RANS model (momentum forcing, divergence of a trace-free tensor, production multiplier, additive and proportional source terms, eddy-viscosity multiplier) in adjoint-based assimilation of DNS mean velocity on a converging–diverging channel, periodic hills and a square cylinder.
* [Chen et al. (2024) — An improved Baldwin–Lomax algebraic wall model for high-speed canonical turbulent boundary layers using established scalings](/sources/chen-2024.md) — Recalibrates the semilocal Baldwin–Lomax algebraic RANS model for compressible boundary layers with the Griffin–Fu–Moin inner transformation, a van Driest outer scaling and a two-layer temperature–velocity relation, halving velocity and temperature errors against 12 supersonic and hypersonic DNS.
* [Cheylan et al. (2023) — Analysis of the immersed boundary method for turbulent fluid-structure interaction with Lattice Boltzmann method](/sources/cheylan-2023.md) — Studies the Lagrangian weight of direct-forcing immersed boundaries coupled to the lattice Boltzmann solver ProLB, proposes an explicit weight that needs no linear solve, gives guidelines on grid, time step, kernel and marker spacing, and applies the coupling to fluid–structure cases up to a shear-improved Smagorinsky LES at Re = 10 000.
* [Coelho et al. (2022) — Global fluid simulation of plasma turbulence in a stellarator with an island divertor](/sources/coelho-2022.md) — Extends the GBS two-fluid drift-reduced Braginskii code to non-axisymmetric fields and runs a global, flux-driven simulation of a five-period stellarator with a 5/9 island divertor, finding a coherent field-aligned ballooning-like m=4 mode, no broad-band turbulence and no blobs.
* [Cogo et al. (2022) — Direct numerical simulation of supersonic and hypersonic turbulent boundary layers at moderate-high Reynolds numbers and isothermal wall condition](/sources/cogo-2022.md) — DNS database of four zero-pressure-gradient boundary layers over a cold isothermal wall at Mach 2 and 5.86 up to Re_tau≈2000, run with the GPU code STREAmS, assessing compressible velocity transformations, temperature–velocity relations, Morkovin scaling, TKE budgets, uniform momentum and temperature zones, and spanwise spectra.
* [Constant et al. (2024) — An improved immersed boundary method for turbulent flow simulations on Cartesian grids: extension of a global geometric approach for thin boundary layers and strong flow incidence](/sources/constant-2024.md) — Extends a ghost-cell immersed boundary method with a wall function for steady Spalart–Allmaras RANS on octree Cartesian grids by a locally adapted modelling height and a higher-order wall-pressure reconstruction from a second image-point layer, cutting drag errors on NACA 0012, a multi-element airfoil and the NASA CRM high-lift model.
* [Dahlin et al. (2022) — STITCH: A Subgrid-scale Model for Energy Buildup in the Solar Corona](/sources/dahlin-2022.md) — Implements in a full-MHD adaptive-mesh code the STITCH term, a parametrized boundary source in the induction equation that stands for unresolved helicity condensation, gives a discretely divergence-free face-flux discretization, and reproduces a resolved filament-channel eruption at one fifteenth of the cost.
* [Dang et al. (2022) — Direct numerical simulation of compressible turbulence accelerated by graphics processing unit: An open-source high accuracy accelerated computational fluid dynamic software](/sources/dang-2022.md) — Presents OpenCFD-SCU, an open-source CUDA/HIP finite-difference DNS code for compressible wall turbulence with Steger–Warming splitting and a sensor-switched hybrid upwind/WENO scheme, reports GPU optimizations and scaling to 24,576 GPUs, and validates it on a Mach 2.9, 24° compression ramp.
* [Davidovits et al. (2022) — Turbulence generation by shock interaction with a highly nonuniform medium](/sources/davidovits-2022.md) — Simulates strong shocks crossing media with order-unity density fluctuations in an idealized laser-driven shock tube, and fits the post-shock velocity dispersion and turbulent Mach number with Richtmyer–Meshkov-inspired scalings and Favre-averaged turbulent shock-jump relations that predict reduced post-shock pressure and density.
* [Delassaux et al. (2023) — Numerical analysis of unsteady flow features around a realistic Estate vehicle with hybrid RANS/LES methods](/sources/delassaux-2023.md) — Compares realizable k-ε and k-ω SST RANS with SST-based delayed detached-eddy simulation on a full-scale simplified Estate car against S2A wind-tunnel data: RANS matches the drag by error cancellation, DDES reproduces the single-bubble wake, and a too-fine surface mesh triggers grid-induced separation.
* [Domaradzki (2024) — Autonomous large-eddy simulations of turbulence using eddy viscosity derived from the subgrid-scale similarity stress tensor](/sources/domaradzki-2024.md) — Carries the autonomous spectral LES method into physical space: an eddy viscosity with no adjustable constant is computed each step from the filtered energy transfer of a similarity-type stress, and reproduces the inertial range of forced isotropic turbulence and Comte-Bellot–Corrsin decay.
* [Domingo & Vervisch (2023) — Recent developments in DNS of turbulent combustion](/sources/domingo-2023.md) — Invited review of direct numerical simulation of turbulent flames from 2017 to 2022: resolution limits in Re_T, Da and Ka, DNS embedded in LES as a closure test bed (deconvolution, CNN closures), flame-resolved burner simulations, and the downsizing of real geometries.
* [Duan & Wang (2024) — Calibrating sub-grid scale models for high-order wall-modeled large eddy simulation](/sources/duan-2024.md) — Tests the Smagorinsky, WALE and Vreman models in a high-order flux-reconstruction solver, keeps their coefficients and recalibrates the filter width; finds implicit LES best when well resolved, and recommends Vreman with a new intermediate filter width (element size over the square root of the order) for high-order wall-modelled LES.
* [Duraisamy (2021) — Perspectives on machine learning-augmented Reynolds-averaged and large eddy simulation models of turbulence](/sources/duraisamy-2021.md) — Single-author perspective on machine-learning augmentation of RANS and LES closures: representations of learned corrections, the distinction between a priori and model-consistent training (field inversion, FIML, integrated inference and learning), feature selection, constraints, and the open problems of identifiability, discretization error and generalization.
* [Duraisamy et al. (2019) — Turbulence Modeling in the Age of Data](/sources/duraisamy-2019.md) — Review of how experimental and DNS data are used to quantify and reduce the model-form uncertainty of RANS closures: a four-layer taxonomy of modelling assumptions, realizability-constrained perturbations, Bayesian calibration, field inversion, machine-learned discrepancies and the field-inversion-and-machine-learning paradigm.
* [Dzanic et al. (2022) — Partially-averaged Navier–Stokes simulations of turbulence within a high-order flux reconstruction framework](/sources/dzanic-2022.md) — Couples compressible PANS with a k-ω SST closure to second- and fourth-order flux reconstruction at equal degrees of freedom, and shows on the periodic hill and the Re_D=3900 cylinder that high order helps PANS more than unmodelled implicit LES and lowers its optimal f_k.
* [Eivazi et al. (2022) — Physics-informed neural networks for solving Reynolds-averaged Navier–Stokes equations](/sources/eivazi-2022.md) — Solves the two-dimensional incompressible RANS equations with a physics-informed neural network that outputs mean velocity, pressure and Reynolds stresses, trained on boundary data and equation residuals without a turbulence model; tested on Falkner–Skan, zero- and adverse-pressure-gradient boundary layers, a NACA4412 suction side and the periodic hill.
* [Ferrand et al. (2022) — An In-depth Numerical Study of Exact Laws for Compressible Hall Magnetohydrodynamic Turbulence](/sources/ferrand-2022.md) — Tests two exact laws for the energy cascade rate of isothermal compressible Hall-MHD turbulence term by term on decaying 1024³ pseudo-spectral simulations, finds them numerically equivalent in the inertial range, and shows that their stationary form survives free decay through a time-derivative reservoir term.
* [Ferrer et al. (2023) — HORSES3D: A high-order discontinuous Galerkin solver for flow simulations and multi-physics applications](/sources/ferrer-2023.md) — Reference description of HORSES3D, an open-source Fortran discontinuous Galerkin spectral element framework: split-form entropy-stable discretizations, anisotropic p-adaptation by truncation-error estimation, explicit and implicit time integration, and modules for RANS, explicit and implicit LES, multiphase flow, shock capturing, particles, aeroacoustics and immersed boundaries.
* [Fischer et al. (2022) — NekRS, a GPU-accelerated spectral element Navier–Stokes solver](/sources/fischer-2022.md) — Describes NekRS, the GPU port of the Nek5000 spectral element incompressible-flow solver built on libParanumal kernels, with its time stepping, matrix-free operators, gather–scatter communication and Chebyshev-accelerated p-multigrid pressure preconditioning, and reports scaling to all 27,648 V100 GPUs of Summit for reactor-flow cases.
* [Flad & Gassner (2017) — On the use of kinetic energy preserving DG-schemes for large eddy simulation](/sources/flad-2017.md) — Shows that implicit LES with de-aliased DGSEM fails when the cutoff lies deep in the inertial range, and proposes a dissipation-free kinetic-energy-preserving split-form DG baseline on which explicit Smagorinsky-type closures, with optional small-scale damping, succeed for coarse decaying isotropic turbulence and channel flow.
* [Foldes et al. (2023) — Efficient kinetic Lattice Boltzmann simulation of three-dimensional Hall-MHD turbulence](/sources/foldes-2023.md) — Presents FLAME, a multi-GPU lattice Boltzmann code for weakly compressible three-dimensional Hall-MHD that adds the Hall term through the magnetic equilibrium with a local current solve, verifies second-order convergence on an exact solution, matches the pseudo-spectral code GHOST on the Orszag–Tang vortex, and runs decaying Hall-MHD turbulence.
* [Fowler et al. (2022) — A Lagrangian relaxation towards equilibrium wall model for large eddy simulation](/sources/fowler-2022.md) — Derives LaRTE, a wall-stress model in which the friction-velocity vector obeys a Lagrangian transport equation on the wall relaxing towards equilibrium with the inertial time scale of the wall-modelled layer, and adds a Stokes-layer model for fast pressure-gradient fluctuations, tested in channel and sudden-spanwise-pressure-gradient flows.
* [François et al. (2022) — Zonal Detached Eddy Simulation of the Fan-Outlet Guide Vanes Stage of a Turbofan Engine: Part I—Methodology, Numerical Setup, and Aerodynamic Analysis](/sources/francois-2022.md) — Applies ZDES mode 2 with the Spalart–Allmaras model in elsA to the ACAT1 fan–outlet-guide-vane stage at approach (380 million cells), and validates global performance, mean and RMS wake profiles against hot-wire data and RANS.
* [Gan et al. (2022) — On the Existence of Fast Modes in Compressible Magnetohydrodynamic Turbulence](/sources/gan-2022.md) — Compares spatial polarization projection with a space–time (4D FFT) dispersion-relation filter to measure the Alfvén, fast and slow wave content of driven compressible MHD turbulence; finds most power is non-wave and the fast-mode fraction far smaller than spatial projection suggests.
* [Gillyns et al. (2022) — Implementation and Validation of an Algebraic Wall Model for LES in Nek5000](/sources/gillyns-2022.md) — Implements an algebraic equilibrium wall-stress model, based on Reichardt's law with time-filtered input sampled at the top of the first spectral element, in the spectral element code Nek5000, and validates it in channel flow at Re_τ = 1000 and 5200 for different first-element heights and wall-normal meshes.
* [Goc et al. (2021) — Large eddy simulation of aircraft at affordable cost: a milestone in computational fluid dynamics](/sources/goc-2021.md) — Wall-modelled LES of the JAXA Standard Model high-lift aircraft with two low-dissipation codes (charLES, Alya), in free air and inside the wind tunnel: maximum lift within five lift counts, stall onset and tunnel effects captured, at costs comparable to workshop RANS and turnaround under a day on GPUs.
* [Griffin et al. (2023) — Near-wall model for compressible turbulent boundary layers based on an inverse velocity transformation](/sources/griffin-2023.md) — Proposes a one-ODE compressible wall-stress model that maps the incompressible mixing-length solution through the inverse total-stress-based velocity transformation and closes temperature with a matching-anchored generalized Reynolds analogy, more accurate than the classical model under strong heat transfer in a priori and a posteriori tests.
* [Haering et al. (2022) — Active model split hybrid RANS/LES](/sources/haering-2022.md) — Proposes active model split (AMS) hybrid RANS/LES: the subgrid stress is split into a mean-stress part from a scaled RANS model and an energy-transfer part from an anisotropic tensor viscosity, with active forcing driven by an anisotropic resolution-adequacy parameter; tested on channel flow and the periodic hill.
* [Helm & Martín (2022) — Large eddy simulation of two separated hypersonic shock/turbulent boundary layer interactions](/sources/helm-2022.md) — LES datasets of two separated hypersonic compression-ramp interactions (Mach 7.2, 33°; Mach 9.1, 34°) with a dynamic mixed SGS model and bandwidth-optimized WENO, validated against filtered DNS, assessing Reynolds-analogy and turbulent-Prandtl assumptions, and showing that dropping the scale-similar term lengthens the Mach 7 separation by about 30%.
* [Hoy & Bermejo-Moreno (2022) — Fluid–structural coupling of an impinging shock–turbulent boundary layer interaction at Mach 3 over a flexible panel](/sources/hoy-2022.md) — Couples compressible wall-modelled LES with an equilibrium wall model to a damped nonlinear finite-element panel solver to simulate the Mach 3 impinging shock–boundary-layer interaction over a flexible panel at experimental conditions, and finds that flexibility lengthens separation by about 60% and slows boundary-layer recovery.
* [Huang et al. (2022) — Direct numerical simulation of hypersonic turbulent boundary layers: effect of spatial evolution and Reynolds number](/sources/huang-2022.md) — DNS of spatially developing zero-pressure-gradient boundary layers at Mach 2.5, 4.9 and 10.9 up to Re_tau≈1200 over domains longer than 300 inflow thicknesses, quantifying inflow recovery length, skin-friction and heat-transfer correlations, velocity transformations, Reynolds analogies and the effect of strong wall cooling on turbulent structure.
* [Inagaki & Kobayashi (2023) — Analysis of anisotropic subgrid-scale stress for coarse large-eddy simulation](/sources/inagaki-2023.md) — Splits the filtered-DNS subgrid stress of channel flow at Re_tau 400 into an energy-matching eddy-viscosity part and an energy-transfer-free anisotropic stress, and shows from the resolved Reynolds-stress budgets, physical and spectral, that on coarse grids the anisotropic stress produces spanwise fluctuations no eddy viscosity can supply.
* [Kang et al. (2023) — Neural-network-based mixed subgrid-scale model for turbulent flow](/sources/kang-2023.md) — A small single-point neural network predicts all SGS stress components from the resolved strain rate and the resolved Leonard stress; it reproduces backscatter, runs stably without clipping, generalizes across Reynolds number and grid through invariant normalization, and beats dynamic Smagorinsky and dynamic mixed models in isotropic turbulence and dynamic Smagorinsky in channel flow.
* [Kim et al. (2022) — Deep reinforcement learning for large-eddy simulation modeling in wall-bounded turbulence](/sources/kim-2022.md) — Trains a pointwise neural-network SGS stress model for channel-flow LES by deep reinforcement learning, rewarding only mean viscous and Reynolds shear stress profiles of filtered DNS; the tensor models beat Vreman and dynamic Smagorinsky at Re_tau 180–360, and fail to align with the resolved strain like filtered DNS.
* [Kim et al. (2024) — Large eddy simulation of flow over a circular cylinder with a neural-network-based subgrid-scale model](/sources/kim-2024.md) — Trains pointwise fully connected neural-network SGS models on box-filtered DNS of the cylinder wake at Re 3900 and introduces a late-fusion architecture of grid- and test-filtered inputs that, unlike plain concatenation, generalizes a posteriori to untrained grids and to Re 5000 and 10 000.
* [Knopp (2022) — An Empirical Wall Law for the Mean Velocity in an Adverse Pressure Gradient for RANS Turbulence Modelling](/sources/knopp-2022.md) — Builds an empirical inner-layer wall law for adverse-pressure-gradient turbulent boundary layers from high-Reynolds-number experiments: a log law whose upper edge shrinks with the inner-scaled pressure-gradient parameter, topped by a half-power law, with power-law correlations in Re_τ and the pressure-gradient parameter for the region edges and slope.
* [Kochurin et al. (2022) — Three-dimensional direct numerical simulation of free-surface magnetohydrodynamic wave turbulence](/sources/kochurin-2022.md) — Pseudo-spectral simulations of capillary wave turbulence on a ferrofluid surface under a horizontal magnetic field show a transition from the isotropic Kolmogorov–Zakharov spectrum to an anisotropic regime whose k⊥^-3 elevation spectrum and square-root-of-flux and v_A^(-3/2) prefactor match weak shear-Alfvén wave turbulence.
* [Kou et al. (2023) — Jump penalty stabilization techniques for under-resolved turbulence in discontinuous Galerkin schemes](/sources/kou-2023.md) — Compares a solution-jump and a gradient-jump interface penalty as implicit LES stabilizations of high-order discontinuous Galerkin schemes, through eigensolution analysis, forced Burgers turbulence, the Taylor–Green vortex and a Kelvin–Helmholtz instability, and against Smagorinsky and Vreman models adapted to DG.
* [Krais et al. (2021) — FLEXI: A high order discontinuous Galerkin framework for hyperbolic–parabolic conservation laws](/sources/krais-2021.md) — Describes the open-source FLEXI tool chain (HOPR mesh generator, DGSEM solver with split forms, mortars and finite-volume subcell shock capturing, POSTI post-processing), its implementation, scaling and reproducibility features, and shows implicit LES, shock–vortex and wall-modelled cavity-noise applications.
* [Kurz et al. (2023) — Deep reinforcement learning for turbulence modeling in large eddy simulations](/sources/kurz-2023.md) — Trains with reinforcement learning (PPO, Relexi coupled to FLEXI) an element-local convolutional policy that sets an elementwise Smagorinsky coefficient in implicitly filtered DG LES of forced isotropic turbulence, arguing that supervised targets are ill-posed there; the policy beats implicit LES and static Smagorinsky and matches the dynamic model.
* [Lederer et al. (2023) — High-order projection-based upwind method for implicit large eddy simulation](/sources/lederer-2023.md) — Introduces high-order projection-based upwinding (HOPU), which confines the upwind dissipation of an exactly divergence-free mixed finite-element method to the high-order part of the velocity jump, and compares it with implicit LES and with a projection-based variational multiscale WALE model in channel and periodic-hill flow.
* [Lewis et al. (2023) — Large Eddy Simulation of the ACAT1 fan stage for broadband noise prediction](/sources/lewis-2023.md) — Compares two wall-modelled LES of the ACAT1 fan–outlet-guide-vane stage at approach (95 and 210 million cells, both within published WMLES wall-mesh criteria) with RANS and measurements; the refinement removes a stator separation, improves wakes and spectra, and the wall pressure feeds acoustic analogies for broadband noise.
* [Li & Liu (2022) — Numerical investigation of corner separation flow using Spalart-Allmaras model with various modifications](/sources/li-2022.md) — Steady RANS comparison of Spalart–Allmaras variants (rotation and curvature corrections, helicity correction, strain-based production, quadratic constitutive relations and their blends) on compressor corner separation in the PVD cascade and on NASA Rotor 67, finding the helicity correction most accurate.
* [Liu et al. (2022) — A subgrid turbulent mean field dynamo model for cosmological galaxy formation simulations](/sources/liu-2022.md) — Proposes a subgrid dynamo closure for RAMSES: an isotropic mean-field alpha effect set by the subgrid turbulent velocity of a one-equation SGS energy model and quenched at a tunable fraction of that energy, applied to a cosmological Milky Way-like zoom-in from a 10^-20 G seed.
* [Liu et al. (2024) — Improved prediction of turbomachinery flows using Reynolds stress model with γ transition model](/sources/liu-2024.md) — Couples the SSG/LRR-ω Reynolds-stress model with Menter's one-equation γ transition model in OpenFOAM and shows on the T3 flat plates and two compressor cascades that the coupled model captures laminar separation bubbles and improves corner-separation predictions over SST and the fully turbulent model.
* [Lozano-Durán & Bae (2023) — Machine learning building-block-flow wall model for large-eddy simulation](/sources/lozanoduran-2023.md) — Introduces the building-block-flow wall model: a neural-network classifier and per-regime predictors, trained on solver-consistent wall-modelled LES of seven canonical flows, that return the wall stress with a confidence score; validated a posteriori from laminar boundary layers to the NASA high-lift and Juncture Flow models.
* [Lozano-Durán et al. (2022) — Performance of Wall-Modeled LES with Boundary-Layer-Conforming Grids for External Aerodynamics](/sources/lozanoduran-2022.md) — Wall-modelled LES of the NASA Juncture Flow with charLES on constant-size and boundary-layer-conforming Voronoi grids: a cost model for conforming grids, error-scaling laws by flow region, a split of wall-model errors into external and internal parts, and non-monotonic grid convergence in channel flow.
* [Maeyama et al. (2022) — Multi-scale turbulence simulation suggesting improvement of electron heated plasma confinement](/sources/maeyama-2022.md) — Multi-scale gyrokinetic simulations of a burning-plasma-like tokamak flux tube at electron temperature above ion temperature show that ion-scale trapped-electron-mode and electron-scale ETG turbulence suppress each other, reducing the electron heat flux from 33.63 to 5.66 gyro-Bohm units and upshifting the critical temperature ratio of TEM-dominated transport.
* [Martin-Alvarez et al. (2022) — Towards convergence of turbulent dynamo amplification in cosmological simulations of galaxies](/sources/martinalvarez-2022.md) — Shows with 18 cosmological zoom-in MHD runs in RAMSES that a quasi-Eulerian refinement, which keeps the whole galaxy at the finest cell size, resolves more turbulence and gives faster small-scale dynamo growth scaling as Re^1/2 than the standard quasi-Lagrangian refinement.
* [Mateo-Gabín et al. (2022) — An entropy stable spectral vanishing viscosity for discontinuous Galerkin schemes: Application to shock capturing and LES models](/sources/mateogabin-2022.md) — Constructs a spectral vanishing viscosity for the Gauss–Lobatto DGSEM that is provably entropy stable, by placing a modal Legendre filter between symmetric factors of the artificial-dissipation matrix, and applies it as an SVV-filtered Smagorinsky LES model on the inviscid Taylor–Green vortex and as shock capturing.
* [McConkey et al. (2022) — Deep structured neural networks for turbulence closure modeling](/sources/mcconkey-2022.md) — Proposes a non-negative optimal eddy viscosity and a linear/non-linear split of the Reynolds-stress anisotropy, learned by two neural networks and injected into a v2-f-type RANS model; shows the split improves the conditioning of the injection by an order of magnitude on the periodic hill.
* [Menter et al. (2022) — An Algebraic LCTM Model for Laminar–Turbulent Transition Prediction](/sources/menter-2022.md) — An algebraic local-correlation-based transition model coupled to k-ω SST: the intermittency follows from the ratio of a vorticity Reynolds number to a local onset correlation, with a sublayer limiter, harmonic averaging of k against hysteresis, and a separation-bubble term; validated on flat plates, airfoils and a turbine cascade.
* [Moratilla-Vega et al. (2024) — Installed Jet Noise Analysis Using a Coupled LES/APE High-Order Method](/sources/moratillavega-2024.md) — Couples a second-order σ-model LES of a cold Mach 0.75 round jet, isolated and below a flat plate, one-way and in memory to a fifth-order DG solver of the acoustic perturbation equations; far-field spectra match experiment within 1–2 dB below St 0.7, and the plate changes the jet's sources little but scatters and reflects their noise.
* [Morsbach et al. (2024) — Large Eddy Simulation of a Low-Pressure Turbine Cascade with Turbulent End Wall Boundary Layers](/sources/morsbach-2024.md) — Wall-resolved sixth-order DGSEM implicit LES of the T161 low-pressure turbine cascade with synthetic turbulent end-wall boundary layers and free-stream turbulence, matching experiment, and used as reference for SST and SSG/LRR-ω RANS with γ-Re_θ transition, which miss the midspan separation bubble and overpredict secondary losses.
* [Mozaffari et al. (2022) — Average-based mesh adaptation for hybrid RANS/LES simulation of complex flows](/sources/mozaffari-2022.md) — Proposes adaptive grid refinement for hybrid RANS/LES driven by a time-averaged Hessian criterion, so the mesh converges to a quasi-static topology; calibrated on an IDDES of a backward-facing step and applied with DDES-SST to a ship hull at drift and a delta wing.
* [Mozaffari et al. (2024) — Lattice Boltzmann k-ω SST based hybrid RANS/LES simulations of turbulent flows](/sources/mozaffari-2024.md) — Implements k-ω SST RANS and the SST-based IDDES and continuous-eddy-simulation hybrids in the ProLB lattice Boltzmann solver, tests them on NACA 23012, NACA 0012 up to 45° and the Ahmed body, and finds the equilibrium wall model suppresses slant separation.
* [Pacciani et al. (2025) — A Reformulation of the Laminar Kinetic Energy Model to Enable Multi-mode Transition Predictions](/sources/pacciani-2025.md) — Reformulates the authors' laminar-kinetic-energy transition model with a local production length scale, an intermittency coupling to k-ω SST, a pressure-gradient-dependent onset threshold and separation-aware terms, calibrated on the ERCOFTAC T3 plates and validated on LS89, T106C, T108 and S809 as a base for machine-learnt corrections.
* [Pereira et al. (2021) — Modeling and simulation of transitional Rayleigh–Taylor flow with partially averaged Navier–Stokes equations](/sources/pereira-2021.md) — Applies variable-density PANS with the scale-aware BHR linear eddy-viscosity closure to transitional Rayleigh–Taylor mixing at Atwood number 0.5, compares three strategies for the resolution parameters, and finds that RANS overpredicts mixing while PANS with f_k at most 0.35 reproduces the no-closure reference.
* [Petrocchi & Barakos (2024) — Transonic buffet simulation using a partially-averaged Navier-Stokes approach](/sources/petrocchi-2024.md) — Applies SST-based partially-averaged Navier–Stokes with a dynamic, grid-dependent unresolved-energy ratio to transonic buffet on the OAT15A aerofoil in a half wind tunnel; PANS predicts buffet and stall cells where URANS converges to a steady state, at about 3% more cost per step.
* [Placco et al. (2023) — Large-Eddy Simulation of the unsteady supersonic flow around a Mars entry capsule at different angles of attack](/sources/placco-2023.md) — Implicit LES with the GPU finite-difference code STREAmS (fifth-order WENO, ghost-point immersed boundary) of a Mars entry capsule at Mach 2 and four angles of attack, describing wake topology, turbulent kinetic energy, aerodynamic coefficients, lift spectra and a POD low-order model of the fishtail wake mode.
* [Prakash et al. (2022) — Optimal Clipping of Structural Subgrid Stress Closures for Large-Eddy Simulation](/sources/prakash-2022.md) — Proposes optimal clipping, the Frobenius-nearest backscatter-free projection of a structural subgrid stress, which yields a parameter-free local mixed model; with Clark's gradient model it keeps the a priori stress correlation lost by standard clipping and improves wall-resolved channel LES at Re_tau 590.
* [Prieto Saavedra et al. (2024) — An implicit large-eddy simulation perspective on the flow over periodic hills](/sources/prietosaavedra-2024.md) — Implicit LES of the periodic hill at Re_h = 5600, 10 600 and 37 000 with the SUPG/PSPG-stabilized continuous-Galerkin finite-element code Lethe and no subgrid model, studying how time step, averaging time and mesh affect reattachment and Reynolds stresses.
* [Ruan et al. (2023) — Magnetohydrodynamic Turbulence Formation in Solar Flares: 3D Simulation and Synthetic Observations](/sources/ruan-2023.md) — A 3D resistive MHD solar-flare simulation with adaptive mesh refinement and synthetic EUV spectra attributes flare turbulence to the Kelvin–Helmholtz instability between reconnection downflows and reflected upflows, sheared perpendicular to the guide field; nonthermal velocities exceed 200 km/s near the termination shock, and the Alfvénic turbulence carries about 10% of the downflow kinetic energy.
* [Saini et al. (2023) — Numerical Dissipation Rate Analysis of Finite-Volume and Continuous-Galerkin Methods for LES of Combustor Flow-Field](/sources/saini-2023.md) — Extends the a posteriori numerical-dissipation-rate analysis to explicit (WALE) and spectral-vanishing-viscosity LES and finds that numerical dissipation dominates the non-viscous dissipation of both a second-order finite-volume and a fifth-order continuous-Galerkin LES, the high-order gain coming mainly from lower numerical dissipation.
* [Schekochihin (2022) — MHD turbulence: a biased review](/sources/schekochihin-2022.md) — Pedagogical review of scaling theories of incompressible MHD turbulence in the reduced-MHD limit: weak turbulence and its condensate, critical balance, dynamic alignment as an intermittency effect, tearing-mediated turbulence, imbalance, residual energy, subviscous turbulence, decaying turbulence and its invariants, and the saturated small-scale dynamo.
* [Seta & Federrath (2022) — Turbulent dynamo in the two-phase interstellar medium](/sources/seta-2022.md) — Simulates the small-scale dynamo in driven, explicitly diffusive compressible MHD turbulence with heating and cooling that splits into cold and warm phases, finding equal kinematic growth rates but lower saturation in the cold phase, both below isothermal predictions, and explains them by vorticity budgets.
* [Seyedi & Zayernouri (2022) — A data-driven dynamic nonlocal subgrid-scale model for turbulent flows](/sources/seyedi-2022.md) — Adds a dynamic procedure, the Germano identity written for the subgrid force, to the fractional (nonlocal) subgrid-scale model, fixes the fractional order from filtered DNS data, and tests the model a priori and a posteriori in homogeneous isotropic turbulence, including backscatter.
* [Sirignano & MacArt (2023) — Deep learning closure models for large-eddy simulation of flows around bluff bodies](/sources/sirignano-2023.md) — Trains one gated neural network, which closes all unclosed LES terms near and away from walls, through the continuous adjoint of the LES equations on short windows of filtered DNS of rectangular prisms; the closure runs stably for thousands of time units and matches or beats dynamic Smagorinsky, AMD and no-model LES out of sample.
* [Slaughter et al. (2023) — Large Eddy Simulation of an Inverted Multi-element Wing in Ground Effect](/sources/slaughter-2023.md) — Wall-resolved implicit LES with Nektar++, stabilized by gradient-jump penalisation and spectral vanishing viscosity, of a spanwise-periodic slice of the Imperial Front Wing in ground effect, characterizing its laminar separation bubbles, wake-induced transition, lift spectra and wake, as a first open motorsport benchmark.
* [Steinhausen et al. (2023) — Flame-vortex interaction during turbulent side-wall quenching and its implications for flamelet manifolds](/sources/steinhausen-2023.md) — A fully resolved detailed-chemistry simulation of a methane-air V-flame quenching at the wall of a turbulent channel verifies that near-wall vortices recirculate cold exhaust gas into the fresh mixture, and an extended quenching flamelet manifold with an exhaust-gas-recirculation dimension removes the tabulation errors this causes, tested a priori.
* [Tang et al. (2023) — Discovering explicit Reynolds-averaged turbulence closures for turbulent separated flows through deep learning-based symbolic regression with non-linear corrections](/sources/tang-2023.md) — Learns explicit algebraic corrections to the k-ε Reynolds-stress anisotropy on Pope's two-dimensional integrity basis by deep symbolic regression, an LSTM expression generator trained with a risk-seeking policy gradient; trained on one periodic hill, the closure improves anisotropy, separation and velocity on other hills and a backward-facing step.
* [Vadrot et al. (2023a) — Log-law recovery through reinforcement-learning wall model for large-eddy simulation](/sources/vadrot-2023.md) — Introduces VYBA23, a multi-agent reinforcement-learning wall model whose first state is renormalized so that the neutral line of the state-action map no longer depends on the Reynolds number; trained once at Re_tau = 10^4 without high-fidelity data, it recovers the log law in half-channel LES up to Re_tau = 10^10.
* [Vadrot et al. (2023b) — Survey of machine-learning wall models for large-eddy simulation](/sources/vadrot-2023-b.md) — A posteriori comparison of three published machine-learning wall models (two supervised networks and a multi-agent reinforcement-learning model) against the equilibrium wall model in half-channel LES from Re_tau = 180 to 10^10, explaining their log-layer behaviour through the extrapolation properties of the activation functions and the state-action map.
* [Vela-Martín (2022) — Subgrid-scale models of isotropic turbulence need not produce energy backscatter](/sources/velamartin-2022.md) — Uses the gauge freedom of the subgrid-scale stress (adding a divergence-free tensor) and a projected-gradient optimisation on filtered isotropic-turbulence DNS to remove nearly all local backscatter, arguing that backscatter is conservative spatial flux and that purely dissipative SGS models suffice away from walls.
* [Vicente Cruz & Lamballais (2023) — Physical/numerical duality of explicit/implicit subgrid-scale modelling](/sources/vicentecruz-2023.md) — Spectral comparison of constant-coefficient Smagorinsky LES and implicit LES by spectral vanishing viscosity in sixth-order compact schemes, on the Taylor–Green vortex at Re=40000 and pipe flow at Re_tau=1000–2000, with a priori and a posteriori spectral eddy viscosities.
* [Wang (2024) — On the implicit Large Eddy Simulation of turbomachinery flows using the Flux Reconstruction method](/sources/wang-2024-b.md) — Introduces the flux-reconstruction implicit-LES solver ATHOS with a local modal filter gated by the Persson–Peraire indicator, gives a threshold range for it, and compares it with an entropy filter and HLLC against Roe fluxes on the Taylor–Green vortex, the T106C cascade and the LS89 vane.
* [Wang et al. (2023b) — Multiple-GPU accelerated high-order gas-kinetic scheme for direct numerical simulation of compressible turbulence](/sources/wang-2023-b.md) — Ports a WENO-based high-order gas-kinetic scheme to single and multiple GPUs with CUDA and CUDA-aware MPI, reports speed-ups and strong scaling on the Taylor–Green vortex, a compressible Taylor–Green Mach sweep, and a single- against double-precision comparison in incompressible and compressible channel DNS.
* [Wang et al. (2023c) — Ensemble data assimilation-based mixed subgrid-scale model for large-eddy simulations](/sources/wang-2023-c.md) — Proposes EnKF-MM, a mixed SGS model of the gradient (Clark) model plus a Smagorinsky term whose single coefficient is calibrated a posteriori by an ensemble Kalman filter against filtered-DNS energy spectra; in forced and decaying isotropic turbulence and a temporal mixing layer it beats the dynamic Smagorinsky, dynamic mixed and pure gradient models.
* [Wang et al. (2024) — Comparative study on predicting turbulent kinetic energy budget using high-order upwind scheme and non-dissipative central scheme](/sources/wang-2024.md) — Compares a second-order kinetic-energy-preserving central split form with a sixth-order optimized upwind scheme in LES of channel flow and the periodic hill, finds the upwind scheme better for the turbulent-kinetic-energy budget on coarse grids, and applies it to DDES of the 30P30N high-lift airfoil.
* [Yan et al. (2022) — Data-driven turbulence modeling in separated flows considering physical mechanism analysis](/sources/yan-2022.md) — Applies field inversion and machine learning to the Spalart–Allmaras production term on a stalling S809 airfoil, parameterized periodic hills at Re_b = 5600 and an iced GLC305 airfoil, using priors shaped by nonequilibrium turbulence in separating shear layers, and selecting networks by a posteriori error instead of training loss.
* [Zhao et al. (2023a) — High-order gas-kinetic schemes with non-compact and compact reconstruction for implicit large eddy simulation](/sources/zhao-2023.md) — Extends the high-order gas-kinetic scheme with seventh-order non-compact and fifth-order compact reconstructions and uses them as implicit LES of the Taylor–Green vortex and of channel flow at Re_τ = 180 and 395, showing that higher order or compactness lowers the numerical dissipation of the fifth-order baseline.
* [Zhao et al. (2023b) — Turbulence simulations with an improved interior penalty discontinuous Galerkin method and SST k-ω model](/sources/zhao-2023-b.md) — Couples an interior penalty discontinuous Galerkin discretization of the compressible RANS equations, with a jump-based lifting term that avoids the viscous homogeneity tensor, to a first-order finite-volume k-ω SST model with a dilatational compressibility correction, validated from laminar verification cases to a Mach 2.92 cavity–ramp flow.
* [van Gastelen et al. (2024) — Energy-conserving neural network for turbulence closure modeling](/sources/vangastelen-2024.md) — Builds a discretize-first neural closure for 1D Burgers and Korteweg–de Vries whose skew-symmetric-plus-dissipative structure and auxiliary subgrid-energy variables make it stable for any weights, conserve momentum and allow backscatter, matching a vanilla CNN in accuracy but not in robustness.

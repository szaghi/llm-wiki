# Software

Codes and libraries embodying methods.

* [ANSYS Fluent](/software/ansys-fluent.md) — ANSYS Fluent is the commercial cell-centred finite-volume code in which bundle sources run RANS, SST-DDES, DES, SBES and wall-resolved LES of cars, impellers, pitching airfoils and hypersonic fins, and implement transition models; this page records the versions, schemes and model forms they report.
* [AthenaK](/software/athenak.md) — AthenaK is the Kokkos performance-portable rewrite of the Athena++ block-based adaptive-mesh-refinement framework, with Godunov finite-volume hydrodynamics, MHD, two-fluid, relativistic, radiation and particle modules; this page records its design, numerics, first-order flux correction, turbulence demonstration runs up to 10,240^3 cells, and GPU performance and scaling.
* [CNS3D](/software/cns3d.md) — CNS3D is the block-structured Godunov-type finite-volume code of Kokkinakis and Drikakis for compressible flow, with WENO reconstruction up to eleventh order and SSP Runge–Kutta time stepping; this page records its numerics, its low-Mach velocity corrections including NEHA, and its implicit-LES uses in a low-Mach channel and a shock/boundary-layer interaction.
* [COOLFluiD](/software/coolfluid.md) — COOLFluiD is an open-source C++ component-based multi-physics platform; this page records the implicit high-order flux-reconstruction RANS solver built in it for the log-ω SST and Langtry–Menter transition models, with its semi-analytical Jacobian, source-term linearization, wall treatment, verification and per-degree-of-freedom cost scaling.
* [CaLES](/software/cales.md) — CaLES is an open-source GPU finite-difference solver for large-eddy simulation of incompressible wall-bounded flows, built on the DNS code CaNS; this page records its numerics, closures and wall model, its GPU design and performance on A100 nodes, and its validation, including non-monotonic grid convergence of wall-modelled LES.
* [CharLES](/software/charles.md) — CharLES is Cascade Technologies' compressible, low-dissipation, second-order finite-volume LES code on Voronoi grids; this page records its scheme, meshing, GPU cost and the wall-modelled LES of aircraft (including its HLPW-4 high-lift submission), juncture flows, bumps, compressible channels and a wall-modelled LES grid-convergence study, machine-learning wall models trained in it, and the CharlesX variant of a detonation-engine LES.
* [FLEXI](/software/flexi.md) — FLEXI is an open-source high-order DGSEM framework for compressible scale-resolving simulation, with the HOPR mesh generator and POSTI post-processing; this page records its numerics, turbulence features, parallel performance and the LES studies run with it, including a high-order fluid–structure coupling.
* [GALÆXI](/software/galaexi.md) — GALÆXI is the GPU port of the FLEXI DGSEM framework in CUDA Fortran for compressible flow on curved unstructured hexahedra, with split-form DG and subcell finite-volume shock capturing; this page records its kernel and stream design, memory, scaling to 1024 A100 GPUs, verification and energy-to-solution against FLEXI.
* [GHOST](/software/ghost.md) — GHOST (Geophysical High-Order Suite for Turbulence) is a pseudo-spectral community code with hybrid MPI/OpenMP/CUDA parallelism, second-order Runge–Kutta time stepping and two-thirds de-aliasing; in the sources it runs decaying compressible Hall-MHD DNS and serves as the reference for a lattice Boltzmann MHD code.
* [HORSES3D](/software/horses3d.md) — HORSES3D is an open-source Fortran DGSEM framework for compressible and incompressible flow with RANS, explicit and implicit LES, shock capturing, multiphase, particles, aeroacoustics and immersed boundaries; this page records its numerics, turbulence options including jump-penalty implicit LES and self-tuning explicit modal filtering, adaptation, solvers and parallel performance.
* [LESGO](/software/lesgo.md) — LESGO is an open-source incompressible LES code, pseudo-spectral in the wall-parallel directions and second-order finite-difference on a staggered wall-normal grid; this page records its numerics and closures, the wall-model studies hosted in it (LaRTE, machine-learning and reinforcement-learning wall models), its wind-turbine-wake LES over hills and its role as reference in an actuator-line cross-verification.
* [Nek5000 and NekRS](/software/nek5000.md) — Nek5000 is Argonne's open-source continuous spectral element solver for incompressible flow, and NekRS its GPU successor in C++/OCCA; this page records the shared formulation, NekRS's GPU kernels, preconditioning and scaling to all of Summit, wall-modelled LES with Nek5000, RANS with the k-τ model and its implicit source treatment, and k-τ SST DDES of airfoils in stall.
* [Nektar++](/software/nektar-plus-plus.md) — Nektar++ is an open-source spectral/hp element framework with continuous and discontinuous Galerkin solvers; this page records the incompressible and acoustic solvers, stabilizations (spectral vanishing viscosity, gradient-jump penalisation), the wall-modelled SVV implicit LES, meshing and costs used by the scale-resolving and aeroacoustic studies of the bundle.
* [OpenCFD-SCU](/software/opencfd-scu.md) — OpenCFD-SCU is an open-source CUDA-C/HIP finite-difference code for DNS of compressible wall-bounded turbulence, ported from the CPU code OpenCFD-SC; this page records its numerics, parallel and GPU design, reported performance, its compression-ramp and swept-ramp DNS up to 31 billion points, wall-temperature ramp DNS, and the CPU and reacting relatives OpenCFD-SC and OpenCFD-Comb.
* [OpenFOAM](/software/openfoam.md) — OpenFOAM is the open-source unstructured finite-volume toolbox that bundle sources use as baseline RANS solver for data-driven closures, as second-order LES solver, as host of in-house compressible, reacting and two-scale solvers and of a wall-model library, and as reference for high-order codes; this page records the solvers, schemes and versions they report.
* [PHengLEI](/software/phenglei.md) — PHengLEI is the compressible multi-block structured-grid solver of the China Aerodynamics Research and Development Center; this page records the finite-difference numerics, nonlinear weights, time integration and turbulence treatments (SATES variants, SST-IDDES and AIDDES, Smagorinsky LES) with which four bundle sources run high-lift airfoils, cylinders and a supersonic base flow in it.
* [ProLB](/software/prolb.md) — ProLB is a lattice Boltzmann solver on octree Cartesian grids with a regularized collision, eddy-viscosity closures and immersed boundaries; in the sources it runs wall-modelled LES of the Ahmed body, turbulent fluid–structure interaction, k-ω SST RANS and hybrids, and Spalart–Allmaras-based ZDES mode 2 on a flat plate, a step and a high-lift airfoil.
* [PyFR](/software/pyfr.md) — PyFR is the open-source Python framework for high-order flux reconstruction on mixed unstructured grids, generating kernels for CPU and GPU back ends; this page records its v2.0.3 design, its four stabilization options for under-resolved runs, its synthetic-eddy inflow, its validation and scaling on Taylor–Green vortices, and the studies run with it.
* [RAMSES](/software/ramses.md) — RAMSES is an octree adaptive-mesh-refinement code with a second-order Godunov MHD solver and constrained transport; in the sources it hosts cosmological galaxy-formation runs whose dissipation is purely numerical, a one-equation subgrid turbulence model, a subgrid dynamo, and a comparison of refinement strategies for the turbulent dynamo.
* [Relexi](/software/relexi.md) — Relexi is the open-source reinforcement-learning framework of the FLEXI group that trains turbulence-model policies on HPC systems by launching FLEXI LES as environments and exchanging states and actions through SmartSim; this page records its components, training settings and the hardware and costs reported.
* [STREAmS](/software/streams.md) — STREAmS is an open-source multi-GPU finite-difference solver for compressible wall-bounded flows on Cartesian grids, combining energy-preserving central schemes with WENO shock capturing; this page records its numerics as configured in each study and its use for boundary-layer DNS with wall cooling and supersonic and hypersonic shock impingement, implicit LES of a capsule wake, and thermally perfect channel DNS.
* [TRACE](/software/trace.md) — TRACE is DLR's turbomachinery flow solver, with a second-order finite-volume solver for RANS, URANS and DDES and a high-order DGSEM solver for implicit LES; this page records the configurations, transition and Reynolds-stress closures, inflow generation, flux blending and costs reported by the cascade studies of the bundle.
* [Xcompact3d](/software/xcompact3d.md) — Xcompact3d (successor of Incompact3d) is an open-source Fortran framework for incompressible flow with sixth-order compact finite differences, a spectral Poisson solver and pencil decomposition; this page records its numerics, its implicit spectral vanishing viscosity, the immersed boundaries it hosts, its MHD module and the studies run with it.
* [elsA](/software/elsa.md) — ONERA's compressible cell-centred finite-volume solver for structured multi-block grids, as used in the sources: host of the zonal detached-eddy simulation of a turbofan fan stage, structured Spalart–Allmaras RANS reference for NASA Rotor 37, and a cited URANS and wall-resolved reference for buffet and airfoil studies.
* [hpMusic](/software/hpmusic.md) — hpMusic is the University of Kansas high-order flux-reconstruction (FR/CPR) solver for unstructured meshes; this page records its numerics as the sources configure them, its use for implicit LES with adaptive h-refinement of turbine cascades, for subgrid-model calibration in wall-modelled LES, and its HLPW-4 high-lift submission.

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [ACAT1 fan stage](/benchmarks/acat1-fan-stage.md) — The ACAT1 fan and outlet-guide-vane stage of the TurboNoiseBB project, run at approach (tip relative Mach 0.57) with hot-wire wake data between rotor and stator: a test of zonal DES and of wall-modelled LES on 95 to 380 million cells for the rotor wakes that drive broadband interaction noise.
* [Ahmed body](/benchmarks/ahmed-body.md) — The Ahmed body, a bluff car-like body with a slanted rear whose angle sets the separation regime: at 35° a test of immersed-boundary wall-modelled LES and of SST-based hybrid RANS/LES on lattice Boltzmann grids, at 25° the calibration case of a DDES procedure and a generalization test of symbolic-regression RANS corrections.
* [Backward-facing step](/benchmarks/backward-facing-step.md) — Turbulent flow over a sudden channel expansion with fixed separation at the step edge and downstream reattachment: the reattachment length measures LES filter widths at Re_H about 5700, adaptive IDDES meshes at 28,000 and learned RANS closures at 300 to 5000, and the curved-step variant at 13,700 trains corrected SST models.
* [Circular-cylinder wake](/benchmarks/circular-cylinder-wake.md) — Flow past a circular cylinder, chiefly at Re_D=3900 in the shear-layer transition regime, with separation, shear-layer roll-up and a turbulent wake: the test of neural-network SGS models across grids and Reynolds numbers, of high-order PANS against unmodelled under-resolved simulation, and of immersed-boundary lattice Boltzmann numerics; at Re_D=80 the laminar validation of mean-flow data assimilation on a discontinuous Galerkin discretization.
* [Compressor cascades](/benchmarks/compressor-cascade.md) — Linear compressor cascades as turbomachinery benchmarks: the low-speed V103 and PVD cascades for laminar separation, bypass transition and corner separation, the NACA65 K48 at Mach 0.67, and the transonic TCTA with an oscillating bow shock, run by transitional DDES, RANS transition and Reynolds-stress models and implicit DG LES.
* [ERCOFTAC T3 flat-plate transition cases](/benchmarks/ercoftac-t3-flat-plate.md) — The ERCOFTAC T3 series of flat-plate boundary layers under free-stream turbulence, at zero (T3A, T3B, T3A-) and variable pressure gradient (T3C1–T3C5): the calibration and first validation case of RANS transition models, where onset is predicted well at zero pressure gradient and tends to be delayed in adverse pressure gradient.
* [Flow past a square cylinder](/benchmarks/square-cylinder-flow.md) — Turbulent flow past a square cylinder at Re 22,000 with separation fixed at the front corners and a shedding wake: a test of variational data assimilation into Spalart–Allmaras RANS, where momentum sources fit the DNS mean flow, eddy-viscosity multipliers fail, and good mean flows coexist with wrong Reynolds stresses.
* [Homogeneous isotropic turbulence (decaying and forced)](/benchmarks/homogeneous-isotropic-turbulence.md) — Turbulence in a triply periodic box, decaying or forced: a priori and a posteriori test of subgrid closures, learned closures and neural-operator surrogates, of filter widths and scheme dissipation, DNS up to Taylor-scale Reynolds number 2500 on 32768^3 points, supersonic isothermal and compressible runs, and driven MHD turbulence with and without bulk viscosity.
* [Imperial Front Wing](/benchmarks/imperial-front-wing.md) — The Imperial Front Wing, a three-element inverted race-car wing in ground effect proposed as an open motorsport benchmark; a spanwise-periodic slice at Re 220,000 and ride height 0.36 chords, computed by stabilized spectral/hp implicit LES, shows laminar separation bubbles and wake-induced transition that lower-fidelity models must reproduce.
* [JAXA Standard Model high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/jaxa-standard-model.md) — The JAXA Standard Model, a half-span civil transport in landing configuration from the Third AIAA High-Lift Prediction Workshop at chord Reynolds number 1.96 million and Mach 0.172, on which wall-modelled LES predicts maximum lift within five lift counts on grids of 32 to 185 million cells.
* [NACA 0012 airfoil](/benchmarks/naca-0012-airfoil.md) — The symmetric NACA 0012 airfoil, static at chord Reynolds numbers of 1 to 6 million from attached incidence to 45° and transonic at Mach 0.804, and pitching in deep dynamic stall at 135,000: a validation case of RANS discretizations and near-wall treatments, of data-driven RANS corrections, and of the span of wall-resolved LES.
* [NASA CRM high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/nasa-crm-high-lift.md) — The NASA High-Lift Common Research Model (CRM-HL), a half-span transport in landing configuration at Mach 0.2 and mean-chord Reynolds number 5.49 million: a test of a learned wall model in wall-modelled LES at 7° and 19°, and of immersed-boundary wall-function RANS at 17.05° on a 2.4-billion-cell Cartesian grid.
* [NASA Juncture Flow](/benchmarks/nasa-juncture-flow.md) — The NASA Juncture Flow wing–fuselage model, with smooth-body corner separation at the wing-root trailing edge (crank-chord Reynolds number 2.4 million, Mach 0.189, 5°): wall-modelled LES predicts wall pressure within 5% but mean velocity only within 3–8% on the fuselage and about 100% in the corner, which a learned wall model does not fix.
* [NASA Rotor 37](/benchmarks/nasa-rotor-37.md) — The NASA Rotor 37 transonic axial-compressor rotor: a mesh-converged steady RANS benchmark of Spalart–Allmaras with anisotropic mesh adaptation against structured grids and experiment, and, unwound at mid-span into a linear cascade at ground idle, the application case of a GPU discontinuous Galerkin LES code.
* [NASA wall-mounted hump](/benchmarks/nasa-wall-mounted-hump.md) — The NASA wall-mounted hump at chord Reynolds number 936,000, a separation behind a two-dimensional bump with measured reattachment at 1.15 chords: a training case of conditioned field inversion and symbolic regression, and an unseen test of a learned blend of RANS corrections.
* [Periodic hill](/benchmarks/periodic-hill.md) — The streamwise-periodic channel with a hill constriction, a smooth-wall separation and reattachment test: at hill-height Reynolds number about 10,600 for hybrid RANS/LES, implicit LES and learned RANS corrections, at 5600 for finite-element implicit LES and, as a parametrized family, learned RANS corrections, and at 2800 for data assimilation and DG and gas-kinetic implicit LES.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor mixing](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-mixing.md) — Buoyancy-driven mixing of a heavy fluid placed above a light one: a transient flow that passes from linear laminar growth through bubbles, spikes and Kelvin–Helmholtz transition to turbulent mixing, here at Atwood number 0.5 as a test that RANS fails and partially-averaged Navier–Stokes recovers at a fraction of the resolution.
* [Shock–turbulent boundary layer interaction](/benchmarks/shock-turbulent-boundary-layer-interaction.md) — A turbulent boundary layer meeting a shock from a compression ramp, an impinging oblique shock, a transonic airfoil or a compressor blade: separation length, unsteadiness and wall heating test subgrid models, span and DNS cost from Mach 2.3 to 9.1, RANS data assimilation at Mach 2.28, and transonic buffet of an airfoil and a compressor cascade.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — The triply periodic vortex flow that transitions from a single-mode analytic field to decaying turbulence: at Re 40,000 against a 5400³ DNS to separate Smagorinsky and implicit LES errors, at Re 1600 and 5000 to split numerical dissipation and test subgrid models, supersonic at Mach 1.25 for shock capturing, inviscid for energy conservation, and in 2D for commutator errors.
* [Temporally developing mixing layer](/benchmarks/temporal-mixing-layer.md) — A periodic shear layer between two counter-flowing streams that transitions and then grows linearly in momentum thickness: the inhomogeneous a posteriori test of subgrid models calibrated by ensemble Kalman filtering and of neural-operator surrogates of LES, against filtered DNS on grids of 64×64×32 to 64×128×128.
* [Turbine cascades (T106, T161, VKI LS89)](/benchmarks/turbine-cascade.md) — Linear turbine cascades as transition and loss benchmarks: the high-lift low-pressure T106C, T108 and T161 cascades with laminar separation at exit Reynolds numbers near 10⁵, and the transonic VKI LS89 vane with bypass transition and heat transfer, run by implicit DG and flux-reconstruction LES and by RANS transition models.
* [Turbulent boundary layers: zero and adverse pressure gradient, compressible](/benchmarks/turbulent-boundary-layer.md) — Spatially developing turbulent boundary layers on flat walls: zero- and adverse-pressure-gradient layers as wall-model, log-law and physics-informed-network tests and as the non-regression test of data-driven RANS corrections, supersonic and hypersonic layers on cooled walls as DNS databases for compressibility transformations, LES validation and the calibration of algebraic RANS, and the incoming layer of shock interactions.
* [Turbulent channel flow](/benchmarks/turbulent-channel-flow.md) — Pressure-driven flow between two parallel walls, periodic in the wall-parallel directions: DNS at Re_τ 100 to 395, wall-resolved, implicit and very coarse LES at 180 to 2000 (learned and super-resolution subgrid models, schemes, grid adaptation), wall-modelled LES at Re_τ 544 to 10¹⁰, hybrid RANS/LES to 20,000, RANS training data, and compressible channels.

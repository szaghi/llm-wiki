# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [ACAT1 fan stage](/benchmarks/acat1-fan-stage.md) — The ACAT1 fan and outlet-guide-vane stage of the TurboNoiseBB project, run at approach (tip relative Mach 0.57) with hot-wire wake data between rotor and stator: a test of zonal DES and of wall-modelled LES on 95 to 380 million cells for the rotor wakes that drive broadband interaction noise.
* [Ahmed body](/benchmarks/ahmed-body.md) — The Ahmed body, a bluff car-like body with a slanted rear whose angle sets the separation regime: at 35° a test of immersed-boundary wall-modelled LES and of SST-based hybrid RANS/LES on lattice Boltzmann grids, at 25° the calibration case of a DDES procedure and a generalization test of symbolic-regression RANS corrections.
* [Backward-facing step](/benchmarks/backward-facing-step.md) — Turbulent flow over a sudden expansion with fixed separation at the step edge and downstream reattachment: it measures LES filter widths, adaptive IDDES meshes, learned RANS closures, high-order RANS wall functions and the k-τ model, and ZDES shielding on a lattice Boltzmann solver, at step Reynolds numbers 300 to 40,000; the curved variant trains corrected SST models.
* [Caradonna–Tung rotor](/benchmarks/caradonna-tung-rotor.md) — The two-bladed untwisted NACA 0012 model rotor of Caradonna and Tung in hover, collective 8°, tip Mach 0.439 and 0.877: a test of how long a scheme preserves the tip-vortex wake, run with immersed-boundary DDES on moving Cartesian grids and with WENO-type URANS on overset grids.
* [Circular-cylinder wake](/benchmarks/circular-cylinder-wake.md) — Flow past circular cylinders, chiefly at Re_D=3900, with separation, shear-layer roll-up and a turbulent wake: it tests neural-network SGS models, PANS, implicit LES with compact schemes, VMS LES with immersed boundaries and 2D URANS time integration; tandem cylinders test hybrid RANS/LES, and laminar cylinders validate solver numerics.
* [Compressor cascades](/benchmarks/compressor-cascade.md) — Linear compressor cascades as turbomachinery benchmarks: the low-speed V103 and PVD cascades for laminar separation, bypass transition and corner separation, the NACA65 K48 at Mach 0.67, and the transonic TCTA with an oscillating bow shock, run by transitional DDES, RANS transition and Reynolds-stress models and implicit DG LES.
* [ERCOFTAC T3 flat-plate transition cases](/benchmarks/ercoftac-t3-flat-plate.md) — The ERCOFTAC T3 series of flat-plate boundary layers under free-stream turbulence, at zero (T3A, T3B, T3A-) and variable pressure gradient (T3C1–T3C5): the calibration and validation case of RANS transition models, also in high-order discretizations, where onset is predicted well at zero pressure gradient and tends to be delayed in adverse pressure gradient without a correction.
* [Eppler 387 airfoil](/benchmarks/eppler-387-airfoil.md) — Low-Reynolds-number airfoil with a laminar separation bubble on the suction side, measured by McGhee et al. at Re_c from 60,000 to 300,000: it tests the bubble terms of RANS transition models and the transition and turbulent boundary layer that an implicit LES resolves on high-order meshes.
* [Flow past a square cylinder](/benchmarks/square-cylinder-flow.md) — Turbulent flow past a square cylinder with separation fixed at the front corners and a shedding wake: at Re 22,000 a test of variational data assimilation into Spalart–Allmaras RANS, where momentum sources fit the DNS mean flow and eddy-viscosity multipliers fail; at Re 10,000 a 2D URANS test of the k-√k-L model.
* [Homogeneous isotropic turbulence (decaying and forced)](/benchmarks/homogeneous-isotropic-turbulence.md) — Turbulence in a triply periodic box, decaying or forced: a priori and a posteriori test of subgrid closures, learned closures and neural-operator surrogates, of filter widths and scheme dissipation, DNS up to Taylor-scale Reynolds number 2500 on 32768^3 points, supersonic isothermal and compressible runs, and driven MHD turbulence with and without bulk viscosity.
* [Imperial Front Wing](/benchmarks/imperial-front-wing.md) — The Imperial Front Wing, a three-element inverted race-car wing in ground effect proposed as an open motorsport benchmark; a spanwise-periodic slice at Re 220,000 and ride height 0.36 chords, computed by stabilized spectral/hp implicit LES, shows laminar separation bubbles and wake-induced transition that lower-fidelity models must reproduce.
* [JAXA Standard Model high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/jaxa-standard-model.md) — The JAXA Standard Model, a half-span civil transport in landing configuration from the Third AIAA High-Lift Prediction Workshop at chord Reynolds number 1.96 million and Mach 0.172, on which wall-modelled LES predicts maximum lift within five lift counts on grids of 32 to 185 million cells.
* [NACA 0012 airfoil](/benchmarks/naca-0012-airfoil.md) — The symmetric NACA 0012 airfoil: static RANS at chord Reynolds numbers of 1 to 6 million up to 45° and transonic, high-order workshop cases, low-Reynolds-number unsteady separation, pitching in dynamic stall, and a rod–airfoil noise case: it validates high-order RANS, near-wall and immersed-boundary treatments, data-driven corrections, output-based adaptation, and LES and DDES of dynamic stall.
* [NACA 4412 airfoil](/benchmarks/naca-4412-airfoil.md) — The cambered NACA 4412 airfoil near maximum lift, chord Reynolds number about 1.6 million at 12°–14° with trailing-edge separation: a test of wall models in WMLES and of RANS, DES and RSM-based hybrids whose lift and drag errors stay large, with a debated experimental basis; at 200,000 a PINN boundary-layer case.
* [NASA CRM high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/nasa-crm-high-lift.md) — The NASA High-Lift Common Research Model (CRM-HL), a half-span transport in landing configuration at Mach 0.2: at mean-chord Reynolds number 5.49 million it tests a learned wall model in WMLES and immersed-boundary wall-function RANS, and its wing-body form at root-chord Reynolds number 5.6 million tests anisotropic mesh adaptation for DG RANS.
* [NASA Juncture Flow](/benchmarks/nasa-juncture-flow.md) — The NASA Juncture Flow wing–fuselage model, with smooth-body corner separation at the wing-root trailing edge (crank-chord Reynolds number 2.4 million, Mach 0.189, 5°): wall-modelled LES predicts wall pressure within 5% but mean velocity only within 3–8% on the fuselage and about 100% in the corner, which a learned wall model does not fix.
* [NASA Rotor 37](/benchmarks/nasa-rotor-37.md) — The NASA Rotor 37 transonic axial-compressor rotor: a mesh-converged steady RANS benchmark of Spalart–Allmaras with anisotropic mesh adaptation against structured grids and experiment, and, unwound at mid-span into a linear cascade at ground idle, the application case of a GPU discontinuous Galerkin LES code.
* [NASA wall-mounted hump](/benchmarks/nasa-wall-mounted-hump.md) — The NASA wall-mounted hump at chord Reynolds number 936,000 and Mach 0.1, a separation behind a two-dimensional bump whose measured reattachment the sources print at 1.10 or 1.15 chords: a training case of field inversion and symbolic regression, an unseen test of blended RANS corrections, and a test of hybrid RANS/LES and nonequilibrium wall models.
* [Periodic hill](/benchmarks/periodic-hill.md) — The streamwise-periodic channel with a hill constriction, a smooth-wall separation and reattachment test: at hill-height Reynolds number about 10,600 for hybrid RANS/LES, wall models, implicit LES and learned corrections; from 5600 to 37,000 for implicit LES and compiled hybrid results; as a parametrized family at 5600; and at 2800 for data assimilation and implicit LES.
* [RAE 2822 airfoil](/benchmarks/rae-2822-airfoil.md) — The supercritical RAE 2822 airfoil in transonic flow, case 9 of Cook et al. at corrected Mach 0.734, incidence 2.79° and Reynolds number 6.5 million: a steady RANS test of high-order DG discretizations, shock treatment and mesh adaptation, where uniform refinement gained nothing and adaptation decided accuracy.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor mixing](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-mixing.md) — Buoyancy-driven mixing of a heavy fluid placed above a light one: a transient flow that passes from linear laminar growth through bubbles, spikes and Kelvin–Helmholtz transition to turbulent mixing, here at Atwood number 0.5 as a test that RANS fails and partially-averaged Navier–Stokes recovers at a fraction of the resolution.
* [Shock–turbulent boundary layer interaction](/benchmarks/shock-turbulent-boundary-layer-interaction.md) — A turbulent boundary layer meeting a shock from a compression ramp, an expansion–compression corner, an impinging oblique shock, a shock train, a transonic bump, airfoil or blade: separation and interaction length, unsteadiness, energy transfer and wall heating test subgrid and wall models, immersed boundaries, implicit LES, DNS from Mach 1.5 to 9.1, data-driven corrections and buffet.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — The triply periodic vortex flow that transitions from a single-mode analytic field to decaying turbulence: at Re 40,000 to separate Smagorinsky and implicit LES errors; at Re 100 to 10,000 to split numerical dissipation and test subgrid models, filters and split forms; supersonic for shock capturing; inviscid for energy conservation; laminar and 2D for filter and commutator analysis.
* [Temporally developing mixing layer](/benchmarks/temporal-mixing-layer.md) — A periodic shear layer between two counter-flowing streams that transitions and then grows linearly in momentum thickness: the inhomogeneous a posteriori test of subgrid models calibrated by ensemble Kalman filtering and of neural-operator surrogates of LES, against filtered DNS on grids of 64×64×32 to 64×128×128.
* [Turbine cascades (T106, T161, VKI LS89)](/benchmarks/turbine-cascade.md) — Linear turbine cascades as transition, loss and vortex benchmarks: the high-lift low-pressure T106A, T106C, T108 and T161 cascades with laminar separation near exit Reynolds number 10⁵, the transonic VKI LS89 vane with bypass transition and heat transfer, and a transonic rotor cascade with tip clearance whose tip-leakage vortex a hybrid RANS/LES resolves.
* [Turbulent boundary layers: zero and adverse pressure gradient, compressible](/benchmarks/turbulent-boundary-layer.md) — Spatially developing turbulent boundary layers on flat walls: zero- and adverse-pressure-gradient layers test wall models, RANS wall functions, high-order RANS discretizations, hybrid RANS/LES shielding, the log law and data-driven corrections; supersonic and hypersonic layers are DNS databases for compressibility transformations, wall cooling and pressure fluctuations, test subgrid and wall models, and feed shock interactions.
* [Turbulent channel flow](/benchmarks/turbulent-channel-flow.md) — Pressure-driven flow between two parallel walls, periodic in the wall-parallel directions: DNS at Re_τ 100 to 800 (including rough walls and thermally perfect gases), wall-resolved, implicit and very coarse LES at 180 to 2000, wall-modelled and immersed-boundary LES and a priori wall-model tests at Re_τ 544 to 10¹⁰, hybrid RANS/LES to 20,000, high-order RANS, and compressible channels.

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [30P30N three-element high-lift airfoil](/benchmarks/30p30n-high-lift-airfoil.md) — The 30P30N slat–main–flap airfoil in three forms: the modified BANC-III geometry at Mach 0.17, chord Reynolds number 1.7 million and 5.5°, where hybrid and LES approaches and scheme dissipation are compared; the workshop RANS case at Mach 0.2, 9 million and 16°; and hybrid runs at Mach 0.2, 9 million over 8–24°, with four hybrids compared at 21°.
* [ACAT1 fan stage](/benchmarks/acat1-fan-stage.md) — The ACAT1 fan and outlet-guide-vane stage of the TurboNoiseBB project, run at approach (tip relative Mach 0.57) with hot-wire wake data between rotor and stator: a test of zonal DES and of wall-modelled LES on 95 to 380 million cells for the rotor wakes that drive broadband interaction noise.
* [Ahmed body](/benchmarks/ahmed-body.md) — The Ahmed body, a bluff car-like body with a slanted rear whose angle sets the separation regime: at 35° a test of immersed-boundary wall-modelled LES and of SST-based hybrid RANS/LES on lattice Boltzmann grids, at 25° the calibration case of a DDES procedure and a generalization test of symbolic-regression RANS corrections.
* [Backward-facing step](/benchmarks/backward-facing-step.md) — Turbulent flow over a sudden expansion with fixed separation at the step edge and downstream reattachment: it measures LES filter widths, adaptive IDDES meshes, learned RANS closures, high-order RANS wall functions and the k-τ model, and ZDES shielding on a lattice Boltzmann solver, at step Reynolds numbers 300 to 40,000; the curved variant trains corrected SST models.
* [Caradonna–Tung rotor](/benchmarks/caradonna-tung-rotor.md) — The two-bladed untwisted NACA 0012 model rotor of Caradonna and Tung in hover, collective 8°, tip Mach 0.439 and 0.877: a test of how long a scheme preserves the tip-vortex wake, run with immersed-boundary DDES on moving Cartesian grids and with WENO-type URANS on overset grids.
* [Circular-cylinder wake](/benchmarks/circular-cylinder-wake.md) — Flow past circular cylinders, chiefly at Re_D=3900, with separation, shear-layer roll-up and a turbulent wake: it tests neural-network SGS models, PANS, implicit LES, VMS LES and 2D URANS; the drag crisis, the supercritical regime and tandem cylinders test hybrid RANS/LES and immersed-boundary wall models, a Mach-3 cylinder tests wall models, and laminar cylinders validate solver numerics.
* [Compressor cascades](/benchmarks/compressor-cascade.md) — Linear compressor cascades as turbomachinery benchmarks: the low-speed V103 and PVD cascades for laminar separation, bypass transition and corner separation, the NACA65 K48 at Mach 0.67 with and without tip clearance, and the transonic TCTA with an oscillating bow shock, run by transitional DDES, RANS transition and Reynolds-stress models and implicit DG LES.
* [Delta-wing and concentrated vortex flows](/benchmarks/delta-wing-vortex-flow.md) — Separation-induced vortices of swept wings and aircraft (sharp and blunt delta wings, the AVT-183 diamond wing, vortex breakdown on the AVT-080 wing, SACCON, CAWAPI F-16XL, X-31, AVT-316): RANS predicts coherent sharp-edge vortices, while secondary vortices, blunt-edge onset, breakdown and hysteresis remain unanchored and favour hybrid RANS/LES with adaptive meshes.
* [ERCOFTAC T3 flat-plate transition cases](/benchmarks/ercoftac-t3-flat-plate.md) — The ERCOFTAC T3 series of flat-plate boundary layers under free-stream turbulence, at zero (T3A, T3B, T3A-) and variable pressure gradient (T3C1–T3C5): the calibration and validation case of RANS transition models, also in high-order discretizations, where onset is predicted well at zero pressure gradient and tends to be delayed in adverse pressure gradient without a correction.
* [Eppler 387 airfoil](/benchmarks/eppler-387-airfoil.md) — Low-Reynolds-number airfoil with a laminar separation bubble on the suction side, measured by McGhee et al. at Re_c from 60,000 to 300,000: it tests the bubble terms of RANS transition models, the transition and turbulent boundary layer that an implicit LES resolves on high-order meshes, and transitional hybrid RANS/LES up to stall.
* [Flow past a square cylinder](/benchmarks/square-cylinder-flow.md) — Turbulent flow past a square cylinder with separation fixed at the front corners: at Re 21,400–22,000 a test of data assimilation into Spalart–Allmaras RANS (momentum sources fit the DNS mean flow, eddy-viscosity multipliers fail), of a DNS-database subgrid closure, SST-DDES variants and a wall-modelled immersed boundary; at Re 10,000 a 2D URANS test of the k-√k-L model.
* [Gaussian (Boeing) bump](/benchmarks/gaussian-bump.md) — The Boeing Gaussian speed bump, a smooth-body separation behind a Gaussian hill at bump-width Reynolds number 1 or 2 million (spanwise-periodic) or 3.41 million (3D): wall-modelled LES on it depends more on the subgrid model, the mesh and cell anisotropy than on the wall condition, its separation converges non-monotonically, and it calibrates Spalart–Allmaras constants.
* [Hammerhead launch-vehicle buffet](/benchmarks/hammerhead-launch-vehicle-buffet.md) — Transonic buffet behind the payload fairing of the NASA Model 11 hammerhead launch vehicle at Mach 0.81 and zero incidence, a massively separated axisymmetric shear-layer flow on which Reynolds-stress-based IDDES variants are compared with SST-IDDES through the pressure-fluctuation peak, shear-layer breakdown and resolved spectra.
* [Homogeneous isotropic turbulence (decaying and forced)](/benchmarks/homogeneous-isotropic-turbulence.md) — Turbulence in a triply periodic box, decaying or forced: a priori and a posteriori test of subgrid closures, learned closures and neural-operator surrogates, of filter widths and scheme dissipation, DNS up to Taylor-scale Reynolds number 2500 on 32768^3 points, supersonic isothermal and compressible runs, and driven MHD turbulence with and without bulk viscosity.
* [Imperial Front Wing](/benchmarks/imperial-front-wing.md) — The Imperial Front Wing, a three-element inverted race-car wing in ground effect proposed as an open motorsport benchmark; a spanwise-periodic slice at Re 220,000 and ride height 0.36 chords, computed by stabilized spectral/hp implicit LES, shows laminar separation bubbles and wake-induced transition that lower-fidelity models must reproduce.
* [JAXA Standard Model high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/jaxa-standard-model.md) — The JAXA Standard Model, a half-span civil transport in landing configuration from the Third AIAA High-Lift Prediction Workshop at chord Reynolds number 1.96 million and Mach 0.172, on which wall-modelled LES predicts maximum lift within five lift counts on grids of 32 to 185 million cells.
* [NACA 0012 airfoil](/benchmarks/naca-0012-airfoil.md) — The symmetric NACA 0012 airfoil: static RANS and DDES at chord Reynolds numbers of 1 to 6 million up to 90° and transonic, high-order workshop cases, low-Reynolds-number unsteady separation, pitching in dynamic stall, and a rod–airfoil noise case: it validates high-order RANS, near-wall and immersed-boundary treatments, data-driven corrections, output-based adaptation, and LES and DDES of dynamic stall.
* [NACA 4412 airfoil](/benchmarks/naca-4412-airfoil.md) — The cambered NACA 4412 airfoil near maximum lift, chord Reynolds number about 1.6 million at 12°–14° with trailing-edge separation: a test of wall models in WMLES and of RANS, DES and RSM-based hybrids whose lift and drag errors stay large, with a debated experimental basis; at 200,000 a PINN boundary-layer case, at 400,000 and 5° a surveyed DNS.
* [NASA CRM high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/nasa-crm-high-lift.md) — The NASA High-Lift Common Research Model (CRM-HL), a half-span transport in landing configuration at Mach 0.2: at mean-chord Reynolds number about 5.5 million nine HLPW-4 codes test wall-modelled LES of maximum lift and stall, IDDES resolves its vortex system, a learned wall model and immersed-boundary RANS are tested, and its wing-body form tests DG mesh adaptation.
* [NASA Juncture Flow](/benchmarks/nasa-juncture-flow.md) — The NASA Juncture Flow wing–fuselage model, with smooth-body corner separation at the wing-root trailing edge (crank-chord Reynolds number 2.4 million, Mach 0.189, 5°): wall-modelled LES predicts wall pressure within 5% but mean velocity only within 3–8% on the fuselage and about 100% in the corner, which a learned wall model does not fix.
* [NASA Rotor 37](/benchmarks/nasa-rotor-37.md) — The NASA Rotor 37 transonic axial-compressor rotor: a mesh-converged steady RANS benchmark of Spalart–Allmaras with anisotropic mesh adaptation against structured grids and experiment, and, unwound at mid-span into a linear cascade at ground idle, the application case of a GPU discontinuous Galerkin LES code.
* [NASA wall-mounted hump](/benchmarks/nasa-wall-mounted-hump.md) — The NASA wall-mounted hump at chord Reynolds number 936,000 and Mach 0.1, a separation behind a two-dimensional bump whose measured reattachment the sources print at 1.10 or 1.15 chords: a training case of field inversion and symbolic regression, an unseen test of blended RANS corrections and of recalibrated Spalart–Allmaras constants, and a test of hybrid RANS/LES and nonequilibrium wall models.
* [Periodic hill](/benchmarks/periodic-hill.md) — The streamwise-periodic channel with a hill constriction, a smooth-wall separation and reattachment test: at hill-height Reynolds number about 10,600 for hybrid RANS/LES, wall models, implicit LES and learned corrections; from 5600 to 37,000 for implicit LES and compiled hybrid results; as a parametrized family at 5600; and at 2800 for data assimilation and implicit LES.
* [RAE 2822 airfoil](/benchmarks/rae-2822-airfoil.md) — The supercritical RAE 2822 airfoil in transonic flow, case 9 of Cook et al. at corrected Mach 0.734, incidence 2.79° and Reynolds number 6.5 million: a steady RANS test of high-order DG discretizations, shock treatment and mesh adaptation, where uniform refinement gained nothing and adaptation decided accuracy.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor mixing](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-mixing.md) — Buoyancy-driven mixing of a heavy fluid placed above a light one: a transient flow that passes from linear laminar growth through bubbles, spikes and Kelvin–Helmholtz transition to turbulent mixing, here at Atwood number 0.5 as a test that RANS fails and partially-averaged Navier–Stokes recovers at a fraction of the resolution.
* [SD7003 airfoil](/benchmarks/sd7003-airfoil.md) — The Selig–Donovan SD7003 airfoil at chord Reynolds numbers 23,000 to 200,000, whose laminar separation bubble and separation-induced transition test implicit LES (the High-Order CFD Workshop case at 60,000 and 4° or 8°) and transitional RANS calibrations, against implicit LES, DNS, PIV and wind-tunnel references.
* [Shock–turbulent boundary layer interaction](/benchmarks/shock-turbulent-boundary-layer-interaction.md) — A turbulent boundary layer meeting a shock from a compression or swept ramp, an impinging oblique shock, a shock train, a bump, airfoil, blade or duct corner: separation, interaction length, unsteadiness, amplification, recovery, wall heating, wall temperature and panel flexibility test subgrid, wall and RANS models, implicit LES, DNS from Mach 1.5 to 9.1 and buffet.
* [Supersonic axisymmetric base flow](/benchmarks/supersonic-axisymmetric-base-flow.md) — The Mach 2.46 flow behind the blunt base of a cylindrical afterbody measured by Herrin & Dutton, a test of how fast a hybrid RANS/LES model and its scheme let the separated shear layer become turbulent; set-ups, reference data and the recirculation, reattachment, shear-stress and base-pressure results of SATES, IDDES, AIDDES and DDES runs.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — Triply periodic vortex flow that transitions from a single-mode field to decaying turbulence: at Re 40,000 it separates Smagorinsky and implicit LES errors; at Re 100–10,000 it splits numerical dissipation and tests subgrid models, filters and split forms; compressible to Mach 2.5 for shock capturing; with an imposed field for MHD; inviscid for energy conservation; laminar and 2D for commutators.
* [Temporally developing mixing layer](/benchmarks/temporal-mixing-layer.md) — A periodic shear layer between two counter-flowing streams that transitions and then grows linearly in momentum thickness: the inhomogeneous a posteriori test of gradient-type and ensemble-Kalman-calibrated subgrid models and of neural-operator surrogates of LES, against filtered DNS on grids of 64×64×32 to 128×128×64.
* [Turbine cascades (T106, T161, VKI LS89)](/benchmarks/turbine-cascade.md) — Linear turbine cascades as transition, loss, vortex and mesh-adaptation benchmarks: the high-lift low-pressure T106A, T106C, T108 and T161 cascades with laminar separation near exit Reynolds number 10⁵, the transonic VKI LS89 vane with bypass transition and heat transfer, and a transonic rotor cascade whose tip-leakage vortex a hybrid RANS/LES resolves.
* [Turbulent boundary layers: zero and adverse pressure gradient, compressible](/benchmarks/turbulent-boundary-layer.md) — Spatially developing turbulent boundary layers on flat walls: zero- and adverse-pressure-gradient layers test wall models, RANS wall functions, high-order RANS discretizations, hybrid RANS/LES shielding, the log law and data-driven corrections; supersonic and hypersonic layers are DNS databases for compressibility transformations, wall cooling and pressure fluctuations, test subgrid and wall models, and feed shock interactions.
* [Turbulent channel flow](/benchmarks/turbulent-channel-flow.md) — Pressure-driven flow between two parallel walls, periodic in the wall-parallel directions: DNS at Re_τ 100 to 800 (including rough walls, thermally perfect gases and MHD), wall-resolved, implicit and very coarse LES at 180 to 2000, wall-modelled and immersed-boundary LES and a priori wall-model tests at Re_τ 544 to 10¹⁰, hybrid RANS/LES to 20,000, high-order RANS, and compressible channels.
* [Turbulent round jets](/benchmarks/turbulent-round-jet.md) — Free round jets issuing into quiescent or co-flowing surroundings: their potential core, transition, spreading and centreline decay test compressible LES and its Reynolds-stress budgets from Mach 0.45 to 1.8 at Re 500,000, DNS and LES mesh-adaptation criteria at Re 4000, hp-adaptive hybrid RANS/LES, jet-noise chains and real-fluid DNS.

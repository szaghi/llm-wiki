# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Homogeneous isotropic turbulence (decaying and forced)](/benchmarks/homogeneous-isotropic-turbulence.md) — Turbulence in a triply periodic box, decaying from a prescribed spectrum or sustained by forcing, used as a priori database and a posteriori test of subgrid-scale closures: here Re_λ from 70 to 324, LES of 24³ to 144³ degrees of freedom against filtered DNS spectra.
* [JAXA Standard Model high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/jaxa-standard-model.md) — The JAXA Standard Model, a half-span civil transport in landing configuration from the Third AIAA High-Lift Prediction Workshop at chord Reynolds number 1.96 million and Mach 0.172, on which wall-modelled LES predicts maximum lift within five lift counts on grids of 32 to 185 million cells.
* [NASA Juncture Flow](/benchmarks/nasa-juncture-flow.md) — The NASA Juncture Flow wing–fuselage model, with smooth-body corner separation at the wing-root trailing edge at crank-chord Reynolds number 2.4 million, Mach 0.189 and 5° incidence, on which wall-modelled LES predicts wall pressure within 5% but mean velocity only within 3–8% on the fuselage and about 100% in the separated corner.
* [Periodic hill](/benchmarks/periodic-hill.md) — The streamwise-periodic channel with a hill constriction at hill-height Reynolds number about 10,600, a smooth-wall separation and reattachment test for hybrid RANS/LES, implicit LES and learned RANS corrections, here run on grids 14 to 60 times smaller than wall-resolved LES.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — The triply periodic vortex flow started from a single-mode analytic field that transitions to turbulence and decays, used at Re 40,000 against a 5400³ DNS to separate explicit Smagorinsky and implicit LES errors, and in its 2D analytic form to compare continuous and discrete commutator errors.
* [Turbulent channel flow](/benchmarks/turbulent-channel-flow.md) — Pressure-driven flow between two parallel walls, periodic in the wall-parallel directions, the canonical wall-bounded test: here a priori DNS data at Re_τ 395 to 2000, wall-modelled LES at Re_τ 1000 to 10¹⁰, hybrid RANS/LES at 5200 and 20,000, and compressible channels to bulk Mach 4.

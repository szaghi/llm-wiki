# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Backward-facing step](/benchmarks/backward-facing-step.md) — Turbulent flow over a sudden expansion of a channel, with fixed separation at the step edge, a free shear layer and reattachment downstream; here the reattachment length as a measure of filter-width choices in wall-resolved LES at Re_H about 5700 and of adaptive meshing for IDDES at Re_H 28,000.
* [Homogeneous isotropic turbulence (decaying and forced)](/benchmarks/homogeneous-isotropic-turbulence.md) — Turbulence in a triply periodic box, decaying from a prescribed spectrum or sustained by forcing: a priori database and a posteriori test of subgrid closures (Re_λ 70 to 418, LES of 24³ to 144³ degrees of freedom), a dissipation test of schemes for hybrid RANS/LES, a DNS test up to supersonic turbulent Mach numbers, and driven MHD turbulence.
* [JAXA Standard Model high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/jaxa-standard-model.md) — The JAXA Standard Model, a half-span civil transport in landing configuration from the Third AIAA High-Lift Prediction Workshop at chord Reynolds number 1.96 million and Mach 0.172, on which wall-modelled LES predicts maximum lift within five lift counts on grids of 32 to 185 million cells.
* [NASA Juncture Flow](/benchmarks/nasa-juncture-flow.md) — The NASA Juncture Flow wing–fuselage model, with smooth-body corner separation at the wing-root trailing edge at crank-chord Reynolds number 2.4 million, Mach 0.189 and 5° incidence, on which wall-modelled LES predicts wall pressure within 5% but mean velocity only within 3–8% on the fuselage and about 100% in the separated corner.
* [Periodic hill](/benchmarks/periodic-hill.md) — The streamwise-periodic channel with a hill constriction, a smooth-wall separation and reattachment test: at hill-height Reynolds number about 10,600 for hybrid RANS/LES and implicit LES on coarse grids, as the parametrized hill family at 5600 for learned RANS corrections, and at 2800 for data assimilation and physics-informed networks.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor mixing](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-mixing.md) — Buoyancy-driven mixing of a heavy fluid placed above a light one: a transient flow that passes from linear laminar growth through bubbles, spikes and Kelvin–Helmholtz transition to turbulent mixing, here at Atwood number 0.5 as a test that RANS fails and partially-averaged Navier–Stokes recovers at a fraction of the resolution.
* [Shock–turbulent boundary layer interaction](/benchmarks/shock-turbulent-boundary-layer-interaction.md) — A supersonic or hypersonic turbulent boundary layer meeting a shock from a compression ramp or an impinging oblique shock: separation length, low-frequency unsteadiness and wall heating test the subgrid model, the span of the domain and the DNS cost, here from Mach 2.9 to 9.1 by LES, DNS and wall-modelled LES.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — The triply periodic vortex flow started from a single-mode analytic field that transitions to turbulence and decays: at Re 40,000 against a 5400³ DNS to separate Smagorinsky and implicit LES errors, at Re 1600 to measure numerical dissipation and test subgrid models, inviscid to tune spectral vanishing viscosity, in 2D for commutator errors, and as a parallel-scaling test.
* [Turbulent boundary layers: zero and adverse pressure gradient, compressible](/benchmarks/turbulent-boundary-layer.md) — Spatially developing turbulent boundary layers on flat walls: zero- and adverse-pressure-gradient layers as wall-model, log-law and physics-informed-network tests, supersonic and hypersonic layers on cooled walls as DNS databases for compressibility transformations and as LES validation, and the incoming layer of shock interactions.
* [Turbulent channel flow](/benchmarks/turbulent-channel-flow.md) — Pressure-driven flow between two parallel walls, periodic in the wall-parallel directions, the canonical wall-bounded test: here DNS at Re_τ 180, wall-resolved LES at 180 to 2000 (learned subgrid models, grid adaptation), a priori data at 395 to 2000, wall-modelled LES at Re_τ 1000 to 10¹⁰, hybrid RANS/LES to 20,000, and compressible channels to bulk Mach 4.

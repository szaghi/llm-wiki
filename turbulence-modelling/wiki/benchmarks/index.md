# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [ACAT1 fan stage](/benchmarks/acat1-fan-stage.md) — The ACAT1 fan and outlet-guide-vane stage of the TurboNoiseBB project, run at approach (tip relative Mach 0.57) with hot-wire wake data between rotor and stator: a test of zonal DES and of wall-modelled LES on 95 to 380 million cells for the rotor wakes that drive broadband interaction noise.
* [Ahmed body](/benchmarks/ahmed-body.md) — The Ahmed body, a bluff car-like body with a slanted rear whose angle sets the separation regime: at 35° a test of immersed-boundary wall-modelled LES and of SST-based hybrid RANS/LES on lattice Boltzmann grids, at 25° the calibration case of a DDES procedure applied to an estate car.
* [Backward-facing step](/benchmarks/backward-facing-step.md) — Turbulent flow over a sudden channel expansion, with fixed separation at the step edge, a free shear layer and downstream reattachment: the reattachment length as a measure of LES filter widths at Re_H about 5700, of adaptive IDDES meshes at 28,000, of a transferred learned RANS closure at 5000, and of a probabilistic learned closure at 300–1100.
* [Circular-cylinder wake](/benchmarks/circular-cylinder-wake.md) — Flow past a circular cylinder, chiefly at Re_D=3900 in the shear-layer transition regime, with separation, shear-layer roll-up and a turbulent wake: the test of neural-network SGS models across grids and Reynolds numbers, of high-order PANS against unmodelled under-resolved simulation, and of immersed-boundary lattice Boltzmann numerics.
* [ERCOFTAC T3 flat-plate transition cases](/benchmarks/ercoftac-t3-flat-plate.md) — The ERCOFTAC T3 series of flat-plate boundary layers under free-stream turbulence, at zero (T3A, T3B, T3A-) and variable pressure gradient (T3C1–T3C5): the calibration and first validation case of RANS transition models, where onset is predicted well at zero pressure gradient and tends to be delayed in adverse pressure gradient.
* [Homogeneous isotropic turbulence (decaying and forced)](/benchmarks/homogeneous-isotropic-turbulence.md) — Turbulence in a triply periodic box, decaying (including Comte-Bellot–Corrsin grid turbulence) or forced: a priori and a posteriori test of subgrid closures, learned, ensemble-calibrated and autonomous ones included, of filter widths for high-order LES and of scheme dissipation, DNS up to supersonic turbulent Mach numbers, and driven MHD turbulence.
* [Imperial Front Wing](/benchmarks/imperial-front-wing.md) — The Imperial Front Wing, a three-element inverted race-car wing in ground effect proposed as an open motorsport benchmark; a spanwise-periodic slice at Re 220,000 and ride height 0.36 chords, computed by stabilized spectral/hp implicit LES, shows laminar separation bubbles and wake-induced transition that lower-fidelity models must reproduce.
* [JAXA Standard Model high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/jaxa-standard-model.md) — The JAXA Standard Model, a half-span civil transport in landing configuration from the Third AIAA High-Lift Prediction Workshop at chord Reynolds number 1.96 million and Mach 0.172, on which wall-modelled LES predicts maximum lift within five lift counts on grids of 32 to 185 million cells.
* [NACA 0012 airfoil](/benchmarks/naca-0012-airfoil.md) — The symmetric NACA 0012 airfoil at chord Reynolds numbers of 1 to 6 million, from attached incidence to near stall and to 45° massive separation, and transonic at Mach 0.804: a validation case of high-order DG RANS, of immersed-boundary wall-modelled RANS and of lattice Boltzmann hybrid RANS/LES.
* [NASA CRM high-lift configuration](/benchmarks/nasa-crm-high-lift.md) — The NASA High-Lift Common Research Model (CRM-HL), a half-span transport in landing configuration at Mach 0.2 and mean-chord Reynolds number 5.49 million: a test of a learned wall model in wall-modelled LES at 7° and 19°, and of immersed-boundary wall-function RANS at 17.05° on a 2.4-billion-cell Cartesian grid.
* [NASA Juncture Flow](/benchmarks/nasa-juncture-flow.md) — The NASA Juncture Flow wing–fuselage model, with smooth-body corner separation at the wing-root trailing edge (crank-chord Reynolds number 2.4 million, Mach 0.189, 5°): wall-modelled LES predicts wall pressure within 5% but mean velocity only within 3–8% on the fuselage and about 100% in the corner, which a learned wall model does not fix.
* [Periodic hill](/benchmarks/periodic-hill.md) — The streamwise-periodic channel with a hill constriction, a smooth-wall separation and reattachment test: at hill-height Reynolds number about 10,600 for hybrid RANS/LES, implicit LES and scheme dissipation, at 5600 for stabilized finite-element implicit LES and, as a parametrized family, learned RANS corrections, and at 2800 for data assimilation and DG implicit LES.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor mixing](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-mixing.md) — Buoyancy-driven mixing of a heavy fluid placed above a light one: a transient flow that passes from linear laminar growth through bubbles, spikes and Kelvin–Helmholtz transition to turbulent mixing, here at Atwood number 0.5 as a test that RANS fails and partially-averaged Navier–Stokes recovers at a fraction of the resolution.
* [Shock–turbulent boundary layer interaction](/benchmarks/shock-turbulent-boundary-layer-interaction.md) — A turbulent boundary layer meeting a shock from a compression ramp, an impinging oblique shock or a transonic airfoil: separation length, low-frequency unsteadiness and wall heating test subgrid models, domain span and DNS cost from Mach 2.9 to 9.1, with RANS tests at Mach 2.92 and the transonic buffet of the OAT15A airfoil for PANS.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — The triply periodic vortex flow that transitions from a single-mode analytic field to decaying turbulence: at Re 40,000 against a 5400³ DNS to separate Smagorinsky and implicit LES errors, at Re 1600 and 5000 to split numerical dissipation and test subgrid models, filter widths and filters, inviscid for energy conservation, and in 2D for commutator errors.
* [Turbine cascades (T106, T161, VKI LS89)](/benchmarks/turbine-cascade.md) — Linear turbine cascades as transition and loss benchmarks: the high-lift low-pressure T106C, T108 and T161 cascades with laminar separation at exit Reynolds numbers near 10⁵, and the transonic VKI LS89 vane with bypass transition and heat transfer, run by implicit DG and flux-reconstruction LES and by RANS transition models.
* [Turbulent boundary layers: zero and adverse pressure gradient, compressible](/benchmarks/turbulent-boundary-layer.md) — Spatially developing turbulent boundary layers on flat walls: zero- and adverse-pressure-gradient layers as wall-model, log-law and physics-informed-network tests, supersonic and hypersonic layers on cooled walls as DNS databases for compressibility transformations, LES validation and the calibration of algebraic RANS, and the incoming layer of shock interactions.
* [Turbulent channel flow](/benchmarks/turbulent-channel-flow.md) — Pressure-driven flow between two parallel walls, periodic in the wall-parallel directions: DNS at Re_τ 100 to 395, wall-resolved and implicit LES at 180 to 2000 (learned subgrid models, convection schemes, grid adaptation), a priori data at 395 to 2000, wall-modelled LES at Re_τ 544 to 10¹⁰ (learned wall models, high-order filter widths), hybrid RANS/LES to 20,000, and compressible channels.

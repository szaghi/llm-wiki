# Results

Theorems, proved properties and barriers.

* [Decaying MHD turbulence and its invariants](/results/decaying-mhd-turbulence.md) — Decaying MHD turbulence is a selective decay whose power laws follow from the conserved quantity that constrains it (anastrophy, magnetic helicity, Saffman-type invariants such as the Hosking invariant) and from a reconnection-controlled decay time, as reviewed and extended by Schekochihin (2022, §12).
* [Grid-point requirements of LES and DNS](/results/les-grid-point-requirements.md) — How the number of grid points and time steps of DNS, wall-resolved LES and wall-modelled LES scales with Reynolds number, the explicit WMLES cost model with boundary-layer and leading-edge resolution parameters, the practical sizing rules the sources use, and why their WMLES cost exponents differ.
* [Small-scale turbulent dynamo](/results/small-scale-dynamo.md) — The small-scale (fluctuation) dynamo amplifies magnetic fields in folds at the resistive scale; Schekochihin (2022) argues that in saturation the folds are torn, setting the reversal scale at L Rm^-1/3 and seeding a local, critically balanced RMHD-like cascade below it.
* [Tearing-mediated MHD turbulence](/results/tearing-mediated-mhd-turbulence.md) — Below a disruption scale proportional to Rm^-4/7, aligned sheets of MHD turbulence are torn faster than they cascade; the resulting recursive, tearing-mediated cascade has a k_perp^-11/5 envelope and ends at a Kolmogorov-scaled dissipation cutoff (Schekochihin 2022, after Mallet et al. and Loureiro & Boldyrev).

## Chunk 2 — founding sources (2026-10-14)

Ingested here in full; the same papers are Source-only pages in the
plasma-propulsion archive's bundle (D23). Read by one reader agent per paper
(page 1 checked against the filename: all matched), drafted from the notes,
audited read-only before staging.

### Contradictions

None opened. Judged qualifications (prose on the pages):
* **Vortex lift on waveriders** (wang-2018-d vs zhao-2018,
  [waverider](wiki/definitions/waverider.md)): different bodies and Mach numbers;
  zhao shows nonlinear lift only on the cuspidal waverider, wang attributes
  nonlinear lift to vortex lift without showing a vortex; neither claims a
  general result.
* **Mass capture** (zuo-2018 vs liu-2018-d): two different normalisations, not
  competing values; the registry now defines $\varphi$.
* **Total-pressure recovery** spread (0.104–0.984): different devices, stations
  and Mach numbers.

### Printed inconsistencies (details under "Recorded as printed" on each Source page)

* zuo-2018: mass capture 0.997 (table) vs 0.992 (text); "1.42 times" vs 2.42× in
  Table 1; the "+3.73 %" recovery gain compares two different back pressures.
* wang-2018-d: planform length 5.0 (Table 2) vs about 6.71 from its own Eqs. 5–6
  (reader's integration); aerodynamic centre in $L_{\mathrm{local}}$ vs $L_{\mathrm{total}}$;
  reference area never stated.
* zhao-2018: "over 10 %" L/D gain vs 9.78 % in Table 4; Fig. 17 caption α = 0° vs
  10° in the text.
* fedele-2018: closed-configuration ballistic coefficient 142 kg/m² printed, 148
  from its own inputs; heat-shield 76 cm vs 0.66 m reference length.
* knight-2018: Fig. 20 ordinate $\ell_{\mathrm{sep}}/\delta$ (text) vs $\lambda_{\mathrm{sep}}/d$ (plot); St
  defined twice in §2.9.
* liu-2018-d: Eqs. 24–25 keep a spurious $-(\gamma-1)$ term; Table 6 interval widths
  5–10× the Fig. 20 spread; PCE order and sample count not stated.
* liu-2018-c: width correlation promised, not given; J = 0 point in the length fit.
* meng-2018: Table 2 outlet entries look swapped; 0.093 % vs 0.93 % in Table 3.
* moradi-2018: abstract's "+15 % He lateral" belongs to the back jet (Fig. 9); no
  validation case; grid counts inconsistent with dimensions.
* cheng-2018: 10.78 % called "optimal" but is the efficiency at maximum power.

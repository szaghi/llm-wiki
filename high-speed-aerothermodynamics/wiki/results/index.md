# Results

Theorems, proved properties and impossibility results.

* [Peak heating of transitional shock/boundary-layer interactions](/results/transitional-swbli-peak-heating.md) — A shock interacting with a transitional boundary layer can produce a peak wall heat flux well above both the laminar and the fully turbulent interaction of the same strength, about 2.2–3.7 times the Holden turbulent correlation (mean 3.4 on the hollow-cylinder flare) in the reviewed hypersonic datasets.

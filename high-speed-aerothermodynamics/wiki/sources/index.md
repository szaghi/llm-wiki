# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Cheng et al. (2018) — Performance evaluation and comparison of electricity generation systems based on single- and two-stage thermoelectric generator for hypersonic vehicles](/sources/cheng-2018.md) — Quasi-one-dimensional models of single- and two-stage SiGe thermoelectric generators heated by hydrogen fuel leaving the engine-wall cooling channels and cooled by fresh fuel, showing that the single stage gives the higher power density (16.53 kW/m² at 1200 K) and the two-stage the higher efficiency at maximum power (10.78%).
* [Fedele & Mungiguerra (2018) — Aerodynamics and flight mechanics activities for a suborbital flight test of a deployable heat shield capsule](/sources/fedele-2018.md) — Reports the trajectory analysis and preliminary all-regime aerodynamic database of MINI-IRENE, a suborbital demonstrator with an umbrella-like deployable heat shield: 3-DoF re-entry loads, a sin²-of-log-Knudsen bridging function between DSMC and continuum data, Newtonian and Mach-independence estimates, and static stability and trim from 0° to 180°.
* [Knight & Mortazavi (2018) — Hypersonic shock wave transitional boundary layer interactions - A review](/sources/knight-2018.md) — Reviews hypersonic shock-wave/transitional-boundary-layer interaction research since 1993 over fourteen canonical configurations, proposes a four-class categorisation of interactions, shows that transitional interactions can exceed laminar and turbulent peak heating by factors up to about 3.7 over the Holden turbulent correlation, and names the tuned inflow forcing of DNS as the open problem.
* [Liu H. et al. (2018) — Uncertainty and sensitivity analysis of flow parameters on aerodynamics of a hypersonic inlet](/sources/liu-2018-d.md) — Propagates uncertainty in Mach number, unit Reynolds number, angle of attack, freestream and wall temperature through SST RANS of a two-ramp hypersonic inlet at Mach 5.9, in both its started and unstarted states inside the Mach-number hysteresis loop, with point-collocation polynomial chaos and Sobol' indices.
* [Liu Y. et al. (2018) — Structures of near-wall wakes subjected to a sonic jet in a supersonic crossflow](/sources/liu-2018-c.md) — Visualises with NPLS and surface oil flow the near-wall wake behind a sonic nitrogen jet in a Mach 2.95 crossflow for momentum flux ratios 2.3–28.9, divides it into a collision-shock-induced V-shaped separation, a reattachment region and a mixing region, and fits the separation length as a power law in the momentum flux ratio.
* [Meng et al. (2018) — Parameterization and optimization for shape-transition curved isolator](/sources/meng-2018.md) — Parameterises a curved scramjet isolator whose cross-section changes shape by blending entrance and exit sections with area rescaling along a B-spline offset line, optimises three offset-line variables for total-pressure recovery with a genetic algorithm coupled to SST RANS at Mach 2, and compares four isolators under back pressure.
* [Moradi et al. (2018) — The influence of coolant jet direction on heat reduction on the nose cone with Aerodome at supersonic flow](/sources/moradi-2018.md) — RANS (SST k–ω) study of sonic coolant injection from the front, back or top of an aerodome on a spiked nose cone at Mach 5, finding that top (lateral) injection reduces the nose-cone heat load by about 90%, front (opposing) injection the least, and that the coolant gas matters mainly at the back position.
* [Wang et al. (2018) — Design methodology of the waverider with a controllable planar shape](/sources/wang-2018-d.md) — Extends osculating-cone waverider design so that the planform curve becomes a design input, via a geometric relation among flow capture, inlet capture and planform curves, and shows on Mach 6 designs that low-sweep planforms raise Mach 0.4 lift-to-drag ratio from 12.1 to 18.3–20.1 at nearly unchanged design-point lift-to-drag ratio.
* [Zhao et al. (2018) — Design and high speed aerodynamic performance analysis of vortex lift waverider with a wide-speed range](/sources/zhao-2018.md) — Derives osculating-cone design relations for delta-winged and cuspidal waveriders with a straight constant-sweep leading-edge segment and shows by Fluent RANS at Mach 3–10 that, at equal volumetric efficiency, the cuspidal waverider has the highest lift-to-drag ratio (up to 18.5% above a general osculating-cone waverider) and the only nonlinear lift rise.
* [Zuo & Huang (2018) — Numerical investigation of bleeding control method on section-controllable wavecatcher intakes](/sources/zuo-2018.md) — Compares an inward-turning parent flow (internal conical flow A plus truncated Busemann) with planar compressions at Mach 4, argues that it aggravates shock/boundary-layer interaction, and shows by RANS that a 20 mm bleed slot at the reflected-shock impingement of a section-controllable intake removes a throat normal shock and raises the sustainable back-pressure ratio from 73.4 to 76.7.

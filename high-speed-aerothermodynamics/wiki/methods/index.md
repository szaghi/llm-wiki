# Methods

Algorithms and schemes.

* [Aeroheating reduction by spikes and coolant jets](/methods/aeroheating-reduction-spikes-and-jets.md) — Reduction of the heat load on a blunt nose by a forward spike carrying an aerodisk or aerodome, combined with coolant injection from the cap; at Mach 5 a lateral jet from the top of the aerodome is computed to cut the nose-cone heat load by about 90%, an opposing jet the least.
* [Boundary-layer bleed for intake control](/methods/boundary-layer-bleed.md) — Removes low-momentum boundary-layer fluid through a wall slot at a shock impingement to suppress interaction-induced separation in an intake; on a Mach-4 inward-turning intake a 20 mm slot bleeding about 2.5% of the captured mass removed a throat normal shock and raised the sustainable back-pressure ratio from 73.4 to 76.7.
* [Fuel-cooled thermoelectric generators for hypersonic vehicles](/methods/thermoelectric-generator-for-hypersonic-vehicles.md) — On-board electricity generation by thermoelectric modules placed between fuel already heated in the engine-wall cooling channels and fresh fuel, modelled as a quasi-one-dimensional nodal network; single-stage SiGe modules give the higher power density, two-stage modules the higher efficiency at maximum power.
* [Inward-turning intake design](/methods/inward-turning-intake-design.md) — Designs a three-dimensional inward-turning intake by streamline tracing in an axisymmetric internal parent flow (internal conical flow A, Busemann, or their coupling ICFC) over osculating planes, trading higher recovery and shorter, less wetted compression than planar wedges for a harder shock/boundary-layer interaction.
* [Non-intrusive polynomial chaos and Sobol' indices](/methods/non-intrusive-polynomial-chaos.md) — Uncertainty propagation that fits a polynomial-chaos surrogate to a set of deterministic CFD runs at collocation points of the uncertain inputs and reads means, variances and variance-based Sobol' sensitivity indices directly from the expansion coefficients; applied to a started and unstarted hypersonic inlet.
* [Osculating-cone waverider design](/methods/osculating-cone-waverider-design.md) — Inverse design of waveriders and inward-turning intakes that splits a three-dimensional shock surface into osculating planes, assigns each a local conical (or wedge) parent flow with a common shock angle, and builds the surface by streamline tracing from prescribed base-plane capture curves.
* [Re-entry capsule aerodynamics across flow regimes](/methods/reentry-capsule-aerodynamics-across-regimes.md) — Building a capsule aerodynamic database from free-molecular to continuum flow — Knudsen-number regime map, DSMC in rarefied flow, a sin²-of-log-Knudsen bridging function in the transitional regime, Newtonian and Navier–Stokes estimates in continuum flow — and using it with the ballistic coefficient for trajectory loads, static stability and trim.
* [Shape-transition isolator design](/methods/shape-transition-isolator-design.md) — Parameterises a curved isolator whose cross-section changes shape by linearly blending entrance and exit sections with area rescaling and translating them along a B-spline offset line, whose three free variables are optimised for exit total-pressure recovery by a multi-island genetic algorithm coupled to RANS.
* [SST k–ω turbulence model in high-speed flow](/methods/sst-k-omega-model.md) — Menter's two-equation shear-stress-transport model is the RANS closure of four of the bundle's inlet, isolator, aeroheating and waverider studies, once with a compressibility correction, and is validated in them only against wall pressure.

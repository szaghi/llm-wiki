# high-speed-aerothermodynamics — Knowledge Bundle

Hypersonic and supersonic flow around and inside vehicles: inlets, isolators and bleed, waverider design, shock-wave/boundary-layer interaction, jets in supersonic crossflow, aerodynamic heating and its reduction, suborbital flight aerodynamics, and the uncertainty quantification of high-speed flow predictions.

**Start here:** the [Notation registry](/notation.md), which is normative
for every page, and the [update log](/log.md).

**Corpus:** 10 sources, all from *Acta Astronautica* vol. 151 (2018), copied
from the plasma-propulsion archive where they had been filed (D23): one review
([Knight & Mortazavi 2018](/sources/knight-2018.md)) and nine research papers.
The [bibliography](/bibliography.md) lists the works they cite.

## Reading guide

1. **The interaction at the heart of high-speed flow.**
   [Shock-wave/boundary-layer interaction](/definitions/shock-wave-boundary-layer-interaction.md)
   (classification, correlations, standard test configurations), then
   [peak heating of transitional interactions](/results/transitional-swbli-peak-heating.md).
2. **Air intakes.** [Inlet start, unstart and hysteresis](/definitions/inlet-start-and-unstart.md)
   (recovery, mass capture, back pressure, contraction ratio), the
   [isolator and shock train](/definitions/isolator-shock-train.md),
   [inward-turning intake design](/methods/inward-turning-intake-design.md),
   [shape-transition isolator design](/methods/shape-transition-isolator-design.md)
   and [boundary-layer bleed](/methods/boundary-layer-bleed.md).
3. **Vehicles.** [Waverider](/definitions/waverider.md) and
   [osculating-cone waverider design](/methods/osculating-cone-waverider-design.md);
   [re-entry capsule aerodynamics across flow regimes](/methods/reentry-capsule-aerodynamics-across-regimes.md).
4. **Jets and heating.** [Transverse jet in supersonic crossflow](/definitions/jet-in-supersonic-crossflow.md),
   [aeroheating reduction by spikes and coolant jets](/methods/aeroheating-reduction-spikes-and-jets.md),
   and the on-board use of that heat:
   [fuel-cooled thermoelectric generators](/methods/thermoelectric-generator-for-hypersonic-vehicles.md).
5. **Modelling and uncertainty.** [SST k–ω turbulence model](/methods/sst-k-omega-model.md)
   and [non-intrusive polynomial chaos with Sobol' indices](/methods/non-intrusive-polynomial-chaos.md).

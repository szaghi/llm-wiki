# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Arfken, Weber & Harris (2013) — Mathematical Methods for Physicists, 7th ed. (§12.1, Chs. 14–15, §§18.1, 18.3, 18.4)](/sources/arfken-2013.md) — Graduate-level textbook read for its general treatment of orthogonal polynomials (Rodrigues formula from the Sturm–Liouville equation, Schlaefli integral, generating functions), its Bessel-function chapter (J, Y, Hankel, modified, asymptotic and spherical Bessel functions), its Legendre-function chapter (Legendre, associated Legendre with the Condon–Shortley phase, spherical harmonics, second-kind functions) and its Hermite, Laguerre and Chebyshev sections.
* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering, 3rd ed. (Ch. 18 and §27.4)](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Undergraduate textbook read for its special-functions chapter (Legendre, associated Legendre, spherical harmonics with the addition theorem, Chebyshev, Bessel, spherical Bessel, Laguerre, Hermite) and its numerical-integration section (trapezium, Simpson, Gauss–Legendre, Gauss–Chebyshev, Gauss–Laguerre and Gauss–Hermite rules with tables).

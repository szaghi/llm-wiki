# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Associated Legendre functions](/definitions/associated-legendre-functions.md) — The regular solutions P_l^m of the associated Legendre equation for integers 0<=|m|<=l, built as (1-x^2)^{m/2} times the m-th derivative of P_l; orthogonal in l at fixed m with norm 2(l+m)!/((2l+1)(l-m)!); the two founding sources differ by the Condon–Shortley factor (-1)^m.
* [Bessel functions](/definitions/bessel-functions.md) — Solutions of Bessel's equation x^2y''+xy'+(x^2-nu^2)y=0: the regular J_nu (Frobenius series), the second-kind Y_nu, the Hankel functions, and the modified I_nu and K_nu; series, recurrences, integral representations, zeros, Wronskians and large-argument asymptotics as numerics uses them.
* [Chebyshev polynomials](/definitions/chebyshev-polynomials.md) — The polynomials T_n(cos theta)=cos(n theta) and U_n(cos theta)=sin((n+1)theta)/sin(theta), orthogonal on [-1,1] with weights (1-x^2)^{-1/2} and (1-x^2)^{1/2}; trigonometric and explicit forms, special values, the companion non-polynomial solutions V_n and W_n, their origin as Gegenbauer polynomials, and the equal-ripple (minimax) property used in numerical analysis.
* [Hermite polynomials](/definitions/hermite-polynomials.md) — The polynomial solutions H_n of Hermite's equation y''-2xy'+2ny=0 with leading coefficient 2^n (the physicists' standardization), orthogonal on the whole real line with weight exp(-x^2) and norm 2^n n! sqrt(pi); explicit form, parity, special values and the Hermite functions exp(-x^2/2)H_n.
* [Laguerre and associated Laguerre polynomials](/definitions/laguerre-polynomials.md) — The polynomial solutions L_n of Laguerre's equation with L_n(0)=1, orthonormal on [0,inf) with weight exp(-x), and the associated polynomials L_n^{(m)}=(-1)^m d^m L_{n+m}/dx^m, orthogonal with weight x^m exp(-x) and norm (n+m)!/n!; explicit forms, special values and Laguerre functions.
* [Legendre polynomials](/definitions/legendre-polynomials.md) — The polynomial solutions P_n of Legendre's equation, standardized by P_n(1)=1 and orthogonal with unit weight on [-1,1] with norm 2/(2n+1); explicit form, parity, special values, bound |P_n|<=1, Legendre series, and the second-kind functions Q_n.
* [Orthogonal polynomials with respect to a weight function](/definitions/orthogonal-polynomials.md) — A sequence of polynomials p_n of exact degree n with zero weighted inner product on an interval; defines weight function, norm h_n, the orthogonal expansion and its coefficients, and the Sturm–Liouville origin of the classical families.
* [Spherical Bessel functions](/definitions/spherical-bessel-functions.md) — The radial solutions j_l, y_l (and Hankel combinations h_l) of the Helmholtz equation in spherical coordinates, sqrt(pi/2x) times Bessel functions of order l+1/2; elementary closed forms, Rayleigh formulae, recurrences, small- and large-argument limits, orthogonality on [0,a] with weight r^2, and the modified functions i_l, k_l.
* [Spherical harmonics](/definitions/spherical-harmonics.md) — The functions Y_l^m(theta,phi) proportional to P_l^m(cos theta) e^{i m phi}, normalized to be orthonormal on the unit sphere; both founding sources give the same functions (Condon–Shortley phase), the Laplace series, rotation closure within each l, and recurrences in l and m.
* [Zernike polynomials and the Fourier–Zernike basis](/definitions/zernike-polynomials.md) — The radial polynomials R_n^m(r) (0<=m<=n, n-m even) and the real Zernike functions R_n^|m|(r)cos(m phi) or sin(|m| phi) on the unit disc, whose r^|m| factor builds in the regularity of analytic functions at the origin; their product with a Fourier series in a periodic coordinate is the Fourier–Zernike spectral basis, with exponential radial convergence measured in one code.

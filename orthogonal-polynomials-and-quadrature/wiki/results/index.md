# Results

Theorems, proved properties, impossibility results.

* [Fourier–Bessel series](/results/fourier-bessel-series.md) — For fixed order nu, the functions J_nu(alpha_{nu,k} rho/a), k=1,2,..., are orthogonal on [0,a] with weight rho and norm (a^2/2) J_{nu+1}^2(alpha_{nu,k}); the Lommel-type boundary identity behind it, the Dirichlet and Neumann variants, and the expansion coefficients.
* [Generating functions of the classical polynomials and Bessel functions](/results/generating-functions.md) — Closed-form functions g(x,t) whose Taylor or Laurent coefficients in t are the Legendre, associated Legendre, Chebyshev, Gegenbauer, Hermite, Laguerre, associated Laguerre polynomials and the integer-order Bessel functions; how recurrences follow from them, and the two different Chebyshev T_n generating functions of the founding sources.
* [Legendre expansion of the inverse distance (axial multipole expansion)](/results/legendre-expansion-of-inverse-distance.md) — 1/|r-r'| = sum_l r_<^l / r_>^{l+1} P_l(cos gamma), the Legendre generating function read geometrically; converges except at r=r'; gives the axial multipole expansion, whose lowest non-vanishing moment alone is independent of the expansion centre.
* [Rodrigues formula](/results/rodrigues-formula.md) — For a Sturm–Liouville equation p y''+q y'+lambda y=0 with p quadratic and q linear, the degree-n polynomial solution is proportional to (1/omega) d^n/dx^n [omega p^n], omega the weight; the general proof (Arfken §12.1) and the formulae of the Legendre, associated Legendre, Chebyshev, Hermite and Laguerre families, with the scale factors that fix each standardization.
* [Spherical harmonic addition theorem](/results/spherical-harmonic-addition-theorem.md) — P_l(cos gamma) = 4 pi/(2l+1) sum_m Y_l^m(Omega) [Y_l^m(Omega')]^*, with gamma the angle between two directions; proved in Riley et al. from the closure relation and the rotation closure of each degree-l subspace; Arfken et al. defer it beyond the chapters read.
* [Three-term recurrences of the classical polynomials](/results/three-term-recurrences.md) — The recurrences p_{n+1}=(A_n x+B_n)p_n-C_n p_{n-1} of the Legendre, Chebyshev, Hermite, Laguerre and associated Laguerre polynomials (and the Legendre functions of the second kind and Bessel functions in their order), as stated family by family in both founding sources; their use to evaluate p_n(x) from p_0 and p_1, and what the sources leave out.

# Operators

Discrete operators with an algebraic identity.

* [Logarithmic mean](/operators/logarithmic-mean.md) — The logarithmic mean of two positive numbers, the jump of a quantity divided by the jump of its logarithm, is the mean that makes two-point fluxes entropy-conservative for entropies with logarithmic terms; the page gives its identity, its stable and division-reduced evaluation, its cost, and the fluxes that use it.
* [Summation-by-parts operator](/operators/summation-by-parts-operator.md) — A first-derivative matrix D with a symmetric positive-definite norm M is summation-by-parts when MD + DᵀM equals the boundary operator; LGL collocation (DGSEM) has this property with diagonal norm, which yields the strong–weak equivalence, the skew-symmetric flux-differencing operator, the flux form of nodal DG and its entropy proofs, and a sparse first-order SBP operator for low-order schemes.

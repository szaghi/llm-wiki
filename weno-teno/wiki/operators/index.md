# Operators

Discrete operators with an algebraic identity.

* [Summation-by-parts operator](/operators/summation-by-parts-operator.md) — A first-derivative matrix D with a symmetric positive-definite norm M is summation-by-parts when MD + DᵀM equals the boundary operator; LGL collocation (DGSEM) has this property with diagonal norm, which yields the strong–weak equivalence, the skew-symmetric flux-differencing operator and the flux form of nodal DG.

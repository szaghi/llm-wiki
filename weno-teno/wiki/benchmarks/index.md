# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Double Mach reflection](/benchmarks/double-mach-reflection.md) — Two-dimensional Euler benchmark in which a Mach 10 shock at 60° to a reflecting wall forms a double Mach stem, slip line and wall jet; used to rank the numerical dissipation of shock-capturing schemes by the Kelvin–Helmholtz roll-up along the slip line.
* [High-Mach astrophysical jets](/benchmarks/high-mach-astrophysical-jet.md) — Mach 80 and Mach 2000 gas jets injected into a light ambient medium, and their relativistic and MHD relatives; near-vacuum, extreme-Mach-number robustness tests that most high-order schemes pass only with a positivity- or invariant-domain-preserving limiter.
* [Isentropic vortex](/benchmarks/isentropic-vortex.md) — Smooth exact solution of the two-dimensional Euler equations, an isentropic vortex advected by a uniform stream on a periodic box; used for design-order convergence studies and, with an amplified vortex strength, as a stress test of entropy-conservative flux implementations.
* [Kelvin–Helmholtz instability](/benchmarks/kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md) — Periodic two-dimensional Euler shear-layer benchmark, in sharp, exponentially smoothed and tanh-smoothed variants; used as a visual dissipation gauge, a positivity stress test for high-order DG, and a probe of the non-convergence of inviscid shear-flow simulations.
* [KPP rotating wave](/benchmarks/kpp-rotating-wave.md) — Two-dimensional scalar conservation law with the nonconvex flux (sin u, cos u) and piecewise-constant data, whose entropy solution is a rotating spiral; high-order schemes that are non-oscillatory but not entropy stable converge to a wrong, nonentropic solution.
* [Mach 3 forward-facing step](/benchmarks/forward-facing-step.md) — Two-dimensional Euler benchmark of Mach 3 flow in a wind tunnel with a step, run to t=4; it tests robustness at the expansion corner and the resolution of the Kelvin–Helmholtz roll-up of the vortex sheet from the triple point.
* [Orszag–Tang vortex](/benchmarks/orszag-tang-vortex.md) — Periodic two-dimensional ideal-MHD benchmark in which smooth vortical velocity and magnetic fields steepen into interacting shocks and current sheets; used for high-order MHD shock capturing, density-bound limiting and divergence control.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor instability](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) — Two-dimensional Euler benchmark with gravity, in the Xu–Shu setting: a heavy and a light gas, γ=5/3, a single-mode interface perturbation on [0,1/4]×[0,1] run to t=1.95; used to compare the small-scale structure and the left–right symmetry of low-dissipation schemes.
* [Shock–bubble interaction](/benchmarks/shock-bubble-interaction.md) — A planar shock hitting a circular region of different density or material, from the one-dimensional Euler analogue to the Mach 6 air–helium interaction and Baer–Nunziato and shallow-water variants; tests interface sharpness, robustness at material contrasts and the resolution of the vortical roll-up behind the shock.
* [Shock–vortex interaction](/benchmarks/shock-vortex-interaction.md) — An isentropic vortex advected through a stationary Mach 1.1 shock on [0,2]×[0,1]; the reference setup tests the resolution of the shocked vortex through pressure profiles against fine-grid references, and amplified or non-Euler variants test bound preservation and non-conservative multiphase schemes.
* [Shu–Osher shock–entropy-wave problem](/benchmarks/shu-osher-problem.md) — One-dimensional Euler benchmark of a Mach 3 shock running into a sinusoidal density (entropy) wave, with its Titarev–Toro high-frequency variant; the bundle's most widely run test of how well a shock-capturing scheme resolves the short waves generated behind a shock without oscillating at the shocklets.
* [Sod and Lax shock tubes](/benchmarks/sod-and-lax-shock-tubes.md) — The two most often run one-dimensional Euler Riemann problems with exact solutions, Sod's mild tube and Lax's stronger one, run by most sources as the first check of shock and contact capturing; records each source's placement, grid and final time and the printed variants.
* [Taylor–Green vortex](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — Three-dimensional periodic vortex flow that transitions to turbulence, run inviscid at low Mach number to compare the kinetic-energy loss and enstrophy growth of shock-capturing schemes, and viscous at Re=1600 to compare their dissipation rate with DNS; the bundle's smooth-turbulence resolution test for TENO-type schemes.
* [Two-dimensional Riemann problems](/benchmarks/two-dimensional-riemann-problems.md) — Four-quadrant Riemann problems of the two-dimensional Euler equations in the Lax–Liu classification, chiefly configurations 3 and 12; used as diagonally symmetric visual tests of resolution, of the jet's sideband and neck instabilities and of scale invariance.
* [Woodward–Colella interacting blast waves](/benchmarks/woodward-colella-blast-wave.md) — One-dimensional Euler problem of two strong blast waves between reflecting walls, pressures 1000, 0.01 and 100, run to t=0.038; a one-dimensional robustness test whose resolution is measured by the resolution of the density peaks and contacts after the blasts collide.

# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Approximate dispersion relation](/definitions/approximate-dispersion-relation.md) — The modified-wavenumber characterization of a (possibly nonlinear) spatial scheme, its sign convention for dissipation and anti-dissipation, the Hu-type dispersion-to-dissipation design ratio, and what the chunk-1 sources read off it for WENO, TENO, THINC and ROUND schemes.
* [Boundary variation diminishing (BVD)](/definitions/boundary-variation-diminishing.md) — BVD is the selection principle that, in each cell, picks among candidate reconstructions (polynomial WENO or TENO versus THINC) the one that minimizes the jump of reconstructed values at cell interfaces, and so the dissipation term of the Riemann flux; this page records how the chunk-1 sources define, use and contrast it.
* [Carbuncle phenomenon](/definitions/carbuncle-phenomenon.md) — The carbuncle is a numerical instability that low-dissipation approximate Riemann solvers such as HLLC and Roe can develop near strong shocks; the bundle's sources avoid it by switching to the more dissipative, carbuncle-free HLL solver at strong shocks.
* [Conservative finite-difference scheme](/definitions/conservative-finite-difference-scheme.md) — A conservative finite-difference scheme evolves point values by differences of numerical fluxes at half points, du_i/dt = −(f̂_{i+1/2} − f̂_{i−1/2})/Δx, with the flux built so that the difference approximates ∂_x f to high order; the sources build it by reconstructing split point fluxes, by interpolating states with derivative corrections, or in fluctuation form.
* [ENO property](/definitions/eno-property.md) — A reconstruction has the ENO property when, near a discontinuity, it effectively draws only on candidate stencils that do not cross it, so the solution is captured essentially non-oscillatorily; for weighted schemes this means the weights of discontinuous stencils vanish asymptotically, a condition that a too-large or absolute ε can silently defeat.
* [Normalised-variable diagram](/definitions/normalised-variable-diagram.md) — The normalised-variable diagram plots the normalised interface value of a three-cell compact reconstruction against the normalised cell average; Deng's unified version (UND) adds the accuracy point, the third-order line, diffusion and anti-diffusion regions, a CFL-dependent CBC-TVD region and ENO and high-resolution regions, on which TVD, NVD, WENO3, THINC, ROUND and learned schemes are compared.
* [Numerical dissipation and anti-dissipation](/definitions/numerical-dissipation.md) — Numerical dissipation is the part of a scheme's error that damps resolved content, anti-dissipation the part that amplifies or steepens it; this page fixes the three ways the chunk-1 sources measure and control it (flux viscosity term, leading truncation error on the normalised-variable diagram, imaginary part of the modified wavenumber) and the design levers they use.
* [Smoothness indicators](/definitions/smoothness-indicator.md) — A smoothness indicator β_k is a quadratic functional of the data on candidate stencil S_k, of size O(Δx²) on smooth data and O(1) across a jump; the Jiang–Shu form, a scaled sum of squared derivative L² norms of the candidate polynomial, underlies WENO, TENO and Hermite-WENO weights in the bundle.
* [Total variation diminishing (TVD) and slope limiters](/definitions/total-variation-diminishing.md) — TVD reconstruction limits the interface value of a second-order scheme by a limiter function of the slope ratio inside Sweby's region; this page collects the limiter formulas of the chunk-1 sources (MinMod, generalized minmod, SuperBee, van Leer, van Albada, OSPRE, MC, Deng's shape-preserving limiter), their normalised-variable reading, and the roles limiters play inside WENO, central-upwind and DG schemes.
* [Troubled-cell and shock indicators](/definitions/troubled-cell-indicator.md) — A troubled-cell (shock, discontinuity) indicator is a cheap local functional of the discrete solution that decides where a high-order scheme must be limited, blended or switched; the page defines the indicators the sources use — modal energy, WENO deviation, undivided-difference, TENO-flag and flattener types — with their parameters and stated limitations.

# Results

Theorems, proved properties and barriers.

* [Equivalence of DG and subcell finite volumes](/results/dg-subcell-finite-volume-equivalence.md) — Vilar and Abgrall prove that a degree-N DG scheme on any polygonal cell split into as many subcells as degrees of freedom, with an invertible projection onto subcell means, is exactly a finite-volume scheme on those means with explicit reconstructed fluxes on the interior subcell faces and the DG numerical flux on the cell boundary.
* [Lax–Wendroff theorem](/results/lax-wendroff-theorem.md) — The Lax–Wendroff theorem makes conservation in flux form, with consistent numerical fluxes, the condition for a convergent scheme to converge to a weak solution; the sources invoke it for local conservation, Active Flux, subcell fluxes of DGSEM and conservative multi-medium schemes, extend it to a vanishing conservation defect, and show wrong shock speeds without conservation.
* [Local conservation is equivalent to a flux form](/results/local-conservation-flux-form.md) — Coppola and Veldman prove that a banded periodic matrix has vanishing column sums iff it factors as (I − T⁻¹)F, so for volume-consistent schemes global conservation of a linear invariant implies local conservation with an explicit face flux, and every globally conservative finite-difference scheme is a cell-centred finite-volume scheme; the same zero-sum-to-flux step makes subcell limiting and time blending conservative.

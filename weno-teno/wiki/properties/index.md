# Properties

Constraints and invariants a method can hold.

* [Accuracy at critical points](/properties/critical-point-accuracy.md) — A nonlinear reconstruction keeps its design order at a critical point, where the first m derivatives vanish, only if its weights still converge fast enough to the linear weights there; WENO-JS and WENO-Z lose orders, and mapped, ZN and related weight designs restore them up to a scheme-dependent critical-point order.
* [Asymptotic preservation](/properties/asymptotic-preservation.md) — A scheme for a singularly perturbed problem is asymptotic preserving if, as the perturbation parameter vanishes, it becomes a consistent scheme for the limit problem under parameter-independent stability constraints; the bundle has three cases: relaxation limits of Euler with gravity and friction, the incompressible limit of full Euler, and the hydrodynamic limit of discrete kinetic schemes.
* [Bound preservation](/properties/bound-preservation.md) — A scheme is bound-preserving when its discrete solution stays in the convex admissible set G of the PDE; the page states the definition and its invariant-domain form, the admissible sets of the sources with their convexity, the Zhang–Shu cell-average route with its viscous, reacting-flow and relativistic extensions, the flux-limiting, convex-limiting, filtering and a posteriori (MOOD) routes, and the geometric-quasilinearization proofs.
* [Divergence-free magnetic field](/properties/divergence-free-magnetic-field.md) — The magnetic field of MHD obeys div B = 0; discretizations enforce it exactly, locally, by cleaning or not at all. Wu and Shu show that bound-preserving high-order MHD needs a discrete divergence-free condition, recovered by a Godunov-symmetrization source; the page adds Powell-type sources, locally divergence-free reconstruction, constrained transport, projection and GLM cleaning, and divergence-preserving prolongation on adaptive meshes.
* [Entropy stability](/properties/entropy-stability.md) — A scheme is entropy stable when its discrete total entropy obeys the entropy inequality of the PDE; the page gives flux- and scheme-level conditions, including non-conservative and moving-mesh forms, entropy-stable DG and FR, correction terms, dissipation, limiting and filtering, discrete entropy tests, the KPP evidence, the limits of the property and the minimum entropy principle.
* [Free-stream preservation](/properties/free-stream-preservation.md) — A discretization on curvilinear or moving grids is free-stream preserving when a uniform state stays an exact discrete steady solution, which requires discrete metric identities; the page covers the DGSEM identity, corrected and subcell metrics, warped-mesh tests, geometric conservation laws on moving meshes with the Duan–Tang proof, and an untested TENO claim.
* [Kinetic energy preservation](/properties/kinetic-energy-preservation.md) — A semi-discretization is kinetic-energy-preserving when its convective terms neither create nor destroy discrete kinetic energy, which Coppola and Veldman reduce to the skew-symmetry of C − ½diag(d_mass); the page gives that criterion, face-flux conditions, the second-order barrier, SBP proofs including the rotational form, correction terms, and DG two-point fluxes.
* [Scale and affine invariance](/properties/scale-invariance.md) — A reconstruction or limiter is scale-invariant when it commutes with multiplication of the data by a constant, and affine-invariant when it also commutes with adding a constant; classical WENO weights have neither property because ε is absolute, and the sources reach it by normalised variables, relative ε, or globally normalised damping.
* [Stationary contact preservation](/properties/stationary-contact-preservation.md) — A scheme preserves stationary contacts when an isolated discontinuity of a linearly degenerate field with zero speed stays exactly on the mesh; Balsara et al. obtain it in FD-WENO through HLLI anti-diffusion, Renac in multicomponent DGSEM through contact-resolving fluxes, Lax–Friedrichs-type FD-WENO and central-upwind schemes lack it, and for moving interfaces the weaker interface equilibrium condition applies.
* [Symmetry preservation](/properties/symmetry-preservation.md) — Symmetry preservation has two meanings in the sources: keeping the mirror symmetry of a symmetric flow under round-off or on an unstructured mesh, which low-dissipation schemes lose unless implemented consistently, and preserving the skew-symmetry of the continuous convective operator, which yields discrete kinetic-energy conservation; this page states both and the evidence on each.
* [Well-balancing](/properties/well-balancing.md) — A scheme for a balance law is well-balanced if it preserves a steady state such as the lake at rest or a hydrostatic atmosphere exactly; in this bundle it follows from affine- or scale-invariant WENO interpolation, stationary-contact-preserving fluctuations, equilibrium variables, hydrostatic reconstruction for Euler with gravity, and evolution of the deviation from a known equilibrium.

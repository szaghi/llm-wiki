# Summary

Liu & Wu (2025) extend the [oscillation-eliminating DG](/methods/oedg.md)
(OEDG) method of [Peng et al. (2024)](/sources/peng-2024.md) to 2D ideal MHD
and add two structure-preserving properties: a locally
[divergence-free](/properties/divergence-free-magnetic-field.md) (LDF)
magnetic field and provable positivity of density and internal energy. The
[DG](/methods/discontinuous-galerkin.md) unknowns are split into the six
non-magnetic components, in a modal Legendre $\mathbb P^N$ space, and
$(B_1,B_2)$, in the Li–Shu LDF space of vector polynomials with zero
divergence in each cell. After every SSP-RK3 stage an OE step damps the modal
coefficients exactly. For the six components it is component-wise (one
damping coefficient per component, applied by a Hadamard product). For
$(B_1,B_2)$ one coefficient per edge, the maximum over the two components,
scales whole LDF basis vectors, so the damped field stays divergence-free in
each cell.

For positivity the authors use the symmetrizable form with the
[Godunov–Powell source term](/definitions/godunov-powell-source-term.md)
$-(\nabla\cdot B)S(u)$. The discrete source is added only to the cell-average
equations, as an upwind, wave-speed-weighted split of the normal-field jump
at each interface; because the field is LDF, only interface jumps contribute.
The flux is a positivity-preserving [HLL flux](/methods/hll-riemann-solvers.md)
with the Wu–Shu wave-speed bounds. With the
[geometric quasilinearization](/methods/geometric-quasilinearization.md) of the
admissible set, a general convex decomposition of the cell average and new
two-state inequalities, Theorem 1 proves that the cell averages stay
admissible under an explicit CFL condition, provided the solution is
admissible at the decomposition nodes; a
[Zhang–Shu-type limiter](/methods/zhang-shu-scaling-limiter.md) enforces that.
Two corollaries give the CFL bound for the Zhang–Shu decomposition and for
the [optimal cell-average decomposition](/methods/optimal-cell-average-decomposition.md)
of [Cui et al. (2024)](/sources/cui-2024.md), which is milder and needs fewer
nodes ([bound preservation](/properties/bound-preservation.md)).

The tests use $N=2$, SSP-RK3, HLL and CFL 0.12: smooth sine waves in 1D and
2D, two Ryu–Jones tubes and the [Brio–Wu tube](/benchmarks/brio-wu-shock-tube.md),
an MHD [Leblanc problem](/benchmarks/leblanc-shock-tube.md) with plasma beta
$4\times10^{-8}$, the [Orszag–Tang vortex](/benchmarks/orszag-tang-vortex.md),
the rotor and the low-beta [blast wave](/benchmarks/mhd-blast-wave.md), the
[shock–cloud interaction](/benchmarks/mhd-shock-cloud-interaction.md) and
magnetized Mach 800 and 10000 [jets](/benchmarks/high-mach-astrophysical-jet.md).
Switching off the OE step, the limiter or the source makes the hardest tests
fail.

# Key claims

Registry notation: $N$ the DG degree (source $k$), $G$ the admissible set,
$S(u)=(0,B,v,v\cdot B)^\top$, $v_*$, $B_*$ the free auxiliary variables of the
GQL, $s^-\le0\le s^+$ the HLL speeds (source $V^-$, $V^+$), $\theta$ the
limiter factor, $\varpi$ the boundary weights of a decomposition, $\sigma^m$
the OEDG damping coefficients. Page-local: $\mathsf w_\mu$ the Gauss weights
on $[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$; $\hat{\mathsf w}_1=1/(L(L-1))$ the endpoint weight of
the $L$-point Gauss–Lobatto rule, $L=\lceil(N+3)/2\rceil$; $\delta^m_{ij}$ the
damping rate; $a^x_{ij}$, $a^y_{ij}$ the spectral radii at the cell average
(source $\beta^x$, $\beta^y$); $a_1$, $a_2$ the maximal enlarged speeds in the
corollaries; $\phi_\ell=a_\ell/\Delta x_\ell$, $\phi_*=\max\{\phi_1,\phi_2\}$,
$\delta_a=(\phi_1-\phi_2)/(\phi_1+\phi_2)$; $\rho e$ the internal energy
(source $\mathcal E$).

1. The LDF OE step removes spurious oscillations, keeps the field locally divergence-free and keeps conservation, local compactness and optimal convergence rates of DG; it runs as an independent module after each RK stage (Abstract, §1, Remarks 1–2). **Stated** (optimal order **Shown**, Tables 1–2)
2. The damping equations (16) and (27) are linear ODEs, because their coefficients are frozen at the stage solution, and are solved exactly mode by mode (Eqs. (23)–(25), (33)–(35)). **Derived**
3. The OE step leaves the cell average unchanged and, for $(B_1,B_2)$, the two constant modes (Eqs. (24), (34)). **Derived**
4. The OE step needs no characteristic decomposition, is scale- and evolution-invariant "as with the OE technique in [20]" and is stable under the normal CFL condition (Remarks 1–2). **Stated** (proofs referred to Peng et al.)
5. The LDF OE step keeps the LDF property, because each LDF basis function is multiplied by a scalar (Remark 2, Eq. (35)). **Derived**
6. Damping rate (17): $\delta^m_{ij}=a^x_{ij}\big(\sigma^m_{i+1/2,j}+\sigma^m_{i-1/2,j}\big)/\Delta x+a^y_{ij}\big(\sigma^m_{i,j+1/2}+\sigma^m_{i,j-1/2}\big)/\Delta y$, with $\sigma^m_{i+1/2,j}=\frac{(2m+1)\Delta x^m}{2(2N-1)\,m!}\sum_{\lvert\alpha\rvert=m}\frac{\frac1{\Delta y}\int\lvert[\![\partial^\alpha u^{(c)}_h]\!]\rvert\,dy}{\lVert u^{(c)}_h-\mathrm{avg}(u^{(c)}_h)\rVert_{L^\infty(\Omega)}}$ per component $c$, $\partial^\alpha$ weighted by $\lvert\alpha\rvert!/(\alpha_1!\alpha_2!)$; for $(B_1,B_2)$ the maximum over the two components (Eqs. (17)–(19), (28)–(29)). **Stated** (design)
7. Positivity of DG cell averages for the conservative MHD system is tied to globally coupled discrete divergence-free conditions that a local limiter cannot enforce; the Godunov–Powell source removes the effect of divergence errors on positivity (§3, citing Wu 2018 and Wu & Shu 2018, 2019). **Stated**
8. The source is discretized only in the cell-average equation (45); the higher moments evolve with the unmodified OEDG scheme, because weak positivity involves only the cell averages (§1, §3.2). **Stated** (design), justified by Theorem 1
9. For an LDF field the cell-interior divergence vanishes and the source reduces to interface jumps of the normal field, split between the two cells with weights $-s^-/(s^+-s^-)$ and $s^+/(s^+-s^-)$, which sum to 1 (Eqs. (46)–(49), Fig. 2). **Derived**
10. **Theorem 1** (§3.6): if the OE-damped, limited solution is LDF and admissible at all decomposition nodes (58), and the HLL speeds satisfy (62), then the cell-average update (59) gives $\bar u^{n+1}_{ij}\in G$ under the CFL condition (66), $\Delta t\le\min\{\Delta x\,\varpi^\pm_1/\alpha^\mu_{1,i\pm1/2},\ \Delta y\,\varpi^\pm_2/\alpha^\mu_{2,j\pm1/2}\}$, with speeds enlarged by $\lvert\mathscr B^\pm\rvert/\sqrt\rho$ of the split jumps. In the proof the $v_*\cdot B_*$ terms cancel because the quadrature divergence of the LDF field vanishes. **Proved**
11. Lemma 3 (a new two-state inequality for HLL-type combinations on rectangles) and Lemma 4 (the HLL intermediate-state decomposition) specialize Theorems 1–2 of Wu & Shu to two states; the authors say this simplifies and improves the general-mesh estimates on Cartesian meshes (§1, Remark 3). **Proved** (by citation)
12. **Corollary 1** (Zhang–Shu decomposition): $\Delta t\le\Delta x\Delta y\,\hat{\mathsf w}_1/(a_2\Delta x+a_1\Delta y)$. **Proved**
13. **Corollary 2** (optimal decomposition, $\mathbb P^2$ and $\mathbb P^3$): $\Delta t\le\Delta x\Delta y/\max\{6a_1\Delta y+2a_2\Delta x,\ 6a_2\Delta x+2a_1\Delta y\}$. **Proved**
14. Remark 3: for $\mathbb P^N$ the optimal-decomposition CFL is $\Delta t(a_1/\Delta x+a_2/\Delta y)\le\bar\varpi_\star(\delta_a,\mathbb P^N)$, with $\bar\varpi_\star=1/2$ for $\mathbb P^1$, $1/(4+2\lvert\delta_a\rvert)$ for $\mathbb P^2$, $\mathbb P^3$, and a closed form for $\mathbb P^4$, $\mathbb P^5$ (from Cui et al.). **Stated**
15. The optimal decomposition gives a milder positivity CFL and needs fewer nodes than the Zhang–Shu one (§1, §3.3). **Stated** *(compiler check, not in the sources: for $N=2$, $L=3$ and $\hat{\mathsf w}_1=1/6$, so Corollary 1 gives $\Delta t(\phi_1+\phi_2)\le1/6$; with $\phi_1\ge\phi_2$, $6\phi_1+2\phi_2=(\phi_1+\phi_2)(4+2\delta_a)$, so Corollary 2 gives $\Delta t(\phi_1+\phi_2)\le1/(4+2\lvert\delta_a\rvert)\ge1/6$, equal only at $\lvert\delta_a\rvert=1$)*
16. SSP time stepping is a convex combination of forward-Euler steps, so the positivity analysis carries over (§3.2, §3.6). **Stated**
17. The limiter (54)–(55) keeps the cell average and, being a scalar scaling, the LDF property, and does not reduce the accuracy of DG (§3.4). **Stated**
18. 1D sine wave ($N=2$): $L^1$ orders 3.18, 3.08, 3.04, 3.02, 3.01 from 100 to 3200 cells (Table 1). **Shown**
19. 2D sine wave: $L^1$ orders 2.92, 4.24, 4.61, 3.55, 3.19 from $15^2$ to $480^2$, which the authors describe as "consistently achieves third-order accuracy" (Table 2, §4.2.1). **Shown**
20. Shock tubes (800 cells, 4000-cell references): all discontinuities captured "without any noticeable spurious oscillations" (§4.1.2, Figs. 3–5). **Shown**
21. MHD Leblanc ($\beta_{\mathrm p}=4\times10^{-8}$, 2000 cells): no negative density or pressure (§4.1.3, Fig. 6). **Shown**
22. Orszag–Tang ($400^2$): no negative pressure at any time, whereas Jiang and Wu report negative pressure near $t\approx3.9$ (§4.2.2). **Shown**
23. Rotor ($200^2$, $400^2$): no distortion of the central Mach-number region, read by the authors as effective divergence control, and no overshoots in the $B_1$, $B_2$ slices, unlike the third-order finite-volume scheme of [Liu et al. (2021)](/sources/liu-2021.md) (§4.2.3, Figs. 9–11). **Shown**
24. Shock–cloud ($400^2$): without the LDF OE step the run fails; without the Godunov–Powell source or the limiter the cell-average pressure becomes negative (§4.2.4). **Shown** (stated outcome, no figure of the failures)
25. Jets: Mach 800 with $B_2=\sqrt{2000}$ and Mach 10000 with $B_2=\sqrt{20000}$, $200\times600$ cells, no negative density or pressure; disabling the OE step, the limiter or the upwind source makes "the code immediately fail" (§4.2.6, Figs. 14–15). **Shown**
26. The LDF OEDG method is "naturally extensible to general meshes"; unstructured meshes are future work (§1, §5). **Stated**

# Printed slips

- Table 1 (1D sine wave): the mesh column reads "100 × 100", …, "3200 × 3200" for a one-dimensional problem; the orders fit halving $h$ from 100 to 3200 cells *(compiler check, not in the sources: $\log_2(7.3204\times10^{-6}/8.0642\times10^{-7})=3.18$)*.
- Remark 3, last sentence: "our above PP CFL condition (2) is notably improved"; Eq. (2) is the divergence constraint, not a CFL condition; the CFL conditions of the paper are Eq. (66) and Corollaries 1–2.
- §3.2: "an 'un-wind' discretization" for upwind (printed "un- wind" across a line break).
- Lemma 3 writes $\lvert B^*\rvert^2$ where Lemmas 1, 4 and Theorem 1 write $\lVert B^*\rVert^2$.
- Symbol reuse: $\eta$ is a local coordinate and the LDF mode index; $\mu$ a Gauss-node, component and degree index; $\ell$ the direction and the RK stage; $\delta$ the anisotropy ratio and the damping rate.

# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Abgrall & Barsukow (2023) — Extensions of Active Flux to arbitrary order of accuracy](/sources/abgrall-2023.md) — Three routes to arbitrary-order Active Flux in one dimension (larger finite-difference stencils, extra interior point values, extra moments), each with linear stability analysis, transonic upwinding and order-reducing limiting, plus a proof that even orders are impossible with symmetric interior points.
* [Abgrall & Nassajian Mojarrad (2024) — An Arbitrarily High Order and Asymptotic Preserving Kinetic Scheme in Compressible Fluid Dynamic](/sources/abgrall-2024.md) — Extends to two space dimensions an arbitrarily high-order, computationally explicit, asymptotic-preserving discrete kinetic (BGK/relaxation) scheme: deferred-correction time stepping with an implicit but explicitly computable relaxation term, upwind finite differences in vertex-residual form, a flux limiter and a MOOD fallback, with a linear stability analysis allowing CFL numbers above one.
* [Abgrall et al. (2022) — Reinterpretation and extension of entropy correction terms for residual distribution and discontinuous Galerkin schemes: Application to structure preserving discretization](/sources/abgrall-2022.md) — Recasts Abgrall's entropy correction term as the explicit minimum-norm solution of an equality-constrained quadratic problem, transfers it to SBP–SAT discontinuous Galerkin with a mass-weighted variant, and extends it to entropy inequalities, several constraints, kinetic-energy preservation and fully discrete deferred-correction schemes.
* [Abgrall et al. (2026) — Bound-Preserving Point-Average-Moment PolynomiAl-Interpreted (PAMPA) Scheme: One-Dimensional Case](/sources/abgrall-2026.md) — A priori bound-preserving version of the third-order 1D PAMPA scheme: high- and low-order fluxes for cell averages and residuals for interface point values are blended with explicit optimal coefficients, and positivity for Euler follows from geometric quasilinearization with a closed-form eigenvalue minimisation.
* [Alberti et al. (2025) — Continued development of an entropy-aware high-order modal Discontinuous Galerkin solver for the Navier–Stokes equations](/sources/alberti-2025.md) — Modal DG for compressible Navier–Stokes with entropy-projected operators: compares five spreading terms of Abgrall's entropy correction, traces the entropy integration error to the rational pressure, and makes BR1 and BR2 viscous terms entropy stable by projecting the viscous fluxes onto the DG space.
* [Allegrini & Vignal (2025) — Study of a New Low-Oscillating Second-Order All-Mach Number IMEX Finite Volume Scheme for the Full Euler Equations](/sources/allegrini-2025.md) — Analyses IMEX flux splittings of the low-Mach-scaled full Euler equations for asymptotic stability, asymptotic consistency and contact preservation, and builds from the Toro–Vázquez-Cendón splitting a linear, decoupled all-Mach finite-volume scheme with implicit upwinding, a second-order ARS(2,2,2)–MUSCL version and a TVD blend.
* [Arun et al. (2022) — A unified asymptotic preserving and well-balanced scheme for the Euler system with multiscale relaxation](/sources/arun-2022.md) — First-order finite-volume scheme for the isentropic Euler equations with gravity and friction under a multiscale scaling: a semi-implicit step reformulated as an explicit hyperbolic-parabolic update, hydrostatic reconstruction in pressure and a balanced mass flux, asymptotic preserving for both the porous-medium and the transport limit and well-balanced for hydrostatic states.
* [Bader & Zhu (2025) — AFiD-MHD: A finite difference method for magnetohydrodynamic flows](/sources/bader-2025.md) — Code paper extending the staggered second-order finite-difference solver AFiD to incompressible Boussinesq MHD in a plane layer, with three exact discrete treatments of the solenoidal constraint: a staggered projection cleaning, a Lagrange-multiplier projection and a Coulomb-gauge vector potential.
* [Bai & Li (2022) — Simulating compressible two-phase flows with sharp-interface discontinuous Galerkin methods based on ghost fluid method and cut cell scheme](/sources/bai-2022.md) — Sharp-interface Runge–Kutta DG solver for gas–gas and gas–water flows with stiffened-gas fluids: level-set interface, cut cells updated by a high-order real–ghost mixing projection instead of cell merging, ghost-fluid states, an HLLC–HLL flux and quadtree AMR, validated on shock tubes, Richtmyer–Meshkov, shock–bubble and underwater-explosion problems.
* [Balsara et al. (2023) — Efficient WENO-Based Prolongation Strategies for Divergence-Preserving Vector Fields](/sources/balsara-2023.md) — High-order prolongation of face-collocated, divergence-constrained vector fields on AMR hierarchies: an analytically exact fourth-order divergence-preserving reconstruction, and WENO-ADPT, a face-matching WENO interpolation of orders 3–5 followed by a least-squares touch-up that restores the fine-zone constraint to round-off.
* [Balsara et al. (2024) — Efficient Finite Difference WENO Scheme for Hyperbolic Systems with Non-conservative Products](/sources/balsara-2024.md) — Extends point-value finite-difference WENO to hyperbolic systems with non-conservative products through a fluctuation form with one reconstruction per zone and an HLLI Riemann solver, which preserves stationary contacts exactly; design order to nine is shown for Euler, Baer–Nunziato, debris-flow and two-layer shallow-water systems.
* [Barsukow et al. (2026) — A generalized Active Flux method of arbitrarily high order in two dimensions](/sources/barsukow-2026.md) — Semi-discrete Active Flux of arbitrary order on 2D Cartesian grids, built as a hybrid finite element with shared node and edge point values plus moments in a serendipity-based minimal space; only Gauss edge points give a stable semi-discretization, and orders 3–7 converge on smooth tests.
* [Basile et al. (2022) — Unstructured h- and hp-adaptive strategies for discontinuous Galerkin methods based on a posteriori error estimation for compressible flows](/sources/basile-2022.md) — Isotropic metric-based h- and hp-adaptation for modal DG on unstructured and hybrid meshes: a combined modal-energy and interface-jump error estimator, a modal-decay smoothness indicator for the h/p choice, a size law with degree-dependent rates and a dof-growth-controlled target error, tested on steady laminar and RANS subsonic flows.
* [Başkaya et al. (2024) — Assessment of immersed boundary methods for hypersonic flows with gas–surface interactions](/sources/baskaya-2024.md) — Compares body-fitted, strictly conservative cut-cell and non-conservative ghost-cell immersed-boundary finite-volume solvers on eight reacting hypersonic benchmarks; ghost-cell methods fail on cold-wall heat flux and ablation blowing, which the authors attribute to wall conservation errors.
* [Bernardini et al. (2023) — STREAmS-2.0: Supersonic turbulent accelerated Navier-Stokes solver version 2.0](/sources/bernardini-2023.md) — Two-page CPC new-version announcement of STREAmS-2.0, a GPU (CUDA) and CPU finite-difference DNS code for compressible wall turbulence: object-oriented Fortran rewrite, KEEP split convective schemes with WENO-Z shock capturing, new inflow conditions, thermally perfect gas and grid stretching.
* [Berta et al. (2024) — A 4th-order accurate finite volume method for ideal classical and special relativistic MHD based on pointwise reconstructions](/sources/berta-2024.md) — Fourth-order constrained-transport finite-volume scheme for classical and relativistic MHD in PLUTO that reconstructs interface values from point values with pointwise WENO-Z, MP5 and WENO3, converts fluxes with Laplacian corrections, and reduces order near discontinuities with a new derivatives-ratio detector.
* [Bezgin et al. (2022) — WENO3-NN: A maximum-order three-point data-driven weighted essentially non-oscillatory scheme](/sources/bezgin-2022.md) — Third-order finite-volume WENO on the three-cell stencil whose two nonlinear weights come from a small neural network with invariant difference features, a test-time TENO-like cut-off and a training loss that penalizes deviation from the linear weights, which restores the design order lost by earlier learned schemes.
* [Bezgin et al. (2023) — JAX-Fluids: A fully-differentiable high-order computational fluid dynamics solver for compressible two-phase flows](/sources/bezgin-2023.md) — Software paper on JAX-Fluids, a Python/JAX Cartesian finite-volume solver for compressible single- and two-phase (sharp-interface level-set) flows that is differentiable end to end, with WENO/TENO reconstruction, Riemann solvers or characteristic flux splitting, validation tests, single-GPU performance and an end-to-end-trained Rusanov dissipation coefficient.
* [Bhoriya et al. (2023) — High-order finite-difference entropy stable schemes for two-fluid relativistic plasma flow equations](/sources/bhoriya-2023.md) — Semi-discrete entropy-stable finite-difference schemes of orders two to four for the two-fluid relativistic plasma equations: closed-form entropy-conservative fluxes for 3D-velocity relativistic hydrodynamics, TeCNO dissipation on ENO-reconstructed scaled entropy variables, entropy-neutral Lorentz sources, and ARK-IMEX stepping for the stiff sources.
* [Bielawski et al. (2023) — Highly-scalable GPU-accelerated compressible reacting flow solver for modeling high-speed flows](/sources/bielawski-2023.md) — GPU (CUDA) port of an unstructured second-order OpenFOAM finite-volume solver for compressible reacting flows: face loops with atomics, boundary-only host–device transfers, static and adaptive cell-batched chemistry integrators, scaling to 6144 V100 GPUs, detonation-engine and scramjet demonstrations, and a central/HLLC/AUSMDV dissipation comparison.
* [Boscarino et al. (2022) — High Order Semi-implicit WENO Schemes for All-Mach Full Euler System of Gas Dynamics](/sources/boscarino-2022.md) — An asymptotic-preserving and asymptotically accurate semi-implicit scheme for the full Euler equations at all Mach numbers: explicit material waves, implicit acoustic waves via one linear elliptic pressure equation per stage, new type-A stiffly accurate IMEX Runge–Kutta tableaux, and fifth-order finite-difference WENO.
* [Browne et al. (2022) — A nonlinear compressible flow disturbance formulation for adaptive mesh refinement wavepacket tracking in hypersonic boundary-layer flows](/sources/browne-2022.md) — Nonlinear disturbance equations for the compressible Navier–Stokes equations, discretized by fifth-order finite-difference WENO with a disturbance-form Rusanov splitting on a block-structured AMR mesh that tracks the wavepacket, validated on a Mach 9.81 cone and a Mach 5.35 flat plate.
* [Bryngelson et al. (2021) — MFC: An open-source high-order multi-component, multi-phase, and multi-scale compressible flow solver](/sources/bryngelson-2021.md) — Program description of MFC 1.0, an open-source Fortran/MPI finite-volume solver for diffuse-interface multi-component and bubbly compressible flows: WENO reconstruction of primitive or characteristic variables, HLLC fluxes, Gaussian face quadrature, five- and six-equation models, verification, validation and parallel scaling.
* [Cai et al. (2024) — Provably convergent Newton–Raphson methods for recovering primitive variables with applications to physical-constraint-preserving Hermite WENO schemes for relativistic hydrodynamics](/sources/cai-2024.md) — Three Newton–Raphson pressure recoveries for special relativistic hydrodynamics, proved positivity-preserving and quadratically convergent for every admissible state, and their use in physical-constraint-preserving finite-volume Hermite WENO schemes in 1D, 2D and axisymmetric coordinates with a convex-decomposition proof.
* [Calhoun et al. (2023) — The Cartesian Grid Active Flux Method with Adaptive Mesh Refinement](/sources/calhoun-2023.md) — First implementation of the third-order Cartesian Active Flux method on quadtree patch-based adaptive meshes in ForestClaw: conservative coarse–fine transfer of averages and point values, subcycling without time interpolation, refluxing, and constant-state-preserving variants for variable-velocity transport, with third-order convergence on AMR grids.
* [Cernetic et al. (2023) — High-order discontinuous Galerkin hydrodynamics with sub-cell shock capturing on GPUs](/sources/cernetic-2023.md) — TENET-GPU, a modal DG code for the compressible Euler and Navier–Stokes equations on Cartesian meshes in C++/CUDA, with viscous interface fluxes by local L2 recovery and limiter-free shock capturing by a time-evolved, SPH-style artificial-viscosity field; shock widths scale as h/p and GPU nodes run about 8 times faster than CPU nodes.
* [Chamarthi (2023) — Efficient high-order gradient-based reconstruction for compressible flows](/sources/chamarthi-2023-b.md) — Raises gradient-based reconstruction to a seventh-order upwind scheme by two free parameters in the second derivative, limits it with a monotonicity-preserving limiter (MEG7), and restores high order on nonlinear problems by switching between flux and state reconstruction with a density-based discontinuity detector.
* [Chamarthi (2023) — Gradient based reconstruction: Inviscid and viscous flux discretizations, shock capturing, and its application to single and multicomponent flows](/sources/chamarthi-2023.md) — Point-value finite-difference scheme that computes high-order cell-centre gradients once and reuses them in an α-damping viscous flux, a κ = 1/3 gradient-based interface reconstruction and the curvature of a monotonicity-preserving limiter (MEG6, MIG4), tested against TENO5 on single- and two-species flows.
* [Chandravamsi et al. (2023) — On the application of gradient based reconstruction for flow simulations on generalized curvilinear and dynamic mesh domains](/sources/chandravamsi-2023.md) — Extends the gradient-based MEG6 and MIG4 schemes and the α-damping viscous flux to 3D curvilinear, multi-block and deforming meshes, with a free-stream-preserving metric recipe and its discrete proof, an implicit LES of a screeching Mach 1.35 jet against WENO-Z, and an OpenACC single-GPU port.
* [Charles & Ray (2025) — Learning WENO for Entropy Stable Schemes to Solve Conservation Laws](/sources/charles-2025.md) — DSP-WENO: a third-order sign-preserving WENO reconstruction for entropy-stable TeCNO schemes whose weight perturbations are convex combinations, chosen by a small network, of the vertices of a hand-built feasible polygon, so that consistency, the sign property and third order hold by construction.
* [Chen & Chen (2026) — High-order positivity-preserving and dissipation-adaptive computational framework with sharp-interface immersed boundaries for high-Mach-number shock-obstacle interactions](/sources/chen-2026.md) — Finite-difference solver coupling adaptive-cut-off TENO5, the Hu–Adams–Shu positivity-preserving flux limiter at every SSPRK(5,3) stage and a ghost-cell immersed-boundary method whose interpolations are convex combinations, with the TENO cut-off raised on stencils that touch ghost points; tested on supersonic and hypersonic flows past cylinders and wedges.
* [Chen & Deng (2023) — Nonlinear weights for shock capturing schemes with unconditionally optimal high order](/sources/chen-2023.md) — Exponential-mapping nonlinear weights for fifth-order point-value interpolation that keep fifth order at critical points of any order using only local stencil data, tested inside a staggered weighted compact nonlinear scheme (WCNS-EXP) against WCNS-JS, -Z and -MR on one- and two-dimensional Euler benchmarks.
* [Chen & Fu (2024) — Efficient dimension-by-dimension adaptive TENO-based limiter and troubled-cell indicator for nodal-based high-order spectral difference method](/sources/chen-2024.md) — Compact dimension-by-dimension limiter for third- and fourth-order nodal spectral difference schemes, a direct convex combination of Hermite quadratic and cubic candidates with WENO or adaptive-TENO weights, plus a TENO-based troubled-cell indicator with per-direction flags, compared with KXRCF on Euler benchmarks.
* [Chen & Wu (2022) — A physical-constraint-preserving finite volume WENO method for special relativistic hydrodynamics on unstructured meshes](/sources/chen-2022-b.md) — Third-order finite-volume WENO scheme on triangles for special relativistic hydrodynamics, proved physical-constraint-preserving through geometric quasilinearization and an HLL flux, with scale-invariant Zhu–Qiu weights that make the scheme homogeneous and three provably convergent pressure-recovery root finders.
* [Chen et al. (2022) — A vertex-based reconstruction for cell-centered finite-volume discretization on unstructured grids](/sources/chen-2022.md) — Extends the vertex-based weighted-least-squares gradient (VWLSQ) of second-order cell-centered unstructured finite volumes with vertex-averaged face gradients for viscous fluxes, which avoid odd–even decoupling, and with a WENO-type nonlinear weighting of vertex gradients that replaces slope limiters for shock capturing.
* [Chen et al. (2023) — High order asymptotic preserving finite difference WENO schemes with constrained transport for MHD equations in all sonic Mach numbers](/sources/chen-2023-b.md) — Extends the semi-implicit IMEX Runge–Kutta finite-difference WENO all-Mach framework to ideal MHD with vector-potential constrained transport: one linear elliptic pressure equation per stage, a capped Lax–Friedrichs speed, formal proofs of asymptotic preservation and asymptotic accuracy, and tests from sonic Mach 1 to 0.
* [Chertock et al. (2023) — Local characteristic decomposition based central-upwind scheme](/sources/chertock-2023.md) — Rewrites the second-order central-upwind flux as a central average plus diffusion in local characteristic variables, then gives each characteristic field its own one-sided speeds; the resulting LCD central-upwind scheme is upwind for any linear system and much less dissipative on 1D and 2D Euler tests.
* [Ching et al. (2025) — Positivity-preserving and entropy-bounded discontinuous Galerkin method for the chemically reacting, compressible Navier-Stokes equations](/sources/ching-2025.md) — Extends a positivity-preserving, entropy-bounded DG method to multicomponent reacting Navier–Stokes flow: a Lax–Friedrichs-type penalty applied to the viscous flux alone with a separate HLLC inviscid flux, a split cell-average update with the entropy bound on the convective half only, curved elements and a gradient fix for zero concentrations.
* [Chu et al. (2022) — Fifth-order A-WENO schemes based on the path-conservative central-upwind method](/sources/chu-2022.md) — Extends the second-order path-conservative central-upwind scheme for non-conservative hyperbolic systems to fifth order in the A-WENO finite-difference framework through a global-flux formulation and a new five-point quadrature for cell integrals, and applies it, well-balanced, to the one- and two-dimensional two-layer shallow water equations.
* [Chu et al. (2025) — New adaptive low-dissipation central-upwind schemes](/sources/chu-2025.md) — Second-order low-dissipation central-upwind schemes whose characteristic-variable slope limiter switches between an overcompressive SBM limiter in rough cells and Minmod2 elsewhere, driven by a minmod-based shock indicator or a weak-local-residual indicator; tested on 1D and 2D Euler problems.
* [Chu et al. (2025) — New Low-Dissipation Central-Upwind Schemes. Part II](/sources/chu-2025-c.md) — Modifies the projection step of the Kurganov–Xin low-dissipation central-upwind schemes so that the subcell jump in the Riemann fan moves with the intermediate velocity; the new built-in anti-diffusion removes the oscillations of the original schemes, notably near domain boundaries, on 1D and 2D Euler tests.
* [Chu et al. (2025) — Novel local characteristic decomposition based path-conservative central-upwind schemes](/sources/chu-2025-b.md) — Extends the local-characteristic-decomposition central-upwind flux to nonconservative balance laws by applying it to the flux-globalized quasi-conservative form, giving well-balanced LCD-PCCU schemes in 1D and 2D that are less dissipative than PCCU on multifluid and thermal rotating shallow-water tests.
* [Chudzik et al. (2024) — Active Flux Methods for Hyperbolic Systems Using the Method of Bicharacteristics](/sources/chudzik-2024.md) — Third-order Cartesian Active Flux methods whose point values are updated by bicharacteristic (evolution-Galerkin) operators: Maxwell via two acoustic systems, EG2 acoustics with a proved order and eigenvalue stability limits, the first unsplit linearised-Euler Active Flux, and a nonlinear Euler extension with iterated local linearisation.
* [Ciallella et al. (2023) — Arbitrary High Order WENO Finite Volume Scheme with Flux Globalization for Moving Equilibria Preservation](/sources/ciallella-2023.md) — Arbitrary-order WENO finite-volume scheme for the shallow-water equations with bathymetry and Manning friction, built on flux globalization: Gauss-quadrature global-flux averages, WENO reconstruction of the global flux and a Roe upwind flux; exact for constant discrete global flux, exactly lake-at-rest preserving, with deferred-correction time stepping.
* [Ciallella et al. (2024) — Very high order treatment of embedded curved boundaries in compressible flows: ADER discontinuous Galerkin with a space-time Reconstruction for Off-site data](/sources/ciallella-2024.md) — Transfers the Reconstruction for Off-site Data (ROD) boundary treatment to modal ADER-DG on triangles, in a space-only and a space-time variant whose constrained least-squares system has the identity as normal matrix, recovering high order on linear conformal and fully embedded meshes of curved domains.
* [Cicchino & Nadarajah (2025) — Discretely nonlinearly stable weight-adjusted flux reconstruction high-order method for compressible flows on curvilinear grids](/sources/cicchino-2025.md) — Builds nonlinearly stable flux reconstruction for 3D Euler on curvilinear hexahedra with a sum-factorized weight-adjusted inverse of the FR mass matrix, proves free-stream preservation, conservation and entropy conservation, and proves that an uncollocated scheme cannot be exactly kinetic-energy preserving unless surface nodes are volume nodes.
* [Cicchino et al. (2022) — Nonlinearly stable flux reconstruction high-order methods in split form](/sources/cicchino-2022.md) — Shows that energy-stable flux reconstruction in split form admits no general nonlinear stability proof when the correction filter acts only on surface terms, and builds a split form that applies the filter to the volume terms as well, proved conservative and energy stable for Burgers' equation on modal, uncollocated or overintegrated bases.
* [Coppola & Veldman (2023) — Global and local conservation of mass, momentum and kinetic energy in the simulation of compressible flow](/sources/coppola-2023.md) — Matrix framework for convective semi-discretizations proving that global conservation implies local conservation, deriving duality relations and a skew-symmetry criterion for kinetic-energy preservation that extend split forms to non-uniform grids and biased operators, and mapping finite-difference split forms onto finite-volume fluxes.
* [Crin-Barat & Manea (2026) — Asymptotic-preserving finite difference method for partially dissipative hyperbolic systems](/sources/crinbarat-2026.md) — Proves that the second-order central-difference semi-discretization of linear partially dissipative hyperbolic systems keeps, uniformly in the mesh width, the hypocoercive decay rate under the Kalman rank condition and the diffusive relaxation limit of damped linearized Euler at rate O(epsilon^2), using a discrete Littlewood–Paley decomposition adapted to the difference operator.
* [Cui et al. (2024) — On Optimal Cell Average Decomposition for High-Order Bound-Preserving Schemes of Hyperbolic Conservation Laws](/sources/cui-2024.md) — Poses and solves the optimal cell average decomposition problem behind the Zhang–Shu bound-preserving framework: proves the classic decomposition optimal in 1D and for 2D Q^N but not for 2D P^N, gives optimal decompositions and larger bound-preserving CFL numbers for P^N.
* [Danis et al. (2025) — Tensor-train WENO scheme for compressible flows](/sources/danis-2025.md) — Evolves a finite-difference WENO5-JS solver for 3D Euler entirely in tensor-train format: shift operators on single cores, cross interpolation for the Lax–Friedrichs splitting and the WENO reconstruction, rounded SSP-RK3 and a mesh-dependent truncation tolerance; fifth order is kept and low-rank problems run up to two or three orders of magnitude faster.
* [Dao & Nazarov (2022) — A High-Order Residual-Based Viscosity Finite Element Method for the Ideal MHD Equations](/sources/dao-2022.md) — Explicit high-order continuous Lagrange finite elements for ideal MHD stabilized by a residual-based artificial viscosity, with a mass-diffusive viscous regularization and a strictly positive residual normalization, combined with projection, pseudo-time or GLM divergence cleaning and tested on smooth, Brio–Wu, Orszag–Tang and rotor problems.
* [De Michele & Coppola (2023) — Asymptotically entropy-conservative and kinetic-energy preserving numerical fluxes for compressible Euler equations](/sources/demichele-2023-b.md) — Short note deriving purely algebraic two-point fluxes for the Euler equations that preserve kinetic energy and pressure equilibrium and approach entropy conservation as a truncation order grows (AEC fluxes), by expanding the logarithmic means of Ranocha's flux; tested on a density wave and the inviscid Taylor–Green vortex.
* [De Michele & Coppola (2023) — Numerical treatment of the energy equation in compressible flows simulations](/sources/demichele-2023.md) — Analysis of which energy-like equation (total or internal energy, pressure, total enthalpy, sound speed, entropy) to discretize alongside kinetic-energy-preserving central mass and momentum fluxes: induced discrete equations, local conservation, pressure-equilibrium conditions, six new formulations, and robustness tests on the isentropic and Taylor–Green vortices.
* [de Romémont et al. (2026) — A data-driven learned discretization approach in finite volume schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws and varying boundary conditions](/sources/deromemont-2026.md) — Second-order MUSCL finite-volume scheme whose slopes are three-point stencils with coefficients predicted by a convolutional network, kept under the van Albada limiter and trained against decimated fine-grid solutions with entropy and total-variation penalties; boundary types enter through padding, slip walls through transfer learning.
* [De Vanna et al. (2023) — Effect of convective schemes in wall-resolved and wall-modeled LES of compressible wall turbulence](/sources/devanna-2023.md) — Parametric study, in the URANOS solver, of how WENO-Z (orders 3, 5, 7), TENO5, TENO-A5, a sixth-order energy-preserving central scheme and their Ducros-switched hybrids change wall-resolved and wall-modelled LES of Mach 2 and Mach 5.86 turbulent boundary layers; recommends a hybridization threshold of about 0.2.
* [De Vanna et al. (2023) — On the coupling between wall-modeled LES and immersed boundary method towards applicative compressible flow simulations](/sources/devanna-2023-c.md) — Couples an equilibrium wall model, imposed through an effective ghost-point viscosity and conductivity, with a sharp-interface ghost-point immersed boundary method in the GPU solver URANOS, switching pointwise between wall-resolved and wall-modelled treatment; validated on channel, pipe, Mach 2 boundary layer and Mach 2.91 compression ramp.
* [De Vanna et al. (2023) — URANOS: A GPU accelerated Navier-Stokes solver for compressible wall-bounded flows](/sources/devanna-2023-b.md) — Software paper on URANOS, a Fortran/OpenACC/MPI high-order finite-difference solver for compressible wall-bounded flows: energy-preserving central scheme hybridized with WENO-Z, TENO or TENO-A by a priori shock sensors, conservative viscous fluxes, LES and wall models, GPU porting and scaling, and channel and boundary-layer validation.
* [Del Grosso et al. (2024) — A well-balanced, positive, entropy-stable, and multi-dimensional-aware finite volume scheme for 2D shallow-water equations with unstructured grids](/sources/delgrosso-2024.md) — Cell-centred finite-volume scheme for 2D shallow water on polygonal grids whose subface fluxes come from a Lagrangian simple Riemann solver closed by a nodal velocity solver: node-wise conservative, positive, entropy-stable and lake-at-rest preserving, with a second-order extension and no carbuncle where the two-point version shows it.
* [Dematté et al. (2024) — An all Mach number scheme for visco-resistive magnetically-dominated MHD flows](/sources/dematte-2024.md) — Cell-centred semi-implicit finite-volume scheme for ideal and visco-resistive MHD: an MHD flux-vector splitting with an explicit advective part and an implicit pressure part, solved by nested Picard iterations and GMRES, with staggered or vector-potential constrained transport and a time step set by the flow speed alone.
* [Deng (2023) — A unified framework for non-linear reconstruction schemes in a compact stencil. Part 1: Beyond second order](/sources/deng-2023.md) — Recasts three-cell compact finite-volume reconstructions (linear, TVD, NVD, WENO3, THINC, LDLR, RBF) as functions of one normalised variable, proves accuracy and scale-invariance criteria, builds the Unified Normalised-variable Diagram and designs a new TVD limiter, a scale-invariant WENO3 and the ROUND schemes.
* [Deng et al. (2025) — Efficient ROUND schemes on non-uniform grids applied to discontinuous Galerkin schemes with Godunov-type finite volume sub-cell limiting](/sources/deng-2025.md) — Extends the unified normalised-variable diagram and the ROUND reconstruction to arbitrary non-uniform grids, with a grid-dependent accuracy point and dissipation regions, and uses it as the finite-volume scheme on Gauss-weight subcells of a hard-switching DG subcell limiter driven by a BVD-type troubled-element indicator.
* [Ding & Wu (2024) — A New Discretely Divergence-Free Positivity-Preserving High-Order Finite Volume Method for Ideal MHD Equations](/sources/ding-2024.md) — High-order finite-volume WENO scheme for 2D ideal MHD that enforces a quadrature-based discrete divergence-free condition by a minimal-norm projection of interface point values, discretizes the Godunov–Powell source by face jumps, and is proved positivity-preserving through geometric quasilinearization.
* [Ding & Wu (2024) — GQL-based bound-preserving and locally divergence-free central discontinuous Galerkin schemes for relativistic magnetohydrodynamics](/sources/ding-2024-b.md) — Arbitrary-order central DG schemes on overlapping meshes for special relativistic MHD with a general equation of state, proved bound-preserving through geometric quasilinearization; in 2D locally divergence-free spaces and a discretized symmetrization source cancel the divergence error, and the optimal cell-average decomposition is extended to overlapping meshes.
* [Du & Yang (2022) — High-order bound-preserving discontinuous Galerkin methods for multicomponent chemically reacting flows](/sources/du-2022-b.md) — Bound-preserving local DG with a positivity-preserving Lax–Friedrichs total flux and a conservative modified exponential Runge–Kutta stepper for reacting multi-species Navier–Stokes, keeping density, pressure and mass fractions in [0,1] by preserving the sum of mass fractions exactly.

# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Abgrall & Barsukow (2023) — Extensions of Active Flux to arbitrary order of accuracy](/sources/abgrall-2023.md) — Three routes to arbitrary-order Active Flux in one dimension (larger finite-difference stencils, extra interior point values, extra moments), each with linear stability analysis, transonic upwinding and order-reducing limiting, plus a proof that even orders are impossible with symmetric interior points.
* [Abgrall & Nassajian Mojarrad (2024) — An Arbitrarily High Order and Asymptotic Preserving Kinetic Scheme in Compressible Fluid Dynamic](/sources/abgrall-2024.md) — Extends to two space dimensions an arbitrarily high-order, computationally explicit, asymptotic-preserving discrete kinetic (BGK/relaxation) scheme: deferred-correction time stepping with an implicit but explicitly computable relaxation term, upwind finite differences in vertex-residual form, a flux limiter and a MOOD fallback, with a linear stability analysis allowing CFL numbers above one.
* [Abgrall et al. (2022) — Reinterpretation and extension of entropy correction terms for residual distribution and discontinuous Galerkin schemes: Application to structure preserving discretization](/sources/abgrall-2022.md) — Recasts Abgrall's entropy correction term as the explicit minimum-norm solution of an equality-constrained quadratic problem, transfers it to SBP–SAT discontinuous Galerkin with a mass-weighted variant, and extends it to entropy inequalities, several constraints, kinetic-energy preservation and fully discrete deferred-correction schemes.
* [Arun et al. (2022) — A unified asymptotic preserving and well-balanced scheme for the Euler system with multiscale relaxation](/sources/arun-2022.md) — First-order finite-volume scheme for the isentropic Euler equations with gravity and friction under a multiscale scaling: a semi-implicit step reformulated as an explicit hyperbolic-parabolic update, hydrostatic reconstruction in pressure and a balanced mass flux, asymptotic preserving for both the porous-medium and the transport limit and well-balanced for hydrostatic states.
* [Bai & Li (2022) — Simulating compressible two-phase flows with sharp-interface discontinuous Galerkin methods based on ghost fluid method and cut cell scheme](/sources/bai-2022.md) — Sharp-interface Runge–Kutta DG solver for gas–gas and gas–water flows with stiffened-gas fluids: level-set interface, cut cells updated by a high-order real–ghost mixing projection instead of cell merging, ghost-fluid states, an HLLC–HLL flux and quadtree AMR, validated on shock tubes, Richtmyer–Meshkov, shock–bubble and underwater-explosion problems.
* [Balsara et al. (2023) — Efficient WENO-Based Prolongation Strategies for Divergence-Preserving Vector Fields](/sources/balsara-2023.md) — High-order prolongation of face-collocated, divergence-constrained vector fields on AMR hierarchies: an analytically exact fourth-order divergence-preserving reconstruction, and WENO-ADPT, a face-matching WENO interpolation of orders 3–5 followed by a least-squares touch-up that restores the fine-zone constraint to round-off.
* [Balsara et al. (2024) — Efficient Finite Difference WENO Scheme for Hyperbolic Systems with Non-conservative Products](/sources/balsara-2024.md) — Extends point-value finite-difference WENO to hyperbolic systems with non-conservative products through a fluctuation form with one reconstruction per zone and an HLLI Riemann solver, which preserves stationary contacts exactly; design order to nine is shown for Euler, Baer–Nunziato, debris-flow and two-layer shallow-water systems.
* [Basile et al. (2022) — Unstructured h- and hp-adaptive strategies for discontinuous Galerkin methods based on a posteriori error estimation for compressible flows](/sources/basile-2022.md) — Isotropic metric-based h- and hp-adaptation for modal DG on unstructured and hybrid meshes: a combined modal-energy and interface-jump error estimator, a modal-decay smoothness indicator for the h/p choice, a size law with degree-dependent rates and a dof-growth-controlled target error, tested on steady laminar and RANS subsonic flows.
* [Bezgin et al. (2022) — WENO3-NN: A maximum-order three-point data-driven weighted essentially non-oscillatory scheme](/sources/bezgin-2022.md) — Third-order finite-volume WENO on the three-cell stencil whose two nonlinear weights come from a small neural network with invariant difference features, a test-time TENO-like cut-off and a training loss that penalizes deviation from the linear weights, which restores the design order lost by earlier learned schemes.
* [Boscarino et al. (2022) — High Order Semi-implicit WENO Schemes for All-Mach Full Euler System of Gas Dynamics](/sources/boscarino-2022.md) — An asymptotic-preserving and asymptotically accurate semi-implicit scheme for the full Euler equations at all Mach numbers: explicit material waves, implicit acoustic waves via one linear elliptic pressure equation per stage, new type-A stiffly accurate IMEX Runge–Kutta tableaux, and fifth-order finite-difference WENO.
* [Browne et al. (2022) — A nonlinear compressible flow disturbance formulation for adaptive mesh refinement wavepacket tracking in hypersonic boundary-layer flows](/sources/browne-2022.md) — Nonlinear disturbance equations for the compressible Navier–Stokes equations, discretized by fifth-order finite-difference WENO with a disturbance-form Rusanov splitting on a block-structured AMR mesh that tracks the wavepacket, validated on a Mach 9.81 cone and a Mach 5.35 flat plate.
* [Bryngelson et al. (2021) — MFC: An open-source high-order multi-component, multi-phase, and multi-scale compressible flow solver](/sources/bryngelson-2021.md) — Program description of MFC 1.0, an open-source Fortran/MPI finite-volume solver for diffuse-interface multi-component and bubbly compressible flows: WENO reconstruction of primitive or characteristic variables, HLLC fluxes, Gaussian face quadrature, five- and six-equation models, verification, validation and parallel scaling.
* [Chen & Wu (2022) — A physical-constraint-preserving finite volume WENO method for special relativistic hydrodynamics on unstructured meshes](/sources/chen-2022-b.md) — Third-order finite-volume WENO scheme on triangles for special relativistic hydrodynamics, proved physical-constraint-preserving through geometric quasilinearization and an HLL flux, with scale-invariant Zhu–Qiu weights that make the scheme homogeneous and three provably convergent pressure-recovery root finders.
* [Chen et al. (2022) — A vertex-based reconstruction for cell-centered finite-volume discretization on unstructured grids](/sources/chen-2022.md) — Extends the vertex-based weighted-least-squares gradient (VWLSQ) of second-order cell-centered unstructured finite volumes with vertex-averaged face gradients for viscous fluxes, which avoid odd–even decoupling, and with a WENO-type nonlinear weighting of vertex gradients that replaces slope limiters for shock capturing.
* [Chertock et al. (2023) — Local characteristic decomposition based central-upwind scheme](/sources/chertock-2023.md) — Rewrites the second-order central-upwind flux as a central average plus diffusion in local characteristic variables, then gives each characteristic field its own one-sided speeds; the resulting LCD central-upwind scheme is upwind for any linear system and much less dissipative on 1D and 2D Euler tests.
* [Chu et al. (2022) — Fifth-order A-WENO schemes based on the path-conservative central-upwind method](/sources/chu-2022.md) — Extends the second-order path-conservative central-upwind scheme for non-conservative hyperbolic systems to fifth order in the A-WENO finite-difference framework through a global-flux formulation and a new five-point quadrature for cell integrals, and applies it, well-balanced, to the one- and two-dimensional two-layer shallow water equations.
* [Cicchino et al. (2022) — Nonlinearly stable flux reconstruction high-order methods in split form](/sources/cicchino-2022.md) — Shows that energy-stable flux reconstruction in split form admits no general nonlinear stability proof when the correction filter acts only on surface terms, and builds a split form that applies the filter to the volume terms as well, proved conservative and energy stable for Burgers' equation on modal, uncollocated or overintegrated bases.
* [Coppola & Veldman (2023) — Global and local conservation of mass, momentum and kinetic energy in the simulation of compressible flow](/sources/coppola-2023.md) — Matrix framework for convective semi-discretizations proving that global conservation implies local conservation, deriving duality relations and a skew-symmetry criterion for kinetic-energy preservation that extend split forms to non-uniform grids and biased operators, and mapping finite-difference split forms onto finite-volume fluxes.
* [Cui et al. (2024) — On Optimal Cell Average Decomposition for High-Order Bound-Preserving Schemes of Hyperbolic Conservation Laws](/sources/cui-2024.md) — Poses and solves the optimal cell average decomposition problem behind the Zhang–Shu bound-preserving framework: proves the classic decomposition optimal in 1D and for 2D Q^N but not for 2D P^N, gives optimal decompositions and larger bound-preserving CFL numbers for P^N.
* [Dao & Nazarov (2022) — A High-Order Residual-Based Viscosity Finite Element Method for the Ideal MHD Equations](/sources/dao-2022.md) — Explicit high-order continuous Lagrange finite elements for ideal MHD stabilized by a residual-based artificial viscosity, with a mass-diffusive viscous regularization and a strictly positive residual normalization, combined with projection, pseudo-time or GLM divergence cleaning and tested on smooth, Brio–Wu, Orszag–Tang and rotor problems.
* [Deng (2023) — A unified framework for non-linear reconstruction schemes in a compact stencil. Part 1: Beyond second order](/sources/deng-2023.md) — Recasts three-cell compact finite-volume reconstructions (linear, TVD, NVD, WENO3, THINC, LDLR, RBF) as functions of one normalised variable, proves accuracy and scale-invariance criteria, builds the Unified Normalised-variable Diagram and designs a new TVD limiter, a scale-invariant WENO3 and the ROUND schemes.
* [Du & Yang (2022) — High-order bound-preserving discontinuous Galerkin methods for multicomponent chemically reacting flows](/sources/du-2022-b.md) — Bound-preserving local DG with a positivity-preserving Lax–Friedrichs total flux and a conservative modified exponential Runge–Kutta stepper for reacting multi-species Navier–Stokes, keeping density, pressure and mass fractions in [0,1] by preserving the sum of mass fractions exactly.
* [Du et al. (2022) — An improved simple WENO limiter for discontinuous Galerkin methods solving hyperbolic systems on unstructured meshes](/sources/du-2022.md) — Characteristic-wise simple WENO limiter for Runge–Kutta DG on triangles and on polygonal meshes from point clouds that transforms and combines only two polynomials per direction, keeping accuracy under heavy over-flagging and saving roughly 6–55% CPU on shocked Euler problems.
* [Duan & Tang (2022) — High-order accurate entropy stable adaptive moving mesh finite difference schemes for special relativistic (magneto)hydrodynamics](/sources/duan-2022.md) — Sixth-order entropy-conservative and fifth-order entropy-stable finite-difference schemes on adaptive moving curvilinear meshes for special-relativistic hydrodynamics and MHD, with discrete geometric conservation laws, free-stream preservation and WENO-based TeCNO dissipation with a sign switch.
* [Dzanic & Witherden (2022) — Positivity-preserving entropy-based adaptive filtering for discontinuous spectral element methods](/sources/dzanic-2022-b.md) — Entropy filtering: an element-wise exponential modal filter for nodal DG and flux reconstruction whose strength is the smallest that restores positivity and a local discrete minimum entropy principle at the solution nodes, found by bisection, implemented in PyFR and tested from shock tubes to a transonic delta-wing LES.
* [Dzanic et al. (2022) — Utilizing time-reversibility for shock capturing in nonlinear hyperbolic conservation laws](/sources/dzanic-2022.md) — Artificial-viscosity shock capturing whose indicator is the defect of a forward step followed by a time-reversed step: a vector-valued viscosity, one per conserved component, that needs no entropy pair and is O(h) at shocks, tested in nodal DG/flux reconstruction (PyFR) on Euler and MHD problems.
* [Edoh (2022) — A new kinetic-energy-preserving method based on the convective rotational form](/sources/edoh-2022.md) — Div-rot, a momentum-conserving split convective form that reduces to the rotational form for solenoidal velocity, with SBP proofs of kinetic-energy and helicity preservation, a proof that EMAC is not discretely KEP, new one-parameter KEP families, and a square-root-density compressible extension.
* [Fan et al. (2021) — Positivity-preserving high order finite volume hybrid Hermite WENO schemes for compressible Navier-Stokes equations](/sources/fan-2021.md) — Positivity-preserving finite-volume hybrid Hermite WENO for compressible Navier–Stokes: Zhang's Lax–Friedrichs-type total flux and a density-then-internal-energy scaling limiter on point values, with sufficient conditions and CFL proved in 1D and 2D, a derivative reconstruction for the viscous terms, and large cost savings from hybridization.
* [Fan et al. (2022) — Positivity-preserving high order finite difference WENO schemes for compressible Navier-Stokes equations](/sources/fan-2022.md) — Conservative finite-difference WENO for compressible Navier–Stokes that keeps density and internal energy positive: a Lax–Friedrichs-type splitting of the total flux with Zhang's viscous coefficient, a Gauss–Lobatto sufficient-condition theorem, a scaling limiter and halve-and-restart SSP-RK3 stepping.
* [Fan et al. (2023) — A robust fifth order finite difference Hermite WENO scheme for compressible Euler equations](/sources/fan-2023.md) — Fifth-order finite-difference Hermite WENO scheme (HWENO-R) that drops the target-point derivative from the flux reconstruction, uses a Hermite quartic plus two linear candidates with WENO-ZQ-type weights, and limits the derivative variables only in the Runge–Kutta stage values; it runs Sedov, Leblanc and double-rarefaction tests without positivity limiters.
* [Fu (2021) — Very-high-order TENO schemes with adaptive accuracy order and adaptive dissipation control](/sources/fu-2021.md) — Proposes TENO-AA: nested symmetric large stencils, tested largest-first against the three TENO5 stencils, supply the whole flux, with fallback to cut-off TENO5 and a sensor-driven cut-off threshold; explicit TENO8-AA and TENO10-AA, finite-difference Euler tests.
* [Fu (2023) — Review of the High-Order TENO Schemes for Compressible Gas Dynamics and Turbulence](/sources/fu-2023.md) — Single-author review of the targeted ENO (TENO) family: incremental-width stencils, strong scale separation and sharp cut-off, spectral optimization, TENO-N, hybrid, THINC, finite-volume, compact and unstructured variants, adaptive dissipation (TENO-A) as implicit LES, and applications and codes.
* [Hiejima (2022) — A high-order weighted compact nonlinear scheme for compressible flows](/sources/hiejima-2022.md) — Explicit WCNS-type interpolation of primitive variables on TENO-like pyramids of substencils, at fifth to eleventh interpolation order with Jiang–Shu or TENO weights, feeding an AUSMDV flux with a two-point flux difference; Sod, Lax, Shu–Osher and double Mach reflection tests.
* [Hong et al. (2022) — A Modified TENO Scheme with Improved Efficiency](/sources/hong-2022.md) — TENO-M: a cheaper targeted ENO scheme that runs TENO5 as a discontinuity detector and enlarges the target stencil point by point with three-point stencils and a relative-difference test, with full six- and eight-point target tables, spectral analysis and timings.
* [Hu et al. (2022) — A shock-stable HLLEM scheme with improved contact resolving capability for compressible Euler flows](/sources/hu-2022.md) — Traces the carbuncle of the HLLEM solver to undamped transverse density and shear-velocity perturbations, by saw-tooth linear analysis and matrix stability analysis, and cures it with a parameter-free pressure-ratio switch on both anti-diffusion terms (RHLLEM), with a THINC–MUSCL BVD density in the dissipation term (RHLLEM-LD).
* [Huang & Zahr (2022) — A robust, high-order implicit shock tracking method for simulation of complex, high-speed flows](/sources/huang-2022.md) — Makes high-order implicit shock tracking (HOIST) robust: a boundary-preserving affine node parametrization, a modified SQP solver with new merit penalty and adaptive mesh penalty, and element collapse, straightening and re-initialization, so that a shock-agnostic mesh converges to a shock-aligned DG solution at near-optimal rates.
* [Huang et al. (2023) — A Five-Point TENO Scheme with Adaptive Dissipation Based on a New Scale Sensor](/sources/huang-2023-b.md) — Replaces the discontinuity sensor that drives the adaptive cut-off of the five-point TENO5-A scheme by a five-point scale sensor that, with exact derivatives, returns the reduced wavenumber of a sinusoid at every point, critical points included, and maps it through a tanh to the cut-off threshold; tested numerically against WENO5-Z, TENO5 and TENO5-A.
* [Huang et al. (2026) — A unified framework for non-linear reconstruction schemes in a compact stencil. Part 2: Learning operators from neural networks](/sources/huang-2026.md) — Projects the trained WENO3-NN network onto the normalised-variable diagram, finds that it tends to the central scheme inside cells holding a critical point, and fits two closed-form compact operators, ROUND and the anti-dissipative LD-ROUND, that are cheaper and more accurate than the network.
* [Jain & Moin (2022) — A kinetic energy–and entropy-preserving scheme for compressible two-phase flows](/sources/jain-2022.md) — Non-dissipative central split-form discretization of the five-equation model with a conservative diffuse-interface regularization, built from consistency conditions for kinetic-energy conservation, interface equilibrium and pressure work, stable in under-resolved inviscid two-phase turbulence where single-phase KEEP schemes diverge.
* [Jeong et al. (2022) — Development of a WENO scheme based on radial basis function with an improved convergence order](/sources/jeong-2022.md) — WENO-G: finite-difference WENO whose candidate and global interpolants are cell-average Gaussian-RBF interpolants with RBF-dependent ideal weights and a locally fitted shape parameter, reaching sixth order on a five-point stencil, with smoothness indicators from generalized undivided differences with exponential vanishing moments.
* [Ji et al. (2022) — A Class of New High-order Finite-Volume TENO Schemes for Hyperbolic Conservation Laws with Unstructured Meshes](/sources/ji-2022.md) — First finite-volume TENO on unstructured meshes (TENO3–TENO6 in UCNS3D): one large least-squares stencil kept whole when smooth, otherwise an equal-weight mean of the surviving third-order directional stencils, compared with unstructured WENO and CWENO on standard Euler tests.
* [Kanbar et al. (2022) — Well-Balanced Central Scheme for the System of MHD Equations with Gravitational Source Term](/sources/kanbar-2022.md) — Second-order Riemann-solver-free unstaggered central scheme for 2D ideal MHD with gravity: it evolves the deviation from a known steady state for exact well-balancing and applies Touma's unstaggered constrained transport to keep a centred discrete divergence of B at round-off, tested on stratified atmospheres.
* [Kou et al. (2022) — Immersed boundary method for high-order flux reconstruction based on volume penalization](/sources/kou-2022.md) — Couples a volume-penalization (Brinkman) immersed boundary method with high-order flux reconstruction on Cartesian grids for compressible Navier–Stokes flows past static and rigidly moving bodies, with a closed-form implicit Strang-split forcing, IDW-IP surface reconstruction and local p-refinement near walls.
* [Kuzmin & Vedral (2023) — Dissipation-based WENO stabilization of high-order finite element methods for scalar conservation laws](/sources/kuzmin-2023.md) — Continuous-Galerkin finite elements stabilized by a cellwise blend of high-order projection stabilization and low-order artificial viscosity, with the blending factor computed from the deviation of the solution from a Hermite WENO reconstruction that never overwrites it, plus an O(h^{N+1/2}) consistency analysis.
* [Lesur et al. (2023) — IDEFIX: A versatile performance-portable Godunov code for astrophysical flows](/sources/lesur-2023.md) — Code paper for IDEFIX, a Kokkos-based C++17 second-order Godunov finite-volume code for astrophysical hydrodynamics and non-ideal MHD on Cartesian and curvilinear grids, with constrained transport, super time-stepping and orbital advection, validated on standard tests and benchmarked on CPUs and GPUs up to 1024 GPUs.
* [Li et al. (2022) — A class of high-order finite difference schemes with minimized dispersion and adaptive dissipation for solving compressible flows](/sources/li-2022.md) — Makes the dissipation of the fourth-order MDCD finite-difference scheme adapt locally through a scale sensor (the effective scaled wavenumber), builds a WENO-CU6-type nonlinear counterpart and a hybrid with a new Ren-type shock detector, and tests them on shock, vortex and Taylor–Green problems.
* [Li et al. (2022) — High order weighted essentially non-oscillatory WENO-ZN schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws](/sources/li-2022-b.md) — Extends the fifth-order WENO-ZN weights to arbitrary odd order: a global indicator from the squared maximal undivided difference and an adaptive function replacing the WENO-Z constant, with critical-point order analysis and seventh-order Euler tests.
* [Li et al. (2022) — High-order accurate entropy stable adaptive moving mesh finite difference schemes for (multi-component) compressible Euler equations with the stiffened equation of state](/sources/li-2022-c.md) — Extends entropy-stable adaptive moving-mesh finite differences to single- and two-component Euler flows with the stiffened equation of state: curvilinear entropy-conservative two-point fluxes, discrete geometric conservation laws, fifth-order multi-resolution WENO dissipation with a sign switch, and variational mesh redistribution, tested in 2D and 3D.
* [Li et al. (2022) — Moment-Based Multi-Resolution HWENO Scheme for Hyperbolic Conservation Laws](/sources/li-2022-d.md) — Sixth-order finite-volume Hermite WENO that evolves cell averages and first moments on a 3-cell (3×3) stencil, reconstructs only Gauss–Lobatto point values with a multi-resolution WENO-Z hierarchy, and replaces the first moment in KXRCF-flagged cells for stability and sharper shocks.
* [Li et al. (2023) — Scale-Invariant Multi-resolution Alternative WENO Scheme for the Euler Equations](/sources/li-2023.md) — Proves that the multi-resolution A-WENO interpolation of Wang et al. (2021) is not scale-invariant, repairs it with a six-point global indicator and a descaled regularization (MR-AWENO-Si), and uses the scale invariance to build a well-balanced characteristic-wise scheme for the Euler equations with gravity.
* [Liang et al. (2022) — A fifth-order low-dissipation discontinuity-resolving TENO scheme for compressible flow simulation](/sources/liang-2022.md) — Improves TENO5-THINC with a parameter-free discontinuity detector built from the TENO stencil flags, which also catches steps located at a cell interface, and with a two-steepness tanh interpolation of the interface flux that replaces the cell-average-constrained THINC reconstruction.
* [Liu et al. (2021) — A new locally divergence-free WLS-ENO scheme based on the positivity-preserving finite volume method for ideal MHD equations](/sources/liu-2021.md) — Second- and third-order finite-volume scheme for 2D ideal MHD: a conservative WLS-ENO reconstruction made locally divergence-free for the magnetic field by coefficient matching and two-round averaging, inside the Wu–Shu positivity-preserving framework with HLL flux, upwind Godunov–Powell term and scaling limiter.
* [Luo & Wu (2021) — An improved WENO-Z+ scheme for solving hyperbolic conservation laws](/sources/luo-2021-b.md) — Short note showing by approximate-dispersion-relation analysis that WENO-Z+ has negative dissipation near one wavenumber, and proposing WENO-Z+M, with a square-rooted extra term and an adaptive coefficient, which it reports as more accurate than the underlying linear fifth-order scheme.
* [Luo et al. (2021) — A moving discontinuous Galerkin finite element method with interface condition enforcement for compressible flows](/sources/luo-2021.md) — MDG+ICE, a variant of Corrigan's moving DG with interface condition enforcement: space-time DG for the Euler equations whose vertex positions are fixed by testing the flux-jump condition in a continuous trace space, with a central flux, no limiter, least-squares solves and mesh-topology repair.
* [Matsushita & Aoki (2021) — Gas-liquid two-phase flows simulation based on weakly compressible scheme with interface-adapted AMR method](/sources/matsushita-2021.md) — Fully explicit weakly compressible solver for low-Mach gas–liquid flows on GPUs: an artificial-sound-speed pressure equation instead of the Poisson solve, a conservative phase field coupled with a level set, WENO3 blended with first-order upwind near the density jump, and interface-adapted tree-based block AMR scaling to 128 GPUs.
* [Mossier et al. (2022) — A p-Adaptive Discontinuous Galerkin Method with hp-Shock Capturing](/sources/mossier-2022.md) — A p-adaptive DGSEM in FLEXI that switches troubled elements to a second-order TVD finite-volume scheme on 2N+1 subcells per direction, with one modal-decay indicator driving both shock detection and degree changes; speedups of 1.3 to 4 on standard compressible tests.
* [Nguyen et al. (2022) — Fully compressible multiphase model for computation of compressible fluid flows with large density ratio and the presence of shock waves](/sources/nguyen-2022.md) — A fully conservative multiphase model with phasic mass equations and mixture momentum and energy under pressure–temperature equilibrium and stiffened-gas phases, solved by a second-order MUSCL–HLLC finite-volume scheme on primitive variables with a compressive limiter on the volume fractions.
* [Pazner (2021) — Sparse invariant domain preserving discontinuous Galerkin methods with subcell convex limiting](/sources/pazner-2021.md) — Invariant-domain-preserving nodal DGSEM: a sparsified low-order operator with corrected metric terms and Guermond–Popov graph viscosity gives a p-independent first-order IDP scheme, blended with the high-order DG target by element-wise or dimension-by-dimension subcell convex limiting, with a modal smoothness indicator that relaxes the bounds.
* [Peng et al. (2022) — A solver for simulating shock-induced combustion on curvilinear adaptive meshes](/sources/peng-2022.md) — Extension of the AMROC block-structured AMR framework to mapped curvilinear meshes: a second-order MUSCL–Hancock finite-volume scheme with a capacity function and rotated AUSM or Roe/HLL fluxes, area-weighted restriction and Newton-solved bilinear prolongation, verified by manufactured solutions and validated on detonations and Lehr's blunt-body shock-induced combustion.
* [Peng et al. (2024) — OEDG: Oscillation-eliminating discontinuous Galerkin method for hyperbolic conservation laws](/sources/peng-2024.md) — Introduces OEDG: after every Runge–Kutta stage of a standard DG scheme, an exactly solved damping step acts as a solution-dependent modal filter. Scale- and evolution-invariant damping coefficients remove the stiffness of oscillation-free DG and its unit dependence; an optimal fully discrete error estimate is proved for linear advection.
* [Puppo et al. (2023) — Quinpi: Integrating Conservation Laws with CWENO Implicit Methods](/sources/puppo-2023.md) — Third-order implicit finite-volume framework (Quinpi) for 1D scalar conservation laws: CWENO3 weights frozen from a first-order implicit predictor make the reconstruction linear, a DIRK3 corrector, CWENO-type blending in time with the predictor, and a conservative flux correction; third order on Burgers at time steps of one and ten cell widths.
* [Ranocha et al. (2023) — Efficient Implementation of Modern Entropy Stable and Kinetic Energy Preserving Discontinuous Galerkin Methods for Conservation Laws](/sources/ranocha-2023.md) — Implementation and single-core performance study of flux-differencing DGSEM in Trixi.jl and FLUXO: a surface-absorbing flux-differencing operator, skew-symmetry halving, directional curvilinear volume fluxes, division-reduced logarithmic means, precomputation and SIMD, with measured costs against overintegration and Gauss collocation.
* [Renac (2021) — Entropy stable, robust and high-order DGSEM for the compressible multicomponent Euler equations](/sources/renac-2021.md) — Entropy-stable, positivity-preserving Gauss–Lobatto DGSEM for the multicomponent Euler equations of a mixture of polytropic gases: a new entropy-conservative flux with three logarithmic means, an entropy-stable positive relaxation flux, and a proof of cell-average positivity on unstructured quadrilaterals.
* [Rueda-Ramírez et al. (2021) — An entropy stable nodal discontinuous Galerkin method for the resistive MHD equations. Part II: Subcell finite volume shock capturing](/sources/ruedaramirez-2021.md) — Entropy-stable shock capturing for the split-form LGL DGSEM of resistive GLM-MHD: element-wise blending with a native subcell finite-volume scheme extended to non-conservative terms, a blending lemma preserving entropy stability, a second-order TVD-ES subcell scheme with the sign property, and tests up to Io's plasma interaction.
* [Rueda-Ramírez et al. (2022) — Subcell limiting strategies for discontinuous Galerkin spectral element methods](/sources/ruedaramirez-2022.md) — Unified four-ingredient framework for blending nodal DGSEM with a compatible subcell finite-volume scheme, element-wise or subcell-wise and a priori or a posteriori, with proofs that Pazner's sparse invariant-domain-preserving DG equals the LLF subcell scheme and that the Hennemann subcell metrics solve its conservation condition exactly.
* [Sciacovelli et al. (2021) — Assessment of a high-order shock-capturing central-difference scheme for hypersonic turbulent flow simulations](/sources/sciacovelli-2021.md) — Finite-difference central scheme with a ninth-order Jameson-type adaptive scalar dissipation and a three-factor shock sensor (Bhagatwala–Lele, Ducros, Jameson), assessed on shock tubes, a real-gas reacting shock tube, an underexpanded jet, compressible Taylor–Green vortices and supersonic and hypersonic boundary layers as DNS and implicit LES.
* [Sousa & Scalo (2022) — A unified Quasi-Spectral Viscosity (QSV) approach to shock capturing and large-eddy simulation](/sources/sousa-2022.md) — Proposes the quasi-spectral viscosity (QSV), one explicit, spectrally shaped eddy viscosity for sixth-order compact finite differences that serves both as an LES subfilter-scale closure and as shock capturing, built from Padé and Fejér filter residuals and tested against LAD and standard LES models.
* [Takagi et al. (2022) — A novel high-order low-dissipation TENO-THINC scheme for hyperbolic conservation laws](/sources/takagi-2022.md) — Hybrid finite-difference reconstruction that runs fifth-order TENO in smooth regions and switches to THINC in cells flagged by a parameter-free discontinuity detector built only from TENO's binary stencil flags; fifth order on smooth data, sharper contacts and more small-scale structure on standard Euler tests, at about 17 % extra cost over TENO5.
* [Tsoutsanis et al. (2021) — CWENO Finite-Volume Interface Capturing Schemes for Multicomponent Flows Using Unstructured Meshes](/sources/tsoutsanis-2021.md) — Arbitrary-order unstructured finite-volume CWENO (high-order central plus second-order directional polynomials) applied to the five-equation diffuse-interface model in UCNS3D: design order 3–6, lower cost than equal-order WENO, primitive-variable reconstruction against interface oscillations, and multiphase shock–bubble tests.
* [Wan & Xia (2022) — A hybrid WENO scheme for steady-state simulations of Euler equations](/sources/wan-2022.md) — Fifth-order finite-difference hybrid WENO (WENO-H) for steady Euler problems: a three-region detector switches between the linear scheme, a smooth transition blend and a modified CWENO-type reconstruction blended with its linear counterpart, so that residues reach round-off where CWENO and MRWENO schemes stall.
* [Wang & Don (2022) — Affine-invariant WENO weights and operator](/sources/wang-2022-b.md) — Makes WENO-Z, WENO-D and WENO-JS weights invariant under affine maps of the data by scaling the regularization with the stencil's mean absolute deviation, proves the affine-invariance theorem, and shows intrinsic well-balancing for shallow water without weight linearization.
* [Wang et al. (2021) — An efficient fifth-order finite difference multi-resolution WENO scheme for inviscid and viscous flow problems](/sources/wang-2021.md) — Cheaper fifth-order finite-difference multi-resolution WENO: nested central 1/3/5-point stencils in a Legendre basis, smoothness indicators independent of the linear weights, a min-of-differences indicator for the constant, WENO-Z-type weights and a CWENO-type combination, extended to Navier–Stokes with sixth-order central viscous fluxes.
* [Wang et al. (2022) — A second-order-accurate immersed boundary ghost-cell method with hybrid reconstruction for compressible flow simulations](/sources/wang-2022.md) — Sharp-interface ghost-cell immersed-boundary method for the compressible Euler equations on Cartesian AMROC grids: a bilinearly complete quadratic normal extrapolation for ghost cells too close to the wall, switched in hybrid with the baseline mirror-point method, tested against a cut-cell method.
* [Wang et al. (2023) — Fifth-Order A-WENO Schemes Based on the Adaptive Diffusion Central-Upwind Rankine-Hugoniot Fluxes](/sources/wang-2023-b.md) — Fifth-order A-WENO finite-difference schemes for the 1D and 2D Euler equations, using as two-point flux a central-upwind Rankine–Hugoniot flux whose one-sided speeds are reduced in smooth regions and, in 2D, scaled by a numerical dissipation switch; visibly less dissipative than the adaptive-diffusion central-flux A-WENO scheme.
* [Wong et al. (2021) — A positivity-preserving high-order weighted compact nonlinear scheme for compressible gas-liquid flows](/sources/wong-2021.md) — Explicit WCNS with incremental-stencil WENO interpolation for the five-equation gas–liquid model: convexity of the admissible set under stiffened-gas mixing, a proved positivity- and boundedness-preserving first-order HLLC scheme, and interpolation and flux limiters that make the fifth-order scheme admissibility-preserving.
* [Wu & Shu (2023) — Geometric Quasilinearization Framework for Analysis and Design of Bound-Preserving Schemes](/sources/wu-2023-b.md) — Introduces geometric quasilinearization: a convex admissible set with nonlinear constraints is rewritten as a family of affine constraints indexed by free auxiliary variables, giving short bound-preservation proofs and provably bound-preserving high-order DG schemes for multicomponent MHD.
* [Ye et al. (2022) — An alternative formulation of targeted ENO scheme for hyperbolic conservation laws](/sources/ye-2022.md) — Builds AFTENO, a finite-difference scheme that puts a TENO interpolation of point values, fed to any monotone two-point flux, inside the alternative-formulation WENO flux with even-derivative corrections; coefficients up to order eight, fifth-order tests on Euler and Navier–Stokes problems.
* [Yu & Hesthaven (2022) — A data-driven shock capturing approach for discontinuous Galekin methods](/sources/yu-2022.md) — Artificial-viscosity shock capturing for nodal discontinuous Galerkin methods in which a small feedforward neural network, trained on canonical functions with known regularity, predicts the modal decay rate that sets the element viscosity, in 1D and on unstructured triangles.
* [Zheng et al. (2021) — A high order conservative finite difference scheme for compressible two-medium flows](/sources/zheng-2021.md) — Fifth-order conservative finite-difference scheme for two-medium flows: alternative-formulation WENO on characteristic primitive variables, level-set interface tracking, cell merging with a low-order interface flux, and a smooth discontinuity indicator that blends low- and high-order fluxes, with accuracy propositions.
* [Zheng et al. (2022) — A fourth-order conservative semi-Lagrangian finite volume WENO scheme without operator splitting for kinetic and fluid simulations](/sources/zheng-2022.md) — Fourth-order, mass-conservative, positivity-preserving 2D semi-Lagrangian finite-volume scheme without dimensional splitting: cubic-curved upstream cells, a 13-cell WENO-ZQ polynomial reconstruction, clipping with Green line integrals and a CF4 exponential integrator, applied to Vlasov–Poisson, guiding-center and incompressible Euler flows.

# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Arfken, Weber & Harris (2013) — Mathematical Methods for Physicists, 7th ed.](/sources/arfken-2013.md) — Graduate textbook of mathematical methods for physics; this bundle reads only its ODE chapter (first-order methods, singular points, Frobenius series and Fuchs' theorem, Wronskian and the number of solutions, variation of parameters) and its PDE chapter (first-order characteristics, classification by factorisation, Table 9.1 of boundary data, separation of variables in three coordinate systems, uniqueness for Laplace, d'Alembert, heat-flow solutions).
* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering, 3rd ed.](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Undergraduate-to-graduate textbook of mathematical methods; this bundle reads only its ODE chapters (first- and higher-order linear equations, Wronskian, variation of parameters, ODE Green's functions) and its two PDE chapters (model equations, classification, characteristics, general solutions, d'Alembert, Table 20.1 of boundary data, uniqueness for Poisson, separation of variables, transform methods, PDE Green's functions and images).

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Flow past a circular cylinder](/benchmarks/flow-past-circular-cylinder.md) — The circular cylinder on a background grid plus a body-fitted annular grid as the basic overset test: subcritical and supercritical Euler flow (1983), manufactured and Mach 2 impulsive-start Navier–Stokes convergence studies (1990), fourth-order incompressible flow at Re 100 and Wannier's Stokes solution (1994), and shock diffraction (1995).
* [Multi-element airfoils](/benchmarks/multi-element-airfoil.md) — High-lift and flapped airfoils as overset test cases: the SKF 1.1 with manoeuvre flap on a three-level Chimera hierarchy (1983), the inviscid three-element airfoil of the conservative interface (1995), the 30P30N assembled automatically by PEGASUS 5 (2003), and the two-flap Joukowsky airfoil on a composite grid (1990).
* [Wing–pylon–store separation and related moving-body cases](/benchmarks/store-separation.md) — The store released from a wing (with or without pylon) as the reference moving-body overset problem: Meakin and Suhs's AFATL wing and store at Mach 1.05 with steady validation and a prescribed fall, their Space Shuttle booster separation, and Noack's 3.6-million-point wing/pylon/finned-store assembly with per-step timings.

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Flow past a circular cylinder](/benchmarks/flow-past-circular-cylinder.md) — The circular cylinder on a background grid plus a body-fitted annular grid as the basic overset test: Euler flow (1983), manufactured and Mach 2 Navier–Stokes convergence studies (1990), fourth-order incompressible flow (1994), shock diffraction (1995), interpolation order at Re 100 and side-by-side cylinders (2005), a moving cylinder and Maxwell tests (2006), and stationary and rotating cylinders at Re 200 (2021).
* [Flow past a sphere](/benchmarks/flow-past-sphere.md) — The sphere as the three-dimensional overset test body: Henshaw's fourth-order incompressible flow in a channel on two pole-free sphere patches plus a Cartesian grid (1994), fourth-order Maxwell convergence and Mie scattering on a sphere grid (2006), and Horne and Mahesh's GPU unstructured solver at Re 3700 and a freely falling sphere (2021).
* [Isentropic vortex convection](/benchmarks/isentropic-vortex.md) — An inviscid vortex convected across the overlap of two grids, with its exact translated solution, used to measure the order of accuracy that the inter-grid coupling preserves: second order limits high-order compact schemes, while DG coupling by WENO reconstruction keeps order N+1.
* [Lid-driven cavity](/benchmarks/lid-driven-cavity.md) — The driven cavity on a background grid with an overlapping inner grid, used to check incompressible overset solvers against single-grid references: the square cavity at Re 1000 for an ADER scheme and a long two-lid Stokes cavity for a conservative pressure correction.
* [Multi-element airfoils](/benchmarks/multi-element-airfoil.md) — High-lift and flapped airfoils as overset test cases: the SKF 1.1 with manoeuvre flap on a three-level Chimera hierarchy (1983), the inviscid three-element airfoil of the conservative interface (1995), the 30P30N assembled automatically by PEGASUS 5 (2003), and the two-flap Joukowsky airfoil on a composite grid (1990).
* [Shock–cylinder interaction](/benchmarks/shock-cylinder-interaction.md) — A shock wave meeting a circular cylinder on an overlapping grid, with the shock crossing the interpolation boundary: a Mach 1.5 shock driving a free rigid cylinder with self-convergence, AMR equivalence and cost data, detonations around moving cylinders, and the Mach 3 bow shock with drag, stand-off distance and patch-refinement gains.
* [Taylor–Green vortex (two-dimensional exact decaying solution)](/benchmarks/taylor-green-vortex.md) — The two-dimensional Taylor–Green exact solution of the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations as an overset accuracy test: a periodic vortex array crossed by a rotated overset patch for order of accuracy and kinetic-energy conservation up to Re 1e9, and a translating decaying vortex for interpolation order at high and low cell Reynolds number.
* [Wing–pylon–store separation and related moving-body cases](/benchmarks/store-separation.md) — The store released from a wing (with or without pylon) as the reference moving-body overset problem: Meakin and Suhs's AFATL wing and store at Mach 1.05 with steady validation and a prescribed fall, their Space Shuttle booster separation, Noack's 3.6-million-point wing/pylon/finned-store assembly with per-step timings, and Roget and Sitaraman's 15-million-cell parallel assembly scaling.

# Isentropic vortex convection

An isentropic vortex superposed on a uniform flow is an exact, smooth solution of the Euler equations that translates without change. Convecting it across an overset boundary isolates the error the inter-grid coupling adds to a high-order scheme.

- [Sherer & Scott (2005)](/sources/sherer-2005.md) convect the vortex on direct-injection multi-block grids and on curvilinear overset systems with a hole, with high-order compact differences and filters ([high-order discretizations on overlapping grids](/methods/high-order-overset-discretization.md)). Second-order [interpolation](/methods/inter-grid-interpolation.md) makes the whole method second order; sixth-order interpolation approaches the direct-injection result; inner/outer cell-volume ratios of 4 and 16 cause no degradation, the filter removing spurious waves generated by the volume jump (§5.2, Figs. 15, 18).
- [Kochi & Ramakrishna (2022)](/sources/kochi-2022.md) convect it across an embedded square and the same square rotated by $45^\circ$ with a DG scheme coupled by WENO reconstruction: $L_2$ density orders about $N+1$ for $N=1$–3, errors no larger than on a single grid ([discontinuous Galerkin methods on overlapping grids](/methods/discontinuous-galerkin-on-overset-grids.md), §4.2, Table 2).

## Qualifications

- Kochi & Ramakrishna (2022) write the exact solution of their Example 1 as $\rho(x,y,0)$, though it depends on $t$, and give an element size $1/20$ for $40\times40$ elements on $[0,10]\times[-5,5]$, which is $1/4$ *(compiler check, not in the sources)*.

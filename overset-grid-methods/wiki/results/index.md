# Results

Theorems, proved properties and barriers.

* [Interpolation order and overlap requirements](/results/interpolation-order-requirement.md) — Chesshire and Henshaw's lemmas on the width of inter-grid interpolation needed to keep a composite-grid discretization at its design order: width at least 2s+1 when the overlap shrinks with h, s+1 when it stays fixed, generalized to sp+1 and sp/2+1, and confirmed in 1-D, 2-D Poisson, fourth-order Navier–Stokes and Maxwell, sixth-order compact and finite-volume elliptic, advective and Navier–Stokes tests.
* [Well-posedness and energy stability of overset problems](/results/overset-well-posedness.md) — Energy-method theorems for the continuous overset problem of linear symmetric hyperbolic systems on two overlapping domains: characteristic coupling is well posed in one dimension; weighted penalty coupling at the overlap boundaries and inside the overlap is energy bounded, equivalent to the single-domain problem and conservative in one and two dimensions.

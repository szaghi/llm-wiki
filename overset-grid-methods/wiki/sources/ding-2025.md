# Summary

For inter-grid boundaries of curvilinear structured overset grids in flows with strong discontinuities and complex shock reflections, Ding et al. extend multi-resolution weighted essentially non-oscillatory (WENO) reconstruction to nonlinear *interpolation* at arbitrary receptor locations on unequal-size nested sub-stencils. Accuracy near discontinuities degrades progressively down to first order, so several discontinuities can sit in one stencil (classical WENO allows one); the linear weights and smoothness indicators do not depend on the interpolation location. A freestream-preserving discretization is also extended to the overset framework ([nonlinear inter-grid interpolation](/methods/nonlinear-inter-grid-interpolation.md), [shock capturing on overlapping grids](/methods/shock-capturing-on-overset-grids.md)).

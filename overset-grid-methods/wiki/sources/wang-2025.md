# Summary

Overset grids need hole cutting and an iterative exchange that are complex and time-consuming; immersed-boundary methods suffer non-physical pressure oscillations with moving boundaries. Wang et al. combine the two: two body-force terms from a diffuse-interface immersed-boundary method enter the background momentum equations — the first imposes the no-slip condition of the body surface implicitly in the background, the second transfers the overset information — so no hole cutting is performed; pressure interpolation addresses the oscillations ([immersed boundaries on overlapping grids](/methods/immersed-boundary-on-overset-grids.md), [hole cutting](/methods/hole-cutting.md), [fluid–structure interaction](/methods/fluid-structure-interaction-on-overset-grids.md)).

3. The combined strategy avoids hole cutting and is applied to fluid–structure interaction with large-amplitude motion (Abstract, numerical section). **Shown**

# Summary

A global magnetohydrodynamic code for the solar corona and wind on the [Yin–Yang grid](/definitions/yin-yang-grid.md): single-fluid MHD with nonadiabatic effects (electron heat conduction, optically thin radiative cooling, empirical coronal heating) and the semirelativistic Boris correction, solved on each of the two partial spherical shells, with the boundary conditions in the ghost zones of the overlapping domains updated from the other domain, parallelized with MPI. Standard test problems validate the code, and a solar-wind solution for a dipolar surface field represents solar minimum.

# Summary

Ding et al. automate structured overset grid assembly for a cell-centred finite-difference solver with four enhancements: a hybrid [hole cutting](/methods/hole-cutting.md) combining the efficiency of approximate cutting with the accuracy and robustness of direct cutting (small gaps, thin cuts); an improved implicit hole-boundary optimization with quality comparison that reduces donor-search work ([overlap optimization](/methods/overlap-optimization.md)); an interpolation-cell cancellation that minimizes the overlap, useful for large high-order stencils; and island removal without wall distances. For high-order inter-grid boundaries an optimized interpolation minimizes spectral errors and is more accurate than Lagrange interpolation for periodic problems that cross inter-grid boundaries often ([nonlinear and optimized inter-grid interpolation](/methods/nonlinear-inter-grid-interpolation.md)). Assembly is shown on a [multi-element airfoil](/benchmarks/multi-element-airfoil.md), a circular array of cylinders, multiple spheres and a wing–pylon–store configuration ([store separation](/benchmarks/store-separation.md)).

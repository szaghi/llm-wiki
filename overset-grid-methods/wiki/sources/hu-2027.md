# Summary

Instead of interpolating receptor values, Hu et al. reconstruct them physically: virtual control volumes and interfaces are built along the overset boundaries, and a lattice Boltzmann flux solver reconstructs the conservative variables there as the transferred solution. On two- and three-dimensional steady and unsteady benchmarks, including moving grids, the reconstruction generally outperforms conventional interpolation, especially in reducing accumulated solution-transfer errors, with smoother solutions across the overlap and faster convergence ([inter-grid interpolation](/methods/inter-grid-interpolation.md), [conservative overset interface](/methods/conservative-overset-interface.md)).

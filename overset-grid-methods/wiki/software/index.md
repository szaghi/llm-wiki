# Software

Codes and libraries embodying methods.

* [CMPGRD and its solver suite (CGINS, CGNVT, CGES, CGMG, DSK)](/software/cmpgrd.md) — Chesshire and Henshaw's Fortran composite overlapping grid generator: component-grid mappings and stretching, the ordering-based six-step classification that builds the composite grid with given interpolation and discretization widths, donor search by Newton inversion of the mapping, the DSK data structure, and the solvers built on it, with grid-construction and per-step timings.
* [DCF and DCF3D (Meakin's domain connectivity function and code)](/software/dcf3d.md) — Meakin's domain-connectivity line: the 1989 Domain Connectivity Function, a modified PEGASUS run every time step and coupled cyclically to flow solver and body dynamics with a band-restricted search, and the later DCF3D code that the 2003–2005 sources list among connectivity tools, with assembly integral to the solver and object X-ray hole cutting.
* [OVERFLOW (OVERFLOW-D, OVERFLOW 2)](/software/overflow.md) — NASA's structured overset compressible Navier–Stokes solver: its 1989 origin as Buning's iblank rewrite of Steger's F3D scheme, the relative-motion branch OVERFLOW-D with six-degree-of-freedom dynamics and contact detection, their 2001 merger as OVERFLOW 2, and its use as the consumer of PEGASUS connectivity and of object X-ray hole cutting.
* [Overture (and its grid generator Ogen)](/software/overture.md) — Henshaw's C++ framework for PDEs on composite overlapping grids and its overlapping grid generator Ogen: inputs and point classification, the per-step incremental connectivity update for moving and deforming grids with its measured cost, the P++ parallel arrays and donor-side parallel interpolation, the solvers built on it, and how other assembly papers place it.
* [PEGASUS (PEGSUS) versions 1–4](/software/pegasus.md) — The AEDC Chimera domain-connectivity preprocessor from its 2-D origin with the XMERA solver (1983) through the 3-D PEGSUS with XMER3D (1985) to PEGASUS 4 (1991): what it computes, the hierarchy restriction of the early versions, its per-step cost in moving-body use, and the manual effort that motivated PEGASUS 5.
* [PEGASUS 5](/software/pegasus-5.md) — The 2003 from-scratch Fortran 90 rewrite of the PEGASUS overset connectivity preprocessor: boundary-condition-driven automation, Cartesian hole map with line-of-sight test, exhaustive ADT plus stencil-jumping donor search, cell-difference-parameter donor choice, overlap optimization, surface projection for viscous grids, restart and master–worker MPI, with its measured costs and labour savings.
* [SUGGAR](/software/suggar.md) — Noack's stand-alone overset grid assembler for moving-body simulation (2005): structured, unstructured and general polyhedral grids for node- and cell-centred solvers, binary-tree Cartesian hole cutting, a body hierarchy in which siblings cut each other, cell-centred fringe and donor-weight options, and per-step assembly coupled to solver and motion code through FIFOs, with timings.

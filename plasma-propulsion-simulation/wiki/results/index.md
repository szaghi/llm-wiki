# Results

Theorems, proved properties and impossibility results.

* [Instability-enhanced electron–ion friction and effective mobility](/results/instability-enhanced-friction-force.md) — The azimuthal electron drift instability produces a momentum-conserving electron–ion friction force, equal to the density–field fluctuation correlation, that drives axial electron transport; a quasi-linear closure in the electron distribution slope reproduces it in PIC when fed PIC data, while the ion-trapping saturation and Maxwellian closures do not.
* [Thrust decomposition of a magnetic nozzle](/results/magnetic-nozzle-thrust-decomposition.md) — The thrust of a magnetic-nozzle rf thruster splits into the source electron-pressure force on the back wall, the Lorentz force of the azimuthal electron-diamagnetic current on the nozzle field and the axial momentum lost to the radial wall, each measured separately and matched by integrals of measured electron-pressure profiles within 20-30%.

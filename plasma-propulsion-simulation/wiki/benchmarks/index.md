# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Axial–azimuthal Hall-thruster PIC benchmark (Charoy et al. 2019)](/benchmarks/axial-azimuthal-pic-benchmark.md) — Collisionless 2D axial–azimuthal PIC test case of a Hall-thruster discharge with imposed radial magnetic field and imposed ionization source, reproduced by seven codes in 2019 and later used to test GPU, semi-implicit and reduced-order PIC and kinetic transport theory.
* [SPT-100 as a validation case](/benchmarks/spt-100.md) — The Soviet-designed stationary plasma thruster (annular channel of 100 mm diameter, xenon, typically run at 300 V and 5 mg/s) whose geometry and published measurements are the most common reference set-up for Hall-thruster simulations, from 1D fluid and 2D hybrid models to axial–azimuthal PIC.

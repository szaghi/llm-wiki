# Summary

The paper avoids the impassable singular states of a single-gimbal control
moment gyroscope (SGCMG) cluster by shaping the reference angular-velocity
(hence angular-momentum) trajectory of a rest-to-rest attitude maneuver,
rather than the gimbal-angle trajectory. It introduces a four-element
attitude parameter set, the "σ-parameters" (§2, Eqs. 1–2), built from two
successive rotations (about $z_t$ by $\theta_{bt}$, then about $e$ by
$\phi_{bt}$), with kinematics (Eq. 14) and direction-cosine relations
(Eqs. 15–18). Commanding the angular velocity as the sum of the two
approximately decoupled rotations, each with a bang-off-bang profile, puts
the angular-velocity trajectory on a cone ("cone-type", §3.1, Eq. 19); a
generalisation to an arbitrary second-rotation axis $e_b$ (§3.2, Eqs. 32–40)
multiplies the available trajectories. A σ-parameter tracking controller is
given with a Lyapunov argument (§4, Eqs. 45–49). The free axis is chosen by a
grid search over the sphere that minimises maneuver time while keeping the
reference momentum at least $M_{\mathrm{safe}}$ from the impassable singular
surface, or crossing it only in the passable direction (§5, Eqs. 70–82).
Simulations of a four-SGCMG cluster compare an eigen-axis bang-off-bang
maneuver (singular at 6.7 s) with the proposed trajectory (no singularity).

# Key claims

Symbols are the paper's (see the notation map).

1. **σ-parameters** (§2.1, Eqs. 1–3):
   $\sigma_{bt}=[\rho_{bt}^T,\theta_{bt}]^T$ with
   $\rho_{bt}=[\cos\phi_{bt},\ e\cdot x_b\sin\phi_{bt},\ e\cdot y_b\sin\phi_{bt}]^T$,
   $\lVert\rho_{bt}\rVert=1$, $\theta_{bt}\in[0,2\pi)$,
   $\phi_{bt}\in[0,\pi)$.
2. **Kinematics** (§2.2, Eqs. 6, 13, 14): $\dot\rho$ is linear in the body
   angular velocity, and
   $\dot\theta_{bt}=[-\rho_2/(1+\rho_0),\ \rho_1/(1+\rho_0),\ 1]\cdot\omega_{bt}^b$
   (Eq. 13), singular only at $\rho_0=-1$ ($\phi_{bt}=\pi$, excluded by the
   range).
3. **Direction-cosine matrix** (§2.3, Eq. 15):
   $C_{bt}=\left(\rho_0I_3+\frac{\rho_v\rho_v^T}{1+\rho_0}-\rho_v^\times\right)C_z(\theta_{bt})$,
   $\rho_v=[\rho_1,\rho_2,0]^T$.
4. **Decoupling** (§2.4, stated by inspection of Eq. 14): rotation about
   $z_t$ changes only $\theta_{bt}$, rotation about $e$ only $\phi_{bt}$;
   "approximate decoupled", not proved for the combined motion.
5. **Cone-type trajectory** (§3.1, Eqs. 19–29): the reference
   $\omega_{rt}=\omega_\theta z_t+\omega_\phi e$ gives
   $\dot\theta_{rt}=\omega_\theta$, $\dot\phi_{rt}=\omega_\phi$ and the
   reference acceleration
   $a_{rt}=\omega_\theta\omega_\phi(z_t\times e)+\dot\omega_\theta z_t+\dot\omega_\phi e$
   (Eq. 21). Bang-off-bang profiles are synchronised by
   $\omega_{\theta\max}/\omega_{\phi\max}=\dot\omega_{\theta\max}/\dot\omega_{\phi\max}=|\theta_{rt0}|/|\phi_{rt0}|$
   (Eq. 24), with $t_1=\omega_{\phi\max}/\dot\omega_{\phi\max}$,
   $t_2=|\phi_{rt0}|/\omega_{\phi\max}$, $t_3=t_1+t_2$ (Eqs. 25–27). The
   trajectory lies on a cone of axis $e$ and half-angle
   $\arctan(|\theta_{rt0}|/|\phi_{rt0}|)$.
6. **Arbitrary second axis** (§3.2.1, Eqs. 32–34, spherical trigonometry):
   any eigen-axis rotation $(E,\Phi)$ can be written as two rotations about
   $l_t$ by $\theta$ and about an arbitrary $e_b$ by $\phi$, with
   $\sin(\theta/2)=\sin\alpha\sin(\Phi/2)$,
   $\tan(\phi/2)=\cos\alpha\tan(\Phi/2)$, $\cos\alpha=e_b\cdot E$.
7. **Tracking controller** (§4.2, Eqs. 45–49): a PD-type law with
   feed-forward reference acceleration and gyroscopic compensation; with the
   Lyapunov function of Eq. 47, $\dot V\le0$ (negative semi-definite), so
   the paper claims stability (not asymptotic stability) of the tracking
   equilibrium.
8. **Passability** (§§5.1–5.2, Eqs. 64–72): singular surface
   $H_s=\sum_i\varepsilon_i\frac{(g_i\times u)\times g_i}{\lVert g_i\times u\rVert}$
   (Eq. 66), passability from the definiteness of $Q_N$ (Eq. 69, after
   Kurokawa, ref. [4]). Stated, argued from Fig. 5 and not proved: a momentum
   trajectory crossing an impassable surface from the $u_{\mathrm{new}}$
   side to the $-u_{\mathrm{new}}$ side ($u_{\mathrm{new}}\cdot T_r\le0$,
   Eq. 72) meets no singular state in gimbal space.
9. **Simulation** (§6, shown): the eigen-axis bang-off-bang reference
   (Case 1) becomes singular at 6.7 s (singular measure $D\to0$, gimbal
   rates diverge, Figs. 7–8); the proposed trajectory (Case 2) keeps $D>0$
   and converges (Figs. 9–12), crossing the impassable surface only in the
   deceleration phase where Eq. 72 holds.

# Methods

Rigid-body attitude dynamics with SGCMG angular momentum $h$ (Eq. 41),
written in auxiliary frames related to body and target frames by a constant
matrix (Eq. 35); σ-parameter kinematics (Eq. 44), controller (Eq. 45),
torque mapped back to the body frame (Eqs. 50–63). SGCMG torque
$T=-h_0A\dot\delta$ (Eq. 64); the singular surface is enumerated by sweeping
$u$ over the unit sphere, with SVD of $A_s$ (Eq. 75) and the $Q_N$ test.
Axis selection: for each candidate $e_b$, reference momentum and torque
(Eqs. 76–77) are discretised into $m$ points, the minimum distance to the
stored singular surface (Eq. 79, written as a squared distance) is computed
after discarding passable crossings (Eq. 80), and $t_3$ is minimised subject
to $M\ge M_{\mathrm{safe}}$ (Eqs. 81–82) and the torque cap
$\max(\mathrm{eig}(J^b))\,a_{\max}\le k_{\mathrm{safe}}T_{\max}$ (Eq. 78).
Steering for the simulation: pseudo-inverse plus null motion along
$\partial D/\partial\delta$ (Eq. 85), with
$D=\sum_{i\ne j}(t_i\times t_j)^T(t_i\times t_j)$ (Eq. 86). Cluster: the
first four units of a regular dodecahedron configuration, skew angle
$\beta=64.3^\circ$ (Fig. 6). Not stated: sphere-grid resolutions, $m$,
integrator, time step, software.

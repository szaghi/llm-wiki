# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Adamovich et al. (2017) — The 2017 Plasma Roadmap: Low temperature plasma science and technology](/sources/adamovich-2017.md) — Nineteen two-author perspective sections on low-temperature plasma science; for this bundle, the 2017 state of electric propulsion (Hall and gridded-ion thrusters, magnetic shielding, nested channels, priorities on electron transport and predictive 3D codes), anomalous transport in E×B discharges as instability-driven, and the modelling ecosystem (GPU, Poisson parallelization, verification and validation, data uncertainty).
* [Ahedo (2023) — Using electron fluid models to analyze plasma thruster discharges](/sources/ahedo-2023.md) — Review of slow-dynamics electron fluid models for Hall and electrodeless thrusters: the drift-diffusive quasi-neutral model with generalized Ohm's and Fourier laws and empirical anomalous terms, field-aligned meshes and the thermalized potential, an extended 1D axial model, kinetically consistent closures (anisotropic pressure, gyroviscosity, convective heat flux, polytropic laws), plume boundary conditions and a Debye-sheath/SEE wall model.
* [Ahmad et al. (2018) — An investigation of millimeter wave reflectarrays for small satellite platforms](/sources/ahmad-2018.md) — Gives a closed-form coupled-resonator model of the reflection coefficient of a printed-patch reflectarray unit cell with fringing, surface-wave, conductor-loss and roughness corrections, proposes 3-bit phase quantization, and measures two 35x35-element 60 GHz reflectarrays (33.26 dBi at boresight, about 60% aperture efficiency).
* [Alpatov, Khoroshylov & Bombardelli (2018) — Relative control of an ion beam shepherd satellite using the impulse compensation thruster](/sources/alpatov-2018.md) — Designs a robust H-infinity controller that holds an ion-beam-shepherd satellite 7 m from a debris object using only small variations of the impulse compensation thruster along track, with the beam force on the debris taken from a self-similar conical far-plume model and bounded by the impulse-transfer thrust, and estimates a 100-day de-orbit for 10.58 kg of xenon against 10.59 kg of xenon plus 12.93 kg of hydrazine for a conventional scheme.
* [An & Wu (2018) — Disturbance rejection dynamic inverse control of air-breathing hypersonic vehicles](/sources/an-2018.md) — Combines nonlinear dynamic inversion with a nonlinear disturbance observer and a state-dependent compensation matrix that cancels mismatched lumped disturbances from the velocity and altitude of an air-breathing hypersonic vehicle in steady state, proves global input-to-state stability, and compares the law in simulation with baseline and integral dynamic inversion.
* [Antoni et al. (2018) — Re-affirming Europe's ambitions in space: Past, present and future perspectives](/sources/antoni-2018.md) — A space-policy review tracing EU treaty history and ESA's origin and programme structure, describing the 2016 ESA–EU Joint Statement, the European Commission's Space Strategy for Europe and ESA's Space 4.0 resolution, and arguing for a single European Space Strategy.
* [Armbruster et al. (2018) — Experimental investigation of self-excited combustion instabilities with injection coupling in a cryogenic rocket combustor](/sources/armbruster-2018.md) — Characterizes a second (type 2) self-excited 1T instability in DLR's 42-injector LOX/H2 combustor BKD, shows a frequency rise with amplitude consistent with flame shortening, shows that the classic NASA velocity-ratio and stability-limit correlations do not predict it, and proposes a triple coupling of the chamber 1T mode with the LOX-post 2L and H2-injector 1L modes.
* [Bai et al. (2018) — Investigation of self-pulsation characteristics for a liquid-centered swirl coaxial injector with recess](/sources/bai-2018.md) — Cold-flow water–air experiments and a 2D axisymmetric-swirl VOF simulation of a recessed liquid-centered swirl coaxial injector attribute self-pulsation to blocking of the annular gas by the conical liquid sheet, fit the pulsation frequency linearly in liquid Reynolds number, report an 8–9 kHz ultra-high-frequency regime, and map the self-pulsation boundaries in the momentum-flux plane.
* [Boyd (2005) — Numerical modeling of spacecraft electric propulsion thrusters](/sources/boyd-2005.md) — Review of 2005 device-flow modelling for resisto-jets, arcjets, gridded ion, Hall and pulsed plasma thrusters, choosing between continuum and kinetic (DSMC, PIC, hybrid PIC–fluid) methods by Knudsen number and Debye length; ranks predictive accuracy at about 5 % for resisto-jets and ion thrusters and 10–20 % for arcjets, Hall thrusters and PPTs, the Hall limit being the unresolved anomalous electron mobility.
* [Bu (2018) — Guaranteeing prescribed performance for air-breathing hypersonic vehicles via an adaptive non-affine tracking controller](/sources/bu-2018.md) — Designs a longitudinal tracking controller for a flexible air-breathing hypersonic vehicle directly on non-affine models, combining a prescribed-performance error transformation, one minimal-learning-parameter neural network per subsystem and a high-order tracking differentiator, proves semi-global uniform ultimate boundedness, and reports two simulated climbs qualitatively.
* [Cai et al. (2018) — Improved tracklet association for space objects using short-arc optical measurements](/sources/cai-2018-b.md) — Proposes an improved virtual-particle method for associating short-arc optical tracklets, minimising a noise-model-free distance between orbits in nonsingular canonical variables by a genetic algorithm per orbit-revolution valley, tests it on 308 Mount Stromlo tracklets against the IVP and BVP methods, and adds a common-ellipse method that cuts false associations of constellation tracklets.
* [Cai et al. (2018) — Three-dimensional particle simulation of ion thruster plume impingement](/sources/cai-2018.md) — Applies the 3D unstructured hybrid PIC-DSMC code EX-PWS (macroparticle Xe, Xe+, Xe2+ and charge-exchange ions, isothermal Boltzmann electrons, Maxwell-reflection plate with species-dependent accommodation) to the LIPS-200 ion-thruster plume striking a 300 mm plate, matching torsional-balance force measurements at 0.6-1.0 m within 16.36% and mapping the plate's perturbation of ions, neutrals and charge-exchange wings.
* [Cerf (2018) — Optimal injection point for launch trajectories with parametric thrust profile](/sources/cerf-2018.md) — Shows with the maximum principle that a launcher upper stage with a parametric (linear or bilevel) thrust profile has constant mass-weighted mass costate and velocity-costate modulus, that minimum-fuel injection occurs at an apsis with zero flight-path angle, and reduces the problem to a small nonlinear program checked on a GTO example.
* [Charoy et al. (2020) — A comparison between kinetic theory and particle-in-cell simulations of anomalous electron transport in E × B plasma discharges](/sources/charoy-2020.md) — Uses 2D axial–azimuthal PIC-MCC (LPPic) runs of the 2019 Hall-thruster benchmark and nine variants to show that the instability-enhanced electron–ion friction force drives axial electron transport without wall or neutral collisions, and that a simplified quasi-linear closure fed with PIC velocity distributions and wave energies reproduces it while Maxwellian, ion-trapping-saturation and Bohm/wall models do not.
* [Cheng et al. (2018) — Performance evaluation and comparison of electricity generation systems based on single- and two-stage thermoelectric generator for hypersonic vehicles](/sources/cheng-2018.md) — Builds steady quasi-one-dimensional models of single- and two-stage SiGe thermoelectric generators placed between hot and cold hydrogen-fuel channels of a regeneratively cooled hypersonic vehicle, finding the single stage gives the higher maximum power density (16.53 kW/m² at 1200 K) and the two stage the higher efficiency at maximum power (10.78%).
* [Conte & Spencer (2018) — Mission analysis for Earth to Mars-Phobos distant Retrograde Orbits](/sources/conte-2018.md) — Designs impulsive transfers from low Earth orbit and from a lunar distant retrograde orbit to Mars-Phobos distant retrograde orbits, with a four-impulse arrival sequence and patched-conic Lambert legs over the 2020–2042 synodic periods, finding minimum total velocity increments of 5.1856 km/s from LEO and 2.7317 km/s from the lunar orbit.
* [Crusan et al. (2018) — NASA's eXploration Systems and Habitation (X-Hab) Academic Innovation Challenge](/sources/crusan-2018.md) — Describes NASA's X-Hab university design challenge since 2011: its proposal and award cycle, topic generation, selection criteria and systems-engineering review gates, with programme statistics (49 projects from 19 organizations in 15 states) and narrated example projects in habitation, in-space manufacturing, life support and plant growth.
* [Delchambre et al. (2018) — Momentum enhancement factor estimation for asteroid redirect missions](/sources/delchambre-2018.md) — Proposes an end-to-end estimation of the kinetic-impactor momentum enhancement factor from a hovering observer spacecraft, with a linear error budget and a chain of three extended Kalman filters, and shows in a NEOShield-2 simulation that the 1σ uncertainty drops from 0.44 to 0.11 within 7 days after impact.
* [Dhara et al. (2018) — CGR-BF: An efficient contact utilization scheme for predictable deep space Delay Tolerant Network](/sources/dhara-2018.md) — Extends Contact Graph Routing for deep-space Delay Tolerant Networks with reactive bundle fragmentation so that contacts too small for a whole bundle are still used, argues its expected delivery time is never worse than CGR's, and shows in Earth–Mars–Moon and Jupiter/Saturn network simulations gains in transmitted data and goodput that grow with bundle size and link constraint.
* [Dong & Zhu (2018) — Predictive visual servo kinematic control for autonomous robotic capture of non-cooperative space target](/sources/dong-2018.md) — Proposes a predictive, incremental visual-servo kinematic controller for an eye-in-hand manipulator that estimates an unknown target's motion by photogrammetry plus an adaptive extended Kalman filter, argues Lyapunov stability, and shows capture of a wire-suspended target in one laboratory experiment at about 12 s.
* [Eckels et al. (2024) — Hall thruster model improvement by multidisciplinary uncertainty quantification](/sources/eckels-2024.md) — Couples semi-empirical cathode and plume models with a 1D multi-fluid thruster code, replaces the thruster by a multifidelity MISC surrogate with SVD-reduced field outputs, and calibrates 17 parameters on SPT-100 background-pressure data by DRAM MCMC; residual errors far exceed noise and parametric uncertainty, which the authors attribute to model-form error, and Sobol' analysis shows the anomalous-transport coefficients dominate.
* [Fedele & Mungiguerra (2018) — Aerodynamics and flight mechanics activities for a suborbital flight test of a deployable heat shield capsule](/sources/fedele-2018.md) — Reports the flight-mechanics and aerodynamic work for MINI-IRENE, a suborbital demonstrator of a capsule with a deployable umbrella heat shield: a validated 3-DoF trajectory model predicting suborbital and LEO re-entry loads, and a preliminary aerodynamic database across free-molecular, transitional and continuum regimes built from DSMC, a Knudsen bridging function and Navier–Stokes and Newtonian methods, with static stability and trim.
* [Gagg Filho & da Silva Fernandes (2018) — A lunar flyby for a tridimensional Earth-to-Earth mission](/sources/gagg-2018.md) — Formulates two-impulse transfers between non-coplanar circular Earth orbits with an intermediate lunar flyby, in a 3D patched-conic model and in the spatial circular restricted three-body problem, and shows savings of up to about 5 km/s against a single-impulse plane change and 3 km/s against a Hohmann transfer, at flight times of weeks to over 100 days.
* [Gallo et al. (2021) — Numerical Aspects of Particle-in-Cell Simulations for Plasma-Motion Modeling of Electric Thrusters](/sources/gallo-2021.md) — An in-house MATLAB/CUDA particle code for heavy species only (prescribed or Boltzmann electrons) that replaces leapfrog by a fourth-order Runge–Kutta push, bilinear field interpolation by cubic bi-splines of the potential, and adds ray-traced diffuse wall reflection, mass-conserving ionization and GPU list compaction; applied to an SPT-100-like channel and a quasi-1D helicon double-layer thruster.
* [García-Garrido, Pudsey & Mundt (2018) — Numerical simulations of radiative heat effects in a plasma wind-tunnel flow under Mars entry conditions](/sources/garciagarrido-2018.md) — Simulates the supersonic CO2 jet of the HELM arc-heated plasma wind tunnel at 2.1 MJ/kg with two-temperature Navier–Stokes codes (NSMB, US3D) and a P1 radiation solver fed by a compact full-spectrum k-distribution database, matches TALIF temperatures to 10–100 K in the jet core, and finds radiation a minor effect (centreline cooling at most about 1.5%) in this optically thin gas.
* [Ghassabian et al. (2018) — ALCIDES: A novel lunar mission concept study for the demonstration of enabling technologies in deep-space exploration and human-robots interaction](/sources/ghassabian-2018.md) — A desk-study concept for a crewed and tele-robotic lunar south-polar mission to the Amundsen-Ganswindt basin within the HERACLES architecture, with four traverses, a three-phase launch plan and a qualitative EML2-relay communication architecture; no propulsion, plasma or numerical content.
* [Giannetti et al. (2021) — Numerical and experimental investigation of longitudinal oscillations in Hall thrusters](/sources/giannetti-2021.md) — Calibrates three physical parameters of a 1D quasi-neutral fluid Hall-thruster model (neutral speed, wall-loss scaling, two-level Bohm coefficient) by hand on the breathing-mode discharge current of the 5 kW-class HT5k, reproducing its mean, extremes, RMS and 25 kHz frequency, then validates it against fast-diving triple-probe profiles, which it matches only in order of magnitude and trend.
* [Gomez Jenkins, Krejci & Lozano (2018) — CubeSat constellation management using Ionic Liquid Electrospray Propulsion](/sources/gomezjenkins-2018.md) — Optimizes, by Differential Evolution on Gauss's variational equations with J2 and drag, the drift-rate manoeuvre that separates two 3U CubeSats by 180° in right ascension of the ascending node with a projected ionic-liquid electrospray thruster (2500 s, 5.7 W), giving 164.2 d and 85 g at minimum time and 245.1 d and 44 g at minimum propellant.
* [Greer, Paul & Rattner (2018) — Analysis of lithium-combustion power systems for extreme environment spacecraft](/sources/greer-2018.md) — A steady lumped thermodynamic and thermal-resistance model of a liquid-lithium combustion reactor feeding a sodium thermosiphon and a Stirling engine or thermoelectric generator re-assesses the Venus ALIVE concept with atmospheric CO2, sizes a Europa lander burning SF6, compares system mass with batteries over mission duration, and proposes engineering requirements; there is no experimental validation.
* [Grieve et al. (2018) — SpooQySats: CubeSats to demonstrate quantum key distribution technologies](/sources/grieve-2018.md) — Describes the SpooQySats programme of iterative balloon and CubeSat flight qualification of a miniature photon-pair source towards satellite quantum key distribution, reviewing satellite-QKD configurations and trusted-node key relay and reporting polarisation-correlation data from 335 days in orbit.
* [Guo, Zhang & Hu (2018) — Integrated vibration isolation and attitude control for spacecraft with uncertain or unknown payload inertia parameters](/sources/guo-2018.md) — Combines a six-strut magnetic-suspension vibration isolation platform, whose transfer matrix is derived with the bus motion retained, and an adaptive attitude controller for unknown payload inertia, and shows in two simulations that the platform improves attitude stability from about 1e-4 to 1e-6 deg/s.
* [Gómez-San-Juan, Pérez-Grande & Sanz-Andrés (2018) — Uncertainty calculation for spacecraft thermal models using a generalized SEA method](/sources/gomezsanjuan-2018.md) — Proposes the one-dimensional generalized statistical error analysis (OGS), which screens parameters by linear sensitivity, fits one-dimensional non-linear surrogates, maps parameter PDFs by change of variables and convolves them, and shows on the Solar Orbiter EPD-STEP thermal model that it stays within 1.2 °C of Monte Carlo limits at under 3% of its cost.
* [Haley et al. (2018) — Massive scale, long battery life, direct to orbit connectivity for the internet of things](/sources/haley-2018.md) — An industrial overview of the Myriota direct-to-orbit IoT platform (uncoordinated VHF uplink from low-power terminals to LEO satellites that store sampled spectrum for a cloud multiuser receiver), with a livestock water-tank pilot, field SNR statistics and model-predicted battery life; no propulsion or plasma content.
* [Helman (2018) — Galactic distribution of chirality sources of organic molecules](/sources/helman-2018.md) — An astrobiology perspective essay surveying four processes said to produce chiral excess in organic molecules, offering a user-filled spreadsheet to rank galactic sources of chirally enriched organics, and asserting without a model that planetary inner cores behave as high-density plasmas; no equations, data or propulsion content.
* [Hippke (2018) — Benchmarking information carriers](/sources/hippke-2018.md) — An order-of-magnitude comparison of candidate carriers for interstellar communication (photons, inscribed matter, charged, short-lived and hypothetical particles, gravitational waves, neutrinos) by energy per bit, speed, focusing, extinction and lensing, concluding that photons win by orders of magnitude for fast point-to-point links and inscribed matter when latency does not matter.
* [Hippke, Leyland & Learned (2018) — Benchmarking inscribed matter probes](/sources/hippke-2018-b.md) — Order-of-magnitude estimates showing that shielding of a data-carrying interstellar probe adds negligible mass below 0.2c, adopting a storage density of 1e23 bits per gram, and comparing the resulting 1e11 bits per joule at 0.1c with photon links, whose capacity falls as the inverse square of distance unless receivers sit at stellar gravitational lenses.
* [Hou et al. (2018) — Spacecraft angular velocity trajectory planning for SGCMG singularity avoidance](/sources/hou-2018.md) — Avoids impassable singular states of a single-gimbal control moment gyroscope cluster by shaping the reference angular-velocity trajectory of a rest-to-rest maneuver as two approximately decoupled bang-off-bang rotations (a cone-type trajectory) in a new four-element attitude parameter set, with a Lyapunov-stable tracking controller and a grid search for the free rotation axis.
* [Huang et al. (2018) — A new explorer mission for soft X-ray timing - Observation of the Crab pulsar](/sources/huang-2018.md) — Crab pulsar timing from 162 observations by the soft X-ray spectrometer on China's XPNAV-1 satellite: data-reduction rules, epoch folding, FFT cross-correlation phase delays and a weighted least-squares ephemeris correction giving a 55.94 microsecond post-fit RMS residual; no propulsion or plasma content.
* [Huang et al. (2018) — Fixed-time attitude tracking control for spacecraft without unwinding](/sources/huang-2018-b.md) — Two fixed-time attitude tracking controllers on the rotation group (avoiding quaternion unwinding): a nonsingular terminal sliding surface with a Gudermannian reaching law giving initial-condition-independent settling-time bounds, and an adaptive variant converging to residual sets under bounded disturbance, with proved auxiliary lemmas and simulations of a rigid microsatellite.
* [Huang et al. (2018) — Investigation on dynamic behaviors of thermal protection system using a two degree-of-freedom nonlinear theoretical method](/sources/huang-2018-c.md) — Models a ceramic thermal-protection tile on a strain-isolation pad with a cubic-stiffness spring as a two-degree-of-freedom system under Gaussian random acoustic or base excitation, replaces it by an equivalent linear system through statistical linearization, and shows that the equivalent pad stiffness and all responses fall below those of a least-squares linear model as the nonlinearity grows.
* [Joffre et al. (2018) — Trajectory design and guidance for landing on Phobos](/sources/joffre-2018.md) — Designs Phobos landings from libration-point-orbit manifolds with a polynomial open-loop guidance optimised by an interior-point method (about 7 m/s against 16.5 m/s for a forced vertical descent), shows by Monte Carlo on a perturbed truth model that open-loop guidance diverges, and brings a waypoint-based terminal-velocity guidance close to the open-loop cost.
* [Ke et al. (2018) — The influences of aramid fibre and its compound with ceramic on shielding performance of stuffed layer](/sources/ke-2018.md) — Light-gas-gun tests of 17 stuffed Whipple shields hit by 5 mm aluminium spheres at 4.55–4.96 km/s show that Aramid-III and PBO fabrics outperform Aramid-II in a basalt-fabric stuffed layer, that alternating aramid and basalt layers beat block stacking, and that sewing or gluing the layers degrades rear-plate protection.
* [Kim, Jung & Bang (2018) — Linear time-varying model predictive control of magnetically actuated satellites in elliptic orbits](/sources/kim-2018.md) — Designs a constrained linear time-varying model predictive attitude controller for magnetorquer-only small satellites in elliptic low Earth orbits, augmenting the eccentricity-driven disturbance into the state, and for inertial pointing adds exponential data weighting and Laguerre-function parameterisation, which in noise-free nonlinear simulations converge within about two orbits where the conventional controller diverges.
* [Knight & Mortazavi (2018) — Hypersonic shock wave transitional boundary layer interactions - A review](/sources/knight-2018.md) — Reviews hypersonic shock-wave/transitional-boundary-layer interaction research since 1993 in 14 groups of studies, proposes a four-class categorisation of interactions, documents that transitional interactions can exceed laminar and turbulent peak heating, and judges DNS the most promising predictor, limited by ad hoc inflow forcing.
* [Kumar et al. (2018) — Fault-tolerant attitude control of miniature satellites using reaction wheels](/sources/kumar-2018.md) — Proposes an adaptive sliding-mode attitude tracking law for a miniature satellite with four reaction wheels that tolerates wheel failure or fading without fault detection and isolation, proves uniform ultimate boundedness by a Lyapunov argument, and demonstrates attitude recovery in hardware-in-the-loop tests of three fault cases.
* [Le May et al. (2018) — Space debris collision probability analysis for proposed global broadband constellations](/sources/lemay-2018.md) — Estimates with ESA's MASTER-2009 debris flux the collisions expected over a 5-year operational phase for the proposed OneWeb (720 satellites) and SpaceX (4591 satellites) constellations, classed by energy-to-mass ratio and trackability, finding at least one collision near-certain for both and a probability of at least one catastrophic collision of 5.0% for OneWeb and 45.8% for SpaceX under intermediate mitigation.
* [Li & Chen (2018) — Historical-orbital-data-based method for monitoring the operational status of satellites in low Earth orbit](/sources/li-2018-b.md) — Detects orbit-maintenance manoeuvres of a low-Earth-orbit satellite from its public two-line-element history by anomalies in SGP4 prediction errors of the semi-major axis, and declares the satellite retired when the time and the semi-major-axis decrement since the last manoeuvre both exceed mean plus three standard deviations of their past values; applied to RADARSAT, TERRA and METOP-A.
* [Li et al. (2018) — Aging constitutive model of hydroxyl-terminated polybutadiene coating in solid rocket motor](/sources/li-2018.md) — Builds a phenomenological aging constitutive model of an HTPB solid-rocket-motor coating in which NMR crosslink density, fitted by a power law in time with a modified Arrhenius rate, scales a second-order polynomial hyperelastic equilibrium law through a fitted aging function, and verifies the crosslink prediction to 0.19–0.58% at a held-out aging temperature.
* [Li, Yuan & Wang (2018) — Sliding mode control for autonomous spacecraft rendezvous with collision avoidance](/sources/li-2018-c.md) — Designs sliding-mode position and attitude controllers for a chaser approaching a tumbling target, with sliding manifolds along the negative gradient of an artificial potential whose repulsive part diverges at a danger-zone radius, proves finite-time reaching by Lyapunov arguments and shows in separate translation and rotation simulations that the target is reached without entering the danger zone.
* [Liu et al. (2018) — Angular momentum management strategy of the FengYun-4 meteorological satellite](/sources/liu-2018.md) — Presents the reaction-wheel angular-momentum management flown on the geostationary FengYun-4 satellite, splitting the preset wheel momentum into an inertial bias sized against the daily solar-radiation accumulation and a null-space bias, with a Lyapunov null-motion law, a wheel-failure reconfiguration and a thresholded daily bipropellant-thruster dumping law, supported by ten days of flight data and simulations.
* [Liu et al. (2018) — Structural design and optimization of large cable–rib tension deployable antenna structure with dynamic constraint](/sources/liu-2018-b.md) — Proposes a cable–rib tension deployable antenna structure, sizes an 18 m prime-focus design in closed form, selects six units by a frequency-to-surface-density index, minimises mass over lowest natural frequency with a genetic algorithm for three boundary conditions, and validates the finite-element frequency on a 1.8 m prototype within 2.4%.
* [Liu et al. (2018) — Structures of near-wall wakes subjected to a sonic jet in a supersonic crossflow](/sources/liu-2018-c.md) — Mach 2.95 wind-tunnel experiment (NPLS and surface oil flow) on the near-wall wake of a sonic nitrogen jet for seven jet-to-crossflow momentum flux ratios, dividing the wake into three regions and correlating the leeward separation length as a power law in the momentum flux ratio.
* [Liu et al. (2018) — Uncertainty and sensitivity analysis of flow parameters on aerodynamics of a hypersonic inlet](/sources/liu-2018-d.md) — Propagates uniform uncertainty in five freestream and wall inputs through 3-D RANS-SST simulations of a two-ramp hypersonic inlet at Mach 5.9, inside its start/unstart hysteresis loop, with point-collocation non-intrusive polynomial chaos, and ranks the inputs by Sobol' indices for the wall pressure field and three isolator performance parameters.
* [Loureiro, Panades & Silva (2018) — Lessons learned in 20 years of application of Systems Concurrent Engineering to space products](/sources/loureiro-2018.md) — Reviews the Total View Framework and the Systems Concurrent Engineering approach, which applies the systems engineering process simultaneously to product and service elements, presents its 2017 revision adding concepts and architectures for every life-cycle service and hazard-derived modes, illustrates it on the TIM pico-satellite formation and the Brazilian PESE programme, and lists 13 lessons, without quantitative results.
* [Marks & Gorodetsky (2025) — GPU-accelerated kinetic Hall thruster simulations in WarpX](/sources/marks-2025.md) — Runs the axial–azimuthal Hall-thruster PIC benchmark in WarpX on one GPU (1.81 days on an H100 against 2.5 days on 112 CPUs for the fastest 2019 code), shows that resampling below the steady-state particle count heats electrons, that semi-implicit coarsening by 2× stays within 10% in 11.5 h, and that the multigrid electrostatic solver is MPI-communication-bound on multiple GPUs.
* [McDowell (2018) — The edge of space: Revisiting the Karman Line](/sources/mcdowell-2018.md) — Argues from history, upper-atmosphere physics, orbital data and a weight-to-drag ratio at circular orbital speed that the boundary of space lies near 80 km rather than 100 km, finding the effective Kármán line at 66–88 km for typical satellite ballistic coefficients and decaying satellites that survive several perigees below 100 km.
* [Meng et al. (2018) — Parameterization and optimization for shape-transition curved isolator](/sources/meng-2018.md) — Parameterizes a 3-D shape-transition curved scramjet isolator by Barger blending of end sections, area rescaling and a B-spline offset line, optimizes the offset line with a multi-island genetic algorithm coupled to RANS-SST for total-pressure recovery at Mach 2, and compares four 800 mm isolators with and without backpressure.
* [Miranda et al. (2026) — Spectral entropy of plasma oscillations in numerical simulations of a Hall thruster during start-up transients](/sources/miranda-2026.md) — 2D axial–azimuthal electrostatic PIC-MCC (XOOPIC) of an SPT-100-like Hall thruster in xenon and argon at real mass ratio and permittivity, applying the normalized 1D and 2D Fourier spectral entropy to the azimuthal field of the electron drift instability and conjecturing that more ordered fluctuations go with stronger anomalous transport.
* [Moradi et al. (2018) — The influence of coolant jet direction on heat reduction on the nose cone with Aerodome at supersonic flow](/sources/moradi-2018.md) — RANS-SST study at Mach 5 of front (opposing), back (oblique) and top (lateral) coolant jets from an aerodome on a spiked nose cone, with air, CO2 and helium at two jet pressure ratios, finding the top jet reduces the nose-cone heat load most (about 90%) and the opposing jet least.
* [Mungiguerra et al. (2018) — Effects of solar panels on Aerodynamics of a small satellite with deployable aero-brake](/sources/mungiguerra-2018.md) — DSMC (DS3V 2.6) study of a low-Earth-orbit small satellite with a deployable 45-degree aero-brake at 150 km, showing that deployable solar panels remove intermediate equilibria and stabilize the closed configuration but halve the drag-modulation ratio (8.75 to 4.06); off-domain for this bundle except for its account of DSMC practice and Maxwell gas-surface modelling.
* [Muñoz Tejeda, Perales-Díaz & Knoll (2025) — Computational modelling of water-fuelled Hall Effect Thrusters](/sources/munoz-2025.md) — Extends the fully kinetic PIC code PlasmaSim to oxygen- and water-fed Hall thrusters with a two-stage electron-impact reaction set (parent molecule, then dissociation products), a 0D screening model and a partially coupled pseudo-2D axial–radial Poisson solver, and compares thrust, specific impulse and plume ion fractions with WET-HET experiments.
* [Peng et al. (2018) — Experimental investigations on ethylene-air Continuous Rotating Detonation wave in the hollow chamber with Laval nozzle](/sources/peng-2018.md) — Direct-connect experiments of non-premixed ethylene–air continuous rotating detonation in a 100 mm hollow chamber with seven Laval-nozzle contraction ratios map the operating domain, identify single-wave, two-wave and sawtooth modes, report a single-wave average speed of 1915.40 m/s above the computed C-J value, and rank contraction ratios by the relative standard deviation of the instantaneous frequency.
* [Reza, Faraji & Knoll (2022) — Resolving multi-dimensional plasma phenomena in Hall thrusters using the reduced-order particle-in-cell scheme](/sources/reza-2022.md) — Applies the single-region pseudo-2D PIC scheme in the axial–azimuthal, azimuthal–radial and axial–radial Hall-thruster planes against 1D PIC and 2D references: it resolves instability-driven transport without a Bohm model, couples the azimuthal instability to the SEE sheath, and produces a spurious anode sheath in the axial–radial plane.
* [Saravia et al. (2026) — Calibration of a Hall thruster numerical model using a Bayesian approach](/sources/saravia-2026.md) — Bayesian calibration, by nested sampling, of four parameters of a 1D fluid Hall-thruster model (the LANDMARK test case 3) on synthetic data, with a Wasserstein likelihood for the time-resolved discharge current; current-only data leave the plume anomalous coefficient unidentified, while adding thrust and plume electron temperature recovers it within 57 %.
* [Shuvalov et al. (2018) — Control of the drag on a spacecraft in the earth's ionosphere using the spacecraft's magnetic field](/sources/shuvalov-2018.md) — Measures, in a hypersonic rarefied nitrogen plasma beam, the drag of a sphere carrying an internal dipole field, fits it to empirical laws in the magnetic-to-dynamic pressure ratio and the field–flow angle, shows that a plasma jet injected into the mini-magnetosphere multiplies the drag by about 3.8, and extrapolates to a 0.6–0.8 T body field whose ionospheric drag pressure it states to be close to that of two of the four ion-beam-shepherd beams it quotes.
* [Song et al. (2018) — Experimental study of near-blowoff characteristics in a cavity-based supersonic combustor](/sources/song-2018.md) — Direct-connect experiments in an ethylene-fuelled cavity scramjet combustor at inflow Mach 2.52 measure lean blowoff limits against injection distance for one 2 mm and four 1 mm upstream orifices, resolve intermittent local extinction and reignition and a five-step blowoff with high-speed imaging, and use RANS simulations to explain the injection-scheme difference.
* [Sun & Wu (2018) — Ignition mechanism in ablative pulsed plasma thrusters with coaxial semiconductor spark plugs](/sources/sun-2018.md) — Experiments on a parallel-plate PTFE pulsed plasma thruster fired with normal, inclined and no propellant show that the thruster discharges without propellant by surface flashover on the side ceramics, and propose a spark-plug-electron, ablation and ionization ignition sequence whose mean-free-path criterion gives a minimum discharge voltage of about 468 V, against success rates falling from 85% at 600 V to 39% at 450 V.
* [Taccogna & Garrigues (2019) — Latest progress in Hall thrusters plasma modelling](/sources/taccogna-2019.md) — Review of Hall-thruster plasma modelling: quasi-neutral fluid and hybrid models (drift-diffusion electrons, field-aligned meshes, thermalized potential, analytical SEE sheaths, the catalogue of anomalous collision models) and kinetic models (Boltzmann solvers, 1D radial, 2D r–z, azimuthal and 3D PIC-MCC of the electron drift instability and sheaths, plume kinetics, global models).
* [Taccogna et al. (2023) — Plasma propulsion modeling with particle-based algorithms](/sources/taccogna-2023.md) — Perspective review of particle-based (PIC-MCC, DSMC, hybrid) modelling of electrostatic and electromagnetic thrusters: the explicit PIC cycle and its constraints, plasma-wall and gas-wall models, plume models, and the cost-reduction routes (HPC and GPU, sparse-grid, implicit and energy-conserving, reduced-order PIC, machine learning); all results are attributed to earlier work.
* [Takahashi (2019) — Helicon-type radiofrequency plasma thrusters and magnetic plasma nozzles](/sources/takahashi-2019.md) — Single-author review of electrodeless rf magnetic-nozzle thrusters centred on the thrust decomposition F = F_s + F_B + F_w (source electron pressure, diamagnetic Lorentz force on the nozzle, wall momentum loss) and its direct measurement, with current-free double layers, neutral depletion, electron polytropic behaviour, plasma detachment and a table of 18 thrust measurements; simulations appear only as citations.
* [Troyetsky, Bögel & Hara (2026) — Comparison of two-dimensional five-moment fluid and kinetic simulations for the discharge plasma in a Hall effect thruster](/sources/troyetsky-2026.md) — Builds a 2D axial–radial, non-neutral five-moment fluid model of a Hall thruster with electron inertia, two ion fluids and a new energy-limited kinetic flux wall condition, and benchmarks it against an in-house 2D3V PIC-MCC code on an identical set-up: discharge current 10.1 A against 14.7 A, isothermal against non-isothermal field lines, the anisotropic electron pressure the fluid model lacks named as the probable cause of the gap.
* [Veale et al. (2018) — The structural properties of paraffin wax based hybrid rocket fuels with aluminium particles](/sources/veale-2018.md) — Tension and compression tests of SasolWax 0907 paraffin hybrid fuel, pure and with 40 wt% aluminium, at three extension rates and 23–40 °C show that aluminium raises tensile strength by 10–20% and roughly doubles the modulus, that strength rises 60–70% from 1 to 100 mm/min and falls 65–75% from 23 to 40 °C with a turn to ductile failure, and add casting procedures, DSC onsets and thermal conductivities.
* [Wang et al. (2018) — Analysis and reduction of skin-friction in a rocket-based combined-cycle engine flow path operating from Mach 1.5 to 6.0](/sources/wang-2018-c.md) — RANS simulations of a full wide-range RBCC flow path at 16 flight points from Mach 1.5 to 6 decompose skin-friction by component (40.3% of net thrust at Mach 6, combustor 48% of the total), tabulate angle-of-attack effects, and show in a simplified step duct that hydrogen boundary-layer combustion cuts the mean wall shear stress by 57.5% at the cost of a local heat-flux rise.
* [Wang et al. (2018) — Design methodology of the waverider with a controllable planar shape](/sources/wang-2018-d.md) — Extends osculating-cone waverider design so that the planform curve becomes a design input, through a geometric relation among flow capture, inlet capture and planar shape curves solved by predictor-corrector marching with mixed external, internal-cone and wedge parent flows, and shows by CFD that low-sweep planforms raise Mach 0.4 lift-to-drag ratio from 12.1 to 18.3–20.1 at nearly unchanged Mach 6 performance.
* [Yan et al. (2018) — Effect of the heat release on the component coordination in the rocket-based combined cycle engine](/sources/yan-2018.md) — 3D RANS simulations of an integrated kerosene RBCC flow path from flight Mach 3 to 6, checked against direct-connect and shock-tunnel freejet tests, show that the pre-combustion shock train balances the combustion pressure by strengthening as it moves upstream, that the optimal thermal-choking location moves upstream as flight Mach rises, and that distributed heat release keeps the inlet started and raises combustion efficiency.
* [Yang & Cai (2018) — Libration dynamics of electrodynamic tether system for 13 degrees International Geomagnetic Reference Field](/sources/yang-2018.md) — Treats the difference between a degree-13 IGRF geomagnetic field and the nontilted dipole as a perturbation of the classical dumbbell libration equations of an electrodynamic tether and maps, against the electrodynamic parameter, inclination, eccentricity and perigee altitude, how many orbits it takes to turn a dipole periodic solution into tumbling.
* [Ye et al. (2018) — Investigation of RBCC performance improvements based on a variable geometry ramjet combustor](/sources/ye-2018.md) — A kerosene RBCC combustor with hydraulically adjustable expansion angle and geometric throat, designed for isobaric constant-velocity heating, is tested in direct-connect experiments at flight Mach 2–6 with a low-flow rocket pilot and compared by RANS simulation with a fixed-geometry thermal-throat combustor, which it beats in combustion efficiency, total-pressure recovery and specific impulse (+18.6% at Mach 3, +26.2% at Mach 6).
* [Zhang et al. (2018) — Investigation on plume expansion and ionization in a laser ablation plasma thruster](/sources/zhang-2018.md) — Couples a 2D enthalpy heat-conduction model of nanosecond laser ablation of an aluminium target (vaporization, phase explosion, plume shielding) with a 2D axisymmetric multi-species Euler plume model (AUSM+-up, finite-rate ionization to Al3+, inverse bremsstrahlung) inside a ceramic tube, validated on ablation depth and plume fronts, and gives linear fits of plume velocity, temperature, impulse bit and ablated mass over 5-30 J/cm2.
* [Zhao et al. (2018) — Design and high speed aerodynamic performance analysis of vortex lift waverider with a wide-speed range](/sources/zhao-2018.md) — Derives design relations for delta-winged and cuspidal constant-sweep osculating-cone waveriders, compares them by Fluent Euler and RANS-SST simulations with a general osculating-cone waverider of equal volumetric efficiency over Mach 3–10 and angles of attack −2° to 10°, and finds the cuspidal waverider has the highest lift-to-drag ratio everywhere and alone shows a nonlinear vortex-lift rise.
* [Zhou et al. (2018) — Experimental study on a rotating detonation combustor with an axial-flow turbine](/sources/zhou-2018.md) — Hydrogen–air experiments in an annular rotating detonation combustor driving an axial-flow turbine show that a single stable detonation wave forms after a formation process of about 0.159 s with a spark plug and 0.022 s with a pre-detonator, describe the spark-ignited mode sequence, and report wave speeds about 20% below the CEA Chapman–Jouguet values.
* [Zuo & Huang (2018) — Numerical investigation of bleeding control method on section-controllable wavecatcher intakes](/sources/zuo-2018.md) — Compares inviscid parent flowfields at Mach 4 to show an inward-turning ICFC compression beats a 2-D multi-wedge in recovery, length, wetted area and pressure drag at the cost of harder shock/boundary-layer interaction, then shows by RANS that a 20 mm bleed slot at the reflected-shock impingement of a Mach 4 wavecatcher intake removes the throat normal shock and raises the sustainable back-pressure ratio from 73.4 to 76.7.

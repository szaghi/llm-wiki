# Summary

A single-author astrobiology essay. It surveys four processes the author
says can produce chiral excess in organic molecules (quantum tunnelling in
cold interstellar space, circularly polarized light (CPL) from dust,
mineral-surface templating, and electric or magnetic field interactions near
minerals) and ties them to three origin-of-life scenarios (chemical gardens,
geothermal fields, ice substrates). The "method" is a user-filled spreadsheet
(inputs on 0–100, normalized) that ranks sources of chirally enriched
organics in galactic environments; one tab holds the author's subjective
estimates for the prebiotic Earth. A side hypothesis (§1.3, Table 2) proposes
that planetary inner cores are high-density plasmas whose field reversals
mimic stellar surface-flux reversals, in place of a solid-inner-core
geodynamo. The word "plasma" appears only in this asserted analogy: there
is no plasma model, no equation, no simulation and no data analysis.

# Key claims

1. **Four enrichment processes** (abstract, §1, attributed to refs.
   [27–30, 14, 21]): quantum-tunnelling effects, CPL selection, templating
   and electromagnetic-field interaction, located respectively in cold
   interstellar space, regions with energetic photons, dust and magnetic
   fields, and mineral surfaces.
2. **Templating** (§1.1, partly after Hazen, ref. [14]): a crystal face can
   template chiral enrichment by chiral planar tiling, by chiral
   terminations of achiral sublattices (edge effects), or by kinks acting as
   chiral centres; calcite is named the most important achiral-lattice
   template (ref. [15]).
3. **Tectonism conditional** (§1.1, item 4, the author's): if templating on
   magnetically oriented crustal minerals near spreading centres is necessary
   for homochiral abiogenesis, planetary tectonism is a necessary
   habitability feature. A logical conditional, not shown.
4. **CPL sources** (§1.2): CMB circular polarization is minute or
   undetectable (refs. [32,33]); pulsar magnetospheres give no net CPL
   because positron and electron contributions cancel (ref. [34]); white
   dwarfs are CPL sources but exposure is improbable. Hence galactic
   CPL-induced chiral excess is "likely limited" to nebular interactions and
   local magnetic-field dust effects. Stated.
5. **Plasma inner core** (§1.3, Table 2, the author's hypothesis): Earth's
   inner core "may be modeled as a high-density plasma", with reversals
   following stellar surface-flux reversal (poleward migration of remanent
   flux, ref. [35]), claimed "a simpler explanation for geomagnetic reversals
   than a geodynamo model". Table 2 compares the two pictures on transverse
   seismic waves, the geomagnetic field and chemical phase relations. Pure
   assertion: no physical model, no plasma parameter estimate, no reference
   for the plasma-core model.
6. **Proposed chain** (§1.3, hypothesis): magnetic fields charge dust
   surfaces, photons interacting with the dust become CPL, CPL of sufficient
   energy enriches one enantiomer, and magnetic reversal periods would
   change the predominant chirality.
7. **Prebiotic-Earth worksheet** (§3, subjective inputs): "racemization"
   effectiveness "about 25% in total", "approximately 10%" for templating,
   "30%" for mineral electrical and magnetic fields. The author states that
   "much of the needed data are not present, and only subjective estimates
   are possible".

# Methods

No physical model, equations, numerics or code beyond a spreadsheet. The
worksheet (§2) takes 0–100 inputs of relative importance or effectiveness
and normalizes the results; its structure follows the process flow chart of
Fig. 1 (organics in hot cores and the diffuse ISM, cold quantum effects,
minor CPL from CMB, pulsars and white dwarfs, CPL from nebular dust,
magnetic-field effects near star, planet or satellite, reversal processes,
local minerals, chemical-garden, geothermal and ice settings, templating and
seeding, magnetic or electrical effects). It has three tabs (§3):
"Worksheet" (template), "Arbitrary Values" (inputs 10, 20, …, 100, 0, 10, …
to check the formulae) and "Estimated Values" (the author's prebiotic-Earth
estimates). Table 1 is a rubric analysis of the NASA definition of life.

# Tests and results

One case, the prebiotic-Earth tab (§3), under six stated assumptions: hot-core
contributions already absent; current ISM organics flux set low; quantum and
astronomical-CPL effects minimal or nil; near-Earth magnetic-field effects
minimal but not zero; layered clay deposition the most important templating
process (slightly over half of mineral templating), recrystallization
(notably calcite) most of the rest; clay minerals and zeolites the most
important for mineral-electricity effects. Output as in claim 7. No
validation of any kind.

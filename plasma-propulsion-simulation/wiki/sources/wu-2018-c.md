# Summary

The paper designs an "integrated" robust H∞ attitude-tracking controller
for a flexible spacecraft (a rigid hub with flexible appendages, in a
small-angle linearised model): a static output-feedback, PD-like term
$T_u=-K_0y$ plus a feedforward term on the desired attitude. It proves
closed-loop asymptotic stability for positive-definite gains,
independently of the number of retained modes and of the parameter
values (Theorem 1, Remark 1); poses a disturbance-attenuation bound
$\|T\|_\infty<\delta$ as an LMI (Theorem 2, Eqs. 41–46); handles
parameter uncertainty by a polytopic combination of operating modes
(Eqs. 47–49); and derives a spectral-norm bound on the sensor/actuator
non-collocation perturbations that preserves stability (Theorem 3,
Eq. 51). Simulations of a spacecraft with two solar panels, with
beginning-, middle- and end-of-life parameter sets, give roll tracking
errors of ±0.005° and ±0.005°/s after about 2700 s, against ±0.05° and
±0.05°/s for a PD-plus-feedforward baseline (Table 1). The paper has no
plasma or propulsion content.

# Key claims

Symbols are the paper's own (see the notation map).

1. **Model** (§2.1, Eqs. 1–3, attributed to [14,31]):
   $J\ddot\vartheta+\sum_{i=1}^{3}\Gamma_i\ddot\eta_i=T_u+T_d$ and
   $\ddot\eta_i+2\xi_i\omega_i\dot\eta_i+\omega_i^2\eta_i+\Gamma_i^T\ddot\vartheta=0$,
   recast as $M\ddot\psi+D\dot\psi+K\psi=LT_u+LT_d$ with outputs
   $y_1=L^T\psi$, $y_2=L^T\dot\psi$.
2. **Definiteness** (Lemma 1, Eqs. 8–9): $M>0$, $D\ge0$, $K\ge0$ "from
   the modal identity [34]"; stabilisability and detectability by rank
   conditions [33]. Stated with citations.
3. **Stability** (Theorem 1, Eqs. 24–38): with $T_u=-K_0y$,
   $K_0=[K_{01}\ K_{02}]$, $K_{01},K_{02}>0$, the closed loop
   $M\ddot\psi+D^\times\dot\psi+K^\times\psi=LT_d$, with
   $D^\times=D+LK_{02}L^T$ and $K^\times=K+LK_{01}L^T$, is asymptotically
   stable for $T_d=0$. Proved with a Lyapunov matrix built from
   $K^\times$, $M$ and a small scalar weight.
4. **Spillover** (Remark 1): stability depends only on the positive
   definiteness of $M$, $D^\times$, $K^\times$, whatever the number of
   retained modes or the parameter values, so spillover instability "can
   be completely avoided". Stated as a consequence of Theorem 1; it holds
   for exactly collocated outputs only (claim 6).
5. **H∞ bound** (Theorem 2, Eqs. 39–46): a controller with closed-loop
   $\|T\|_\infty<\delta$ (from $T_d$ to a weighted output) always exists;
   the condition reduces to an LMI that holds for sufficiently large
   $\delta$, with an explicit lower bound (Eq. 46). Proved via the
   bounded real lemma [35] and a Schur complement. Parameter
   uncertainty: the LMI is imposed at each vertex of a polytope of
   operating modes (Eqs. 47–49, method attributed to [36,37]).
6. **Non-collocation** (the first Theorem 3, Eqs. 50–60): with measured
   outputs $y_1=(L+\Delta_1)^T\psi$, $y_2=(L+\Delta_2)^T\dot\psi$, the loop
   is asymptotically stable if
   $\|K_{01}\|_s\|\Delta_1\|_s+\|K_{02}\|_s\|\Delta_2\|_s$ stays below an
   eigenvalue bound (Eq. 51). Proved with a Lyapunov function and the
   in-paper Lemma 4.
7. **Integrated loop** (the second Theorem 3, Eqs. 61–67): with the
   feedforward on the desired attitude, the loop is asymptotically stable
   if $K^\times-LK_0K_{v1}-LK_{v2}>0$ (Eq. 63). The proof is sketched
   "following the lines" of Theorem 1.
8. **Simulation** (§4, Table 1): integrated controller ±0.005°,
   ±0.005°/s, convergence 2700 s, torque below 35 N·m; PD-plus baseline
   ±0.05°, ±0.05°/s, 2700 s, below 38 N·m; the LMI gives $\delta=0.007$.
   Shown by simulation, roll axis only.

# Methods

* Model: linear, small-angle; rigid hub plus flexible appendages in modal
  coordinates (three appendages in Eq. 1; the simulations use two solar
  panels with two modes each).
* Control: static output feedback on angle and rate plus feedforward on
  the desired angle; gains from LMIs; polytopic treatment of parameter
  uncertainty; H∞ attenuation via the bounded real lemma [35]; Schur
  complement (Lemma 2); an eigenvalue-ratio bound for Lyapunov solutions
  (Lemma 4, proved in the paper, Eqs. 13–23). The weights are chosen "by
  trial and error" (§4).
* Not given: LMI solver, integrator, time step, and the disturbance
  torque used in the simulations.

# Tests and results

* Parameters (§4): beginning of life, hub inertia diag(3040, 1800,
  3950) kg·m², panel modal frequencies 0.02 and 0.061 rad/s (panel 1),
  0.021 and 0.059 rad/s (panel 2), damping ratio 0.005; middle of life
  diag(2840, 1700, 3630) kg·m²; end of life diag(2735, 1650, 3535)
  kg·m², with slightly lower frequencies.
* Gains: $K_{01}=$ diag(190.2, 190.2, 190.2), $K_{02}=$ diag(20.52,
  20.05, 20.28), $K_{v2}=$ diag(0.0052, −236.27, −236.27); baseline
  $K_p=$ diag(200, 200, 200), $K_D=$ diag(15.2, 15.7, 15.4).
* Initial attitude $(11.5, -8.6, 6.9)$°; desired attitude sinusoidal
  with amplitudes 0.01°, 0.01°, 0.015°.
* Integrated controller (Figs. 1–4): roll error and rate within ±0.005°
  and ±0.005°/s after about 2700 s; panel modes suppressed (Fig. 3).
  Baseline (Figs. 5–8): roll within ±0.05° at about 2700 s.
* Which parameter set is simulated is not stated; the figures are single
  runs.

# Methods

Algorithms and schemes.

* [Bayesian calibration of thruster models](/methods/bayesian-calibration.md) — Inference of the free parameters of a reduced thruster model (anomalous-transport coefficients, neutral speed, loss and facility parameters) as a posterior distribution conditioned on measured or synthetic data, sampled by MCMC or nested sampling, often through a surrogate; it exposes non-identifiable parameters and residual model-form error that manual tuning hides.
* [Boltzmann-relation electrons](/methods/boltzmann-electron-model.md) — Electron model that sets the electron density from the potential by the isothermal Boltzmann relation, either inverted under quasi-neutrality for the potential (plume codes) or inserted into a nonlinear Poisson equation; the simplest fluid closure of hybrid plume, ion-thruster and helicon models, valid for collisionless, unmagnetized or field-aligned isothermal electrons.
* [Direct simulation Monte Carlo (DSMC)](/methods/dsmc.md) — Particle solution of the Boltzmann equation for rarefied gas in which simulated molecules move collisionlessly over a time step and then collide in randomly chosen pairs within each cell, used in electric propulsion for resisto-jet and arcjet nozzles, heavy-species collisions in PIC-DSMC plume and ion-optics codes, and neutral flows.
* [Electron fluid models (drift-diffusion)](/methods/electron-fluid-model.md) — Moment models of the electrons in thruster discharges, reduced in the slow-dynamics limit to a quasi-neutral drift-diffusion model with a generalized Ohm's law, a Fourier heat-flux law and empirical anomalous terms; the closure that hybrid and fully fluid Hall-thruster codes share, and whose heat-flux and transport terms kinetic comparisons show to be inadequate.
* [Five-moment electron fluid model](/methods/five-moment-fluid-model.md) — Non-neutral, inertial fluid model that evolves density, momentum and energy of electrons and ions with Poisson's equation, applied in 2D axial–radial to a Hall thruster with an energy-limited kinetic wall flux and two ion populations; on a shared set-up it gives a discharge current of 10.1 A against 14.7 A from PIC-MCC, for which its authors name the missing anisotropic electron pressure as the probable cause.
* [Gas–surface interaction models](/methods/gas-surface-interaction.md) — Boundary models for neutrals and ions striking a wall in particle codes: Maxwell specular–diffuse reflection, the Schamberg model, normal and tangential momentum and energy accommodation coefficients, sticking, sputtering and ion neutralization, with their measured effect on plume force, satellite drag and propellant utilization.
* [GPU acceleration of particle codes](/methods/gpu-acceleration-of-pic.md) — Running particle-in-cell codes for plasma thrusters on GPUs: particle sorting or atomic deposition, active-particle compaction, memory-bound particle kernels, and the field solver as the strong-scaling bottleneck, with measured timings from a MATLAB/CUDA heavy-species code and from WarpX on the axial–azimuthal benchmark.
* [Hybrid PIC–fluid models](/methods/hybrid-pic-fluid-model.md) — Thruster models that move ions and neutrals as macroparticles (PIC, with MCC or DSMC collisions) and treat electrons as a fluid, usually quasi-neutral, from Boltzmann or polytropic relations to drift-diffusion with a generalized Ohm's law; the standard compromise for Hall-thruster, ion-thruster, helicon and plume simulation.
* [Laser-ablation plume model](/methods/laser-ablation-plume-model.md) — Coupled model of nanosecond laser ablation of a metal target (enthalpy heat conduction with vaporization, phase explosion and plume shielding) and of the ablation plume as a 2D axisymmetric multi-species Euler flow with finite-rate ionization and inverse-bremsstrahlung absorption, applied to the first stage of a laser ablation plasma thruster.
* [Magnetic-field-aligned meshes](/methods/magnetic-field-aligned-mesh.md) — Meshes built from magnetic field lines and their orthogonal family, on which the parallel and perpendicular derivatives of the electron fluid equations are computed directly, removing the numerical diffusion that the strong mobility anisotropy produces on cylindrical meshes; used by HALL2De and HYPHEN, with known boundary and 3D limitations.
* [Monte Carlo collisions and the null-collision method](/methods/monte-carlo-collisions.md) — The stochastic collision step of PIC-MCC, in which each projectile macroparticle collides with probability set by its collision frequency against a background target sampled from fluid or cell properties, with the null-collision method of Vahedi and Surendra as the common implementation and the same machinery reused for anomalous and wall collisions.
* [One-dimensional fluid model of the Hall-thruster discharge](/methods/one-dimensional-fluid-hall-model.md) — Quasi-neutral, unsteady model of the axial (z) structure of a Hall-thruster discharge with fluid neutrals and ions and drift-diffusion electrons, in which the uniform discharge current follows from an integral of the generalized Ohm's law; cheap enough (minutes per run) for parameter calibration, but dependent on an empirical anomalous-transport profile.
* [Particle pushers: leapfrog, Boris and Runge–Kutta](/methods/particle-pushers.md) — Time integration of macroparticle trajectories in PIC codes for thrusters — the leapfrog scheme with the Boris rotation for magnetized electrons, used by the fully kinetic codes, and a fourth-order Runge–Kutta push with spline field interpolation, tested against leapfrog for unmagnetized heavy species.
* [Particle-in-cell with Monte Carlo collisions (PIC-MCC)](/methods/pic-mcc.md) — Kinetic plasma simulation in which macroparticles are pushed in grid fields that their own deposited charge determines, with collisions applied stochastically between pushes; used fully kinetically (electrons and ions as particles) or for heavy species only, in 1D to 3D thruster geometries.
* [Permittivity, ion-mass and geometric scaling in PIC](/methods/pic-acceleration-scaling.md) — Speed-up of fully kinetic PIC by enlarging the vacuum permittivity, reducing the ion mass or shrinking the device, which relaxes the Debye-length and plasma-frequency constraints at the cost of altering sheaths, instabilities and the resolved discharge profiles.
* [Reduced-order (pseudo-2D) PIC](/methods/reduced-order-pic.md) — Particle-in-cell scheme that replaces the 2D Poisson problem by 1D Poisson problems on region-averaged charge densities and approximates the potential as a sum of one-dimensional potentials, cutting cell and particle counts toward those of a 1D run.
* [Semi-implicit, implicit and energy-conserving PIC](/methods/implicit-and-energy-conserving-pic.md) — PIC time-integration schemes (direct-implicit, implicit-moment, energy-conserving, and the semi-implicit electrostatic scheme of Barnes) that relax the explicit Debye-length and plasma-frequency constraints at the price of numerical heating or cooling, with particle resampling and numerical heating as measured in a GPU Hall-thruster benchmark.
* [Spectral entropy of plasma oscillations](/methods/spectral-entropy.md) — The normalized Shannon entropy of the Fourier power spectrum of a field, in one or two dimensions, used as a scalar measure of how organized the azimuthal electric-field fluctuations of a Hall-thruster PIC simulation are; 0 means one mode holds all the power, 1 means power spread uniformly.

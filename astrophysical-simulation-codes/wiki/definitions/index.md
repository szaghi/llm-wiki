# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Reduced speed of light approximation](/definitions/reduced-speed-of-light.md) — Replacing the speed of light c by a smaller ĉ = f_c c in the time derivatives of explicit radiation moment equations to relax the light-speed time step, with two validity criteria: one from ionization-front propagation (RAMSES-RT) and one from time-scale ordering in the streaming and static-diffusion regimes (Hyperion).

# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [Ambipolar C-shock](/benchmarks/ambipolar-c-shock.md) — A steady magnetized C-type shock whose structure is set by ambipolar diffusion or by ion–neutral drag; run as single-fluid MHD with ambipolar diffusion (Athena++ oblique adiabatic case, IDEFIX isothermal case with super time stepping) and as two-fluid ion–neutral MHD (AthenaK), each against a semi-analytic steady profile.
* [Brio–Wu MHD shock tube](/benchmarks/brio-wu-shock-tube.md) — The one-dimensional MHD Riemann problem of Brio and Wu with normal field 0.75 and adiabatic index 2, whose fast rarefactions, compound wave, contact and slow shock test MHD Riemann solvers and the variables in which states are reconstructed.
* [Circularly polarized Alfvén wave](/benchmarks/circularly-polarized-alfven-wave.md) — A finite-amplitude, circularly polarized Alfvén wave, an exact nonlinear solution of ideal MHD, propagated (or held standing) obliquely to a periodic grid; its convergence measures the order of multidimensional MHD schemes, and its regular 3D set-up also serves as a scaling workload.
* [Double Mach reflection](/benchmarks/double-mach-reflection.md) — The Woodward–Colella problem of a Mach 10 shock reflecting at 30 degrees in a gamma = 1.4 gas; used qualitatively to compare reconstructions, carbuncle fixes, and AMR against uniform grids.
* [Field-loop advection](/benchmarks/field-loop-advection.md) — A weak circular loop of magnetic field is advected by a uniform flow oblique to the grid; the retention of loop shape and magnetic energy, and the out-of-plane field staying zero, test how an MHD scheme keeps the divergence-free constraint and its numerical dissipation.
* [Hydrostatic white dwarf](/benchmarks/hydrostatic-white-dwarf.md) — A self-gravitating white dwarf with a realistic (Helmholtz) equation of state mapped from a 1D hydrostatic model onto a 3D Cartesian grid and evolved to see how well equilibrium is kept: spurious velocities, convergence, long-time expansion and the effect of bulk motion across the grid.
* [Jeans wave](/benchmarks/jeans-wave.md) — A small-amplitude self-gravitating sound wave, oblique to a periodic 3D grid, whose Jeans dispersion relation gives a stable oscillation or exponential growth depending on wavelength; it measures the order of accuracy of coupled hydrodynamics and self-gravity, the effect of Poisson-solver error, and energy conservation through collapse.
* [Kelvin–Helmholtz instability](/benchmarks/kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md) — The shear-layer instability run in several distinct set-ups — a smooth periodic layer with AMR (Athena++, Quokka), the viscous stratified benchmark of Lecoanet et al. against a spectral reference (Athena, Athena++, AthenaK tracers), Galilean-invariance tests with bulk flows (Castro), and relativistic magnetized layers (PLUTO, Athena++); it tests AMR artefacts, convergence to a reference, and resolution requirements.
* [Linear wave convergence test](/benchmarks/linear-wave-convergence.md) — A small-amplitude eigenmode of the (magneto)hydrodynamic or radiation equations is propagated on a periodic grid, often oblique to it, most commonly for one period with the L1 error against the initial state measuring the order of accuracy of each wave family; radiation and damped-wave variants compare against dispersion relations or decay rates instead.
* [Liska–Wendroff implosion](/benchmarks/liska-wendroff-implosion.md) — A 2D hydrodynamic implosion in a square box with reflecting walls, low-pressure gas in the corner below a diagonal; a jet forms along the diagonal only if the scheme preserves exact x–y symmetry, which needs unsplit integration, direction-unbiased stencils and floating point without fused multiply–add contraction, on CPUs and on GPUs.
* [Magnetized Fishbone–Moncrief torus](/benchmarks/magnetized-torus-accretion.md) — A Fishbone–Moncrief equilibrium torus around a Kerr black hole, threaded by a weak single poloidal field loop and perturbed, which develops MRI turbulence and accretes in the SANE state; the EHT code comparison runs nine GRMHD codes on it at three resolutions, AthenaK overlays its run on that data set, and Athena++ runs a variant with different torus and spin as a demonstration.
* [MHD blast wave](/benchmarks/mhd-blast-wave.md) — An over-pressured region expanding into a uniformly magnetized medium, whose anisotropic fast and slow shocks test the robustness of MHD schemes at low plasma beta, symmetry preservation, and AMR against uniform grids; run in non-relativistic and relativistic versions.
* [Orszag–Tang vortex](/benchmarks/orszag-tang-vortex.md) — A doubly periodic 2D MHD flow started from smooth sinusoidal velocity and magnetic fields that steepens into interacting shocks and current sheets; used to compare MHD solvers, divergence controls and AMR against uniform-grid references.
* [Radiation shadow test](/benchmarks/radiation-shadow-test.md) — Radiation entering a 1.0 × 0.12 cm box from one side meets a dense, opaque ellipsoidal cloud and must cast a sharp shadow behind it (Hayes & Norman 2003); it separates moment closures that keep beams (M1, local Eddington tensors) from flux-limited diffusion, and exposes the artefacts of local closures.
* [Rayleigh–Taylor instability](/benchmarks/rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) — A heavy fluid resting on a light one in a gravitational field, seeded with a single-mode perturbation; it has no unique converged nonlinear solution and is used to compare the fine structure and artefacts of split and unsplit schemes, and, with a magnetic field, the suppression of small scales.
* [Relativistic magnetic rotor](/benchmarks/relativistic-magnetic-rotor.md) — The Del Zanna et al. (2003) special-relativistic MHD rotor: a dense disk spinning at up to 0.995 c in a uniform magnetized medium, whose maximum Lorentz factor drops from about 10 to about 2 by t = 0.4; run with AMR and GLM cleaning in PLUTO and on a uniform GPU grid with constrained transport in GRaM-X, with visual comparison only.
* [Sedov–Taylor blast wave](/benchmarks/sedov-taylor-blast-wave.md) — A point explosion in a uniform cold medium whose self-similar solution (shock radius growing as $t^{2/5}$ for a spherical blast and $t^{1/2}$ for a cylindrical one) is known analytically; it tests strong-shock capturing, the preservation of spherical or cylindrical symmetry on Cartesian and curvilinear grids, and AMR.
* [Self-gravitating polytrope](/benchmarks/self-gravitating-polytrope.md) — A 3D polytropic star, in equilibrium or perturbed into collapse, on a Cartesian grid with Poisson self-gravity; it exposes energy errors of non-conservative gravity coupling (spurious heating, loss of spherical symmetry, a central hole) and anomalous forces of gravitational stress-tensor discretizations in a light atmosphere.
* [Shu–Osher shock tube](/benchmarks/shu-osher-shock-tube.md) — A shock running into a sinusoidal density field, which leaves a train of short-wavelength entropy oscillations behind it; a qualitative check of how much small-scale structure a shock-capturing scheme keeps at a given resolution, run by Athena, Athena++, Quokka and AthenaK against fine-grid references.
* [Singular isothermal sphere collapse](/benchmarks/singular-isothermal-sphere-collapse.md) — Inside-out collapse of an isothermal sphere with an r^-2 density profile, whose Shu (1977) similarity solution gives a constant accretion rate; it tests sink-particle accretion and the gas flow around a sink, including a sink advected across the grid.
* [Sod shock tube](/benchmarks/sod-shock-tube.md) — The one-dimensional Riemann problem of Sod, density and pressure 1 | 0.125 and 1 | 0.1 at rest with adiabatic index 1.4, producing a rarefaction, contact and shock; a basic check of shock capturing, including across AMR levels.
* [Subcritical radiative shock](/benchmarks/subcritical-radiative-shock.md) — A steady Mach 3 non-equilibrium radiative shock with a radiative precursor and a Zel'dovich temperature spike, compared with the semi-analytic solution of Lowrie & Edwards (2008); it tests the coupling of gas and radiation across a shock and is sensitive to the closure the reference solution assumes.
* [Zel'dovich pancake](/benchmarks/zeldovich-pancake.md) — A 1D cosmological test in which a single sinusoidal mode collapses into a caustic in an expanding matter-only background, forming shocks around a dense central sheet; it checks comoving hydrodynamics with gravity and discriminates between dual-energy switches.

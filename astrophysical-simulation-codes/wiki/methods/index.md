# Methods

Algorithms and schemes.

* [Adaptive mesh refinement in astrophysical codes](/methods/adaptive-mesh-refinement.md) — Structured AMR as built into astrophysical codes: cell-based, patch-based and block-based hierarchies, subcycled versus global time stepping, refluxing and EMF correction, conservative restriction and limited prolongation, nesting rules, refinement criteria and load balancing, compared across Athena++, GAMER, PLUTO, Castro, Enzo and RAMSES.
* [Approximate Riemann solvers](/methods/approximate-riemann-solvers.md) — The interface flux functions of the astrophysical Godunov codes — HLLE/HLL, HLLC, HLLD, Roe, Rusanov/Lax–Friedrichs and the two-shock solver — which code offers which, what the sources recommend and measure, and the positivity fallbacks and carbuncle fixes they wrap around them, including HLL and Lax–Friedrichs fluxes for M1 radiation.
* [Conservative self-gravity coupling](/methods/conservative-self-gravity.md) — Discretizing the self-gravity momentum and energy sources as exact discrete divergences of a gravitational stress tensor and energy flux, so that total momentum and energy are conserved to round-off: face gravity from potential differences, momentum source from face-averaged gravity, energy source from Riemann mass fluxes times time-averaged face gravity, and the curl-free stress tensor that fixes Athena's anomalous accelerations.
* [Constrained transport](/methods/constrained-transport.md) — Staggered discretization of the induction equation: face-area-averaged magnetic field updated from edge-centred EMFs, which keeps the discrete divergence of each cell at its initial value to round-off; production codes build the edge EMFs from the face Godunov fluxes (Gardiner–Stone upwind construction or Balsara–Spicer averaging) and, on AMR, add area-weighted restriction, divergence-free prolongation and an EMF correction at coarse–fine edges.
* [Dual-energy formalism](/methods/dual-energy-formalism.md) — Carrying a non-conservative internal-energy equation alongside total energy, taking the pressure from it where kinetic energy dominates (thermal fraction below η1 = 1e-3 in Enzo) and resynchronizing it from total energy where the thermal fraction exceeds η2 = 0.1; used by Enzo (Bryan et al. 1995 lineage) and, in a dual-energy-like form, by Castro.
* [GLM divergence cleaning](/methods/glm-divergence-cleaning.md) — Mixed hyperbolic–parabolic control of the magnetic divergence (Dedner et al. 2002): a scalar ψ couples to the induction equation, carries divergence errors away at speed c_h and damps them; PLUTO's AMR version and Enzo's MUSCL solver use it on cell-centred fields, PLUTO explicitly to avoid the staggered-field transfer operators that constrained transport needs at coarse–fine interfaces.
* [M1 two-moment radiation transport](/methods/m1-radiation-transport.md) — Radiation transport by the zeroth and first angular moments of the transfer equation closed with the local M1 Eddington tensor, advanced by explicit Godunov schemes with HLL or global Lax–Friedrichs fluxes and operator-split matter coupling, as implemented in RAMSES-RT and Athena's Hyperion module.
* [Poisson solvers for self-gravity on adaptive meshes](/methods/multigrid-self-gravity.md) — Solving the gravitational Poisson equation on AMR hierarchies: Enzo's root FFT plus grid-by-grid multigrid with interpolated Dirichlet data, Castro's level and composite multigrid solves with a post-subcycling correction solve, and Athena++'s globally consistent FAS multigrid V-cycle over the whole octree with conservative level-boundary ghost cells.
* [Unsplit Godunov integrators](/methods/unsplit-godunov-integrators.md) — Second-order, directionally unsplit time integrators of the astrophysical Godunov codes — corner transport upwind (CTU) with characteristic tracing, the van Leer / MUSCL–Hancock predictor–corrector (VL, VL2) and method-of-lines SSP Runge–Kutta — with their Riemann-solve counts, Courant limits, and the evidence the codes give against dimensional splitting.

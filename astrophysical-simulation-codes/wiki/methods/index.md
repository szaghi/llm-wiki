# Methods

Algorithms and schemes.

* [Adaptive mesh refinement in astrophysical codes](/methods/adaptive-mesh-refinement.md) — Structured AMR as built into astrophysical codes: cell-based, patch-based and block-based hierarchies, subcycled versus global time stepping, refluxing and EMF correction, conservative restriction and limited prolongation, nesting rules, refinement criteria and load balancing, compared across Athena++, AthenaK, GAMER, PLUTO, Castro, Enzo, FLASH, RAMSES, Quokka, GRaM-X and ADAM, with the GPU codes' block-size and host-side bookkeeping findings.
* [Approximate Riemann solvers](/methods/approximate-riemann-solvers.md) — The interface flux functions of the astrophysical Godunov codes — HLLE/HLL, HLLC, HLLD, Roe, Rusanov/Lax–Friedrichs and the two-shock solver — which code offers which, what the sources recommend and measure, and the positivity fallbacks and carbuncle fixes they wrap around them, including HLL and Lax–Friedrichs fluxes for M1 radiation.
* [Conservative self-gravity coupling](/methods/conservative-self-gravity.md) — Discretizing the self-gravity momentum and energy sources as exact discrete divergences of a gravitational stress tensor and energy flux, so that total momentum and energy are conserved to round-off: face gravity from potential differences, momentum source from face-averaged gravity, energy source from Riemann mass fluxes times time-averaged face gravity, and the curl-free stress tensor that fixes Athena's anomalous accelerations.
* [Constrained transport](/methods/constrained-transport.md) — Staggered discretization of the induction equation: face-area-averaged magnetic field updated from edge-centred EMFs, which keeps the discrete divergence of each cell at its initial value to round-off; production codes build the edge EMFs from the face Godunov fluxes (Gardiner–Stone upwind construction or Balsara–Spicer averaging) and, on AMR, add area-weighted restriction, divergence-free prolongation and an EMF correction at coarse–fine edges.
* [Density and pressure floors](/methods/numerical-floors.md) — Lower bounds imposed on density, pressure, internal energy or temperature (and, in GRMHD, ceilings on magnetization and Lorentz factor) after an update or a failed recovery; the sources show that floors are non-conservative by construction, that their form and injection frame differ widely between codes, that they set the plasma state of magnetically dominated regions without changing the bulk dynamics, and that first-order flux fallbacks reduce how often they act.
* [Dual-energy formalism](/methods/dual-energy-formalism.md) — Carrying a non-conservative internal-energy equation alongside total energy, taking the pressure from it where kinetic energy dominates (thermal fraction below η1 = 1e-3 in Enzo) and resynchronizing it from total energy where the thermal fraction exceeds η2 = 0.1; used by Enzo (Bryan et al. 1995 lineage), in a dual-energy-like form by Castro, and by Cholla with a single neighbourhood threshold of 0.035 that resynchronizes both ways.
* [General equations of state in Godunov codes](/methods/general-equation-of-state.md) — How finite-volume Godunov codes replace the ideal-gas law by an arbitrary equation of state: Athena++'s three-function interface and gamma-replacement recipe with log-space bilinear tables and a PVRS wave-speed estimate for HLLC, CASTRO's EOS called on density and internal energy with Newton–Raphson temperature inversion, and the verification of both against exact general-EOS Riemann solutions.
* [GLM divergence cleaning](/methods/glm-divergence-cleaning.md) — Mixed hyperbolic–parabolic control of the magnetic divergence (Dedner et al. 2002): a scalar ψ couples to the induction equation, carries divergence errors away at speed c_h and damps them; PLUTO's AMR version and Enzo's MUSCL solver use it on cell-centred fields, PLUTO explicitly to avoid the staggered-field transfer operators that constrained transport needs at coarse–fine interfaces.
* [Godunov's method](/methods/godunov-method.md) — The first-order conservative scheme that solves the Riemann problem between piecewise-constant cell states at every interface and updates the cell averages with the resulting fluxes: Godunov's 1959 derivation as the best monotone scheme, his exact iterative Lagrangian solver, time step and entropy inequality, the order barrier for monotone linear schemes, the Godunov-type generalization of Harten, Lax and van Leer, and the first-order Godunov runs and fallbacks in the bundle's sources.
* [Integrating cooling and chemistry source terms](/methods/radiative-cooling-integration.md) — How codes advance stiff, point-local radiative cooling and non-equilibrium chemistry operator-split from the hydrodynamics: Townsend's exact integration of the isochoric cooling step for a temperature-only cooling function, the multiple-root failure of implicit cooling updates, and sequential first-order backward-difference chemistry updates sub-cycled under a priori (Grackle, Enzo) or a posteriori (RAMSES-RT) 10% change limits.
* [M1 two-moment radiation transport](/methods/m1-radiation-transport.md) — Radiation transport by the zeroth and first angular moments of the transfer equation closed with the local M1 Eddington tensor, advanced by explicit Godunov schemes with HLL or global Lax–Friedrichs fluxes and operator-split or implicit matter coupling, as implemented in RAMSES-RT, Athena's Hyperion module and the GPU AMR code Quokka.
* [Particle-mesh N-body in grid codes](/methods/particle-mesh-gravity.md) — Collisionless (dark-matter) particles coupled to a grid code by cloud-in-cell mass deposit, a mesh Poisson solve for the total gas-plus-particle density, and cloud-in-cell force interpolation, advanced by drift–kick–drift in Enzo and kick–drift–kick in Cholla; force-stencil and Green's-function choices change small-scale matter power by up to tens of per cent.
* [Piecewise parabolic method (PPM)](/methods/ppm.md) — Colella and Woodward's MUSCL-type higher-order Godunov method: a monotonized parabolic reconstruction with contact steepening, characteristic domain-of-dependence averaging, one Riemann solve per interface and added dissipation (flattening, diffusive flux) near shocks, in Lagrange-plus-remap and direct-Eulerian forms; and the variants the production codes of this bundle run, from split direct-Eulerian PPM to method-of-lines PPM inside second-order integrators.
* [Poisson solvers for self-gravity on adaptive meshes](/methods/multigrid-self-gravity.md) — Solving the gravitational Poisson equation on AMR hierarchies: Enzo's root FFT plus grid-by-grid multigrid with interpolated Dirichlet data, Castro's level and composite multigrid solves with a post-subcycling correction solve, Athena++'s globally consistent FAS multigrid V-cycle over the whole octree, FLASH's block solver with an inter-level correction, and Cholla's uniform-grid FFT solve, whose Green's function and gradient stencil change small-scale power by tens of per cent.
* [Porting compressible-flow codes to GPUs](/methods/gpu-porting.md) — The design choices the GPU code papers document — programming model (CUDA, HIP, CUDA Fortran, OpenACC, Kokkos, framework or own backend abstraction), device-resident versus host-resident data, array layout for coalescing, register pressure and kernel fusion, host–device transfers, GPU-aware MPI and compute–communication overlap, and where AMR bookkeeping runs — with the measured consequences each source reports.
* [Primitive-variable recovery in relativistic MHD](/methods/primitive-variable-recovery.md) — The pointwise inversion of the Valencia conserved variables to density, velocity and internal energy in (general-)relativistic MHD: the five schemes (three Newton–Raphson, two one-dimensional) compared by Siegel et al. on tabulated equations of state, and Kastaun et al.'s one-dimensional master function with proved root existence and uniqueness, error bounds, and built-in projection of invalid states.
* [Pseudo-Newtonian and generalized Newtonian potentials](/methods/pseudo-newtonian-potentials.md) — Replacing the point-mass potential of a non-rotating black hole in a Newtonian code by the Paczyński–Wiita or Nowak–Wagoner potential, or by Tejeda and Rosswog's velocity-dependent generalized Newtonian potential, which reproduces exactly the Schwarzschild orbit shapes, characteristic radii, circular-orbit energy and angular momentum and pericentre advance, and adds a three-term acceleration to the code.
* [Sink particles in Eulerian grid codes](/methods/sink-particles.md) — Lagrangian accreting point masses that replace unresolvable gravitational collapse on a grid: creation where gas exceeds a Jeans-derived density threshold (alone, or with converging-flow, potential-minimum, Jeans-instability and boundedness checks), accretion by a Bondi–Hoyle rate or of the bound mass above the threshold, softened gas–particle gravity, particle time-step limits with sub-cycling, and merging.
* [Super-time-stepping (Runge–Kutta–Legendre) for parabolic terms](/methods/super-time-stepping.md) — Explicit Runge–Kutta–Legendre super steps that cover a hyperbolic time step with a number of stages growing as the square root of the ratio of hyperbolic to parabolic step limits, used operator-split for viscosity, conduction, Ohmic and ambipolar diffusion in Athena++ (RKL1/RKL2) and IDEFIX (RKL2), with a stated cost in divergence round-off growth.
* [Unsplit Godunov integrators](/methods/unsplit-godunov-integrators.md) — Second-order, directionally unsplit time integrators of the astrophysical Godunov codes — corner transport upwind (CTU) with characteristic tracing, the van Leer / MUSCL–Hancock predictor–corrector (VL, VL2) and method-of-lines SSP Runge–Kutta — with their Riemann-solve counts, Courant limits, and the evidence the codes give against dimensional splitting.

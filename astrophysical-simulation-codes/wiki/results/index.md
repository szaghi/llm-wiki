# Results

Theorems, proved properties, impossibility results.

* [EHT GRMHD code comparison](/results/eht-grmhd-code-comparison.md) — Nine GRMHD codes run one weakly magnetized torus around a spinning black hole at three resolutions; their horizon fluxes agree better as resolution rises (time-averaged accretion rate max/min from 3.9 to 1.77, non-monotone with 8.6 at N = 128; normalized magnetic flux from 17.4 to 2.8), and the residual differences are traced to MRI resolution, polar boundaries, outer-boundary placement, floors and PPM versus PLM, not to a preferred code.

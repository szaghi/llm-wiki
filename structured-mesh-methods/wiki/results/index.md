# Results

Theorems, proved properties and barriers.

* [Grid-induced truncation error](/results/grid-induced-truncation-error.md) — The truncation error of difference formulas on a curvilinear grid depends on the rate of change of the spacing and on the departure from orthogonality, not only on the spacing: a central difference stays second order only if the spacing varies slowly (x_ξξ of order x_ξ²), and nonorthogonality adds a term in the cotangent of the line angle.

# Summary

Yu, Jiang and Zhang achieve [free-stream preservation](/properties/free-stream-preservation.md) for WENO MHD by interpolating the solution instead of the fluxes and by solving the vector-potential equation with metric terms that cancel exactly ([conservative metric evaluation](/methods/conservative-metric-evaluation.md)).

# Key claims

1. Standard finite-difference WENO does not preserve the free stream in generalized coordinates; the alternative formulation does (introduction). **Stated**
2. A free stream requires a magnetic potential linear in physical space, which standard Hamilton–Jacobi WENO on the lattice may not preserve (introduction). **Derived**
3. The new schemes preserve the free stream on randomized and moving grids where standard WENO errs (conclusions). **Shown**

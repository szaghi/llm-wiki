# Summary

Peng and Deiterding extend a Cartesian block-structured AMR framework to mapped curvilinear meshes ([boundary-fitted coordinate system](/definitions/boundary-fitted-coordinate-system.md)) for detonation simulations.

# Version read

Version of record (open access), Comput. Phys. Commun. 288, 108752 (2023). Abstract, §1 and §2 read by structure; results in outline.

# Key claims

1. Restriction and prolongation on a mapped mesh are weighted by the cell volumes, and coarse–fine flux correction keeps conservation (§2.5). **Stated**
2. 3-D detonations in a 90° pipe bend and a round tube are simulated with rectangular and spinning modes (results). **Shown**

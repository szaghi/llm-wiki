# Summary

Mao and Xi combine a structure-preserving Lagrangian MHD step on curvilinear meshes with mesh smoothing and remapping ([boundary-fitted coordinate system](/definitions/boundary-fitted-coordinate-system.md)).

# Version read

Version of record, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 48, A1338–A1366 (2026). Abstract, §1 and §§3.3–3.4 by structure read; tests in outline.

# Key claims

1. The scheme is the first Lagrangian/ALE method conserving magnetic helicity, with any high-order time integrator (abstract, §1). **Stated**
2. Limited harmonic smoothing with fixed boundary nodes, triggered by quality indicators, controls mesh distortion (§3.3). **Stated**

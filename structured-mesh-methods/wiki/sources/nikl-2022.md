# Summary

Nikl, Kuchařík and Weber build Lagrangian MHD on high-order curvilinear elements ([boundary-fitted coordinate system](/definitions/boundary-fitted-coordinate-system.md)), using de Rham spaces and Piola maps for the magnetic field.

# Version read

arXiv:2110.11669v3 (27 March 2022); metadata from the version of record, J. Comput. Phys. 464, 111158 (2022). Abstract, introduction, method by structure and conclusions read; tests in outline.

# Key claims

1. Mass, momentum, magnetic flux and total energy are conserved and the field stays divergence free exactly (abstract, §3). **Derived**
2. Curvilinear elements track the flow long without tangling or imprinting (conclusions). **Shown**

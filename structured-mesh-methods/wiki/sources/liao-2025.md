# Summary

Liao and Jin extend the [geometric conservation law](/properties/geometric-conservation-law.md) to Runge–Kutta time stepping in cell-centred finite differences.

# Version read

Version of record, J. Comput. Phys. 523, 113661 (2025). Abstract, §1 and §2 by structure read.

# Key claims

1. The time differences that a Runge–Kutta or BDF method applies to cell-centred variables, applied to face time metrics, satisfy the discrete GCL (§2). **Derived**
2. A conservative spatial difference removes the volume-conservation error to machine zero (§2). **Shown**

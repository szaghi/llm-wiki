# Summary

Tang and Tang build [moving mesh](/methods/moving-mesh-pde.md) methods for 1-D and 2-D hyperbolic systems from two independent parts: evolution by a high-resolution scheme and redistribution by a variational mesh equation with a solution update by conservative interpolation.

# Version read

Version of record, SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 41, 487–515 (2003). §§1–4 read; §§5–6 examples in outline.

# Key claims

1. The mesh map minimizes a harmonic-map type functional with monitor matrices; its Euler–Lagrange equation is solved by Gauss–Seidel iteration with a smoothed monitor (§2–§3). **Stated**
2. The conservative interpolation conserves mass, and in 1-D the redistribution step is TVD (§3). **Derived**
3. Shock problems in 1-D and 2-D reach uniform-mesh resolution with far fewer points (§5–§6). **Shown**

# Summary

Wang et al. bring [moving mesh](/methods/moving-mesh-pde.md) adaptation to tokamak MHD, concentrating poloidal points at tearing and kink layers.

# Version read

Version of record, Comput. Phys. Commun. 294, 108951 (2024). Abstract, §1 and §2 by structure read; results in outline.

# Key claims

1. The mesh equation follows from a variational adaptation functional with a monitor function (§2). **Stated**
2. For tearing and internal kink modes the moving mesh is more stable and better converged than a uniform mesh of the same size (results). **Shown**

# Key claims

1. Metric terms written as the curl of a projected field in a commuting de Rham complex are divergence free by construction (§2). **Derived**
2. In 2-D the approach equals Kopriva's curl form; in 3-D it differs (§2). **Derived**
3. Its rounding errors are more than an order of magnitude below the curl form (§3). **Shown**

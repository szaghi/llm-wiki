# Summary

Deng et al. derive the symmetrical conservative metric method from the finite-volume view of face vectors and cell volumes ([conservative metric evaluation](/methods/conservative-metric-evaluation.md)).

# Version read

Version of record, J. Comput. Phys. 239, 90–111 (2013). §§1–4 by structure and key statements, §6 read; §5 tests in outline.

# Key claims

1. With second-order central differences the symmetrical form equals the finite-volume face vectors and the consistent Jacobian the cell volume (§3). **Derived**
2. The geometric view proves the inner operator must equal the outer one (§3, concluding remarks). **Derived**
3. The method improves accuracy and robustness of WCNS on randomized grids and on the ONERA M6 wing (§5). **Shown**

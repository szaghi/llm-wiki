# Summary

Zhu and Hu overcome the incompatibility of upwind schemes with the conservative metric method by writing the dissipation as local differences that share their metrics ([conservative metric evaluation](/methods/conservative-metric-evaluation.md)).

# Version read

arXiv:1811.06325v1 (15 November 2018); metadata from the version of record, J. Comput. Phys. 399, 108907 (2019). §§1–3 and §5 read; §4 tests in outline.

# Key claims

1. Upwind schemes cannot meet the conservative metric method condition because of inconsistent outer operators (§1). **Stated**
2. Central part with symmetrical conservative metrics plus dissipative local differences sharing half-point metrics preserves the free stream (§3). **Derived**
3. A free-stream preserving hybrid linear-upwind/WENO scheme is obtained, the first such hybrid (§1, §3.3). **Stated**

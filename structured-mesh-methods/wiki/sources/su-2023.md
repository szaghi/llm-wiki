# Summary

Su et al. derive a sufficient condition for free-stream preserving WENO and build metrics satisfying it ([conservative metric evaluation](/methods/conservative-metric-evaluation.md)).

# Key claims

1. The upwind dissipation of nonlinear schemes breaks the surface conservation law on curvilinear grids (introduction). **Stated**
2. With the dissipation as sub-stencil reconstructions, identical metric values under those reconstructions make it vanish for uniform flow (method). **Derived**
3. Accuracy is kept by raising the order of the central part of the flux (method). **Stated**

# Results

Theorems, proved properties, impossibility results.

* [Convergence of fixed-point iteration](/results/fixed-point-iteration-convergence.md) — For x_{k+1} = Phi(x_k) with root xi = Phi(xi), the error obeys epsilon_{k+1} ~ Phi'(xi) epsilon_k, so |Phi'(xi)| < 1 governs convergence; vanishing of the first p-1 derivatives of Phi at xi gives p-th order convergence, as for Newton-Raphson (p = 2) and Heron's square-root iteration.
* [Green's representation formula](/results/greens-representation-formula.md) — From Green's second theorem, u(r') = integral of G f over the volume plus the boundary integral of (u dG/dn - G du/dn); a Dirichlet Green's function (G = 0 on the boundary) or a Neumann Green's function (dG/dn = 1/|boundary area|) reduces it to data the problem prescribes.
* [Solvability of linear systems](/results/solvability-of-linear-systems.md) — Rank, range and null space of an M x N matrix, the rank-nullity relation rank A + dim N(A) = N, and the resulting existence and uniqueness criteria for Ax = b: the augmented-matrix rank test, the affine solution set, and the square case det A != 0.
* [Spectral theorem for normal matrices](/results/spectral-theorem-normal-matrices.md) — A normal matrix has an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors and is unitarily diagonalisable, A = sum lambda_k q_k q_k^H; Hermitian eigenvalues are real, anti-Hermitian imaginary, unitary of unit modulus; two normal matrices share an eigenbasis iff they commute.

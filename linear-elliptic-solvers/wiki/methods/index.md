# Methods

Algorithms and schemes.

* [Eigenfunction expansion](/methods/eigenfunction-expansion.md) — Solution of L u = f, or of the shifted problem L u - mu w u = f, for a Hermitian operator by expanding u in its orthonormal eigenfunctions, which yields the Green's function as the bilinear series sum phi_n(x) conj(phi_n(x'))/(lambda_n - mu) and the solvability condition when mu is an eigenvalue.
* [Gaussian elimination with interchange](/methods/gaussian-elimination.md) — Direct solution of a square system by successive elimination of unknowns and back substitution, with rows reordered before each elimination so that the largest remaining coefficient in modulus is on the leading diagonal (partial pivoting) to limit rounding-error growth.
* [Gauss–Seidel iteration](/methods/gauss-seidel-iteration.md) — Stationary iteration for Ax = b that scales each equation by its diagonal entry and updates the unknowns in order, using each new component as soon as it is available; the simultaneous-update predecessor (Jacobi form) and the source's lack of a convergence criterion.
* [Gram–Schmidt orthogonalisation](/methods/gram-schmidt-orthogonalisation.md) — Sequential construction of an orthonormal set from linearly independent vectors or functions by subtracting projections onto the already-normalised predecessors; used for degenerate eigenspaces of normal matrices and Hermitian operators and to build orthonormal polynomial bases.
* [LU decomposition](/methods/lu-decomposition.md) — Factorisation A = LU into unit lower and upper triangular factors by equating entries in row-column order, solution of Ax = b by forward and back substitution for many right-hand sides, determinant as the product of the u_ii, and the Cholesky factorisation A = L L^H for positive definite A.
* [Method of images](/methods/method-of-images.md) — Construction of Dirichlet and Neumann Green's functions for the Laplacian by adding fundamental solutions of image sources placed outside the region: mirror images for planes and lines, the inversion point R^2 r'/|r'|^2 for spheres and discs, and the resulting half-space and Poisson integral formulas.
* [Newton–Raphson method](/methods/newton-raphson-method.md) — Root-finding iteration x_{k+1} = x_k - g(x_k)/g'(x_k) from the tangent line, second-order convergent at a simple root, with its failure modes near stationary points; compared on one test equation with rearrangement, linear interpolation (false position) and binary chopping (bisection).
* [Relaxation for Laplace's equation](/methods/laplace-relaxation.md) — Point relaxation for the five-point discretization of Laplace's equation: guess all interior values, recompute each as the mean of its four current neighbours, and sweep until no value changes significantly; the simplest relaxation method, with a nine-point refinement as an exercise.
* [Singular value decomposition](/methods/singular-value-decomposition.md) — Factorisation A = U Sigma V^H of any M x N matrix into unitary factors and non-negative singular values from the eigenproblems of A^H A and A A^H; gives rank, orthonormal bases of range and null space, and the minimum-norm least-squares solution V Sigma^+ U^H b.
* [Tridiagonal solver](/methods/tridiagonal-solver.md) — Two-sweep elimination for tridiagonal systems: the ansatz x_{i-1} = theta_{i-1} x_i + eta_{i-1} gives forward recurrences for theta_i and eta_i and a backward substitution, at cost linear in N; applied to the central-difference discretization of a two-point boundary-value problem.
* [Variation method](/methods/variation-method.md) — Upper bound and approximation of the smallest eigenvalue of a Hermitian operator: the Rayleigh quotient of any admissible function is a weighted mean of the eigenvalues, so minimising it over a parametrised trial family bounds lambda_min from above, with equality only at an eigenfunction.

# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Arfken, Weber & Harris (2013) — Mathematical Methods for Physicists, 7th ed.](/sources/arfken-2013.md) — Graduate textbook of mathematical methods for physicists; this bundle reads its chapters on determinants and matrices, vector spaces, eigenvalue problems, Sturm–Liouville theory and Green's functions.
* [Riley, Hobson & Bence (2006) — Mathematical Methods for Physics and Engineering, 3rd ed.](/sources/riley-2006.md) — Undergraduate-to-graduate textbook of mathematical methods; this bundle reads its chapters on matrices and vector spaces, eigenfunction methods, Green's functions for inhomogeneous PDEs, and the numerical sections on equations, iteration, simultaneous linear equations and PDEs.

# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Adjoint and Hermitian differential operators](/definitions/hermitian-operator.md) — Formal adjoint of a linear differential operator by integration by parts, self-adjoint versus Hermitian (boundary terms vanish under the imposed conditions), the PDE version, and the properties of a Hermitian operator: real eigenvalues, orthogonal and real-choosable eigenfunctions, completeness and the closure relation.
* [Determinant and Cramer's rule](/definitions/determinant.md) — Determinant by Laplace cofactor expansion, its properties (transpose, row operations, product rule, triangular case), the adjugate formula for the inverse, and Cramer's rule with its case analysis of singular systems.
* [Green's function](/definitions/greens-function.md) — Response G(r, r') of a linear operator to a unit point source, L G = delta(r - r'), obeying the problem's homogeneous boundary conditions, so that u = integral of G f solves L u = f; symmetry G(r,r') = conj G(r',r), the free-space fundamental solutions of the Laplacian in two and three dimensions, and the split G = G_0 + H.
* [Inner-product space](/definitions/inner-product-space.md) — Linear vector space with basis, components and an inner product: axioms, orthonormal bases and the Gram matrix, the function space with a weighted inner product, and the Schwarz, triangle, Bessel and parallelogram relations.
* [Linear operators and matrices](/definitions/linear-operators-and-matrices.md) — Linear operator and its matrix in a basis, matrix algebra, transpose and Hermitian conjugate, trace, inverse, functions of a matrix, and the special square classes: diagonal, triangular, symmetric, orthogonal, Hermitian, unitary and normal.
* [Matrix eigenvalue problem](/definitions/eigenvalue-problem.md) — Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a square matrix: characteristic equation, trace and determinant identities, degeneracy and defective matrices, change of basis by similarity transformation with its invariants, and diagonalisation.
* [Poisson boundary-value problem](/definitions/poisson-boundary-value-problem.md) — Poisson's equation nabla^2 u = f (Laplace for f = 0, Helmholtz with + kappa^2 u) on a region bounded by one or several surfaces, possibly at infinity, with Dirichlet or Neumann data; Neumann data must satisfy the Gauss consistency condition and fix u only up to a constant, and imposing both kinds overdetermines the problem.
* [Quadratic and Hermitian forms](/definitions/quadratic-and-hermitian-forms.md) — Quadratic form x^T A x and Hermitian form x^H A x, reduction to a sum of squares by the eigenvector basis, positive definite and semi-definite matrices, stationarity of the Rayleigh quotient at eigenvectors, and quadratic surfaces.
* [Sturm–Liouville problem](/definitions/sturm-liouville-problem.md) — Second-order eigenproblem -(p u')' + q u = lambda w u with real coefficients, Hermitian on [a,b] under boundary conditions that make [conj(u_i) p u_j']_a^b vanish; the integrating factor that brings any second-order linear equation to this form, natural intervals where p vanishes, and the classical equations in Sturm–Liouville form.

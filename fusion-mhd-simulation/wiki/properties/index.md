# Properties

Constraints and invariants a method can hold.

* [Divergence-free magnetic field](/properties/divergence-free-magnetic-field.md) — The constraint ∇·B = 0 and how the MHD codes of this bundle hold it: exactly by construction when B is the curl of a vector potential or a sum of terms ∇a×∇b plus a harmonic gradient (JOREK, M3D-C1, VMEC/DESC), to machine precision with an analytic harmonic vacuum potential (JOREK3D), or approximately with divergence cleaning when B is evolved directly (NIMROD).

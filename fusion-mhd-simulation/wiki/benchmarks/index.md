# Benchmarks

Standard test problems.

* [cbm18 ideal-ballooning benchmark](/benchmarks/cbm18-ballooning-benchmark.md) — Linear ideal-ballooning test on the cbm18 series of shifted-circular, TOQ-generated pedestal equilibria, whose n = 20 growth rate on cbm18_dens8 (about 0.24 in poloidal-Alfven units for BOUT++ and ELITE) is the reference value for edge MHD codes and a finite-element convergence case for JOREK.
* [CLT–M3D-C1 tearing and resistive-kink benchmark](/benchmarks/clt-m3d-c1-benchmark.md) — Linear and nonlinear code-to-code benchmark of the explicit finite-difference CLT code against the implicit finite-element M3D-C1 code for the 2/1 resistive tearing mode and the 1/1 resistive kink in circular, R0/a = 10, zero-beta equilibria; growth rates agree to 3-24% and are fitted to the eta^(3/5) and eta^(1/3) scalings.
* [DIII-D gas-injection impurity benchmark (Lyons et al. 2019)](/benchmarks/diii-d-mgi-impurity-benchmark.md) — Axisymmetric benchmark of impurity-driven radiative collapse on the DIII-D shot 137611 equilibrium with central argon or neon gas injection, defined by Lyons et al. (2019) for M3D-C1 and NIMROD and later run by JOREK with coronal-equilibrium and collisional-radiative impurity models.
* [DIII-D reversed-shear Alfvén eigenmode benchmark](/benchmarks/diii-d-rsae-benchmark.md) — Linear multi-code verification and validation case of n = 3–6 reversed-shear Alfvén eigenmodes in DIII-D discharge #159243 at 805 ms (Taimourzadeh et al. 2019), with a kinetic-EFIT equilibrium and Maxwellian energetic particles; run by M3D-C1-K and GMEC, whose growth rates sit with the lower group of codes.
* [ITER runaway-electron benchmarks (JOREK–GO, JOREK–DINA)](/benchmarks/iter-runaway-electron-benchmarks.md) — Code-to-code tests of the JOREK runaway-electron fluid model: RE beam formation after neon and deuterium–neon injection in a circular 15 MA ITER-like plasma against the 1D code GO, and simultaneous RE beam formation and cold VDE in a 15 MA ITER free-boundary plasma against the 2D code DINA, preceded by a non-ITER current-conversion test against GO.
* [ITPA n = 6 TAE benchmark](/benchmarks/itpa-tae-benchmark.md) — Linear multi-code benchmark of an n = 6 toroidal Alfvén eigenmode driven by Maxwellian energetic deuterons in a large-aspect-ratio circular tokamak (Könies et al. 2018), scanned in energetic-particle temperature; JOREK, M3D-C1-K and GMEC each report agreement, with JOREK's growth rates at the low edge and its frequencies below all other codes.
* [NLED-AUG energetic-particle benchmark](/benchmarks/nled-aug-benchmark.md) — EUROfusion reference case built on ASDEX Upgrade discharge #31213 at 0.84 s with an off-axis-peaked Maxwellian energetic-deuteron population driving an n = 1 energetic particle mode; used for linear comparisons (JOREK against HYMAGYC, MEGA, ORB5) and for the first nonlinear four-code benchmark (HYMAGYC, MEGA, ORB5, XTOR-K), which agrees qualitatively and disagrees on chirping direction, saturation level and transport.
* [NSTX VDE benchmarks of JOREK, M3D-C1 and NIMROD (axisymmetric and 3D)](/benchmarks/nstx-vde-benchmarks.md) — Code-to-code benchmark of a vertical displacement event of an NSTX plasma in a rectangular resistive wall: an axisymmetric case (Krebs et al. 2020) and its 3D continuation, a hot VDE in which low-n external kinks drive a thermal quench and sideways wall forces; the codes agree on kink onset, thermal-quench duration and vertical force and differ on the toroidal asymmetry of the plasma current.

# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Artola et al. (2021) — 3D simulations of vertical displacement events in tokamaks: A benchmark of M3D-C1, NIMROD, and JOREK](/sources/artola-2021.md) — Three-code benchmark (JOREK, M3D-C1, NIMROD) of a nonlinear 3D hot VDE in an NSTX plasma with a rectangular resistive wall: the codes agree on kink onset, thermal-quench duration and wall forces, and differ on the toroidal asymmetry of the plasma current, which JOREK's reduced-MHD boundary condition suppresses.
* [Bandaru et al. (2024) — Runaway electron fluid model extension in JOREK and ITER relevant benchmarks](/sources/bandaru-2024.md) — Extends the JOREK current-coupled runaway-electron density model with coronal-equilibrium impurities, diffusive deuterium neutrals, the Hesslow partial-screening avalanche source and Compton and tritium seeds, and benchmarks it against GO (1D runaway-beam formation after D/Ne injection in ITER) and DINA (2D runaway-beam formation with a cold VDE in ITER).
* [Bhole et al. (2023) — Stabilized bi-cubic Hermite Bézier finite element method with application to gas-plasma interactions occurring during massive material injection in tokamaks](/sources/bhole-2023.md) — Adds variational-multiscale (diagonal convective residual, fast-magnetosonic time scale) and field-aligned pressure-sensor shock-capturing stabilization to JOREK's bi-cubic Hermite–Bézier/Fourier finite elements for full MHD with coronal-equilibrium impurities, and shows on JET-like neon shattered pellet injection that shock capturing cuts GMRES hours about 2.7-fold while preserving the thermal-quench dynamics.
* [Bogaarts et al. (2022) — Development and application of a hybrid MHD-kinetic model in JOREK](/sources/bogaarts-2022.md) — Extends JOREK's full-f particle module with an anisotropic (CGL) pressure-coupling term that feeds full-orbit energetic-particle markers into the reduced and full MHD models, adds a kernel-based phase-space resonance diagnostic, benchmarks it linearly on ITPA-TAE and AUG-NLED, and applies it to ASDEX Upgrade discharge #31213.
* [Hoelzl et al. (2021) — The JOREK non-linear extended MHD code and applications to large-scale instabilities and their control in magnetically confined fusion plasmas](/sources/hoelzl-2021.md) — Reference overview of the fully implicit non-linear extended-MHD code JOREK: ansatz-based reduced and full MHD models with their extensions, G1 Bézier/Hermite finite elements with a toroidal Fourier series, GMRES with a per-harmonic block preconditioner, STARWALL free-boundary coupling, verification benchmarks, and ELM and disruption applications.
* [Hoelzl et al. (2024) — Non-linear MHD modelling of transients in tokamaks: a review of recent advances with the JOREK code](/sources/hoelzl-2024.md) — Review of 2021–2024 advances with the non-linear extended-MHD code JOREK: ELM-free regimes and RMP ELM control, pellets, kinetic neutrals and impurities, VDEs and JOREK-CARIDDI wall coupling, SPI disruption mitigation, the vertical-force-mitigation mechanism, the current spike, runaway-electron beams, stellarators, energetic particles, particle-in-cell turbulence, flux pumping, and numerical and infrastructure developments.
* [Hu et al. (2021b) — Collisional-radiative non-equilibrium impurity treatment for JOREK simulations](/sources/hu-2021-b.md) — Adds to JOREK an impurity model in which fluid-advected super-particles carry full charge-state distributions evolved with open-ADAS collisional-radiative rates and projected back as radiation, ionization power, Z_eff and density; compares it with coronal equilibrium and single- vs two-temperature models, and benchmarks it against the M3D-C1/NIMROD axisymmetric DIII-D argon and neon gas-injection results.
* [Isernia et al. (2023) — Self-consistent coupling of JOREK and CARIDDI: On the electromagnetic interaction of 3D tokamak plasmas with 3D volumetric conductors](/sources/isernia-2023.md) — Couples reduced-MHD JOREK two-way and fully implicitly to the 3D volumetric eddy-current code CARIDDI through a virtual-casing equivalent surface current, verifies the vacuum response against a tilted toroidal wire, benchmarks VDEs and tearing modes against JOREK-STARWALL and CarMa0NL, and shows that wall ports couple toroidal harmonics.
* [Jiang et al. (2024) — Development of a gyrokinetic-MHD energetic particle simulation code. I. MHD version](/sources/jiang-2024.md) — Presents GMEC_I, the linear reduced-MHD foundation of the GMEC gyrokinetic-MHD hybrid code: an initial-value solver in field-aligned coordinates with high-order finite differences, RK4, a new simple shifted metric and C++20 compile-time symbolic equation generation, benchmarked against the MAS eigenvalue code on ideal ballooning, drift-ballooning and tearing modes.
* [Korving et al. (2023) — Development of the neutral model in the nonlinear MHD code JOREK: Application to E × B drifts in ITER PFPO-1 plasmas](/sources/korving-2023.md) — Derives a conservative plasma–atomic-neutral source model and implements it in JOREK as fluid and Monte Carlo kinetic neutrals, benchmarks both against SOLPS-ITER fuelling scans of ITER PFPO-1, and shows that poloidal E × B drifts drive early inner-target detachment and the dynamic formation of a high-field-side high-density region.
* [Liu et al. (2022) — Hybrid simulation of energetic particles interacting with magnetohydrodynamics using a slow manifold algorithm and GPU acceleration](/sources/liu-2022.md) — Introduces M3D-C1-K, a δf energetic-particle module for the M3D-C1 finite-element MHD code with a GPU-accelerated (OpenACC) slow-manifold Boris pusher or RK4 guiding-centre integration and pressure or current coupling, shown to be nearly equivalent, benchmarked on the linear n = 1 fishbone, the ITPA n = 6 TAE and the DIII-D RSAE cases.
* [Liu et al. (2024) — Development of a gyrokinetic-MHD energetic particle simulation code. II. Linear simulations of Alfvén eigenmodes driven by energetic particles](/sources/liu-2024.md) — Presents GMEC, a reduced-MHD plus gyrokinetic δf particle-in-cell hybrid code in global field-aligned coordinates (five-point finite differences, RK4, MPI+TBB, pressure coupling), verified on guiding-centre orbits and resonant-island widths and benchmarked on linear n = 3 and ITPA n = 6 TAEs and the DIII-D n = 3–6 RSAE case against M3D-K, M3D-C1-K and multi-code results.
* [Ma & Guo (2022) — SCELT (symbolic computation aided eigenvalue and linear code for tokamaks): A full MHD eigenvalue code in toroidal geometry developed with the use of a symbolic computation technique](/sources/ma-2022.md) — SCELT, a C++/MATLAB linear eigenvalue code for compressible resistive MHD in tokamak flux coordinates, built by symbolic computation that derives the linearized equations and discretizes them automatically (poloidal Fourier, radial cubic finite elements); verified on the resistive internal kink, the ideal-kink beta threshold and the tearing-mode scaling.
* [Nikulsin et al. (2022) — JOREK3D: An extension of the JOREK nonlinear MHD code to stellarators](/sources/nikulsin-2022.md) — Implements in JOREK a stellarator reduced MHD model with an analytic Dommaschk-potential vacuum field and a toroidally varying flux-aligned Bézier/Fourier grid initialized from GVEC, shows that W7-A equilibria are preserved, and benchmarks tearing (within 13.2% and 26.2%) and ballooning (within 6.4%) growth rates against CASTOR3D.
* [Panici et al. (2023) — The DESC stellarator code suite. Part 1. Quick and accurate equilibria computations](/sources/panici-2023.md) — Verifies the pseudospectral Fourier–Zernike equilibrium code DESC against VMEC with a common normalized force-balance error: DESC converges exponentially in radial resolution where VMEC converges at first order, is accurate to the magnetic axis, reaches about tenfold lower error at equal runtime on W7-X, and reproduces near-axis Mercier asymptotics where VMEC does not.
* [Saxena et al. (2025) — Bootstrap current modeling in M3D-C1](/sources/saxena-2025.md) — Adds Sauter and Redl bootstrap-current closures to M3D-C1 as a non-inductive current in Ohm's law, extended to quasi-symmetric stellarators through the Landreman isomorphism and evaluated on temperature isotherms as proxy flux surfaces; verifies it against XGCa, NEO and SFINCS for a tokamak and two quasi-axisymmetric stellarators and applies it to nonlinear quasi-axisymmetric stellarator simulations.
* [Seto et al. (2023) — A BOUT++ extension for full annular tokamak edge MHD and turbulence simulations](/sources/seto-2023.md) — Adds to BOUT++ a hybrid field solver that inverts the vorticity–potential Helmholtz equation in 2D orthogonal flux coordinates for the n = 0 and low-n toroidal harmonics and with the flute-ordered 1D radial solver for higher n, verifies it on cbm18 ideal ballooning modes, and shows in DWRBM pedestal-collapse runs that full and quarter tori reach the same energy loss by different collapse paths.
* [Taheri et al. (2022) — Time-discretization of a plasma-neutral MHD model with a semi-implicit leapfrog algorithm](/sources/taheri-2022.md) — Compares Crank–Nicolson (linearized and Jacobian-free Newton–Krylov) and Strang-split (per-field, and interleaved into the leapfrog with a Douglas–Rachford-inspired coupling source) treatments of ionization, recombination and charge exchange in NIMROD's semi-implicit leapfrog on 0D and 1D tests, finding that the leapfrog-interleaved splitting matches CN-JFNK accuracy at about one third of its cost.
* [Vlad et al. (2025) — State of the art of gyrokinetic and hybrid MHD-kinetic codes through non-linear benchmarking to study reactor relevant burning plasmas](/sources/vlad-2025.md) — First nonlinear code-to-code benchmark of HYMAGYC, MEGA, ORB5 and XTOR-K on the NLED-AUG off-axis-peaked energetic-particle case (|n| = 1): the codes agree qualitatively on EPM saturation, core EP-profile filling and a late counter-rotating external TAE, and differ in chirping direction, saturation level and EP transport.
* [Zhang et al. (2021) — Linear and nonlinear benchmarks between the CLT code and the M3D-C1 code for the 2/1 resistive tearing mode and the 1/1 resistive kink mode](/sources/zhang-2021.md) — Code-to-code benchmark of the explicit finite-difference CLT code against the implicit finite-element M3D-C1 code for the linear and nonlinear 2/1 resistive tearing mode and 1/1 resistive kink in a circular, large-aspect-ratio, zero-beta equilibrium; growth rates agree to about 5–10% and follow the resistive scalings near η^(3/5) and η^(1/3).

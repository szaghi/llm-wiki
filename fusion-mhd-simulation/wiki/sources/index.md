# Sources

One page per ingested source, with verified metadata, a notation map, and an
audit list of pages touched.

* [Artola et al. (2021) — 3D simulations of vertical displacement events in tokamaks: A benchmark of M3D-C1, NIMROD, and JOREK](/sources/artola-2021.md) — Three-code benchmark (JOREK, M3D-C1, NIMROD) of a nonlinear 3D hot VDE in an NSTX plasma with a rectangular resistive wall: the codes agree on kink onset, thermal-quench duration and wall forces, and differ on the toroidal asymmetry of the plasma current, which JOREK's reduced-MHD boundary condition suppresses.
* [Artola et al. (2022) — Non-axisymmetric MHD simulations of the current quench phase of ITER mitigated disruptions](/sources/artola-2022.md) — 3D JOREK–STARWALL simulations of ITER mitigated-disruption current quenches at realistic Lundquist number show q95 staying at 3–4, horizontal wall force below 1 MN and a vertical force of 11–14 MN that peaks on the vessel time after the plasma current has vanished, from wall-current diffusion in the up-down asymmetric vessel, so that the force does not rotate.
* [Bandaru et al. (2024) — Runaway electron fluid model extension in JOREK and ITER relevant benchmarks](/sources/bandaru-2024.md) — Extends the JOREK current-coupled runaway-electron density model with coronal-equilibrium impurities, diffusive deuterium neutrals, the Hesslow partial-screening avalanche source and Compton and tritium seeds, and benchmarks it against GO (1D runaway-beam formation after D/Ne injection in ITER) and DINA (2D runaway-beam formation with a cold VDE in ITER).
* [Becoulet et al. (2022) — Non-linear MHD modelling of edge localized modes suppression by resonant magnetic perturbations in ITER](/sources/becoulet-2022.md) — First nonlinear JOREK simulations of ELM suppression by N = 2, 3, 4 RMPs in ITER 15, 12.5 and 10 MA H-mode scenarios with MARS-F-optimized coil phasing: suppression threshold of about 45–60 kAt (coil limit 90 kAt), QH-like locking at 10 MA, linear/nonlinear X-point displacement comparison, and split 3D divertor footprints.
* [Bhole et al. (2023) — Stabilized bi-cubic Hermite Bézier finite element method with application to gas-plasma interactions occurring during massive material injection in tokamaks](/sources/bhole-2023.md) — Adds variational-multiscale (diagonal convective residual, fast-magnetosonic time scale) and field-aligned pressure-sensor shock-capturing stabilization to JOREK's bi-cubic Hermite–Bézier/Fourier finite elements for full MHD with coronal-equilibrium impurities, and shows on JET-like neon shattered pellet injection that shock capturing cuts GMRES hours about 2.7-fold while preserving the thermal-quench dynamics.
* [Bogaarts et al. (2022) — Development and application of a hybrid MHD-kinetic model in JOREK](/sources/bogaarts-2022.md) — Extends JOREK's full-f particle module with an anisotropic (CGL) pressure-coupling term that feeds full-orbit energetic-particle markers into the reduced and full MHD models, adds a kernel-based phase-space resonance diagnostic, benchmarks it linearly on ITPA-TAE and AUG-NLED, and applies it to ASDEX Upgrade discharge #31213.
* [Cathey et al. (2023) — Probing non-linear MHD stability of the EDA H-mode in ASDEX Upgrade](/sources/cathey-2023.md) — First non-linear JOREK reduced-MHD simulations of an ASDEX Upgrade EDA H-mode: ideally stable resistive peeling–ballooning modes (n = 6–9) rotate at the E×B plus projected parallel velocity, couple non-linearly down to n = 2–3, erode the pedestal continuously, and chirp from about 35 to 13 kHz, overlapping the measured 20–40 kHz quasi-coherent mode.
* [Cathey et al. (2024) — Non-linear MHD investigations of high-confinement regimes without type-I ELMs in ASDEX Upgrade and JT-60SA](/sources/cathey-2024.md) — Comparative review of JOREK reduced-MHD simulations of three ASDEX Upgrade regimes without type-I ELMs (QH-mode, low-triangularity small ELMs/QCE, EDA H-mode), plus preliminary JT-60SA linear pedestal-stability scans showing that diamagnetic and E×B flows cut the unstable spectrum from n = 1–30 to n = 3–9 as the resistivity falls to its realistic value.
* [Ebrahimi & Bhattacharjee (2023) — Plasmoid-mediated reconnection during nonlinear peeling–ballooning edge-localized modes](/sources/ebrahimi-2023.md) — NIMROD resistive and two-fluid MHD simulations of peeling–ballooning ELMs in two DIII-D equilibria showing a secondary nonlinear burst in which axisymmetric counter-current sheets suppress the edge peeling drive and finger-like edge current sheets break into plasmoids, the suppression being attributed to the fluctuation-induced emf.
* [Gopakumar et al. (2024) — Plasma surrogate modelling using Fourier neural operators](/sources/gopakumar-2024.md) — Multi-variable Fourier neural operators trained on 2D field-free JOREK blob simulations and on MAST fast-camera movies reach normalised MSE of 1e-5 to 1e-4, roll out 50 steps in 0.03 s (claimed six orders of magnitude faster than JOREK), super-resolve zero-shot and forecast 12 ms of camera frames in 6 ms.
* [Hezaveh et al. (2022) — Simulation of convective transport during frequency chirping of a TAE using the MEGA code](/sources/hezaveh-2022.md) — MEGA hybrid simulation of a chirping n = 6 TAE analysed with action-angle variables in a frame co-moving with the chirping wave, giving a momentum conserved during chirping; holes and clumps carry the same resonant particles (convective bucket transport), and chirping speeds up when dissipation is increased, consistent with Berk–Breizman theory.
* [Hoelzl et al. (2021) — The JOREK non-linear extended MHD code and applications to large-scale instabilities and their control in magnetically confined fusion plasmas](/sources/hoelzl-2021.md) — Reference overview of the fully implicit non-linear extended-MHD code JOREK: ansatz-based reduced and full MHD models with their extensions, G1 Bézier/Hermite finite elements with a toroidal Fourier series, GMRES with a per-harmonic block preconditioner, STARWALL free-boundary coupling, verification benchmarks, and ELM and disruption applications.
* [Hoelzl et al. (2024) — Non-linear MHD modelling of transients in tokamaks: a review of recent advances with the JOREK code](/sources/hoelzl-2024.md) — Review of 2021–2024 advances with the non-linear extended-MHD code JOREK: ELM-free regimes and RMP ELM control, pellets, kinetic neutrals and impurities, VDEs and JOREK-CARIDDI wall coupling, SPI disruption mitigation, the vertical-force-mitigation mechanism, the current spike, runaway-electron beams, stellarators, energetic particles, particle-in-cell turbulence, flux pumping, and numerical and infrastructure developments.
* [Howell et al. (2022) — Growing neoclassical tearing modes seeded via transient-induced-multimode interactions](/sources/howell-2022.md) — NIMROD simulations with heuristic neoclassical stress closures in which a transient boundary magnetic perturbation seeds a 2/1 neoclassical tearing mode in a DIII-D ITER-baseline equilibrium; a toroidal Fourier power-transfer analysis shows that three-wave coupling among core modes drives the slow-growth phase before MRE-like robust growth at 3.4 Wb/s.
* [Hu et al. (2021) — Radiation asymmetry and MHD destabilization during the thermal quench after impurity shattered pellet injection](/sources/hu-2021.md) — JOREK reduced-MHD simulations of argon SPI into a JET-like plasma and neon/hydrogen SPI into ITER L-mode show that the thermal quench is triggered at q = 2 or q = 1 depending on the q profile, that mono-SPI gives a toroidal radiation peaking factor above 2 before and during the thermal quench, and that dual-SPI removes the peaking even with a 1 ms delay.
* [Hu et al. (2021b) — Collisional-radiative non-equilibrium impurity treatment for JOREK simulations](/sources/hu-2021-b.md) — Adds to JOREK an impurity model in which fluid-advected super-particles carry full charge-state distributions evolved with open-ADAS collisional-radiative rates and projected back as radiation, ionization power, Z_eff and density; compares it with coronal equilibrium and single- vs two-temperature models, and benchmarks it against the M3D-C1/NIMROD axisymmetric DIII-D argon and neon gas-injection results.
* [Hu et al. (2023) — Collisional-radiative simulation of impurity assimilation, radiative collapse and MHD dynamics after ITER shattered pellet injection](/sources/hu-2023.md) — 3D JOREK reduced-MHD simulations of neon–deuterium shattered pellet injection into an ITER L-mode plasma with a collisional-radiative impurity model show fragments reaching the core before the edge radiative collapse, a 1/1-kink-triggered thermal quench with a stochastic transport barrier outside q = 1, transient electron temperature holes, and self-regulated assimilation.
* [Hu et al. (2024) — Plasmoid drift and first wall heat deposition during ITER H-mode dual-SPIs in JOREK simulations](/sources/hu-2024.md) — JOREK 3D reduced-MHD simulations of neon-doped dual shattered pellet injection into ITER baseline and degraded H-modes show a pedestal-triggered outward plasmoid drift that expels material and heats the wall, and ray-trace the radiation onto the ITER first wall to compare energy impacts with the stainless-steel and tungsten melt limits.
* [Isernia et al. (2023) — Self-consistent coupling of JOREK and CARIDDI: On the electromagnetic interaction of 3D tokamak plasmas with 3D volumetric conductors](/sources/isernia-2023.md) — Couples reduced-MHD JOREK two-way and fully implicitly to the 3D volumetric eddy-current code CARIDDI through a virtual-casing equivalent surface current, verifies the vacuum response against a tilted toroidal wire, benchmarks VDEs and tearing modes against JOREK-STARWALL and CarMa0NL, and shows that wall ports couple toroidal harmonics.
* [Izzo et al. (2025) — Disruption mitigation modeling for the SPARC tokamak](/sources/izzo-2025.md) — NIMROD simulations of SPARC massive gas injection compare four valve layouts by radiated fraction (>95%) and radiation toroidal peaking (<1.5 in the thermal quench) and combine, for the first time, MGI with the passive n = 1 runaway-electron mitigation coil, whose stochastizing effect requires q0 < 2 and is sensitive to the ex-limiter region parameters.
* [Jiang et al. (2024) — Development of a gyrokinetic-MHD energetic particle simulation code. I. MHD version](/sources/jiang-2024.md) — Presents GMEC_I, the linear reduced-MHD foundation of the GMEC gyrokinetic-MHD hybrid code: an initial-value solver in field-aligned coordinates with high-order finite differences, RK4, a new simple shifted metric and C++20 compile-time symbolic equation generation, benchmarked against the MAS eigenvalue code on ideal ballooning, drift-ballooning and tearing modes.
* [Kong et al. (2025) — 3D MHD modelling of plasmoid drift following massive material injection in a tokamak](/sources/kong-2025.md) — JOREK reduced-MHD study of low-field-side plasmoid drift after a transient deuterium neutral source in a JET H-mode, comparing the drift velocity with shear-Alfvén-wave and Pégourié braking theories and showing that the size of the E×B flow region and the toroidal source extent used in 3D MHD set the saturated drift.
* [Korving et al. (2023) — Development of the neutral model in the nonlinear MHD code JOREK: Application to E × B drifts in ITER PFPO-1 plasmas](/sources/korving-2023.md) — Derives a conservative plasma–atomic-neutral source model and implements it in JOREK as fluid and Monte Carlo kinetic neutrals, benchmarks both against SOLPS-ITER fuelling scans of ITER PFPO-1, and shows that poloidal E × B drifts drive early inner-target detachment and the dynamic formation of a high-field-side high-density region.
* [Liang et al. (2025) — Predictive nonlinear MHD simulations of quiescent H-mode plasma in the HL-3 tokamak](/sources/liang-2025.md) — Predictive JOREK reduced-MHD simulation of HL-3 in realistic lower-single-null geometry reaches, without torque input, a QH-mode with a saturated n = 2 kink-peeling mode and an edge harmonic oscillation of 7.5 kHz fundamental that is asymmetric between high- and low-field sides; the QH-mode is lost, with an ELM-like burst, when q95 drops from 2.5 to 2.3.
* [Liu et al. (2021) — Self-consistent simulation of resistive kink instabilities with runaway electrons](/sources/liu-2021.md) — Adds to M3D-C1 a fluid runaway-electron model whose continuity equation is advanced by a method of characteristics with GPU-pushed massless pseudo-particles and a modified Boris integrator, and applies it to the (2,1) resistive kink that terminates a DIII-D runaway plateau, with over 95% runaway loss to the wall and conversion to Ohmic current.
* [Liu et al. (2022) — Hybrid simulation of energetic particles interacting with magnetohydrodynamics using a slow manifold algorithm and GPU acceleration](/sources/liu-2022.md) — Introduces M3D-C1-K, a δf energetic-particle module for the M3D-C1 finite-element MHD code with a GPU-accelerated (OpenACC) slow-manifold Boris pusher or RK4 guiding-centre integration and pressure or current coupling, shown to be nearly equivalent, benchmarked on the linear n = 1 fishbone, the ITPA n = 6 TAE and the DIII-D RSAE cases.
* [Liu et al. (2022b) — Thermal ion kinetic effects and Landau damping in fishbone modes](/sources/liu-2022-b.md) — Extends M3D-C1-K to kinetic thermal and fast ions, replacing the redundant MHD density and parallel-velocity equations by synchronization from the particle moments and adding the parallel electron-pressure field to the ion equations; verified on ion-acoustic-wave Landau damping, it finds that thermal-ion Landau damping raises the frequency and lowers the growth of n = 1 non-resonant fishbones in DIII-D and NSTX and stabilizes the BAE-like branch.
* [Liu et al. (2024) — Development of a gyrokinetic-MHD energetic particle simulation code. II. Linear simulations of Alfvén eigenmodes driven by energetic particles](/sources/liu-2024.md) — Presents GMEC, a reduced-MHD plus gyrokinetic δf particle-in-cell hybrid code in global field-aligned coordinates (five-point finite differences, RK4, MPI+TBB, pressure coupling), verified on guiding-centre orbits and resonant-island widths and benchmarked on linear n = 3 and ITPA n = 6 TAEs and the DIII-D n = 3–6 RSAE case against M3D-K, M3D-C1-K and multi-code results.
* [Ma & Guo (2022) — SCELT (symbolic computation aided eigenvalue and linear code for tokamaks): A full MHD eigenvalue code in toroidal geometry developed with the use of a symbolic computation technique](/sources/ma-2022.md) — SCELT, a C++/MATLAB linear eigenvalue code for compressible resistive MHD in tokamak flux coordinates, built by symbolic computation that derives the linearized equations and discretizes them automatically (poloidal Fourier, radial cubic finite elements); verified on the resistive internal kink, the ideal-kink beta threshold and the tearing-mode scaling.
* [McDevitt & Tang (2023) — Runaway electron current reconstitution after a nonaxisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic flush](/sources/mcdevitt-2023.md) — Reduced 1D drift-kinetic model of an ITER-like runaway beam flushed by an imposed transient stochastic field, showing that magnetically trapped relativistic electrons populated by neon pitch-angle scattering survive the flush as a 0.1–1% seed that avalanches back after flux-surface rehealing, so that reconstitution comes with only a 2–3 MA current drop.
* [Nardon et al. (2023b) — Non-linear dynamics of the double tearing mode](/sources/nardon-2023-b.md) — JOREK reduced-MHD simulations of a 4/1 double tearing mode in a JET runaway-beam-like plasma, showing that a violent, helicity-conserving global relaxation with a large current spike occurs only at intermediate resistivity, and explaining this by how fast secondary 11/3 and 7/2 tearing modes, destabilized at the outer island edge, grow relative to the primary mode.
* [Nikulsin et al. (2022) — JOREK3D: An extension of the JOREK nonlinear MHD code to stellarators](/sources/nikulsin-2022.md) — Implements in JOREK a stellarator reduced MHD model with an analytic Dommaschk-potential vacuum field and a toroidally varying flux-aligned Bézier/Fourier grid initialized from GVEC, shows that W7-A equilibria are preserved, and benchmarks tearing (within 13.2% and 26.2%) and ballooning (within 6.4%) growth rates against CASTOR3D.
* [Panici et al. (2023) — The DESC stellarator code suite. Part 1. Quick and accurate equilibria computations](/sources/panici-2023.md) — Verifies the pseudospectral Fourier–Zernike equilibrium code DESC against VMEC with a common normalized force-balance error: DESC converges exponentially in radial resolution where VMEC converges at first order, is accurate to the magnetic axis, reaches about tenfold lower error at equal runtime on W7-X, and reproduces near-axis Mercier asymptotics where VMEC does not.
* [Rahman et al. (2024) — Sparsified time-dependent Fourier neural operators for fusion simulations](/sources/rahman-2024.md) — Sparsified Fourier neural operators (ST-FNO), whose layer connectivity follows the field dependencies of the governing equations, are trained on NIMROD two-fluid QH-mode and GTC internal-kink poloidal-plane snapshots, giving 37% and about 2x fewer parameters at baseline accuracy, a claimed 120x memory saving against SuperLU_DIST preconditioning, and a need for frequent retraining when extrapolating in time.
* [Ramasamy et al. (2024) — Nonlinear MHD modeling of soft β limits in W7-AS](/sources/ramasamy-2024.md) — Extends JOREK3D stellarator reduced MHD with parallel flow and a finite-element vacuum field, benchmarks it against CASTOR3D on a W7-AS (2,1) tearing mode (about 3%) and on low-shear interchange modes (within 10% only with parallel flow), and applies it to W7-AS β scans that reproduce resistive, mild, non-disruptive MHD activity and a dominant (2,1) mode.
* [Sato & Todo (2026) — MHD Stability Analysis of High-Beta LHD Plasmas by Kinetic-MHD Hybrid Simulations with Kinetic Thermal and Energetic Ions](/sources/sato-2026.md) — Review of MIPS MHD and MEGA kinetic-MHD simulations of high-beta inward-shifted LHD plasmas: MHD predicts core collapse by resistive ballooning and resonant (3,2) ideal interchange modes, kinetic thermal ions stabilize them through the poloidal precession of helically trapped ions and keep the activity peripheral at S = 10^7, and new runs show energetic ions up to 0.8% beta lowering growth rates without changing saturation.
* [Sato et al. (2024) — Kinetic-magnetohydrodynamic hybrid simulation of infernal modes in circular tokamak plasmas with effects of kinetic thermal ions](/sources/sato-2024.md) — MIPS (MHD) versus MEGA with drift-kinetic δf thermal ions on n = 3 ideal and resistive infernal modes in circular tokamaks: kinetic thermal ions reduce slow growth rates through a phase shift between the electron pressure and the radial flow, and suppress the pressure flattening (beta collapse) of the resistive infernal mode.
* [Saxena et al. (2025) — Bootstrap current modeling in M3D-C1](/sources/saxena-2025.md) — Adds Sauter and Redl bootstrap-current closures to M3D-C1 as a non-inductive current in Ohm's law, extended to quasi-symmetric stellarators through the Landreman isomorphism and evaluated on temperature isotherms as proxy flux surfaces; verifies it against XGCa, NEO and SFINCS for a tokamak and two quasi-axisymmetric stellarators and applies it to nonlinear quasi-axisymmetric stellarator simulations.
* [Seto et al. (2023) — A BOUT++ extension for full annular tokamak edge MHD and turbulence simulations](/sources/seto-2023.md) — Adds to BOUT++ a hybrid field solver that inverts the vorticity–potential Helmholtz equation in 2D orthogonal flux coordinates for the n = 0 and low-n toroidal harmonics and with the flute-ordered 1D radial solver for higher n, verifies it on cbm18 ideal ballooning modes, and shows in DWRBM pedestal-collapse runs that full and quarter tori reach the same energy loss by different collapse paths.
* [Seto et al. (2024) — Two-stage crash process in resistive drift ballooning mode driven ELM crash](/sources/seto-2024.md) — BOUT++ full-annulus simulations of a resistive drift-ballooning-mode pedestal crash show two crashes, a small one triggered by the linear modes that nonlinearly excite 2/1 islands with turbulence confined near their X-points, then the main one driven by down-shifted middle-n turbulence once island harmonics stochastize q = 2; the crash interval shrinks from 103 to 3 Alfvén times as the toroidal wedge number grows from 1 to 4, at unchanged final ELM size.
* [Strauss et al. (2022) — Resistive wall tearing mode disruptions in DIII-D and ITER tokamaks](/sources/strauss-2022.md) — Identifies the resistive wall tearing mode, a (2,1) tearing mode stable with an ideal wall but destabilized by wall resistivity, as the cause of the thermal quench in DIII-D locked-mode shot 154576, using a cylindrical thin-wall dispersion relation, linear M3D-C1 and nonlinear M3D simulations, and extrapolates 75–125 ms thermal quenches for ITER.
* [Taheri et al. (2022) — Time-discretization of a plasma-neutral MHD model with a semi-implicit leapfrog algorithm](/sources/taheri-2022.md) — Compares Crank–Nicolson (linearized and Jacobian-free Newton–Krylov) and Strang-split (per-field, and interleaved into the leapfrog with a Douglas–Rachford-inspired coupling source) treatments of ionization, recombination and charge exchange in NIMROD's semi-implicit leapfrog on 0D and 1D tests, finding that the leapfrog-interleaved splitting matches CN-JFNK accuracy at about one third of its cost.
* [Tang et al. (2025) — Non-linear MHD modeling of shattered pellet injection in ASDEX Upgrade](/sources/tang-2025.md) — JOREK reduced-MHD simulations of neon-doped deuterium SPI into an ASDEX Upgrade H-mode with collisional-radiative impurities find a two-stage thermal quench (stochastic-transport loss of about half the thermal energy, then radiative collapse via a 1/1 mode) and scan neon fraction, fragment size and speed against AUG experiments.
* [Vannini et al. (2025) — Runaway electron beam formation, vertical motion, termination and wall loads in EU-DEMO](/sources/vannini-2025.md) — JOREK–STARWALL study of an upward mitigated hot VDE in EU-DEMO in which a 13 MA runaway beam forms in 2D, is terminated in 3D by n ≤ 7 MHD at q95 ≈ 2.3 (12.3 to 2.8 MA in 1.1 ms, then re-avalanche), and whose 35 MJ is deposited by guiding-centre test markers on the first wall and the sacrificial upper limiters.
* [Vlad et al. (2025) — State of the art of gyrokinetic and hybrid MHD-kinetic codes through non-linear benchmarking to study reactor relevant burning plasmas](/sources/vlad-2025.md) — First nonlinear code-to-code benchmark of HYMAGYC, MEGA, ORB5 and XTOR-K on the NLED-AUG off-axis-peaked energetic-particle case (|n| = 1): the codes agree qualitatively on EPM saturation, core EP-profile filling and a late counter-rotating external TAE, and differ in chirping direction, saturation level and EP transport.
* [Wright & Ferraro (2024) — Investigating nonlinear magnetohydrodynamics in an optimized, reactor-scale quasi-axisymmetric stellarator](/sources/wright-2024.md) — First nonlinear M3D-C1 simulations of a reactor-scale two-field-period quasi-axisymmetric stellarator optimized at β = 2.5% without a stability target: edge pressure-driven high-n modes destroy the flux surfaces within about 1500 Alfvén times for every resistivity model, only even toroidal harmonics are destabilized, and Spitzer resistivity gives a secondary nonlinear acceleration of growth.
* [Zeng et al. (2023) — Triggering of tearing instability by impurity radiation through resistive interchange reversal in a tokamak](/sources/zeng-2023.md) — NIMROD/KPRAD simulations of neon deposited on the q = 2 surface of an EAST-like L-mode, showing that radiative cooling makes the pressure locally hollow and reverses the resistive-interchange parameter, which triggers a 2/1 tearing mode above a threshold impurity density, while strong parallel heat conduction suppresses it.
* [Zhang et al. (2021) — Linear and nonlinear benchmarks between the CLT code and the M3D-C1 code for the 2/1 resistive tearing mode and the 1/1 resistive kink mode](/sources/zhang-2021.md) — Code-to-code benchmark of the explicit finite-difference CLT code against the implicit finite-element M3D-C1 code for the linear and nonlinear 2/1 resistive tearing mode and 1/1 resistive kink in a circular, large-aspect-ratio, zero-beta equilibrium; growth rates agree to about 5–10% and follow the resistive scalings near η^(3/5) and η^(1/3).
* [Zhang et al. (2025) — Quantitative magnetohydrodynamic modelling of flux pumping in ASDEX Upgrade](/sources/zhang-2025.md) — JOREK full visco-resistive two-temperature MHD simulations of flux pumping in the AUG hybrid discharge #36663 with experimental parameters over the resistive timescale: a 1/1 mode clamps q0 near 0.99 through a slow negative dynamo of a few mV/m, matching the experimental order of magnitude, while 2D runs drop q0 to 0.6–0.79.
* [Zhou et al. (2023) — Nonlinear magnetohydrodynamic modeling of current-drive-induced sawtooth-like crashes in the W7-X stellarator](/sources/zhou-2023.md) — M3D-C1 single-fluid MHD simulations of ECCD-driven sawtooth-like crashes in full W7-X geometry: a near-axis current hump creates two ι = 1 surfaces and two successive (1,1) internal kinks; the outer crash matches the measured amplitude (about 50 %) and inversion radius (r/a ≈ 0.4) but is about 10 times too slow.

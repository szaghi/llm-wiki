# Summary

The paper studies the $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$ drift of a pellet-ablation-like
plasmoid toward the low-field side (LFS) with nonlinear reduced MHD in
[JOREK](/software/jorek.md), and compares it with the analytic plasmoid-drift
theory (Parks 2000, Rozhansky 2004, Pégourié 2007); the authors call this the
first quantitative MHD–theory comparison. A transient (0.05 µs) Gaussian D$_2$
neutral source at the LFS midplane ($R=3.482$ m) of the JET H-mode #96874
equilibrium, with toroidal harmonics $n\le10$, stands in for an ablation cloud.
Two braking mechanisms are tested: shear-Alfvén-wave (SAW) braking, by wave
packets launched from both ends of the plasmoid, and Pégourié braking, by
external parallel resistive currents that close the charge separation once the
SAWs have travelled the plasmoid-to-plasmoid connection length. For source
rates $2\times10^{26}$–$1.4\times10^{27}$ atoms/s the drift velocity saturates
within a few µs and scales linearly with the rate, as SAW braking predicts; at
$10^{28}$ atoms/s it reaches about half the linear extrapolation, attributed
(as a hypothesis) to stronger Pégourié braking aided by field-line
stochastization. A wider toroidal source extent brings Pégourié braking in
earlier. The theory formula with the central plasmoid pressure overestimates
the saturated drift by 3–4×; the gap closes when the pressure is averaged over
the near-circular $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$ flow region, which is larger than the
poloidally elongated plasmoid. The drift rises with the toroidal field because
that region shrinks. SAWs are identified directly in the simulation. The
practical conclusion: the toroidally enlarged sources used in 3D MHD of
[shattered pellet injection](/definitions/shattered-pellet-injection.md)
under-predict plasmoid drift.

# Key claims

Page-local symbols (not in the registry): $v_{\mathrm D}$ plasmoid drift
velocity (the $R$ component of $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$ at the source centre),
$v_{\mathrm D,\lim}$ its saturated value; $p_{\mathrm{pl}}$, $p_{\mathrm{bg}}$
plasmoid and background total pressure; $n_{\mathrm{pl}}$ plasmoid electron
density; $\ell_0$ plasmoid half-length along the field line; $L_{\mathrm{con}}$
external plasmoid-to-plasmoid connection length; $\Delta\phi$, $L_\theta$,
$\Delta r$ toroidal, poloidal and radial half-$e^{-1}$ widths of the source.

1. **First quantitative comparison** (§1). "A detailed qualitative and
   quantitative comparison of 3D MHD simulations with existing plasmoid drift
   theories, which is done for the first time". Stated.
2. **Drift theory** (§1, Eqs. 1–3). The plasmoid acceleration is
   $\dot v_{\mathrm D}=2(p_{\mathrm{pl}}-p_{\mathrm{bg}})/(n_{\mathrm{pl}}m_iR)-v_{\mathrm D}\,2B_\phi^2/(\mu_0v_An_{\mathrm{pl}}m_i\ell_0)$,
   with $v_A$ built on the background density. For
   $p_{\mathrm{pl}}\gg p_{\mathrm{bg}}$ SAW braking gives
   $v_{\mathrm D,\lim}\approx\mu_0v_Ap_{\mathrm{pl}}\ell_0/(RB_\phi^2)$.
   Attributed (Parks, Rozhansky, Pégourié).
3. **SAW braking at moderate amplitude** (§3.1, Fig. 3c). $v_{\mathrm D}$
   saturates within a few µs; the $2\times10^{26}$ trace scaled ×7 overlays the
   $1.4\times10^{27}$ trace, i.e. $v_{\mathrm D,\lim}$ is linear in the source
   rate. Shown.
4. **Large amplitude breaks the scaling** (§3.1, Figs. 4, 12). At
   $10^{28}$ atoms/s $v_{\mathrm D}\approx65$ km/s against ≈130 km/s from the
   scaling; the displacement over $t=2.3$–$4.1$ µs gives the same ≈65 km/s.
   Numbers Shown; stronger Pégourié braking "supported by" strong
   stochastization is the authors' "possible explanation" (hypothesis).
5. **Toroidal source extent** (§3.2, Fig. 5). Raising $\Delta\phi$ from 0.5 to
   2 rad makes $v_{\mathrm D}$ drop earlier (shorter $L_{\mathrm{con}}$, earlier
   Pégourié braking), "as expected from theory". Shown.
6. **Implication for SPI modelling** (§3.2, §5). Artificially enlarged toroidal
   source extents make it "difficult to reach drift levels inferred from
   experiments". Stated, inferred from claims 5 and 8.
7. **Central-pressure theory overestimates by 3–4×** (§3.3, Table 1). Eq. 3
   gives 75 / 42.5 / 8.4 km/s against JOREK 20 / 15 / 2.7 km/s for
   $1.4\times10^{27}$ / $10^{27}$ / $2\times10^{26}$ atoms/s. Shown.
8. **Size of the $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$ flow region** (§3.4, Figs. 7–9). The
   electric field is a dipole whose pole separation is set by the toroidal (not
   radial) source size, so the flow region is near-circular in the poloidal
   plane and larger than the poloidally elongated plasmoid; more field lines are
   bent and SAW braking is stronger. With $\Delta r=L_\theta=4$ cm against 8 cm
   $v_{\mathrm D}$ is "very similar" although local $n_e$, $p_e$ differ. Shown;
   stated applicability condition $\Delta r/(R\Delta\phi)<B_{\mathrm{pol}}/B_\phi$.
9. **Effective pressure** (§3.4). The field-line-integrated $p_e$ averaged over
   ≈1600 field lines of the flow region is $7.25\times10^4$ Pa m against
   $2.6\times10^5$ Pa m at the centre (ratio 1/3.6), which "explains well"
   Table 1. Shown ("crude estimation").
10. **Toroidal-field scan** ($0.5F_0$–$3F_0$; §3.4, Figs. 10–11).
    $v_{\mathrm D}$ increases with $F_0$: the shrinking flow region (higher
    effective $p_e$) outweighs the $B_\phi^2$ tension term of claim 2. Shown;
    interpretation qualitative.
11. **SAWs in the simulation** (§4, Figs. 13–16). Peaks of $u$ reach
    $\phi=\pm2.4$ rad at 1.8 µs, a parallel speed ≈$4.7\times10^6$ m/s against
    $v_A\approx4.5\times10^6$ m/s; the SAW energy $\int|\delta\mathbf B_\perp|^2dV$
    peaks at ≈5.5 µs and tracks the total $n>0$ magnetic energy; packets
    complete a toroidal turn by ≈5 µs; $\delta\psi/\psi\sim10^{-3}$ at
    $2\times10^{26}$ atoms/s (linear regime). Shown.
12. **Sign reversal of the SAW current** inside the plasmoid, consistent with
    the non-axisymmetric $J_\phi$ (positive inside, negative outside; §3.4,
    Fig. 15). Shown, qualitative.
13. **Open effects** (§5). Electron–ion energy transfer during assimilation and
    small impurity fractions could reduce drift; recent JET SPI experiments
    "appear to show similar trend". Stated.

# Models and numerics

* **Model.** JOREK ansatz-based reduced MHD with parallel flow and a fluid
  neutral extension ([fluid neutral models](/methods/fluid-neutral-models.md));
  evolved: $\psi$, $j$, $u$, $\varpi$, $\rho$, $p$, $v_\parallel$,
  $\rho_{\mathrm N}$. $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$ and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0$ hold by
  construction (§2). **No model equations are printed**; the paper cites
  Hoelzl et al. 2021 and 2024 and the neutral-model papers. Full MHD and kinetic
  neutrals are mentioned as available but unused.
* **Equilibrium.** JET #96874 (same as Kong et al. 2024, `kong-2024`):
  $R_0=2.96$ m, $I_p\approx3$ MA, $B_{\phi}\approx2.8$ T on axis,
  $W_{\mathrm{th}}\approx7$ MJ, $T_{e}\approx7$ keV and
  $n_{e}\approx0.85\times10^{20}$ m$^{-3}$ on axis, $T_i=T_e$; $F_0=8.274$ T m.
  Fixed boundary, ideal wall for all harmonics; poloidal grid as in Kong et al.
  2024.
* **Toroidal resolution.** $n\le10$, "sufficient to resolve the low n modes
  (n < 3) that are dominant". The Bézier × Fourier discretization is not
  restated.
* **Transport.** $\eta_{\mathrm{Sp}}=2.8\times10^{-8}\,T_e[\mathrm{keV}]^{-3/2}$ Ω m
  up to 2.1 keV and uniform above (core $\eta\approx6\eta_{\mathrm{Sp}}$);
  $\kappa_\parallel\propto T_e^{5/2}$; $\kappa_\perp=D_\perp=2$ m²/s; purely
  convective parallel particle transport; perpendicular kinematic viscosity
  4 m²/s, parallel ≈40 m²/s.
* **Source.** D$_2$ neutrals, Gaussian in three directions, centred at
  $R=3.482$ m on the LFS midplane; $2\times10^{26}$–$10^{28}$ atoms/s for
  0.05 µs ($10^{19}$–$5\times10^{20}$ atoms). Defaults $\Delta\phi=0.5$ rad,
  $L_\theta=\Delta r=4$ cm; scans of $\Delta\phi$ (0.5–2 rad),
  $\Delta r=L_\theta$ (4, 8 cm), $F_0$.
* **Diagnostics.** Field-line tracing for the field-line-integrated density
  ($\pm\pi$ along the line) and the flow-region-averaged pressure (≈1600
  lines); Poincaré map in the $R$–$\phi$ plane; SAW energy "based on linear MHD
  theory".
* **Not stated.** Time integration, solver, parallelism, cost. The
  $10^{28}$ run stops at $t\approx5.5$ µs "due to numerical issues". Runs on
  Marconi-Fusion (CINECA).

# Tests and results

| Source rate [atoms/s] | Eq. 3 [km/s] | JOREK $v_{\mathrm D,\lim}$ [km/s] | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1.4\times10^{27}$ | 75 | 20 | 3.75 |
| $10^{27}$ | 42.5 | 15 | 2.83 |
| $2\times10^{26}$ | 8.4 | 2.7 | 3.11 |
| $10^{28}$ | – | ≈65 (scaling ≈130) | – |

(Ratios are the compiler's arithmetic.)

* $n_e$, $p_e$ peak at 0.1–0.2 µs; plasmoid $T_e$ equilibrates to ≈3.3 keV for
  rates ≤$1.4\times10^{27}$, ≈1 keV at $10^{28}$ (dilution cooling).
  $v_{\mathrm D}$ oscillates negative during deceleration and returns to ≈0 at
  ≈60 µs.
* Displacement: at $10^{28}$ the plasmoid leaves by 2.3 µs and reaches the
  computational boundary at ≈4.1 µs; at $1.4\times10^{27}$ little motion before
  3.5 µs; at $2\times10^{26}$ none evident.
* For $1.4\times10^{27}$: field-line-integrated density $\approx5\times10^{20}$
  m$^{-2}$, roughly constant after 0.35 µs; field-line-integrated $p_e$
  $\approx2.6\times10^5$ Pa m at $T_e\approx3.3$ keV; flow-region average
  $7.25\times10^4$ Pa m at $t=1.82$ µs.
* $B_{\mathrm{pol}}/B_\phi\approx0.23$ at the plasmoid. Outer region stochastic
  at $10^{28}$ ($t\approx2.3$ µs); nested surfaces survive at lower rates (not
  shown).
* No benchmark or code-to-code comparison.

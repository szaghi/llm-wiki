# Summary

The paper presents GMEC (Gyro-kinetic MHD Energetic-particle Code), the
Zhejiang University [hybrid kinetic-MHD](/methods/hybrid-kinetic-mhd.md)
initial-value code for energetic-particle (EP) driven Alfvén eigenmodes in
tokamaks; Part I ([Jiang et al. (2024)](/sources/jiang-2024.md)) covers the
MHD solver, this part the gyrokinetic particle module and the EP–MHD
coupling. The bulk plasma follows the finite-$\beta$ reduced-MHD "model B" of
Part I, with the perturbed EP pressure entering the curvature term of the
vorticity equation ([pressure coupling](/methods/pressure-and-current-coupling.md)).
EPs are pushed with gyrokinetic guiding-centre equations by RK4, with
[δf](/methods/delta-f-method.md) weights and a four-point gyro-average
adapted (after GTC) to non-orthogonal field-aligned coordinates. All fields
live on global field-aligned coordinates built from PEST straight-field-line
coordinates with twist-shift boundary conditions, discretized by five-point
(fourth-order) finite differences and advanced by RK4; the perpendicular
Poisson problem is reduced to 2D and solved by PARDISO; parallelism is MPI
(decomposition in the parallel coordinate only) plus Intel TBB. Verification
covers guiding-centre orbit widths and bounce, transit and precession
frequencies against $O(\varepsilon^2)$ analytic formulas and the FP3D code,
and the resonant-island-width scaling. Benchmarks: an $n=3$ TAE (analytic and
VMEC equilibria, against M3D-K), the ITPA $n=6$ TAE with and without FLR, and
the DIII-D RSAE multi-code case ($n=3$–6). Only linear results with a
single-$n$ filter are shown; nonlinear fluid terms and thermal-ion kinetics
are deferred.

# Key claims

1. **Model** (§I, §II A, Eq. 1). Reduced MHD (model B of Part I) coupled to
   gyrokinetic EPs through $\delta p_h$ in the vorticity equation; electrons
   and thermal ions fluid. Stated.
2. **2D Poisson** (§II B). Field-aligned coordinates exploit
   $k_\parallel\ll k_\perp$: few parallel points, and the vorticity Poisson
   problem becomes 2D by dropping parallel-derivative terms, an
   $O(k_\parallel/k_\perp)$ error. Stated, order estimate.
3. **Global, not flux tube** (§II B, Fig. 2). The field lines of the GMEC
   grid cover the whole flux surface. Shown (illustration).
4. **Dissipation instead of shifted metric** (§II A, App. C, Fig. 21). A
   numerical vorticity diffusion $10^{-6}$ (normalized) suppresses the
   field-aligned-coordinate instability (Part I used a shifted metric) and an
   EP-pressure smoothing $10^{-6}$ suppresses PIC noise; both "affect little"
   $\gamma$ and $\omega$. Shown; the production value sits one decade below
   visible contamination (Tests and results).
5. **Gyro-average** (§II C, Eqs. 13–14). The multi-point gyro-average from
   GTC (for strongly shaped plasmas) works in non-orthogonal $(x,y,z)$, with
   gyro-planes approximated by poloidal planes of equal $\phi$; four points.
   Stated.
6. **Orbit verification** (§III A, Figs. 3–6). Orbit width and bounce,
   transit and precession frequencies agree "very good" with $O(\varepsilon^2)$
   analytic formulas and FP3D for 1 eV particles; for 1 MeV EPs GMEC and FP3D
   are "almost identical". Shown.
7. **Invariants** (§III A). Relative variations of $\mathcal E$ and $P_\phi$
   over $10^4\tau_A$ with $\Delta t=0.01\tau_A$ are below $10^{-7}$;
   $\mathcal E-P_\phi\omega/n$ is conserved in a prescribed single-$n$ wave.
   Shown (numbers stated, no figure).
8. **Resonant island** (§III A, Eq. 36, Fig. 7). Island width
   $\propto\sqrt{\text{wave amplitude}}$; analytic slope $1.2558\times10^{-3}$
   "consistent with" the fitted $1.1409\times10^{-3}$. Shown; ≈9% gap not
   discussed.
9. **$n=3$ TAE** (§III B, Figs. 8–11). At $\beta_h=0.02$ (no FLR):
   $\omega/\omega_A=0.261108$, $\gamma/\omega_A=0.039025$, $m=5,6$ dominant
   at $r/a\approx0.45$ just above the gap tip; the VMEC equilibrium gives
   almost the same; "good agreement" with M3D-K over a $\beta_h$ scan; linear
   fit $\gamma(0)/\omega_A=-0.003983$, critical $\beta_h=0.001867$. Shown.
10. **ITPA $n=6$ TAE** (§III C, Figs. 12–14). At $T_h=400$ keV without FLR:
    $\omega/\omega_A=0.279350$, $\gamma/\omega_A=0.026743$, $m=10,11$ at
    $s\approx0.5$; saturation after $550\tau_A$ "due to particle
    nonlinearity"; growth rates close to the other gyrokinetic, hybrid and
    eigenvalue codes and to M3D-C1-K; ZLR frequencies close to MEGA, FLR
    frequencies "reasonably close"; FLR reduces $\gamma$. Shown.
11. **DIII-D RSAE** (§III D, Figs. 15–17). With FLR, $n=4$:
    $\omega/\omega_A=0.092972$, $\gamma/\omega_A=0.004593$, localized near
    $q_{\min}$ at $s=0.4$, $m=12$ with weak $m=11,13$; structure agrees with
    EUTERPE and GTC; the $n=3$–6 dispersion agrees with the multi-code V&V;
    frequency rises with $n$, most unstable $n=4,5$. Shown; agreement worded
    differently in §I, §III D and §IV.
12. **Cost** (§III B, App. B, Fig. 20, Table I). $2\times10^4$ steps take
    ≈0.4 h (MHD + drift-kinetic) vs ≈3.2 h (MHD + gyrokinetic) on 32 CPUs ×
    28 threads; speed-up ≈20 over 1 CPU × 28 threads; strong-scaling
    efficiency ≈63% at 896 cores; weak scaling "excellent"; gyro-averaging
    dominates the cost. Shown.
13. **Convergence** (App. C, Figs. 21–22). Converged in $\Delta t$,
    grid/markers (≈19 markers per cell held fixed) and dissipation; explicit
    RK4 is unstable for $\Delta t/\tau_A\ge0.04$. Shown.
14. **Inner boundary** (§III B). Its position ($\psi_N<0.1$) does not affect
    the $n=3$ TAE. Stated, no figure.
15. **Generality** (§II D). The precomputed-metric equilibrium file applies
    to any curvilinear coordinates and could be "generalized to simulations
    of stellarators". Stated, not demonstrated.

# Models and numerics

**Bulk plasma** (Eqs. 1–4), linear in the fields, no fluid FLR, ideal Ohm's
law ($E_\parallel=0$), $\Gamma=5/3$; $p_b$ is the bulk (electron + thermal
ion) pressure:

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial\delta\varpi}{\partial t}&=\nabla\times(\delta A_\parallel\mathbf b)\cdot\nabla\frac{\mu_0j_\parallel}{B}+B\,\mathbf b\cdot\nabla\frac{\mu_0\delta j_\parallel}{B}+\frac{2\mu_0}{B}\mathbf b\times(\mathbf b\cdot\nabla\mathbf b)\cdot\nabla(\delta p_b+\delta p_h),\\
\delta\varpi&=\nabla\cdot\Big(\frac{1}{v_A^2}\nabla_\perp\delta\Phi\Big),\qquad
\delta j_\parallel=-\frac{1}{\mu_0B}\nabla\cdot\Big(B^2\nabla_\perp\frac{\delta A_\parallel}{B}\Big),\qquad
\frac{\partial\delta A_\parallel}{\partial t}=-\mathbf b\cdot\nabla\delta\Phi,\\
\frac{\partial\delta p_b}{\partial t}&=-\frac1B\mathbf b\times\nabla\delta\Phi\cdot\nabla p_b-\frac{2\Gamma p_b}{B}\mathbf b\times(\mathbf b\cdot\nabla\mathbf b)\cdot\nabla\delta\Phi,
\end{aligned}$$

with $v_A=B/\sqrt{\mu_0n_im_i}$ and added diffusions
$D_{\varpi}\nabla_\perp^2\delta\varpi$, $D_{p_h}\nabla_\perp^2\delta p_h$
(both $10^{-6}$ normalized; registry lacks a symbol for numerical diffusion
coefficients, see Notation map).

**EP gyrokinetics** (Eqs. 5–7, from Fu et al. 2006 / M3D-K), with
$\langle\cdot\rangle$ the gyro-average,
$\delta\mathbf E=-\nabla\delta\Phi-(\partial_t\delta A_\parallel)\mathbf b$,
$\delta\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\delta A_\parallel\mathbf b)$,
$\mu=m_hv_\perp^2/2B$:

$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{d\mathbf X}{dt}&=\frac{1}{B^*_\parallel}\Big\{v_{\parallel p}\mathbf B^*-\mathbf b\times\Big[\langle\delta\mathbf E\rangle-\frac{\mu}{q_h}\nabla(B+\langle\delta B\rangle)\Big]\Big\},\qquad
m_h\frac{dv_{\parallel p}}{dt}=\frac{q_h}{B^*_\parallel}\mathbf B^*\cdot\Big[\langle\delta\mathbf E\rangle-\frac{\mu}{q_h}\nabla(B+\langle\delta B\rangle)\Big],\\
\mathbf B^*&=\mathbf B+\langle\delta\mathbf B\rangle+\frac{m_h}{q_h}v_{\parallel p}\nabla\times\mathbf b,\qquad B^*_\parallel=\mathbf B^*\cdot\mathbf b .
\end{aligned}$$

Particle nonlinearity "is included fully"; component forms in $(x,y,z)$ are
Eqs. 10–12.

**δf** (Eqs. 15–19). $w_p=\delta f/g$ with marker density $g$,
$dw_p/dt=-(f/g-w_p)f_0^{-1}df_0/dt$, $f_0=f_0(P_\phi,\mathcal E,\mu)$
(slowing-down or Maxwellian). $P_\phi$ is approximated by its flux part,
$P_\phi\approx-q_h\psi$, in all runs. Markers are loaded uniformly in phase
space by Monte Carlo rejection with Jacobian weight.

**Coupling** (Eqs. 20–22). $\delta p_{\parallel h}$, $\delta p_{\perp h}$ are
marker sums of $m_hv_{\parallel p}^2w_p$ and $\tfrac12m_hv_\perp^2w_p$; the
scalar entering Eq. 1 is printed as $(\delta p_{\parallel h}+\delta p_{\perp h})/2$
(Citations). Scatter is trilinear to 8 nodes; for gyrokinetic markers a
pull-back averages over the gyro-points (Eq. 22).

**Gyro-average** (Eqs. 13–14). $N=4$ points at Larmor radius
$\rho_L=m_hv_\perp/q_hB$, converted to grid offsets through the local
non-orthogonality angle between the $x$ and $y$ directions
($\cos$ = normalized radial–parallel metric element); the binormal offset
enforces equal $\phi$.

**Coordinates** (Eqs. 8–9). $x=(\psi_N-\psi_{N,1})/\Delta\psi_N$,
$y=\theta$ (PEST), $z=\phi-\int_0^\theta q\,d\theta'$; twist-shift
$\delta\Phi(x,y+2\pi,z)=\delta\Phi(x,y,z-2\pi q)$; axis excluded. Radial
ideal-conductor conditions $\delta\varpi=\delta\Phi=\delta j_\parallel=0$,
$\partial_\psi\delta p_b=0$. Uniform grid, $x\in[0,1]$, $y,z$ cell-centred on
$[-\pi,\pi]$. Equilibrium quantities and derivatives (including the second
derivative of $B$) precomputed in an equilibrium file: analytic circular
equilibrium ($O(\varepsilon^2)$) or VMEC (DESC possible). Equations expanded
symbolically and coded automatically (Part I).

**Discretization and time.** Five-point fourth-order differences in all
directions; RK4 for both MHD and particles with the same
$\Delta t\approx0.01\tau_A$; trilinear gather/scatter; single-$n$ Fourier
filter each step, domain $1/n$ of the torus.

**Solver and parallelism.** 2D Poisson in $(x,z)$ per CPU by PARDISO with
TBB; MPI decomposition in $y$ with ghost cells and particle migration;
particles leaving $x\notin[0,1]$ are discarded. CPU only (Tianhe clusters).

**Resolution and cost.** $256\times64\times16$, $5\times10^6$ markers, 896
cores. Table I (core-hours): $n=3$ TAE, $2\times10^4$ steps, 353.13
(drift-kinetic) / 2838.60 (gyrokinetic); $n=6$ TAE, $3\times10^4$ steps,
637.47 / 3429.64; $n=4$ RSAE, $9\times10^4$ steps, — / 11 407.92.

**Normalization.** Length $\sqrt{\Delta\psi/B_0}$ (per-radian flux extent),
velocity the axis $v_A$, $\tau_A=R_0/v_A$, $\omega_A=1/\tau_A$.

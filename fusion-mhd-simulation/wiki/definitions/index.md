# Definitions

Precise domain terms.

* [Ballooning mode](/definitions/ballooning-mode.md) — Pressure-driven, high-n MHD instability localized on the outboard (bad-curvature) side of flux surfaces, whose ideal growth rate rises with n without bound in the absence of stabilizing flows; the standard linear verification case of edge and stellarator MHD codes.
* [Bootstrap current and its MHD implementation](/definitions/bootstrap-current.md) — The neoclassical parallel current driven by density and temperature gradients in toroidal geometry, and its inclusion in MHD codes as a non-inductive current J_x in Ohm's law toward which the plasma current relaxes resistively; M3D-C1 uses Sauter or Redl fits (mapped to quasi-symmetric stellarators), JOREK a Sauter-evolved toroidal source current.
* [Disruption: phases, current quench and current spike](/definitions/disruption.md) — A disruption is the rapid loss of thermal energy and then of plasma current of a tokamak discharge; it proceeds through a trigger phase, a thermal quench, a transient plasma-current spike and a current quench, and its consequences (heat loads, electromagnetic wall forces, runaway-electron beams) are what nonlinear MHD codes are asked to predict and mitigate.
* [Edge-localized mode (ELM) and ELM-free regimes](/definitions/edge-localized-mode.md) — Repetitive edge MHD instability of the H-mode pedestal, set off by coupled peeling-ballooning modes, that expels pedestal energy and particles in filaments; with the naturally ELM-free or small-ELM regimes (QH-mode, EDA H-mode, QCE) and the active control methods (RMPs, pellets, vertical kicks) as reproduced by nonlinear MHD codes.
* [Electromagnetic wall forces (vertical and sideways)](/definitions/wall-forces.md) — The electromagnetic wall force is the integral of the Lorentz force over the conducting structures, computed as a Maxwell-stress surface integral; its vertical component is set mainly by the axisymmetric VDE and current quench, its horizontal (sideways) component by low-n kinks and 3D halo currents, and its toroidal rotation matters for resonance with the vessel.
* [Energetic particle mode (EPM) and frequency chirping](/definitions/energetic-particle-mode.md) — Alfvénic mode whose existence and frequency are set by the energetic particles themselves rather than by a gap eigenmode of the bulk plasma, typically sitting in the shear-Alfvén continuum and dominated by a single poloidal harmonic; nonlinearly its frequency sweeps (chirps), and the codes of the NLED-AUG benchmark disagree on the sweep direction.
* [Fishbone instability](/definitions/fishbone.md) — Energetic-particle-modified n = 1 internal kink that acquires a finite real frequency through resonance with trapped energetic particles; benchmarked linearly in this bundle by M3D-C1-K against M3D-K and NIMROD.
* [Grad–Shafranov equation](/definitions/grad-shafranov-equation.md) — The axisymmetric ideal-MHD force balance J×B = ∇p written as an elliptic equation for the poloidal flux per radian, Δ*ψ = −μ0R²p′(ψ) − FF′(ψ); the nonlinear MHD codes solve it on their own finite-element basis to obtain a discretely balanced initial state.
* [Halo current](/definitions/halo-current.md) — The halo current is the current that flows partly in the scrape-off layer and closes through conducting structures in contact with it; its poloidal magnitude (halo fraction), toroidal peaking factor and 3D filamentary structure set the largest wall forces of VDEs, and its modelling depends on sheath and ion-saturation-current boundary conditions and on how the MHD code lets current cross into the wall.
* [Quasi-symmetry](/definitions/quasi-symmetry.md) — Property of a stellarator field whose strength, in Boozer coordinates, depends on the flux label and on a single helical angle combination only, B = B(ψ, Mθ − Nζ); it gives guiding-centre conservation analogous to axisymmetry and lets tokamak neoclassical results, such as bootstrap-current fits, be mapped to the stellarator.
* [Resistive internal kink (m/n = 1/1)](/definitions/resistive-internal-kink.md) — The m/n = 1/1 mode resonant at the q = 1 surface: a rigid helical shift of the core inside q = 1 that resistivity turns into a reconnecting mode with gamma ~ eta^(1/3); its ideal, pressure-driven limit has a poloidal-beta threshold, and reduced MHD models fail to reproduce that limit at finite beta.
* [Resistive tearing mode](/definitions/tearing-mode.md) — Current-driven resistive instability that reconnects the magnetic field at a rational surface q = m/n and forms magnetic islands; its linear growth rate follows gamma ~ eta^(3/5) at low resistivity, the standard verification target of nonlinear MHD codes in tokamak and stellarator geometry.
* [Reversed-shear Alfvén eigenmode (RSAE)](/definitions/reversed-shear-alfven-eigenmode.md) — Shear-Alfvén eigenmode localized at the minimum of a non-monotonic safety-factor profile, dominated by the single harmonic m ≈ n q_min, whose frequency rises with toroidal mode number and follows q_min in time; benchmarked in this bundle on DIII-D #159243.
* [Runaway electrons and their generation mechanisms](/definitions/runaway-electrons.md) — Electrons accelerated to relativistic energies when the parallel electric field exceeds the critical field set by collisional drag; in disruptions they form after the thermal quench by primary (Dreicer, hot-tail, tritium decay, Compton) and secondary (avalanche) generation and can carry most of the plasma current as a beam.
* [Shattered pellet injection and massive material injection](/definitions/shattered-pellet-injection.md) — Massive material injection (MMI) is the deliberate injection of large amounts of deuterium and/or impurities to trigger a controlled disruption; shattered pellet injection (SPI), the basis of the ITER disruption mitigation system, breaks a cryogenic pellet into fragments whose ablation is modelled in MHD codes by per-fragment ablation laws and localized density sources.
* [Thermal quench](/definitions/thermal-quench.md) — The thermal quench is the disruption phase in which the plasma loses most of its thermal energy, by parallel transport along stochastic field lines opened by MHD modes, by impurity radiation, or both; its duration (0.1 ms in NSTX to about 20 ms predicted for unmitigated ITER) is sensitive to parallel heat conduction, impurity atomic model, boundary conditions and numerical stabilization.
* [Toroidal Alfvén eigenmode (TAE)](/definitions/toroidal-alfven-eigenmode.md) — Discrete shear-Alfvén eigenmode in the toroidicity-induced gap between the continua of two neighbouring poloidal harmonics m and m+1, located near q = (m+1/2)/n and destabilized by resonant energetic particles; the standard verification target of hybrid kinetic-MHD codes.
* [Vertical displacement event (hot and cold VDE)](/definitions/vertical-displacement-event.md) — A vertical displacement event is the axisymmetric vertical instability of an elongated plasma after loss of position control, slowed to the wall time by eddy currents; in a hot VDE the thermal quench is caused by the displacement itself and low-n external kinks ($0<nq_a<m$) make the late phase 3D, in a cold VDE the displacement follows the thermal quench.

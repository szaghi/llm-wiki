# Software

Codes embodying methods.

* [BOUT++](/software/bout-plus-plus.md) — Finite-difference plasma-fluid framework for 3D initial-value problems in field-aligned coordinates with quasi-ballooning derivatives and CVODE BDF time integration; a hybrid 2D/1D field solver removes the flute ordering for n = 0 and low-n modes, enabling full-annulus edge simulations, and its ideal-ballooning growth rates agree with ELITE and JOREK within 2–4 %.
* [CARIDDI](/software/cariddi.md) — 3D magneto-quasi-static integral eddy-current code that discretizes only the conductors with edge-element current bases; coupled two-way and fully implicitly to reduced-MHD JOREK through a virtual-casing equivalent surface current, it adds 3D volumetric walls with ports to nonlinear MHD and agrees with JOREK–STARWALL within 2 % (VDE) and 1 % (tearing).
* [CLT](/software/clt.md) — Zhejiang University explicit 3D toroidal MHD code: fourth-order finite differences on a uniform cylindrical (R, φ, Z) grid, fourth-order Runge–Kutta, a cut-cell embedded boundary and an OpenACC GPU port; benchmarked against M3D-C1 on the 2/1 resistive tearing mode and the 1/1 resistive kink.
* [DESC](/software/desc.md) — Pseudospectral 3D ideal-MHD inverse equilibrium code in Python/JAX that expands the flux-surface geometry and stream function in a global Fourier–Zernike basis and drives the force residual to zero at collocation nodes with a Newton-type solver; against VMEC it converges exponentially in radial resolution, stays accurate at the axis and reaches about tenfold lower force error at equal runtime.
* [GMEC](/software/gmec.md) — Zhejiang University gyrokinetic-MHD energetic-particle code: linearized reduced MHD (models A–C) in global field-aligned coordinates with fourth-order finite differences, RK4, shifted-metric stabilization and C++20 compile-time symbolic equation generation, plus gyrokinetic δf energetic particles coupled by pressure; benchmarked against MAS on ballooning and tearing modes and against M3D-K and multi-code results on TAEs and RSAEs.
* [HYMAGYC](/software/hymagyc.md) — Hybrid code coupling linear resistive full MHD (an initial-value derivative of the MARS eigenvalue solver, backward Euler, Fourier × finite elements) to gyrokinetic δf energetic-particle PIC through pressure coupling, with an optional Cartesian (R, Z) stress-tensor grid that filters numerical noise; a reference code of the NLED-AUG benchmarks.
* [JOREK](/software/jorek.md) — Fully implicit nonlinear extended-MHD code for tokamak X-point plasmas: ansatz-based reduced and full visco-resistive MHD on G1 Bézier finite elements times a toroidal Fourier series, linearized Crank–Nicolson/BDF2 steps solved by GMRES with a per-harmonic block preconditioner, free boundary through STARWALL or CARIDDI, and fluid, kinetic and particle extensions for impurities, neutrals, pellets, runaway and energetic particles.
* [JOREK3D](/software/jorek3d.md) — Stellarator extension of JOREK: a reduced MHD model built on the vacuum-field potential, an analytic Dommaschk representation of that field, and flux-surface-aligned Bézier grids whose geometry varies toroidally, initialized from GVEC and benchmarked against CASTOR3D on Wendelstein 7-A tearing (within 13.2 % in a β scan, 26.2 % in an η scan) and ballooning modes (within 6.4 %).
* [M3D-C1](/software/m3d-c1.md) — PPPL implicit 3D extended-MHD code with C1 reduced-quintic (Bell) triangles in the poloidal plane and Hermite cubic elements toroidally, scalar-potential representations of the field and flow, split-implicit time stepping, a meshed vacuum and thick resistive conductors inside the domain, stellarator geometry and a bootstrap-current closure; benchmarked against CLT, JOREK and NIMROD.
* [M3D-C1-K](/software/m3d-c1-k.md) — Kinetic-MHD hybrid built on M3D-C1: δf energetic-particle markers pushed by RK4 guiding-centre integration or a GPU-accelerated slow-manifold Boris algorithm, coupled to the implicit finite-element MHD by pressure or current coupling, which the authors derive to be equivalent; benchmarked on the linear n=1 fishbone, the ITPA n=6 TAE and the DIII-D RSAE cases.
* [MEGA](/software/mega.md) — Hybrid code coupling nonlinear full MHD to drift-kinetic δf energetic particles (with an ad-hoc gyro-average for FLR) through the energetic-particle current on a cylindrical grid; a reference code of the ITPA TAE, AUG-NLED and DIII-D RSAE benchmarks, it saturates about 15× lower and redistributes energetic particles much less than HYMAGYC in the nonlinear NLED-AUG benchmark.
* [NIMROD](/software/nimrod.md) — Initial-value extended-MHD code with high-order C0 nodal spectral elements in the poloidal plane and a toroidal Fourier series, a mixed implicit/semi-implicit leapfrog time advance, diffusive divergence cleaning and a thin resistive wall with a meshed vacuum; benchmarked on NSTX VDEs and impurity gas injection, and extended with a plasma–neutral model whose atomic sources are coupled by Crank–Nicolson/JFNK or Strang splitting.
* [ORB5](/software/orb5.md) — Global nonlinear electromagnetic gyrokinetic particle-in-cell code with all species kinetic (bulk ions, electrons, energetic particles), δf markers pushed by RK4 and fields on cubic B-spline finite elements; in the NLED-AUG energetic-particle benchmark it gives growth rates 1.6–1.7× MEGA's, lower frequencies, upward chirping where the hybrid codes chirp down, and stronger edge transport.
* [SCELT](/software/scelt.md) — Symbolic computation aided eigenvalue and linear code for tokamaks: a C++/MATLAB linear eigenvalue code for compressible resistive MHD in axisymmetric flux coordinates whose equations are derived and discretized automatically (poloidal Fourier, radial cubic finite elements), verified on the resistive internal kink (η^0.3766), ideal-kink β_p threshold and 2/1 tearing (η^0.5590) scalings.
* [STARWALL](/software/starwall.md) — Green's-function thin-wall code that supplies JOREK's free-boundary and resistive-wall response: the vacuum is not meshed, 3D thin walls with holes are linear triangles, and the response matrices enter JOREK implicitly as a natural boundary condition with the wall currents eliminated; validated against CEDRES++, DINA, CASTOR3D, M3D-C1, NIMROD and JOREK–CARIDDI.
* [VMEC](/software/vmec.md) — Variational moments equilibrium code: 3D ideal-MHD inverse equilibria with nested surfaces, Fourier series in the angles and radial finite differences, found by minimizing the MHD energy with a second-order Richardson iteration and multigrid radial continuation; first-order accurate in radius and inaccurate near the axis, it is the standard equilibrium source of the stellarator and EP codes in the cited sources.
* [XTOR-K](/software/xtor-k.md) — Global hybrid code coupling two-fluid MHD to full-f, full-orbit (Boris–Buneman) kinetic ions through a kinetic pressure term, with the implicit Newton–Krylov fluid step and the particle push nested in a Picard iteration; in the NLED-AUG benchmark it reproduces the near-axis EPM at 166 kHz and the counter-rotating TAE at −196 kHz, but its growth rates are buried in full-f noise.

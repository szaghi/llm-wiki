# Results

Theorems, proved properties and impossibility results.

* [Electron temperature hole and the stochastic barrier at q = 1 after SPI](/results/electron-temperature-hole.md) — In JOREK simulations of neon-deuterium SPI into ITER L-mode, transient electron temperature holes form along field lines through the fragment plume because Braginskii parallel conduction collapses in the cold radiating cloud, raising local resistivity and the MHD response; and a transport barrier of the stochastic field just outside q = 1 confines the core during the early thermal quench until edge modes grow.
* [High-field-side high-density region](/results/high-field-side-high-density-region.md) — In JOREK simulations of the ITER PFPO-1 H-mode with kinetic neutrals, poloidal E×B drifts trap a dense, cold, low-pressure region in a dominant high-field-side E×B vortex near the X-point; it forms dynamically over 10–20 ms, only when drifts are included, with a phase-like transition of the upstream density and full inner-target detachment.
* [Reduced-MHD boundary condition suppresses the toroidal asymmetry of the plasma current](/results/reduced-mhd-current-asymmetry.md) — In ansatz-based reduced MHD the poloidal electric field is a pure potential gradient and the boundary condition on the normal velocity makes the potential constant on the boundary, so the boundary acts as a poloidal ideal conductor, no current crosses it and the plasma current is the same in every poloidal plane; JOREK therefore shows below 0.01% toroidal asymmetry of $I_p$ in a 3D VDE where full-MHD codes find 1-4% and experiments 10-20%.
* [Runaway current reconstitution after an MHD flush](/results/runaway-reconstitution-after-mhd-flush.md) — After a low-Z purge triggers large-scale MHD that stochastizes the field and expels a runaway beam (benign termination), magnetically trapped relativistic electrons survive as a seed of 0.1–1% of the beam and avalanche back once the flux surfaces reheal, so an ITER-like beam loses only about 2–3 MA before it reforms; 3D MHD simulations of the flush itself find more than 95% prompt loss and conversion of the current to Ohmic current.
* [Tearing triggered by impurity radiation through resistive-interchange reversal](/results/radiation-triggered-tearing.md) — NIMROD/KPRAD simulations show that neon deposited on the q = 2 surface of a nominally tearing-stable tokamak plasma triggers a 2/1 tearing mode above a threshold impurity density, because radiative cooling makes the pressure locally hollow and reverses the sign of the Glasser–Greene–Johnson resistive-interchange parameter D_R; the classical radiation-exceeds-Ohmic-heating criterion does not set the threshold, and fast parallel conduction suppresses the mode.
* [Vertical-force mitigation by massive material injection](/results/vertical-force-mitigation.md) — Massive impurity injection during a VDE reduces the vertical wall force because the cooled plasma carries a large, predominantly toroidal current in a broad scrape-off layer, which decouples the current centroid from the magnetic-axis motion and reduces its vertical excursion; the poloidal halo current stays moderate.
